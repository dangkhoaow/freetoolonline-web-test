Frequently Asked Questions

How deep is Colca Canyon?

About 3,270 m (10,725 ft) at its deepest, along roughly 100 km in the Arequipa region of southern Peru - one of the deepest canyons on Earth.

What carved Colca Canyon?

The Colca River, cutting through the Andes over a long span of geologic time.

What are the terraces on the canyon walls?

Pre-Inca agricultural terraces built by the Collagua and Cabana peoples. Some have been farmed continuously for more than 1,000 years.

Where can I see Andean condors at Colca Canyon?

The Cruz del Condor viewpoint is the most visited spot - condors ride rising air currents over the gorge, with a wingspan up to about 3.2 m, the widest of any flying bird.

Is Colca Canyon deeper than the Grand Canyon?

Yes - at about 3,270 m it is roughly twice as deep as the Grand Canyon's approximately 1.86 km.

Is this a geological simulation?

No. It is an educational explorer using published figures - the terrace steps, gorge profile, and condor markers are procedural teaching cues.
