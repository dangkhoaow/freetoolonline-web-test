Frequently Asked Questions

What does Yutyrannus mean?

"Yutyrannus" (Chinese: 禹氏暴龍) means "beautiful feathered tyrant." The name honors both the Tujia people of Liaoning, China (yu) and its feathered appearance.

Was Yutyrannus a real dinosaur?

Yes, Yutyrannus huali was a real Early Cretaceous dinosaur discovered in 2012. It was an ancestor of the famous T-rex lineage (Tyrannosauroidea), living about 125 million years ago in what is now northeastern China.

Did Yutyrannus have feathers?

Yes - and this is real, not speculative. Proto-feathers covering Yutyrannus's body were preserved in the 125-million-year-old Yixian Formation fossils. Multiple complete skeletons show evidence of primitive feather structures similar to those found on other feathered dinosaurs from China.

How big was Yutyrannus?

Yutyrannus was about 9 to 9.5 meters (30 feet) long and weighed 1.2 to 1.4 tonnes. It was smaller than its famous descendant T-rex, but still a major predator of its time. The size-comparison toggle in this viewer shows it next to a human figure for reference.

What color was Yutyrannus?

We don't know - fossils don't preserve color or skin tone. The colors shown in this 3D viewer are an artistic reconstruction. Scientists have no evidence of Yutyrannus's actual coloring, so the reconstruction is informed by modern animals and biomechanics, not by fossil evidence.

The skeletal proportions and general anatomy are based on the published fossil descriptions.
