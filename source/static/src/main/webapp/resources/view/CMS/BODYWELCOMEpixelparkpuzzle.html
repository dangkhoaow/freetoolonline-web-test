Welcome to Pixel Park Puzzle - a free browser parking game you can play without an account or install. Press Play to load the ~132 KB canvas engine in a same-origin iframe, then steer each vehicle into its marked bay while you dodge the cones and walls that block the route.

Controls

  • W / S - throttle forward and reverse.
  • A / D - steer left and right.
  • Escape - pause the current level.
  • Digit2 - cycle to the next vehicle when a level has more than one.

Privacy and progress

Progress stays in your current session only and is never uploaded. Nothing leaves this device except the initial page and the game files served from this site.
