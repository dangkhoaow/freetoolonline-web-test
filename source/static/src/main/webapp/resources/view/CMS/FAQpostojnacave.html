Frequently Asked Questions

How long is Postojna Cave?

The Postojna-Planina cave system runs more than 24 km, though the guided visitor route covers about 5 km of it.

What is the Concert Hall?

Postojna Cave's largest chamber, with a floor area of about 3,000 sq m, occasionally used to host concerts.

How do visitors get through the cave?

By a small underground railway first laid in 1872 and later electrified, covering most of the roughly 5 km round trip, plus about 1.7 km on foot.

What lives in Postojna Cave?

Proteus anguinus, the olm - a blind, pale cave salamander found only in the Dinaric karst - lives in the cave's water and is on view in an underground vivarium along the route.

Where is Postojna Cave?

Near the town of Postojna in the Karst region of southwestern Slovenia, carved through limestone by the Pivka River.

Is this a geological simulation?

No. It is an educational explorer using published cave figures - the hall shapes, dripstone columns, and route line are procedural teaching cues.
