Niagara Falls 3D Explorer

Last reviewed: 2026-08-01

Reviewed by FreetoolOnline editorial team

This browser explorer shows Niagara Falls as its two principal cataracts along one shared crestline: the curved Horseshoe Falls on the Canadian side and the straight-edged American Falls on the US side, split by Goat Island, dropping into the Niagara Gorge below, on the border between Ontario, Canada, and New York, USA.

Niagara Falls formed as the last ice age ended and meltwater carved a path over the Niagara Escarpment. Erosion has since pushed the falls about 11 km (7 mi) upstream from where they first formed near Queenston, carving the gorge the river now runs through. About 2,832 cubic meters of water pour over the crestline every second, but the split is uneven: Horseshoe Falls, with its longer curved crest, carries about 90% of that flow, while American Falls carries the remaining 10%. About 4.5 km (2.8 mi) downstream, the river makes a sharp bend and forms the Niagara Whirlpool, a churning basin where the current doubles back on itself.

  • Toggle flow-split colors to see the roughly 90/10 split between Horseshoe and American Falls
  • Toggle the whirlpool marker to find the sharp bend in the gorge downstream
  • Read flow rate, height, width, and gorge length in the panel
  • Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom
  • Runs fully in the browser with the vendored three.js engine - no account, no upload
