Zhangye Danxia's rounded hills are crossed by tilted bands of red, orange, yellow and green rock. Toggle the golden-hour lighting to see how the colors deepen at low sun angles, and the viewing boardwalk markers that protect the fragile rock surface.

About this scene

Published figures: the Zhangye Danxia Geopark covers roughly 300 sq km at elevations around 2,000-3,800 m; the colored layers built up over about 24 million years before tectonic uplift and erosion exposed them.
