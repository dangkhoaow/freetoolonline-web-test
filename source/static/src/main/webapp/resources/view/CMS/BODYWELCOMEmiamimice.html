Welcome to Miami Mice - a free browser beat-em-up with no account or install. Fight feline martial artists on a procedural canvas stage.

Controls

  • Move: WASD on the keyboard, or the left stick on a standard gamepad.
  • Punch: H, or a face button on a gamepad.
  • Kick: J, or a face button on a gamepad.
  • Block: B, or a face button on a gamepad.

Game modes

Choose Story mode to clear waves, or Horde mode for endless pressure.
