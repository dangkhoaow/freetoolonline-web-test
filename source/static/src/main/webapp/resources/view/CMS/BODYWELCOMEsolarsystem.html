All eight planets orbit the sun live on screen - drag to orbit the view, scroll or pinch to zoom, and watch inner planets lap the outer ones exactly as the real period ratios dictate.


Rings, colors, and fullscreen view

  • Saturn is drawn with its ring, and every planet carries its own color.
  • Orbit paths trace thin rings around the sun.
  • Go fullscreen and the orbit controls stay visible on screen.

Runs in your browser

It runs in the browser and needs WebGL, so on a lower-powered phone the scene automatically trims antialiasing and lightens the render to keep the orbits moving smoothly instead of stuttering.
