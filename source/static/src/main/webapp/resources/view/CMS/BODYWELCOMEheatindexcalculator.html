Heat Index Calculator

Last reviewed: 2026-08-04

Enter the air temperature and the relative humidity to get the heat index - the apparent (feels-like) temperature the National Weather Service uses to gauge heat-related risk - along with its official NWS risk category, updated live as you type.

The heat index is computed with the same Rothfusz-regression formula published by the National Weather Service, not a rough estimate. It combines temperature and humidity because high humidity slows the body's ability to cool itself through sweat evaporation, so the same temperature can feel far hotter on a humid day.

The temperature and humidity you type stay in the browser - the heat index is computed on this device, with no upload and no account.
