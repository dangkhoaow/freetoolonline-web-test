Sunrise & Sunset Calculator Step By Step

Last reviewed: 2026-08-04

Sunrise & Sunset Calculator runs in three steps: pick a location, pick a date, then read the result table. This page walks through what each step expects.


Step 1 - pick a location

Go to Sunrise & Sunset Calculator. Choose a city from the dropdown, tap Use my location, or type a latitude and longitude directly. Nothing needs to be installed or signed in.


Step 2 - pick a date

The date field defaults to today. Change it to any past or future date to see how sunrise, sunset, and day length shift for that day at the location you picked.


Step 3 - read the result

The result table shows sunrise, solar noon, sunset, day length, golden hour, and civil dawn/dusk, all in your device's own time zone. Change the location or date again at any time; the table updates immediately.

The calculation runs locally in the browser - no upload and no account.
