Walking the controls, preset by preset

Five moves cover the whole scene. Run them in order and the baseline, the Proxima reading, and the definition of the parsec each land on their own.

  1. Press Earth baseline to see the opposite-orbit ends that form the 2 AU teaching base of the triangle.
  2. Press Proxima 0.7687" and read the panel distance of about 1.30 pc that d = 1/p produces.
  3. Press 1 arcsec = 1 pc, the case that defines the parsec, then 0.5 arcsec to double the distance to 2 pc.
  4. Press d = 1/p to fill the facts panel with the formula and the standard conversions.
  5. Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, and press Fullscreen to keep the controls visible on a projector.

Where the schematic stops

Everything renders on your device with WebGL. The 3D engine loads once (about 0.7 MB) and is cached; no scene data is sent to a server.

The scene is an educational visualization - sight-line angles are exaggerated so the triangle stays readable. It is not a Hipparcos or Gaia reduction pipeline or a precise ephemeris. Proxima Centauri at about 0.7687 arcsec is a teaching preset, not a live catalog query. Catalog reductions, true-to-scale sub-arcsecond geometry, magnitude scales, and galactic-arm tours all sit outside it.
