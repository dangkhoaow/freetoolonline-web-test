Html Minifier Online Step By Step

Last reviewed: 2026-09-03

HTML Minifier Online runs in three steps: open the tool, provide your input, then copy the result. This page walks through what each step expects.


Step 1 - open the tool

Go to HTML Minifier Online. Nothing needs to be installed or signed in before you start.


Step 2 - provide your input

Original line layout is preserved in the source pane for diffing.


Step 3 - copy the result

Minification runs locally; nothing is uploaded.

For unminification of an already-minified block, see the related-tools section.
