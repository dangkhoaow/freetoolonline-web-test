Pipe Rotate Puzzle

Last reviewed: 2026-07-18

Pipe Rotate Puzzle is a link-the-pipes game that runs right in your browser. Rotate each tile until every cell connects back to the power source, and clear the board to move up a level.

Every board is a fresh layout, not just a bigger one

Clearing a level does more than add tiles. The puzzle builds each board procedurally and cycles through five layout styles, so the routing challenge keeps changing even between boards of the same size. Because every board is generated on the fly, no two runs hand you the same pipes twice.

  • Maze and grid layouts.
  • Sparse boards give you fewer tiles to rotate.
  • Dense boards pack the grid.
  • Symmetric boards lay out a mirrored pattern to read.

Your level and theme stay on this device

The grid starts at 3x3 and grows toward 7x7 as you clear levels, so progress is worth keeping. Your current level and your chosen theme - olive or light, set in the settings drawer - are saved in this browser under ftol:piperotatepuzzle keys, and a return visit resumes where you left off. Starting a new game clears the saved level, and nothing is uploaded.
