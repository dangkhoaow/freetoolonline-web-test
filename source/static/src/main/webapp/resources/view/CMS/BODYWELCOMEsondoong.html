Son Doong Cave - World's Largest Natural Cave

Son Doong Cave, located in Quang Binh Province, Vietnam, holds the title of the world's largest natural cave by volume. Its main passage stretches approximately 5.5 to 9 kilometers, with a chamber width of 200 to 240 meters and heights reaching 80 to 200 meters depending on the section. Inside this vast cavern lies a unique ecosystem - a primeval jungle that grows in the dim light filtering through a sinkhole, alongside an underground river that flows through the cave floor.

The cave's name, "Son Doong," translates to "mountain river cave" in Vietnamese. The stalactites hanging from the ceiling and stalagmites rising from the floor have formed over millions of years, creating a spectacular display of geological artistry. When you toggle bioluminescence mode, you'll see the faint glow of cave-dwelling organisms and the luminous vegetation that thrives in this underground world.
