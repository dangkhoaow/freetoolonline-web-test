Frequently Asked Questions

How big was Maiasaura?

The Natural History Museum lists length about 9.0 m and mass about 2500 kg. Standing height is about 2.5 m at the hips for scale consistency. Turn on the human figure to judge the scale beside a 1.8 m person.

Why is Maiasaura called the good mother lizard?

Horner and Makela named it Maiasaura in 1979 (type M. peeblesorum) after nest colonies and nestling fossils in Montana that suggested parental care at the nest - the name means "good mother lizard."

Where did Maiasaura live?

Fossils come from the USA in the Late Cretaceous, about 80-75 million years ago (NHM Dino Directory).

Is the model scientifically accurate?

The body proportions follow published reconstructions, but the skin color and texture are an artistic reconstruction. Fossils preserve bone, not color. This model is not a fossil-accurate skeleton. The figures shown are real published values with ranges where sources disagree.

Did Maiasaura have a tall head crest?

No. Maiasaura is a hadrosaurine duckbill without the tall bony crest of lambeosaurines such as Corythosaurus or Parasaurolophus. It has a broad duck-billed snout.
