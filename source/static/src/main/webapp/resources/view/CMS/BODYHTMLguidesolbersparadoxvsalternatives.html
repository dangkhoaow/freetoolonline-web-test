Where the Olbers' Paradox Explorer wins

The Olbers' Paradox Explorer wins when the question is specifically why the night sky is dark - it is the only page on the site that puts the naive infinite-static model side by side with the real finite-age model to answer that exact puzzle, with the historical dates attached.


Where the Expanding Universe Explorer wins

The Expanding Universe Explorer wins when the question is the expansion mechanism itself - it time-scrubs a stretching galaxy grid directly, which the paradox explorer does not show at all.


A reasonable rule

As a reasonable rule, use the Olbers' Paradox Explorer first to understand why the dark night sky itself is a real puzzle with a real history, then use the Expanding Universe Explorer to see the stretching-space mechanism that is part of the modern answer.
