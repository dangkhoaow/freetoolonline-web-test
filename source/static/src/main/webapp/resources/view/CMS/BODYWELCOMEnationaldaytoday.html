What National Day Is Today?

Last reviewed: 2026-08-05

Look up which "National Day" calendar-tradition observance (if any) falls on today's date.

Covers commonly observed US calendar-tradition days (Pi Day, Star Wars Day, National Coffee Day, and similar) - informal calendar traditions, not an exhaustive or official registry, and not government holidays.


Key features

  • Checks today's date against a curated list of about 35 widely-recognized US "National Day" observances and shows the match, if any, the moment the page loads.
  • When no listed observance falls on today's date, shows a clear "no listed national day for today" message instead of guessing.
  • Renders the full curated list of all 35 national days, sorted by calendar date, as a plain HTML table below the result.
  • Computes the date from your device's own clock with no upload, no account, and no network request.

How it works

The check runs fresh from your device's own clock every time the page loads, so come back tomorrow and it reflects the new date automatically - there is nothing to refresh or update by hand.


Privacy and data handling

Everything computes locally in the browser from a curated static dataset. Nothing is uploaded, no account is needed, and the page makes no network request of any kind.


Controls hidden on this page

The country selector, holiday-countdown timer, and "Copy link" share button belong to this tool's separate holiday-countdown mode and stay hidden here.
