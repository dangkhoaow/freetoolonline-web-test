When to use Charon Serenity Chasma 3D Explorer

Use this explorer when you want to see Charon's Serenity Chasma scale and the freeze-expansion fracture story from New Horizons.

  • Comparing a >200 km chasma and ~1,800 km belt to Earth canyons
  • Introducing pull-apart tectonics on an icy moon
  • Discussing the ancient-ocean hypothesis without overclaiming a live ocean

This is an educational visualization, not a tectonic solver.
