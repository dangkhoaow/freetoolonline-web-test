Frequently Asked Questions

Why did Krakatoa collapse in 1883?

The 1883 eruption was one of the most violent in recorded history. It destroyed roughly two-thirds of the original island, collapsing most of it below sea level and leaving a submerged caldera behind.

Where is the new volcano growing from?

Anak Krakatau ("Child of Krakatoa") emerged from the sea near the middle of the flooded caldera in 1927 and has been growing intermittently since, through repeated small eruptions. Most of its bulk is still hidden below the waterline.

How loud was the 1883 eruption really?

It is commonly cited as one of the loudest sounds ever recorded, reportedly heard about 4,800 km away. The resulting tsunamis killed on the order of 36,000 people around the Sunda Strait.

What happened to Anak Krakatau in 2018?

In December 2018 a large flank collapse removed much of the young cone's height and volume and triggered another deadly tsunami. The volcano has continued rebuilding since.

Where is Krakatoa located?

In the Sunda Strait, Indonesia, between the islands of Java and Sumatra.

Is this a scientific terrain model?

No. It is an educational explorer using published figures - the caldera shape, remnant islands, cone, and 1883 outline are schematic teaching cues, not survey terrain.
