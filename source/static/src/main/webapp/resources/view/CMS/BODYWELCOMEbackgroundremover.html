AI Background Remover

Last reviewed: 2026-09-04

Remove the background from an image in your browser - your image never leaves your device. The AI model runs entirely client-side using your browser's GPU when available, or the WASM CPU backend as a fallback.

Pick the output you need: a transparent PNG ready to place on any new background, the same photo with its background blurred, or the subject on a solid color of your choice. Making a passport or visa photo? The Passport Photo Maker uses this same on-device model to put a compliant white, light-blue, or grey backdrop behind a photo cropped to an exact document size - and the model cache is shared, so it downloads nothing new.


Key features

  • Opens the local file you pick and renders it on an HTML5 canvas right in the browser - no upload and no server round-trip.
  • Exports the cut-out as a downloadable transparent PNG, built entirely client-side - or composites it over a blurred or solid-color background instead.
  • Keeps working with your network off once the model has cached, so there is no upload step to wait on.
  • Processes up to 12 images in one batch, each with its own download link.
  • Offers a no-download Instant engine for photos taken on a solid, even background - a deterministic color-key that never fetches a model at all.

Common uses

  • Product photos and e-commerce listings.
  • Profile pictures.
  • Social media content.
  • Any image where you want to swap or remove the background without installing an app or uploading to a server.

What to expect

  • There is no manual brush or eraser on this page - the optional Soften edges pass smooths the cut line slightly, but rough spots around hair, fur, or glass otherwise stay as the engine produced them.
  • The AI engine is tuned for people and portraits - pets, products, and busy scenes can come out patchy. For objects photographed on a plain backdrop, the Instant engine is usually the better pick.
  • The download is always a PNG - there is no JPG or WebP export option here.

Privacy and data handling

Because the file you pick is opened straight in the browser and the cut-out is built as a downloadable blob without a server round-trip, sensitive images - a passport photo, an unreleased product shot, a client's headshot - stay on your own machine from start to finish.


Which model does the cutting, and how it loads

The AI cut-out comes from MODNet, an openly licensed (Apache-2.0) portrait-matting model, run in-page through Transformers.js on top of ONNX Runtime Web - the same open in-browser AI stack behind a growing number of client-side tools, not a proprietary in-house model. The open license means you can check its terms or compare results against the same model running elsewhere. MODNet is trained on people, which is why portraits are its strength while pets, products, and cluttered scenes can come out patchy. It downloads once, about 26 MB, from the Hugging Face model hub, and the browser then keeps it cached so every later run needs no refetch and no network connection; the Instant engine sidesteps the model entirely and needs no download at all. Whichever engine you use, the mask is applied at your image's exact original width and height, so the PNG you download matches your upload's resolution rather than a downscaled preview.
