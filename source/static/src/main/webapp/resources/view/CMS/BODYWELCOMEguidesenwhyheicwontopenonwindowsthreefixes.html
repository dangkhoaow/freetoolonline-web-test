Three fixes at a glance

The three fixes trade setup effort against quality and offline use. This table lines them up so you can pick the right one in a single glance:

MethodSetup timeKeeps original quality?Works offline?
Fix 1 - HEIF Image Extensions~2 min installYes - 100% (adds a codec, no re-encode)Yes, after install
Fix 2 - IrfanView portable~1-3 min setupYes - 100%Yes
Fix 3 - convert HEIC to JPG online~30-60 sNo - slight loss (about 95%)No - needs a browser

Fix 1 and Fix 2 keep the photo at full quality because they only add a viewer or codec, never re-encode the file. Fix 3 is the fastest path and needs zero install, at the cost of a small one-time quality drop when the photo is written back out as JPG.

HEIC vs JPG vs PNG at a glance

FormatTypical size (12 MP photo)QualityOpens on Windows by default
HEIC~1.5 MBHighNo (Windows 10 and earlier)
JPG~3 MBHigh (lossy)Yes
PNG~8 MBLosslessYes

HEIC keeps high quality at about half the size of the same JPG, but older Windows needs a codec or a conversion to open it. Convert to JPG for the widest compatibility, or use PNG only when you need lossless output.
