Frequently Asked Questions

How tall are the mesas at Wadi Rum?

Jebel Rum, the tallest, reaches about 1,734 m above sea level - roughly 800 m of sheer cliff above the valley floor. It is Jordan's second-highest peak.

What is the Um Fruth Rock Bridge?

A natural rock arch about 15 m above the ground, one of several rock bridges scattered through Wadi Rum that visitors can walk across.

Why is Wadi Rum called the Valley of the Moon?

Its bare red sand, sheer sandstone cliffs, and near-total lack of vegetation give the valley an otherworldly look that has made it a stand-in for extraterrestrial landscapes in film.

What is desert varnish?

A thin dark coating of manganese and iron oxides that slowly builds up on exposed rock surfaces over long, undisturbed spans of time - visible as dark streaking near clifftops and rims.

Where is Wadi Rum?

In the Wadi Rum Protected Area in southern Jordan, a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2011 covering about 720 sq km of desert.

Is this a geological simulation?

No. It is an educational explorer using published park and geographic figures - the mesa field, rock bridge, and color bands are procedural teaching cues.
