Frequently Asked Questions

Why is the Mekong Delta called the Nine Dragons?

The Mekong splits into two branches on entering Vietnam, which further divide into distributaries reaching the sea - historically nine mouths, giving the Vietnamese name Cuu Long, "Nine Dragons." Two mouths have since silted shut, so maps commonly show seven to eight open today.

Why are the delta's channels muddy brown?

The Mekong carries sediment from its long run through six countries; that silt colors the channels brown upstream, lightening toward a more brackish tone near the sea mouths where tidal water mixes in.

Does the Mekong Delta really flood every year?

Yes - a seasonal monsoon flood pulse, roughly June to November, regularly raises water levels and inundates low-lying land near the channels. The flood-season toggle recolors those low-lying areas.

What is the Cai Rang floating market?

A large floating market on a channel near Can Tho, where boats gather each morning to trade produce - one of the most-visited floating markets in the Mekong Delta.

How long is the Mekong River, and which countries does it cross?

Commonly cited at about 4,350-4,900 km depending on the source, flowing through China, Myanmar, Laos, Thailand, Cambodia, and Vietnam before reaching the delta.

Is this a scientific terrain model?

No. It is an educational explorer using published figures - the channel network, plain profile, and vertical scale are schematic teaching cues, not survey hydrology.
