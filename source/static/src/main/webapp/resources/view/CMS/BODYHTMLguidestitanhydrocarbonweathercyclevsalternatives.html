Where the Methane Weather Cycle Explorer wins

The Methane Weather Cycle Explorer wins when the question is about TIME and PROCESS - it is the only page on the site that scrubs through a season to show clouds migrating and rain falling.


Where the Methane Lakes Explorer wins

The Methane Lakes Explorer wins when the question is about LOCATION and STRUCTURE - it toggles a cutaway to show the ice shell and subsurface ocean, which the weather-cycle explorer does not show at all.


A reasonable rule

As a reasonable rule, use the Methane Weather Cycle Explorer first to see how Titan's methane moves through its seasons, then use the Methane Lakes Explorer to see exactly where it ends up.
