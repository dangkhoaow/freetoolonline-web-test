On a laptop screen check, six solid color fields - red, green, blue, black, white, and yellow - each fill the display from edge to edge the instant you click them, and against that flat uniform background a dead pixel, a stuck pixel, or a patch of backlight bleed suddenly has nowhere left to hide, which is exactly why cycling through every color one by one catches faults a normal desktop never would. The whole check runs in your own browser tab, nothing is uploaded or stored, and a warm-grey wash that stays put on the black field - unlike a viewing-angle cast that fades when you tilt the lid - is the one worth a warranty call.
