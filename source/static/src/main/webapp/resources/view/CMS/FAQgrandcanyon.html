Frequently Asked Questions

How deep is the Grand Canyon?

About 1.86 km (over a mile) at its deepest, along a 446 km course that spreads up to 29 km wide.

What carved the Grand Canyon?

The Colorado River, aided by side-stream erosion - geologists date the main incision to roughly the last five to six million years.

How old are the rocks at the bottom?

The Vishnu Basement rocks near the river are about 1.7-1.8 billion years old - the canyon walls expose well over a billion years of Earth history.

What are the main rock layers?

Simplified from top to bottom: Kaibab Limestone, Coconino Sandstone, Hermit Shale, the Supai Group, Redwall Limestone, the Tonto Group, and the Vishnu Basement.

Where is the Grand Canyon?

In northern Arizona, USA - most of it inside Grand Canyon National Park, established in 1919.

Is this a geological simulation?

No. It is an educational explorer using published NPS and USGS figures - layer thicknesses and the stepped profile are schematic teaching cues.
