ROT13 Encoder

Last reviewed: 2026-07-26

Paste text and apply ROT13 in your browser - A-Z and a-z shift by 13; digits and punctuation stay as-is.

Output updates as you type; Copy puts the current result on the clipboard. ROT13 is its own inverse - run it again on the result to restore the original Latin letters.

Prefer this for puzzle spoilers, Usenet-style obfuscation demos, or quick letter-shift checks without installing an editor. Not encryption for secrecy - anyone who knows ROT13 can reverse it instantly.

Does not apply Caesar shifts other than 13, and does not transliterate non-Latin scripts. Nothing you paste is saved between visits.
