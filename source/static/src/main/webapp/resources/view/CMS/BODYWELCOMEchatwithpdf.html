Chat with PDF - Ask Your PDF Questions Privately

Last reviewed: 2026-08-13

Open a PDF and ask questions about it in plain language, right in this page. The file is never uploaded anywhere - the text is read out of the PDF in your browser, the matching passages are found on your device, and every answer cites the page numbers it came from, so you can check the source before relying on it.

What you get without downloading anything

Without any AI model, the tool still finds and shows the most relevant passages for your question, each labeled with its page number. On a long contract, manual, or report, that alone answers the everyday job of locating where the document covers something - type the question, jump to the cited page. A built-in sample document and sample question render a live result the moment the page loads, so you can see the output shape before opening your own file.

Optional on-device upgrades

Two upgrades are offered, and both stay on your device. A small matching model (about 25 MB, one-time download, cached for later visits) upgrades passage search from keyword overlap to matching by meaning, which helps when your wording differs from the document's. Separately, a small language model turns the matching passages into a written answer; its download size is read from the model list and shown before anything is fetched. If your browser ships built-in AI, the page uses it for written answers with zero download instead. The status line under the question box always names which engine produced the latest answer and which matching method ran.

Check the pages, not just the answer

Written answers come from a small on-device model drafting over the matching passages. That is useful for a quick first read, but it is not a professional summarizer - the passages and page numbers are printed with every answer precisely so you can verify the claim against the document itself before acting on it.

Scanned PDFs need OCR first

This tool reads the text layer of a PDF. A scanned or photographed document has no text layer, so it returns nothing here - the page tells you so instead of failing silently. Run the picture through the image-to-text OCR tool first, then ask your questions against the recognized text. Very long documents are read up to the first 300 pages, and the limit is stated in the tool itself.

When a different tool fits better

If you want the complete text of a PDF rather than answers to specific questions, the PDF-to-text converter extracts the whole document in one pass. If you want an open-ended AI conversation that is not grounded in a document, the private AI chat is the site's conversational surface - and the two pages share the same on-device model cache, so a model downloaded on one is instantly available on the other.
