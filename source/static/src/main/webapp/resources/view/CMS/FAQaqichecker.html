Frequently Asked Questions

What does Live AQI Checker by City do?

Check the current air quality (US AQI) for any city, right in the browser.

When should I reach for live aqi checker by city?

A 3-day peak-AQI forecast table below the result answers "will it improve tomorrow" for wildfire-smoke or pollution spikes.

Does this replace an indoor air-quality sensor or a health advisory?

No. It reports outdoor ambient air quality only - not an indoor air-quality sensor reading and not a substitute for local health advisories during a smoke event.
