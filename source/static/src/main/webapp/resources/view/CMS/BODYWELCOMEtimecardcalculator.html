Time Card Calculator

Last reviewed: 2026-10-02

The time card calculator adds up a week of work hours from the clock times you enter. Fill in clock-in, clock-out and break minutes for each day - the daily totals, the weekly total and the decimal-hours figure payroll systems expect update as you type, entirely in your browser.

Each day shows its hours twice: on the clock (7:30) and in decimal form (7.50), because time sheets and payroll software take the decimal number while people think in hours and minutes. The weekly total is shown both ways too, and a reference table below the result spells out the standard minute conversions - 15 minutes is 0.25, 30 is 0.50, 45 is 0.75 - so a part-hour entry never needs guessing. Shifts that end after midnight are handled: when the clock-out time is at or before the clock-in time, the calculator rolls the shift past midnight instead of producing a negative day.

Two optional inputs extend the result. An hourly rate adds a gross-pay line for the week - gross only, before any taxes or deductions. A weekly overtime threshold (preset to 40, but it is your own number to change) splits the total into regular and over-threshold hours whenever the week exceeds it; overtime rules vary by country, state and contract, so the calculator applies the threshold you give it rather than guessing a jurisdiction's rule. Nothing you type is stored or sent anywhere, and no account is needed.

For other time and pay math on this site, the date difference calculator counts calendar days between two dates rather than clock hours, and the hourly to salary calculator converts a wage once you already know your hours - this page is where that hours figure comes from.
