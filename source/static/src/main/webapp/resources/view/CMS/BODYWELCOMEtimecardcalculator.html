Time Card Calculator

Last reviewed: 2026-10-02

The time card calculator adds up a week of work hours from the clock times you enter. Fill in clock-in, clock-out and break minutes for each day - the daily totals, the weekly total and the decimal-hours figure payroll systems expect update as you type, entirely in your browser.


Key features

  • Each day's hours show both on the clock (7:30) and in decimal form (7.50), the number payroll systems expect, and the weekly total is shown both ways too.
  • A minutes-to-decimal reference table below the result lists the standard conversions - 15 minutes is 0.25, 30 is 0.50, 45 is 0.75 - so a part-hour entry never needs guessing.
  • Shifts that end after midnight are handled: when the clock-out time is at or before the clock-in time, the calculator rolls the shift past midnight instead of producing a negative day.
  • An optional hourly rate adds a gross-pay line for the week - gross only, before any taxes or deductions.
  • An optional weekly overtime threshold (preset to 40, but it is your own number to change) splits the total into regular and over-threshold hours whenever the week exceeds it.

Clock time and decimal hours

Time sheets and payroll software take the decimal number while people think in hours and minutes, so each figure here appears both ways - 7 hours 30 minutes on the clock is 7.50 on the sheet.


Overtime and pay figures

Overtime rules vary by country, state and contract, so the calculator applies the threshold you give it rather than guessing a jurisdiction's rule; the figures are a general estimate, not legal, tax or payroll advice.


Privacy

Everything runs locally in the page - no upload, no account, and nothing you type is stored or sent anywhere.
