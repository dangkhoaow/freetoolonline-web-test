Thirteen Hours is a free browser clock-chain puzzle you can play without an account or install. Press Play to load the ~30 KB game in a same-origin iframe, then tap the active spinning clock so its hand chains into the next clock before the timer ends.


Key features

  • Progress mode with 60 levels and star gates.
  • Infinite score mode that respawns clocks.
  • Tap or click the active spinning clock to chain shots.
  • About ~30 KB after Play; progress saves under ftol:thirteenhours.
  • Adapted from Jorge Rubiano's MIT js13k 2024 entry.

When to use each mode

Pick Progress for a structured climb through 60 levels, where the stars you earn gate the later packs. Pick Infinite instead to chase a high score with no end point, on a board that keeps respawning clocks after each clear.


Privacy and data handling

Progress and theme color stay on this device under the ftol:thirteenhours key. Nothing else leaves this device except the initial page and game files served from this site.


About the game

Thirteen Hours is adapted from Jorge Rubiano's MIT js13k 2024 entry. The lobby carries a theme color picker, and a level selector tracks the star requirements across each pack.
