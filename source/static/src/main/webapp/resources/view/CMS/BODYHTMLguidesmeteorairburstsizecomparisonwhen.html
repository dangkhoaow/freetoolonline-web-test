When to Use Meteor Airburst Size Comparison 3D Explorer

Last reviewed: 2026-07-25

Reviewed by FreetoolOnline editorial team

Open the Meteor Airburst Size Comparison 3D Explorer when you need to compare why a large object and a small one produce such different airburst altitudes and outcomes, without switching between separate pages.


A five-minute planetary-defense lesson

A lesson starts when you click Chelyabinsk and Tunguska in turn to show that a smaller, well-instrumented object can still injure people on the ground while a larger, higher-airbursting one can flatten forest with no crater at all, then click "Small bolide" to show how common the harmless cases really are.


Sessions this explorer does not fit

Sessions this page does not fit are one event's minute-by-minute timeline - use the Chelyabinsk Meteor 3D Explorer for that - or a real mission's deflection story - use the DART Asteroid Deflection 3D Explorer for that.
