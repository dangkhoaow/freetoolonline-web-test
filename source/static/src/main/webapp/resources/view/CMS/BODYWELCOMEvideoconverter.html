Have an MKV file instead of MOV? MKV is not one of the containers this picker accepts or produces - the upload filter here covers MOV, FLV, MP4, WMV, GIF, and AVI source files, and the output list stops at MP4, WebM, MOV, AVI, and the audio-only formats above.
