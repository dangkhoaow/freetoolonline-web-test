Companion guide for the Galactic Year: Sun's Orbit 3D Explorer. This page sits alongside the when-to-use guide and the step-by-step walkthrough, so you can move between the numbers table, timing guidance, and control instructions without leaving the guide set.

The explorer itself runs entirely client-side in the browser with the vendored three.js engine - no account, no install, and no scene data leaves your device. For the wider picture, the Space 3D collection also has a Milky Way Map explorer and a Sagittarius A* Star Orbit explorer.
