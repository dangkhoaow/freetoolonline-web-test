How to explore Apollo Landing Sites on the Moon 3D Explorer

Last reviewed: 2026-08-07

Reviewed by FreetoolOnline editorial team

Open the explorer and click each yellow marker to see where the six Apollo crews actually landed.

  1. Open /space-3d/apollo-landing-sites-moon-3d.html and wait for the Moon and six yellow markers to appear.
  2. Click any marker on the Moon - the facts panel fills with that mission's landing date and crew.
  3. Use the dropdown above the scene to jump straight to any of the other five missions.
  4. Drag to rotate the Moon and compare how far apart the six sites really are.
  5. Read the site names table below for a quick side-by-side of all six landings.
