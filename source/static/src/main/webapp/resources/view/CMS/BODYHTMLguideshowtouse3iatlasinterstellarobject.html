How to Use the 3I/ATLAS Interstellar Object 3D Explorer

Last reviewed: 2026-08-12

Reviewed by FreetoolOnline editorial team

Open the 3I/ATLAS Interstellar Object 3D Explorer and slide the selector (0-2) to compare 1I/'Oumuamua, 2I/Borisov, and 3I/ATLAS side by side, each with its own real published orbit, size, and coma-activity figures.

  1. Open /space-3d/3i-atlas-interstellar-object.html and wait for all three objects, their path curves, and the Sun to render.
  2. Drag the selector (0-2) to swap the highlighted object between 1I/'Oumuamua, 2I/Borisov, and 3I/ATLAS - the default position (2) shows 3I/ATLAS.
  3. Read the facts panel for the active object's real discovery date, orbital eccentricity, inclination, perihelion, speed, nucleus size, and coma status.
  4. Check the comparison table below the facts panel - the highlighted row is the active object.
  5. Notice how the three path curves widen as eccentricity rises - 'Oumuamua's is the tightest, 3I/ATLAS's is the widest, reflecting its real eccentricity of about 6.1.
  6. Drag to orbit and scroll or pinch to zoom to see each object's coma (or its absence, for 'Oumuamua) up close.
