How to Explore the Neptune Supersonic Winds 3D Explorer

Last reviewed: 2026-07-25

Reviewed by FreetoolOnline editorial team

Open the Neptune Supersonic Winds 3D Explorer and scrub through 3 real observation eras of Neptune's Great Dark Spot, from its 1989 discovery to today's absence.

  1. Open /space-3d/neptune-supersonic-winds.html and wait for the banded Neptune sphere to render.
  2. Drag the era slider (0-2) to switch between 1989 (storm visible), mid-1990s (Hubble finds it gone), and today (still absent).
  3. Read the facts panel for that era's observation note.
  4. Check the running table below the facts panel - the highlighted row is the active era.
  5. Scroll the facts panel to see the wind-speed comparison table ranking Neptune against Saturn, Jupiter, and Earth.
  6. Drag to orbit and scroll or pinch to zoom on the scene for a closer look at the storm's fixed latitude.
