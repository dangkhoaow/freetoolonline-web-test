The Giant's Causeway is a promontory of about 40,000 interlocking hexagonal basalt columns stepping from the Northern Ireland cliffs into the Atlantic. Toggle the tide level to see the lower columns covered and exposed, and the column-height bands to see how the uneven "steps" are formed.
