Pick a city from the dropdown, tap Use my location, or type a latitude and longitude directly; then choose a date. The result below updates as you change any field - nothing is uploaded and no calculation waits on a server round-trip.

The result table shows sunrise, solar noon, and sunset for the location and date you picked, plus the total day length in hours and minutes.

Golden hour (morning end and evening start) and civil dawn/dusk are shown alongside the main sun times, all in your device's own time zone.

For a full field-by-field walkthrough, see the sunrise sunset calculator step-by-step guide.
