Convert only the part you need. The Video Converter lets you set a start point and a duration before converting, so a long recording becomes one short MP4, or an MP3 of just the audio, instead of a full-length file. Because the conversion runs on our server, large or long videos do not depend on your browser's memory: you upload the file, wait for the result, and download it. If you would rather the file never leave your device, the in-browser HD Video Converter handles lighter jobs locally.
