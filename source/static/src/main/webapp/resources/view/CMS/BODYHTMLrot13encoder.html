Paste text and apply ROT13 in your browser - A-Z and a-z shift by 13; digits and punctuation stay as-is. Output updates as you type; Copy puts the current result on the clipboard.

How to use ROT13 Encoder

  1. Paste or type the text you want to shift into the box.
  2. Read the live output - Latin letters move 13 places; other characters stay unchanged.
  3. Click Copy to put the result on the clipboard, or paste the output back in to reverse the shift.
