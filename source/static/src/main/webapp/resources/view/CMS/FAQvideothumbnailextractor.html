Frequently Asked Questions

Does my video get uploaded anywhere?

No. The video opens directly in your browser and frames are drawn to a canvas on the page - there is no upload and no server. Nothing is stored beyond the page session.

How do I pick the exact frame?

Scrub the timestamp slider - the preview shows the frame you are on, and the frame you see is the frame you save. Then press Capture this frame.

Which image formats can I save?

Lossless PNG (the default) or JPEG at 95, 85 or 70 percent quality. Frames come out at the video's native resolution, and filenames carry the source name and timestamp, e.g. myclip-t2.5s.png.

Can it grab several frames at once?

Yes - one click grabs 6 evenly spaced frames from the clip, each on its own card with a timestamp, pixel size and download link. Up to 24 thumbnails per session.

Why does my video show an error instead of a frame?

The browser could not decode that codec or container. MP4 (H.264) and WebM work in mainstream browsers; some MKV or HEVC files and DRM-protected streams cannot be decoded, and the page tells you so instead of showing a black image.
