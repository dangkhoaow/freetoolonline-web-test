Last reviewed: 2026-08-05

Open the 2026 Total Solar Eclipse Path 3D Explorer when you want the real geography and published local times for one specific, dated event: the August 12, 2026 total solar eclipse, the first to cross mainland Europe since 1999.


Sessions this explorer fits

Readers planning to watch or follow the August 12, 2026 eclipse, students and teachers marking the first-mainland-Europe-since-1999 milestone with a real date attached, and anyone who wants the exact published Reykjavik and Leon totality windows all get a direct answer here.
