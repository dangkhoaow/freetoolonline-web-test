vs Planet Size Comparison 3D Explorer

Planet Size Comparison lines up whole spheres by outer radius. Planet Interior Cutaways slices each planet open to show what is inside - crust, mantle, and core - with a depth reading and an evidence badge per layer.


vs a textbook diagram

A textbook page shows one static cutaway, usually Earth only, with no source cited per layer. Planet Interior Cutaways covers all eight planets, lets you drag-to-orbit and click any layer, and names the mission or method behind each boundary - still free and browser-only.


vs planetarium apps

Planetarium apps target tonight's sky positions, not planetary interiors, and often need install. Planet Interior Cutaways Explorer is a focused interior lesson with published mission evidence (InSight for Mars, Juno for Jupiter) and no ephemeris - load the URL and click a planet.
