Cat Hop Cloud is a free browser luck-hop puzzle you can play without an account or install. Press Play to load the small canvas engine, pick a level, then press number keys 1 through 9 to hop that many clouds forward around the numbered ring.

Each jump spends luck equal to the distance you travel, so short hops are cheap and long hops are expensive. Plan a route that reaches every required checkpoint and still lands you back on cloud 0 with luck to spare.


Key features

  • Press keys 1 through 9 to jump that many clouds forward; each jump costs luck equal to the key pressed.
  • Checkpoints must be visited before you can finish a level.
  • After turn 5, every hop costs an extra +1 luck as a turn penalty.
  • Press R to reset the current level and press I to toggle the level menu and instructions overlay.
  • Ten levels open one after another as you clear each one; your top three scores per level save locally under the ftol:cathopcloud:scores_v2.0.0 key.
  • The level select grid shows your best luck score and turn count for each cleared level.

How to play and win

Win by returning to cloud 0 with luck remaining after every required checkpoint is checked and at least two moves have been made. Balancing the turn penalty against long jumps is the core of each puzzle, and the mix of cloud types decides which route is cheapest.

Privacy and data handling

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing leaves this device except the initial page and the roughly 25 KB of game files served from this site. The game is keyboard only, with no touch hop keys, so it plays best on a desktop keyboard.
