How to Explore the Interstellar Travel Timescales 3D Explorer

Last reviewed: 2026-07-26

Reviewed by FreetoolOnline editorial team

Open the Interstellar Travel Timescales 3D Explorer, pick a vehicle, and drag the time slider to see how far it gets toward Proxima Centauri.

  1. Open /space-3d/interstellar-travel-timescales.html and wait for the Sun, the distance line, and the Proxima Centauri marker to render.
  2. Pick a vehicle from the dropdown: Voyager 1, the Breakthrough Starshot concept, or light itself.
  3. Drag the time slider (a log scale from 1 year to 100,000 years). Low values keep the marker near the Sun; high values move it toward Proxima Centauri.
  4. Read the facts panel for the active vehicle's real or published speed, one-way transit time, elapsed years, percent complete, and remaining light-years.
  5. For the Breakthrough Starshot concept, notice the extra "signal lag after arrival" line once the marker reaches Proxima Centauri.
  6. Drag to orbit and scroll or pinch to zoom to see the Sun-to-Proxima line from a different angle.
