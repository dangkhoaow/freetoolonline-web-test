Where the DART Asteroid Deflection Explorer wins

The DART Asteroid Deflection Explorer wins when the question is whether humanity can deliberately change an asteroid's path on purpose - it is the only page on the site with a before/after orbital-period measurement from a controlled experiment.


Where the Shoemaker-Levy 9 Explorer wins

The Shoemaker-Levy 9 Explorer wins when the question is what happens when a comet meets a planet with no human intervention at all - it is the only page on the site showing a multi-fragment "string of pearls" impact train and a scar large enough to compare directly against Earth's own diameter.


A reasonable rule

As a reasonable rule, use the Shoemaker-Levy 9 Explorer to see how big a natural, planet-scale impact can be, then use the DART Asteroid Deflection Explorer to see the much smaller scale of a real, deliberate impact test designed to protect Earth in the future.
