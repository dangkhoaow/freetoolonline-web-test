How to Convert Hourly Pay to Salary - Step by Step

Last reviewed: 2026-10-01

The hourly to salary calculator turns a wage into weekly, biweekly, monthly and annual gross pay on one screen. These are the exact inputs, what each accepts, and how to flip the direction without retyping.


Step 1 - open the tool

Open the calculator and a full breakdown is already on screen: it loads with 20 per hour, 40 hours per week and 52 paid weeks, so the output shape is visible before you type anything.


Step 2 - pick the direction

The direction select defaults to hourly rate to annual salary. Choose annual salary to hourly rate to work backwards. Switching converts the amount you already entered in place - 20 per hour becomes 41,600 per year, not a blank field - so the result stays equivalent.


Step 3 - enter your numbers

Type the amount, then adjust hours per week and paid weeks per year. Hours accept half-hour steps from 1 to 100 (default 40); paid weeks accept 1 to 53 (default 52). Every keystroke recalculates - there is no submit button.


Step 4 - read the breakdown

The result names the headline figure first - annual salary in one direction, the equivalent hourly rate in the other - followed by a table with the hourly, weekly, biweekly, monthly and annual lines. The note under it says exactly what the numbers are: gross pay before taxes and deductions.


Step 5 - use the reference table

Below the result, a reference table lists common hourly rates from 15 to 50 with their weekly and annual gross pay at a standard 40-hour, 52-week schedule - a quick sanity check against the figure you just computed.
