Tennis Grand Slam Prize Money Lookup

Last reviewed: 2026-08-31

Look up official singles prize money by round for the four tennis Grand Slams - Australian Open, French Open (Roland-Garros), Wimbledon, and the US Open.

A cross-majors comparison table lines up the champion payout, first-round payout, and total pool of all four majors side by side.

Each tournament's figures are shown in its own official payout currency (AUD, EUR, GBP, USD) exactly as published - the page never converts between currencies.

How prize money grows from first round to champion

The 2025 US Open singles payout curve is not a straight line. A first-round exit paid $110,000, and the jumps stay modest through the middle rounds - the Round of 64 loser's $154,000 is about 40% more than that. The big jumps come at the very end of the draw: the champion's $5,000,000 is nearly four times the semi-finalist's $1,260,000, and it works out to more than 45 times the first-round payout - exactly double the runner-up's $2,500,000. The same non-linear shape holds at the other three majors. At the Australian Open the payout climbs from A$132,000 at Round of 128 to A$3,500,000 for the champion, and at the French Open from €78,000 to €2,550,000. Tournament organizers structure the money this way so the largest checks reward reaching the final two rounds rather than simply surviving the early ones. How the four majors' total prize pools compare

The 2025 singles total prize pool - the sum paid out across the entire draw - is A$96,500,000 at the Australian Open, $90,000,000 at the US Open, €56,352,000 at the French Open, and £53,550,000 at Wimbledon. Each figure is published in that tournament's own official currency, and this lookup does not convert between them, so the four totals are not directly comparable in value without a currency conversion the tool intentionally does not perform. What is comparable is the shape within a single tournament: the ratio between its own champion, first-round, and total-pool figures, shown side by side in the comparison table above. At the Australian Open, for example, the champion's A$3,500,000 works out to just over 3.6% of the tournament's A$96,500,000 total pool - the rest is spread across the other 127 players in the singles draw, each paid according to the round they reached.
