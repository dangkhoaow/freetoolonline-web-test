How to draw and download a signature

  1. Choose a pen color and width above the drawing box.
  2. Sign inside the box with your mouse, finger, or stylus.
  3. Use Undo to remove the last stroke, or Clear to start the signature over.
  4. Turn on Transparent background if you plan to paste the signature over other content.
  5. Click Download PNG to save the signature to your device.
