Black Sea 3D Explorer

Last reviewed: 2026-07-27

Reviewed by FreetoolOnline editorial team

This browser explorer shows the Black Sea in cross-section: a thin oxygen-rich surface layer sitting on top of the largest anoxic (oxygen-free) water body on Earth, plus the shallow northwest continental shelf that breaks the pattern near Ukraine and Crimea.

Roughly 90 percent of the Black Sea's volume lies below the chemocline - the boundary, only about 150-200 m down, where dissolved oxygen runs out and hydrogen-sulfide-rich anoxic water takes over all the way to the 2,212 m floor. That permanent stratification (the water column almost never mixes top to bottom) is also why ancient shipwrecks are found remarkably preserved in the deep anoxic layer. At about 436,400 sq km, the sea is bordered by Turkey, Bulgaria, Romania, Ukraine, Russia, and Georgia, and it drains to the Mediterranean through the Bosphorus Strait, the Sea of Marmara, and the Dardanelles.

  • Toggle the oxic/anoxic bands to see the thin oxygen-rich layer versus the vast oxygen-free depths
  • Toggle the chemocline plane to compare the water surface with the oxygen boundary depth
  • Read area, depth, and salinity figures in the panel
  • Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom
