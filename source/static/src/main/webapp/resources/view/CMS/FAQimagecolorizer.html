Frequently Asked Questions

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. The photo colorizer runs entirely on your device; the picture never leaves your browser. The only network request is the one-time download of the colorization model itself, and that happens only after you click the AI button.

Are the colors it adds historically accurate?

No - they are the model's best guess from what it learned, not a record of the real scene. Skin, sky, and foliage usually come out believable; the exact color of clothing, cars, or walls can be wrong. Use the "Show original" toggle to compare before saving.

Why is the model download about 130 MB?

Color prediction needs a full neural network, and this page runs it on your own device instead of a server. The download happens once, with a progress percentage, and is cached by your browser - the next visit skips it. After that, a photo typically takes 15 to 60 seconds.

Does colorizing reduce my photo's sharpness?

No. The model predicts only the color layer; your photo's original full-resolution detail is kept and the colors are blended into it. The result downloads as a PNG re-encoded from the canvas, so the original file's hidden metadata is not carried over.

Can I get a result without downloading the model?

Yes - the "Warm tint" button applies an instant sepia-style tone with no download and no network at all. It is a simple fixed tint, not real color prediction; the AI button is the one that guesses actual colors.
