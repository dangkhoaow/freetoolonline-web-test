Percentage Calculator

Last reviewed: 2026-07-06

Work out a percentage of a number, the reverse (what percent one number is of another), or a percent change, right in the browser, with the result updating as you type.


Key features

  • Pick the calculation you need from the dropdown and enter the two numbers; the result updates as you type - no button to click.
  • Switch calculations at any time; the two number fields carry over so you can compare results without re-typing them.

When to use it

Use it for a discount, a tax add-on, a grade, or comparing two values as a percent change.

Privacy

The two numbers you enter stay in the browser - the result is computed on this device, with no upload and no account. The figures are gone when you leave the page and are never sent to an analytics or tracking service.

Accuracy

The result is a general estimate, not financial or professional advice.
