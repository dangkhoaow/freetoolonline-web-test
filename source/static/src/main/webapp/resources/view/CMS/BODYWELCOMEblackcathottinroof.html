Black Cat on a Hot Tin Roof

Black Cat on a Hot Tin Roof is a free browser rooftop runner you can play without an account or install. Press Play to load the roughly 16 KB game in a same-origin iframe, then take on a 60-second run.


Key features

  • A fixed 60-second timed run - a natural quick-break length.
  • Jump with Space or a click, with hold-for-height and a mid-air double jump.
  • Clear spikes from the side and attack birds from above - two distinct techniques.
  • Collect power-up items that grant Black Cat's super power.
  • Survive the full 60 seconds to face a named antagonist, Black Bird.
  • Your best score is saved on this device (localStorage) so you can try to beat it next time.

How to play

Click once anywhere on the game to enable sound and start the intro, then press Space or click/tap to begin. Jump to clear spikes and drop onto birds from above. On mobile the prompt reads Tap to Play instead of Press Space to Play.


Privacy

Everything runs on this device - nothing leaves it except the initial page and the game files loaded from this site. No upload and no account.
