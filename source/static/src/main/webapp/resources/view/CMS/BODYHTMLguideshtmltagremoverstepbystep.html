HTML Tag Remover - Step by Step

Last reviewed: 2026-07-26

HTML Tag Remover runs in three steps: open the tool, paste your HTML, then copy the plain-text result. This page walks through what each step expects.


Step 1 - open the tool

Go to HTML Tag Remover. Nothing needs to be installed or signed in before you start.


Step 2 - paste your HTML

Paste HTML into the input. Tags, scripts, and style blocks are dropped; the visible text stays in order. The plain-text output updates live as you edit.


Step 3 - copy the result

Click Copy plain text to put the result on the clipboard. Cleanup runs locally - no upload and no account. Nothing you paste is saved between visits.

Does not convert HTML to Markdown and does not encode entities as its primary job. Does not fetch remote URLs - paste only.
