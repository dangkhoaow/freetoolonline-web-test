Frequently Asked Questions

What does Habit Tracker do?

Keeps the daily habits you are building in one browser list, so you can add a habit, check it off once you have done it today, and remove any you have dropped.

When is this habit tracker the right pick?

Reach for it when you want a no-install way to hold yourself to a daily routine - drinking water, stretching, a short walk - without creating an account.

Do my habits and streaks stay saved if I close the tab?

Yes - your habit list and each day's check marks are written to this browser's local storage, so they are still there after a reload; only clearing this site's browser data wipes them.
