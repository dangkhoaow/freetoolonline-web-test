Black Hole Square

Last reviewed: 2026-07-18

Black Hole Square is a free browser tap puzzle you can play without an account or install. Press Play to load the ~40 KB canvas engine in a same-origin iframe, then swipe left on the title to begin.

Controls at a glance:

ActionInput
Absorb piecesTap marked squares so every piece absorbs or is shoved into the black hole
AdvanceClear the board before the move budget shown in the HUD runs out
Skip aheadSwipe left
Reset or go backSwipe right
Reopen a puzzleTap the progress dots under the board to jump to solved puzzles

Good to know:

  • About ~40 KB loads after Play, with touch-friendly canvas scaling.
  • Progress saves in your browser under ftol:blackholesquare:storage (current, solved, solutions) and is never uploaded.

Privacy note: nothing leaves this device except the initial page and game files from this site.
