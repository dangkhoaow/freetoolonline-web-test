Black Hole Square

Black Hole Square is a free browser tap puzzle you can play without an account or install. Press Play to load the roughly 40 KB canvas engine in a same-origin iframe, then swipe left on the title to begin.


Key features

  • Tap marked squares so every piece absorbs into the black hole.
  • Each puzzle has a move budget shown in the HUD; clear the board to advance.
  • Swipe left to skip a board; swipe right to reset or go back.
  • Progress dots under the board reopen solved puzzles.
  • Loads in about 40 KB after Play, with touch-friendly canvas scaling.

Privacy and saved progress

Progress stays in your browser under ftol:blackholesquare:storage - your current level, solved list, and recorded solutions - and is never uploaded. Nothing leaves this device except the initial page and the game files from this site.


How it is built

Black Hole Square is adapted from Quinten Clause (MIT, js13k 2021). This build namespaces localStorage and ships the LICENSE next to the engine.
