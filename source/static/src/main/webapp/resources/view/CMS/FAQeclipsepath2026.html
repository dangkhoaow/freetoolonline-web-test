Frequently Asked Questions

Where does the August 12, 2026 total solar eclipse cross land?

NASA and ESA maps place the path of totality across Greenland, Iceland, northern Russia, the North Atlantic, and Spain, ending with a small corner of Portugal - the first total solar eclipse to cross mainland Europe since 1999.

How long does totality last?

Under two minutes for most of the path. Only locations very near the center of the path in Greenland, northern Russia, or the North Atlantic get a little longer, though still under two and a half minutes, per NASA's published figures.

What time does totality happen in Iceland and Spain?

NASA publishes totality in Reykjavik, Iceland at about 5:48 to 5:49 p.m. local time, and in Leon, Spain at about 8:28 to 8:30 p.m. local time. Nearby Spanish cities such as Burgos, Valladolid, and Soria see roughly 1 minute 42 to 45 seconds of totality.

Will I see anything from the United States or Canada?

Only a partial eclipse - a small bite taken out of the Sun's disk from parts of Alaska, Canada, and the northern United States. Full darkness (totality) is limited to the narrow path across Greenland, Iceland, northern Russia, the North Atlantic, and Spain.

Is the yellow path on the globe the exact eclipse path?

No. It is an illustrative line connecting eight NASA and ESA published waypoint regions with straight spherical segments - not NASA's precise eclipse-prediction center line, which is computed from orbital mechanics and published as detailed map data. For the exact local circumstances at one address, use NASA's or timeanddate.com's official interactive eclipse maps.

How is this different from the Solar Eclipse 3D Explorer?

The Solar Eclipse 3D Explorer teaches the general eclipse mechanism - why the Sun and Moon look almost the same size, how the umbra and penumbra work, and the Saros repeat cycle - with an illustrative, undated shadow sweep. This page instead shows the real geography and published local times for one specific, dated event: the August 12, 2026 total solar eclipse.
