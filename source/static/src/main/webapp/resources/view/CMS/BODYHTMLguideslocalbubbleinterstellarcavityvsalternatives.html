Where the Galactic Year Explorer wins

The Galactic Year Explorer wins when the question is how the Sun moves around the whole Milky Way - it is the only page on the site modeling the Sun's galaxy-wide orbital path and its up-and-down drift through the disk.


Where the Local Bubble Explorer wins

The Local Bubble Explorer wins when the question is what surrounds the Sun right now, close up - it is the only page on the site showing the actual cavity in the interstellar medium the Sun currently sits inside, and which real star-forming regions ring its edge.


A reasonable rule

As a reasonable rule, use the Local Bubble Explorer first to see the Sun's immediate ~1,000-light-year surroundings, then use the Galactic Year Explorer to zoom out to the Sun's ~26,000-light-year journey around the whole galaxy.
