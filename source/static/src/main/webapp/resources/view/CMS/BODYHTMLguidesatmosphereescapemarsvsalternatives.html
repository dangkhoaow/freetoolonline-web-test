Where the Atmosphere Escape: Mars Explorer wins

The Atmosphere Escape: Mars Explorer wins when the question is about CAUSE and TIME - it is the only page on the site that scrubs through billions of years to show a magnetic shield disappearing and an atmosphere escaping because of it.


Where the Planet Atmosphere Scale Heights Explorer wins

The Planet Atmosphere Scale Heights Explorer wins when the question is about STRUCTURE and COMPARISON - it lines up five worlds side by side at one moment in time, which the Mars-only, time-based explorer does not attempt.


A reasonable rule

As a reasonable rule, use the Atmosphere Escape: Mars Explorer first to see why Mars's atmosphere thinned out, then use the Planet Atmosphere Scale Heights Explorer to see how thin Mars's atmosphere is today next to four other worlds.
