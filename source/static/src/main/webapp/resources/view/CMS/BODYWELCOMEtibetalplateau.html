Tibetan Plateau 3D Explorer

Last reviewed: 2026-07-31

Reviewed by FreetoolOnline editorial team

This browser explorer shows the Tibetan Plateau at over 5,000 meters average elevation - the highest major plateau on Earth. A vast expanse of sparse alpine tundra vegetation, rolling terrain, and mountain chains ringed by Himalayan and Kunlun peaks.

The Tibetan Plateau is known as the Roof of the World: a high-altitude region spanning China, Tibet, Bhutan, Nepal, and India. The thin air at over 5,000 meters pressure is only about 40-50% of sea-level atmospheric pressure. Sparse vegetation has adapted to the extreme altitude and climate - sedges, mosses, and hardy shrubs dot the landscape. The plateau is the source region for some of the world's mightiest rivers: the Yangtze, Yellow River, Brahmaputra, Salween, and Mekong all originate here.

  • Toggle the vegetation zones to see the sparse tundra patches
  • Toggle the rim peaks to show/hide the mountain silhouettes
  • Read elevation, climate, and geographic figures in the panel
  • Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom
