The Moon's orbit is an ellipse, not a circle, so its distance from Earth swings between a closest point (perigee) and a farthest point (apogee) every 27.55 days. Drag the slider below to scrub through one cycle and watch the Moon move faster near perigee and slower near apogee - the same real distance swing behind every "supermoon" headline.
