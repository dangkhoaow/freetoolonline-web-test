Final Grade Calculator

Last reviewed: 2026-10-14

The final grade calculator answers the question directly: enter your current grade, how much the final exam counts toward the overall grade, and your target grade - the score you need on the final updates as you type, entirely in your browser.


Key features

  • Solves the weighted-average algebra for the score needed on the final to hit a target overall grade, and flags when the target is already locked in or out of reach this term.
  • A reference table maps a range of hypothetical final scores (0 to 100%) to the resulting overall grade at the current grade and weight entered.
  • Runs entirely client-side - nothing you type is stored or sent anywhere, and no account is needed.

The algebra behind it

Overall grade equals the current grade times its share of the total, plus the final score times the final's weight. Solving that equation for the final score - rather than averaging grades you already know - is what makes this a different calculation from a course-wide GPA.

This is plain weighted-average arithmetic on the numbers you enter, not a substitute for a syllabus's official grading policy - rounding, curves, extra credit and dropped-lowest-score rules vary by course. For other grade math on this site, the GPA calculator averages per-course grades you already have, weighted by credit hours, across a whole semester; this page instead solves for one still-unknown exam score in a single course.
