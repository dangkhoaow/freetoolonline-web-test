Frequently Asked Questions

How big is Fish River Canyon?

About 160 km long, up to 27 km wide, and up to 550 m deep - Africa's largest canyon and among the largest in the world.

What carved Fish River Canyon?

Ancient rifting first fractured the plateau, then hundreds of millions of years of erosion - mainly by the Fish River - deepened and widened the gorge into its present shape.

Where is Fish River Canyon?

In southern Namibia, inside the Ai-Ais/Richtersveld Transfrontier Park.

Can you hike Fish River Canyon?

Yes - the 85 km Fish River Canyon Trail along the rim and floor is one of Africa's best-known multi-day hikes, but it is open only from May to September when the river is low enough to cross.

What is the "big bend" shape in the scene?

It represents the canyon's most famous meander loop, near the area known as Bushman's Paradise, where the river bends sharply back on itself.

Is this a geological simulation?

No. It is an educational explorer using published park and tourism-board figures - the meander shape, terrace bands, and trail markers are schematic teaching cues.
