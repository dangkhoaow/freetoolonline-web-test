Video Cropper cuts every frame of a video down to the part you choose: drag the corners or edges of the crop box, or pick a shape such as Square, 16:9, 9:16, 4:3 or 3:4. All of the work happens inside your browser tab - the video is never uploaded, and the download carries no watermark.

How to crop a video

  1. Click "Choose video" or drop a file on "Drop a video here".
  2. Pick a "Shape" - "Free", "Original", "Square", "16:9", "9:16", "4:3", "3:4" and more - then drag the crop box, or its corners and edges, over the part to keep. Move the slider under the picture to check other moments.
  3. Press "Rotate left", "Rotate right" or "Flip" if the picture is sideways or mirrored, or type exact "Width", "Height", "Left" and "Top" values in pixels.
  4. Choose "Format", "Size" and "Sound", press "Crop video", then click "Download cropped video" when the progress bar finishes.
