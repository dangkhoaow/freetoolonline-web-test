Published figures: NASA and ESA eclipse maps put the path of totality across Greenland, Iceland, northern Russia, the North Atlantic, and Spain, with a small corner of Portugal - the first total solar eclipse to reach mainland Europe since 1999. Reykjavik sees totality about 5:48 to 5:49 p.m. local time; Leon, Spain sees it about 8:28 to 8:30 p.m. local time.
