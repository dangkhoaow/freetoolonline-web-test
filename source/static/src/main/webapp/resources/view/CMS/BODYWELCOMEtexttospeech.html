Text to Speech

Last reviewed: 2026-07-03

Type or paste your text, choose a voice, and press Speak to hear it read aloud right in your browser.

Typical use: hearing a message, an article, or a draft read out loud when you would rather listen than read.

Pick from the voices your browser and operating system provide, then use the Rate slider (0.5 to 2) and Pitch slider (0 to 2) to make the voice faster, slower, higher, or lower. The words are spoken by your browser and never leave your device, and you can press Stop any time to cancel playback.

Read a transcript or subtitle file aloud

Upload a .srt, .vtt, .sbv, or plain .txt file and the tool parses it locally in the tab - the file is never sent anywhere. Subtitle formats are split into timed cues: each entry shows its time range (for example 0:01 - 0:04) with a play button, so you can listen to one line, or press Speak all cues to hear the whole transcript in order. The extracted text also lands in the text box, ready to edit before speaking or exporting.

Download the voice as an MP3 or WAV file

Browsers do not allow recording the system voices used by the Speak button - that is a privacy rule of the platform. So the Generate audio file button uses a separate speech engine (eSpeak) that runs inside the page: it synthesizes the text on your device and hands you a real MP3 or WAV download, still with nothing uploaded. The eSpeak voice sounds more robotic than the natural device voices used for playback; it is the trade-off for a file you can keep, share, or drop into a video editor. Rate and Pitch settings carry over to the generated file, and text up to 20,000 characters is supported per file.

Voices for different languages, detected automatically

As you type or after a file loads, the tool detects the language offline - by writing system for scripts such as Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, Cyrillic, Thai, or Vietnamese diacritics, and by common-word patterns for Latin-alphabet languages such as English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Dutch, Turkish, Polish, and Indonesian. With Auto voice enabled it switches the playback voice to a matching one from your device. Downloadable files are available in 16 languages the built-in eSpeak engine ships voices for (English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Dutch, Turkish, Polish, Czech, Romanian, Swedish, Finnish, Hungarian, Greek, and Chinese); other languages still play aloud through your device voices.
