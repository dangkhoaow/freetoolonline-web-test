Time Zone Converter Step By Step

Last reviewed: 2026-07-24
freetoolonline.com editorial team

Time Zone Converter runs in three steps: pick the source timezone and time, pick the target timezone, then read the converted result. This page walks through what each step expects.


Step 1 - set the date, time and source timezone

Go to Time Zone Converter. The date and time fields start on the current moment, and the From-zone dropdown defaults to the timezone your browser reports. Change either field to the moment you want to convert.


Step 2 - pick the target timezone

Choose a zone in the To-zone dropdown. The result below updates immediately - there is no separate "convert" button to click. Use Swap to flip the two zones in place, or Now to jump both fields back to the current moment.


Step 3 - read the result

The output panel shows the wall-clock reading in both zones side by side, plus how many hours apart they are at that specific date (this can change across a daylight-saving transition). A world-clock row further down lists the current time in several major cities for a quick side glance.
