Frequently Asked Questions

What does this tool show?

It checks today's date against a curated list of commonly observed US "National Day" calendar traditions (Pi Day, Star Wars Day, National Coffee Day, and similar) and shows a match on load, plus the full list sorted by date.

Are these official government holidays?

No. These are informal calendar-tradition days, not government-recognized public holidays.

Is this a complete list of every "National Day"?

No - this is a curated list of commonly observed days, not an exhaustive or official registry. Some regional or niche observances may be missing.

Does the list change from year to year?

No. Each entry uses a fixed calendar month and day - for example, National Pi Day is always March 14 - so the same dates repeat every year; only the day of the week each one falls on shifts.

Does this tool track or remember what I look up?

No. Nothing about your visit is stored beyond the current page session, and nothing is sent to any analytics or tracking service.
