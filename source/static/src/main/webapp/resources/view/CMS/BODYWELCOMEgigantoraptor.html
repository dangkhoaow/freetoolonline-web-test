Gigantoraptor 3D Viewer

Last reviewed: 2026-08-06

Gigantoraptor was a giant oviraptorosaur dinosaur from the Late Cretaceous of China, about 85-70 million years ago. Xu and colleagues named the genus in 2007 from the Erlian Formation of Inner Mongolia.
