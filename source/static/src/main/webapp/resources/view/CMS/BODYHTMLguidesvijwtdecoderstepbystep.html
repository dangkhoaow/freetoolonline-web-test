Jwt Decoder Tung Buoc

Last reviewed: 2026-07-14

JWT Decoder hoat dong theo ba buoc: mo cong cu, dan token, roi doc ket qua da giai ma. Trang nay giai thich tung buoc can gi.


Buoc 1 - mo cong cu

Truy cap JWT Decoder. Khong can cai dat hay dang nhap truoc khi bat dau.


Buoc 2 - dan token

Trong JWT Decoder, dan toan bo token - ba doan (header, payload, chu ky). Thieu doan hoac khong phai base64url JSON hop le se bao loi ro, khong lam hong trang.


Buoc 3 - lay ket qua

JWT Decoder hien header va payload JSON de doc. exp/iat thanh moc thoi gian; exp qua han danh dau EXPIRED. Chu ky hien tho va khong xac minh - khoa ben phat hanh khong bao gio den trinh duyet.

Viec giai ma chay cuc bo ngay trong tab trinh duyet nay - khong can tai len va khong can tai khoan.
