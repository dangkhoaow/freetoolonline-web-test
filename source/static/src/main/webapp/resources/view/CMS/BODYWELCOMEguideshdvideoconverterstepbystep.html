The step before you press convert for audio-only output is the MP3 bitrate choice on Hd Online Video Converter: 128 kbps keeps the file smallest and suits spoken-word or podcast audio where some compression artifact is acceptable, 192 kbps is the tool's own default and a safe general-purpose pick, 256 kbps gives music more headroom, and 320 kbps is the archival setting when storage is not the constraint. The bitrate selector only affects compressed audio output - lossless formats ignore it.
