Frequently Asked Questions

What does the commission calculator do?

It turns a sales amount and a commission rate into the commission earned - or, in tiered mode, a quota and two rates into a base-rate portion plus an accelerator-rate portion, added together.

What is the difference between flat and tiered commission?

Flat pays the same rate on every dollar sold. Tiered pays a base rate up to a quota and a separate, usually higher, accelerator rate only on the amount above the quota - the dollars already sold below quota are never re-rated at the higher percentage.

Is my sales data sent anywhere?

No. Every figure you enter - sales amount, quota, base rate, accelerator rate - stays on this page and recalculates live as you type; none of it is transmitted off your device, and no login or account is required to use the calculator.

Does this calculator include tax, draws or clawbacks?

No. It is gross commission only, before any draw against future commission, clawback on a returned sale, plan cap or tax withholding - those are not part of the calculation. Not payroll or tax advice.
