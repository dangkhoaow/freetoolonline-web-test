Random Name Picker

Last reviewed: 2026-07-11

Spin a wheel of names or options to pick one at random in the browser.


Key features

  • The wheel loads pre-filled with six example entries, so you can spin right away or replace them with your own.
  • Add one name or option per line; each spin animates the wheel and names the winner under the top pointer.
  • Edit the list and spin again any time; entries are not saved between visits.

Common uses

Use it for raffles, deciding who goes first, or picking a random winner from a list.


Privacy

The spin result uses crypto.getRandomValues for fairness and runs locally - no upload and no account.
