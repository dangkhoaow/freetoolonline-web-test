Pregnancy Due Date Calculator Online

Last reviewed: 2026-08-28

Enter the first day of your last period, or a known conception date, and the pregnancy due date calculator returns the estimated due date, how many weeks and days along you are today, and which trimester that falls in. Every figure updates as you change a field, so there is no button to press.


Key features

  • Two methods to choose from: first day of last period (LMP) or conception date. Picking one from the Method dropdown relabels the date field to match.
  • Average cycle length is adjustable from 20 to 45 days and defaults to 28, so a longer or shorter cycle shifts the estimate instead of being ignored.
  • Gestational age is reported in completed weeks plus days, with the matching trimester beside it.
  • Your dates stay in the browser - the due date is worked out on this device, with no upload and no account.

How the estimate is made

From a last-period date the calculator adds 280 days, adjusted up or down by the difference between your cycle length and a 28-day cycle. From a conception date it adds 266 days instead. That is the Naegele rule, the same arithmetic a paper pregnancy wheel uses. About 1 in 20 babies arrive on the exact estimated date, so read the result as a due window rather than an appointment.


Good to know

Results are general estimates and are not medical advice. An early dating ultrasound is more accurate than any date-based formula, so confirm your dates with your care provider.
