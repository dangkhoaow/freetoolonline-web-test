Frequently Asked Questions

How tall are Bora Bora's peaks?

Mount Otemanu, the highest point, is about 727 m (2,385 ft). Mount Pahia, the second peak, is about 661 m (2,169 ft).

How did Bora Bora's lagoon and reef form?

Bora Bora is the eroded core of an extinct shield volcano. As the volcano went extinct and the seafloor beneath it slowly subsided, a fringing coral reef kept growing upward near the surface, leaving the twin peaks standing inside a lagoon ringed by that reef - the "almost atoll" stage of volcanic island subsidence.

How big is the lagoon?

About 40 sq km, larger than Bora Bora's own land area of about 30 sq km.

What are motu?

Motu are small sand-and-coral islets that sit on top of a barrier reef. Bora Bora's reef carries several scattered motu around its rim.

Where is Bora Bora?

In the Society Islands (Leeward group), French Polynesia, in the South Pacific. Its main settlement is Vaitape.

Is this a scientific bathymetric model?

No. It is an educational explorer using published figures - the peak proportions, reef shape, and motu placement are schematic teaching cues, not a survey-grade model.
