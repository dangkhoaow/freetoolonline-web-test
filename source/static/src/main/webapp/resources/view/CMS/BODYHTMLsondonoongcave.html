Son Doong Cave is the world's largest cave by volume (about 14.375 million cubic meters). Three vast chambers connected by narrow passages create a hidden underground valley. Toggle sunlight shafts to see how light enters through roof collapse holes, and the water level to see the Rao Thuong River flowing through the cave floor.

About this scene

Published figures: length about 5.5 km (longest surveyed chamber Doline Hall about 240 m long, 150 m wide, 200 m high); underground river Rao Thuong flows year-round; temperature constant about 20-25°C; unique hanging jungle canopy ecosystem inside; discovered 1991 by local hunters, scientific expedition 2009; located in Phong Nha-Ke Bang National Park, Quang Binh Province, Vietnam.
