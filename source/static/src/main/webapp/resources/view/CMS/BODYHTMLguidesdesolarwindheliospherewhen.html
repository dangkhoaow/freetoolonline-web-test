Solar Wind Heliosphere 3D Explorer - When to Use It, Controls, and Alternatives

Last reviewed: 2026-08-28
freetoolonline.com editorial team

Open the Solar Wind Heliosphere 3D Explorer when you need the outer solar-system bubble - termination shock ~94 AU, heliopause ~121 AU, Voyager 1's 2012 crossing - and skip it when you need a full MHD heliosphere run. It is free and runs in the browser.


The controls, in the order you will use them

Open the Solar Wind Heliosphere 3D Explorer and wait for the Sun plus the nested cyan and magenta spheres. Read the facts panel for termination shock ~94 AU, heliopause ~121 AU (Voyager 1, 2012), and wind ~400 km/s. Click the cyan shock sphere, the magenta heliopause, or the Voyager marker for boundary details. Scrub Wind between 200 and 800 km/s and watch the outward particle-stream rate change. Press Pause wind to freeze the particles, Hide labels to clear the AU callouts, then AU table for the summary.


A five-minute classroom demo

A five-minute classroom demo starts when you open Solar Wind Heliosphere 3D Explorer, scrub the wind speed toward 400 km/s, and pause so students see the particle stream pass through the termination shock before reaching the heliopause where Voyager 1 crossed.

Sessions Solar Wind Heliosphere 3D Explorer does not fit

Sessions Solar Wind Heliosphere 3D Explorer does not fit are MHD equation solving, heliosheath structure forecasting, or precise Earth-position geometry. Use Solar System 3D for the inner planets or Aurora 3D Explorer for solar-wind effects at Earth.
