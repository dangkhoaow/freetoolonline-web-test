How it works: Use the time-scrub slider to advance through one complete Mercurian year (176 Earth days). Watch how Mercury's rotation synchronizes with its orbit - the planet rotates exactly 3 times for every 2 orbits around the Sun, creating this unique 3:2 resonance. The day-lit side (brighter) and night side (darker) show the day-night cycle.

Educational note: This visualization is an artistic, educational approximation. The display scales and animation speeds are adjusted for visibility on a computer screen - they do not represent real speeds or distances. The 3:2 resonance is a genuine orbital and rotational reality, caused by tidal forces from the Sun over millions of years.
