Frequently Asked Questions

What are the keyboard controls?

Use Arrow Keys to move left/right and look up/down, Space to jump, and E to grab and throw enemies. You can customize controls in the game settings menu.

How do I beat the game?

Space Huggers is a survival game without a fixed end - your goal is to survive as long as possible against progressively harder waves of enemies. Each run scores are recorded, challenging you to beat your personal best.

Is Space Huggers free?

Yes, Space Huggers is completely free to play in your browser. No account, no download, no ads. The game is open source under GPL-3.0 license.

Can I play offline?

Once the page loads, the game runs entirely in your browser and uses only local storage for saves. You can play offline, and your progress saves locally.

What browser do I need?

Space Huggers runs on any modern browser that supports HTML5, JavaScript, and Canvas. Works on desktop and mobile devices with keyboard or gamepad support.
