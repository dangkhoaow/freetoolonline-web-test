Frequently Asked Questions

Why is Mammoth Cave the longest cave system on Earth?

Surveyors have mapped more than 400 miles (about 650 km) of connected passages - more than twice the next-longest known system - because a protective sandstone caprock has kept the limestone passages open instead of collapsing as they formed over millions of years.

How many levels does Mammoth Cave have?

At least 5 mapped levels of passages, stacked vertically and linked by shafts and pits, making it a true three-dimensional maze rather than one long corridor.

What is the Echo River?

An underground river that runs through the lower levels of the cave. It was historically used for guided boat tours.

Is Mammoth Cave still being explored?

Yes. Cavers and surveyors add newly connected passages most years, which is why the total surveyed length keeps growing.

What lives in Mammoth Cave?

More than 130 species have been recorded, including the endangered Kentucky cave shrimp and several bat species.

Is this a scientific cave survey map?

No. It is an educational explorer using published figures - the passage shapes, level spacing, and vertical scale are schematic teaching cues, not survey cave geology.
