Frequently Asked Questions

Was Megalosaurus bigger than T-rex?

No, Megalosaurus was smaller. Megalosaurus reached about 8 meters long and weighed around 950 kg, while T-rex was 12 meters long and weighed over 8,000 kg. Megalosaurus hunted about 100 million years BEFORE T-rex existed and was the apex predator of its own Jurassic world.

How do we know what Megalosaurus looked like?

We have many Megalosaurus fossils from around the world, including skulls, teeth, and limb bones. The skeleton is well-documented. We DON'T know the exact skin color or surface texture - those are an artist's educated guess based on modern reptiles and living birds. The 3D model here matches the real skeletal proportions.

Did Megalosaurus have feathers?

There is no fossil evidence of feathers on Megalosaurus. Feathers are known only on some theropods YOUNGER than Megalosaurus, especially in Asia. So the 3D model shows bare reptilian skin as the most likely reconstruction.

What did Megalosaurus eat?

Megalosaurus was a carnivore - a meat-eater with powerful hind legs for running, short arms with claws, and a mouth full of serrated teeth designed for tearing flesh. It hunted large herbivorous dinosaurs and other prey of the Middle Jurassic.

Is this model scientifically accurate?

The SKELETON - bone shape, proportions, and poses - matches real fossils. The SKIN COLOR is an artist's interpretation; fossils never preserve skin color. The SIZE FIGURES (length 8 m, weight 950 kg) are the most commonly published estimates in scientific literature and are cited in the fact panel.

Can I use this 3D model for school or research?

Yes. The Megalosaurus viewer is free to use, runs fully in your browser, and is based on published paleontology figures. For school projects, always cite the real FIGURES (8 meters, 950 kg, 165 Mya) from the fact panel, not just "I saw it online." The model is a starting point for your research, not the primary source.
