When to Play Layer Flip Platformer

Layer Flip Platformer fits when you want a short, keyboard-first skill run: flip light and dark layers with Space, clear hand-crafted stages, and leave without saving. It loads about ~44 KB after Play and needs no account.

Last reviewed: 2026-07-17

When it fits

Layer Flip Platformer fits when you have a few minutes, a keyboard or gamepad, and you enjoy puzzle-platform timing more than endless score chasing. The distinctive mechanic is flipping the active layer mid-jump so platforms and spikes swap.

When it is not the right pick

Layer Flip Platformer is not the right pick if you need touch-only mobile controls (this build has none), if you want an endless runner like Pixel Spike Run, or if you prefer one-tap dash platforming like One Tap Platformer.

What to expect

What to expect is a Layer Flip Platformer with twenty-five hand-crafted stages, death and star counters, procedural Web Audio, and a title menu with controls help. Adapted from ONOFF (js13kGames 2018, MIT). Free, no ads in the iframe game, no install.
