Frequently Asked Questions

What does the Random Team Generator do?

It splits a list of names you paste in into a set number of teams. The list is shuffled fairly, then split evenly across the number of teams you choose, and each team is shown as its own card.

How many teams and names can I use?

You can create between 2 and 20 teams. You need at least as many names as teams - for example, 3 teams needs at least 3 names in the list.

Does it save or upload my list of names?

No. Everything runs in your browser - the names never reach a server, and the list clears when you leave or refresh the page.

Is this the same as the password, QR code, or barcode generator?

No. Those tools each produce a single value (one password, one QR code, one barcode). This tool takes a list of names and splits it into several teams.
