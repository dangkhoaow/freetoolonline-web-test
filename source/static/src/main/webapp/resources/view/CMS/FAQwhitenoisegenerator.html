Frequently Asked Questions

Why is there no sound until I click Play?

Browsers block audio from starting before you interact with the page. The bar visualizer runs on load either way; Play starts the actual audio.

Is the ocean-waves sound a real recording?

No. Every sound here, including ocean waves, is synthesized on-device by the Web Audio API - nothing is a sampled or streamed audio file.

Is anything uploaded, recorded, or saved?

No. All synthesis and playback stay in this tab. The chosen sound, volume, and timer reset to their defaults the next time you visit.

Does it keep playing if I close the tab or lock my phone?

No. Playback stops when the tab closes, and most mobile browsers pause or throttle it once the screen locks.
