Frequently Asked Questions

What does Text to Speech do?

Type or paste your text - or upload a transcript file - pick a voice, and press Speak to hear it read aloud in your browser. You can also generate a downloadable MP3 or WAV. Nothing is uploaded.

Which transcript and subtitle files can I upload?

.srt, .vtt, .sbv, and plain .txt files. Subtitle formats are parsed into timed cues with their time ranges, each playable on its own or all in sequence. Parsing happens locally in the tab; the file never leaves your device.

Can I download the speech as an MP3 or WAV?

Yes. Generate audio file synthesizes the text with an in-page eSpeak engine and produces an MP3 or WAV on your device. The system voices used by the Speak button cannot be recorded to a file - browsers forbid that - so downloads use the compact eSpeak voice, which sounds more synthetic than playback.

How does the language auto-detection work?

Entirely offline: the writing system identifies scripts such as Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, or Vietnamese diacritics, and common-word patterns separate Latin-alphabet languages such as English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, or German. A matching device voice is selected automatically when Auto voice is on; you can always override it in the Voice list.

Which languages can be exported as audio files?

The built-in eSpeak engine ships voices for 16 languages including English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Dutch, Turkish, Polish, Czech, Romanian, Swedish, Finnish, Hungarian, Greek, and Chinese. Languages without an eSpeak voice still play aloud through your device voices.

When should I reach for text to speech?

Reach for it when you would rather listen than read - hearing a message, an article, or a draft out loud. Use the Rate and Pitch sliders to adjust how the voice sounds.
