Simple Interest Calculator Online

Last reviewed: 2026-08-02

Work out simple interest earned on a principal amount over a fixed time period.

Use it to estimate interest on a short-term loan, a bond, or any amount where interest does not compound.


Key features

  • Three live-updating inputs - principal, annual rate percent, and time in years - with the interest and total amount computed as you type.
  • Recalculates on every keystroke in any field, so there is no submit button to click.
  • Money output is formatted as currency, so the interest and total read as proper amounts.
  • An explicit non-compounding note keeps this tool distinct from a compound-interest calculator.
  • Everything runs on this device, so no figures are uploaded and no account is needed.

How it works

The formula is straightforward: interest equals principal times the annual rate times the time in years, with no compounding - so a $1,000 principal at 5% for 2 years earns exactly $100 in interest ($1,000 x 0.05 x 2), not more.
