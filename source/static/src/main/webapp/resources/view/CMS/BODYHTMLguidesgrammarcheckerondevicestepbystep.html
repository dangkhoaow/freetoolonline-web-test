Check Grammar On Your Device - Step by Step

Last reviewed: 2026-08-12
freetoolonline.com Editorial Team

A full run on the Grammar Checker - Private, In Your Browser takes about a minute once an engine is ready: paste your text, run the check, read the corrected version next to a list of every change, and copy the result. This walkthrough goes control by control, including the one-time model download and what each status message means along the way.


Step 1 - read the sample before pasting anything

When the page loads, the input box already holds a short sample text of roughly 110 words seeded with deliberate mistakes, and the output area already shows its corrected version with the full change list - labeled as corrected ahead of time. Nothing was downloaded and nothing ran to produce it; it is there so you can see the output shape first. The prepared answer appears only for that exact built-in sample: your own text never receives a canned result.


Step 2 - paste your text

Replace the sample with your own text. A live word and character count runs under the box as you type. Two boundaries apply here: corrections are validated for English text only, and on the on-device model path the input is capped at 8,000 characters - the cap is stated in the page copy, and when longer text gets cut to fit, the output label says so.


Step 3 - get an engine ready

The page checks for the browser's built-in proofreading AI first. Where Chrome ships it, the status line reports it as available and the check runs with zero download - the page feature-detects this and never assumes it is there. Everywhere else, load a small open model with one click. Llama 3.2 1B is the default, with Llama 3.2 3B and Phi 3.5 mini as upgrades, and the download size is shown next to each model BEFORE anything starts - these are downloads in the hundreds of megabytes, so the page will not begin one silently. A progress bar tracks the load, and the weights are cached by the browser afterwards, so the next visit skips the download. The model choice is shared with the Private AI Chat page through the same cache.

If your browser lacks the graphics support the models need, the page says so honestly instead of pretending - you still get the sample demo and the notice, never a fake correction.


Step 4 - run the check and read the changes

Click the check button. The corrected text streams into the output area, followed by the change list: each fix as a short line, from your original wording to the corrected one with a brief reason. The corrections preserve your wording - errors are fixed, tone and style are left alone. Under the output, the engine-status line names which path produced what you are reading: the built-in browser AI, the named on-device model, or the prepared sample demo.


Step 5 - copy the result and clean up

One click on the copy button puts the corrected text on your clipboard. Since the small models catch common errors but stay below professional cloud checkers, give anything important a final read of your own before it goes out - the change list makes that quick. When you are done with the tool for good, the "Clear cached models" button removes the downloaded weights and returns the disk space.
