How to Use the Shoemaker-Levy 9 Jupiter Impact 3D Explorer - Step by Step

Last reviewed: 2026-08-11

Open the Shoemaker-Levy 9 Jupiter Impact 3D Explorer, press Play or drag the timeline slider, then read the facts panel to see the real published date and figures at each 1992-1994 milestone.


Press Play or drag the timeline slider

Press #t3dSl9PlayBtn to auto-advance through the real 1992-1994 timeline, or drag #t3dSl9Progress (0 to 100) to jump straight to any point. Dragging the slider pauses auto-play.


Read the milestone table

The facts panel lists all 13 real milestones - the 1992 tidal breakup, the 1993 discovery, and 11 individually dated fragment impacts - with the currently active one highlighted. Scrub the slider and watch the highlighted row change to match.


Watch the fragment train strike Jupiter one at a time

Each time the timeline passes an impact milestone, that fragment marker disappears and a dark scar appears on Jupiter's southern hemisphere. By 100% progress, all 11 named fragments have struck in their real July 1994 order.


Compare Fragment G's scar against Earth

Scrub past 40% progress to reach Fragment G, the largest impact. Press #t3dSl9EarthBtn to draw a circle matching Earth's real diameter beside Jupiter, so you can compare it directly against Fragment G's real scar size.


Click Jupiter, a fragment, or a scar

Click any object in the scene to refresh the facts panel with the current milestone without moving the slider.


Drag to orbit and use fullscreen

Drag to orbit the camera, scroll or pinch to zoom, and use the Fullscreen button to expand the whole wrapper for a clearer view of Jupiter and the fragment train.
