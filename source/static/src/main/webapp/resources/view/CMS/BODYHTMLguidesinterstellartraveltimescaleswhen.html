When to Use the Interstellar Travel Timescales 3D Explorer - Best Sessions and Limits

Last reviewed: 2026-07-26

Open the Interstellar Travel Timescales 3D Explorer when you want a human-scale sense of how long different real or published-concept vehicles would take to reach another star - not to look up a specific spacecraft's live position.


A vehicle-comparison timescale, not a live tracker

A session starts by comparing Voyager 1's real speed of about 17 km/s (about 75,000 years to Proxima Centauri) against the published Breakthrough Starshot concept's speed of about 0.2c (about 20 years) and light itself (about 4.25 years) - dragging the time slider on any vehicle moves its marker toward or onto Proxima Centauri.


Sessions this explorer does not fit

Sessions this page does not fit are looking up a real spacecraft's current live position (Voyager 1's own measured distance and heliopause-crossing dates are on the Voyager Interstellar Position 3D Explorer) or exploring Proxima Centauri's own planets and habitable-zone band (that is on the Proxima Centauri System 3D Explorer).
