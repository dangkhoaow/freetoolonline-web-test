Frequently Asked Questions

What does the final grade calculator do?

It solves for the score you need on a final exam to reach a target overall grade, given your current grade and how much the final counts toward the overall grade. It also works in reverse - enter a hypothetical final score and see the overall grade it produces.

How is this different from the GPA calculator?

The GPA calculator averages grades you already have across multiple courses, weighted by credit hours. This tool solves a single-course algebra problem for one still-unknown exam score - a different question the GPA calculator cannot answer.

What does it mean if the score needed is over 100%?

It means the target grade is not reachable this term - even a perfect 100% on the final would not be enough. The result also shows the highest overall grade a perfect final score would produce, so you can see how close a perfect score gets you.

Is my grade data sent anywhere?

No. The calculation runs entirely in your browser - nothing you type is stored or sent to this site's servers or anywhere else, and no account is needed.
