Frequently Asked Questions

What does Reading List Tracker Online do?

Keeps a to-read list of books and articles in this browser - add a title, tick it off once you have read it, and delete anything you have dropped.

When does this reading list help most?

Reach for it to keep a simple to-read versus finished list without installing a reading app or making an account - handy while browsing recommendations.

Will my reading list still be here next time?

Yes - your saved titles live in this browser's local storage, so the reading list survives a reload; only clearing this site's browser data removes it.
