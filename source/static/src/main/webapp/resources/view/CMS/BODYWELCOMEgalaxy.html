Galaxy 3D Simulator

Last reviewed: 2026-08-03
FreetoolOnline editorial team

Up to 100,000 points form the spiral arms on desktop (30,000 on phones so rotation stays smooth) - a real feel for how thin and vast a galaxy's disk is.


Key features

  • Color follows structure - a warm dense core fading to blue-white arms with scattered hot stars - all procedural, no images.
  • Drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, and one click goes fullscreen; a slow auto-rotation keeps the scene moving when you leave it idle.

Real Milky Way figures

The facts panel carries the real Milky Way figures:

Starsmore than 100 billion
Diameterabout 100,000 light-years across
Sun's distance from the centerabout 26,000 light-years out
Sun's orbital periodroughly 230 million years

Art tuned to look right, not physics

Worth being clear about what the spiral is: it is parametric art tuned to look right, not a gravitational simulation - the facts-panel figures are accurate, but the arm shapes themselves are not physics-modeled.

For a step-by-step walkthrough of the spiral-arm controls and the Milky Way figures, read the Galaxy 3D Simulator step-by-step guide. The Space 3D collection also includes a Solar System 3D Explorer and a Black Hole 3D Visualizer.
