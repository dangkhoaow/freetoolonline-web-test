Type or paste your text - or upload a transcript file (.srt, .vtt, .sbv, .txt) - pick a voice, and press Speak to hear it read aloud in your browser. Subtitle files are split into timed cues you can play one by one, the language is detected automatically to pick a matching voice, and Generate audio file produces a downloadable MP3 or WAV entirely on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

Prefer recording your own voice? Capture it with the voice recorder.
