Frequently Asked Questions

How big is the Big Room at Carlsbad Caverns?

Its floor covers about 8.2 acres (roughly 33,000 sq m), making it one of the largest known cave chambers in North America, about 230 m (roughly 750 ft) below the surface at its deepest point.

How did Carlsbad Caverns form?

Sulfuric-acid dissolution: hydrogen sulfide rising from nearby Permian Basin oil and gas deposits mixed with groundwater and oxidized into sulfuric acid, dissolving the surrounding Permian-age reef limestone - a different, larger-scale process than the carbonic-acid karst that carves most limestone caves.

What is the Giant Dome formation?

One of the cave's largest single dripstone formations, about 19 m (62 ft) tall. The 3D scene marks a stand-in for it with a highlight ring you can toggle on or off.

Where is Carlsbad Caverns National Park?

In the Guadalupe Mountains near the city of Carlsbad, New Mexico, USA. The park has been a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1995.

How long is the Big Room Trail?

A roughly level, self-guided loop about 1.25 miles (2 km) - the standard visitor route through the chamber.

Who discovered Carlsbad Caverns?

Local cowboy Jim White explored the cave in the early 1900s. It was declared a national monument in 1923 and a national park in 1930.

Is this a scientific terrain model?

No. It is an educational explorer using published figures - the chamber shape, dripstone forest, and Giant Dome marker are procedural teaching cues, not survey cave geology.
