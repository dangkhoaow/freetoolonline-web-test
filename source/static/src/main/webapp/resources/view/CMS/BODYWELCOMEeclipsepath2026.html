Where the August 12, 2026 path of totality crosses land

This is the first total solar eclipse to cross mainland Europe since 1999. NASA and ESA maps place the path over Greenland's ice sheet, the west coast of Iceland near Reykjavik, open North Atlantic water, and northern Spain before it ends near the Mediterranean coast and a small corner of Portugal, just before local sunset.

How long totality lasts, and who sees only a partial eclipse

Most places along the path see under two minutes of totality; only locations very near the center of the path in Greenland, northern Russia, or the North Atlantic get a little longer, though still under two and a half minutes. Alaska, Canada, and the northern United States see only a partial eclipse - a small bite out of the Sun's disk, not full darkness.
