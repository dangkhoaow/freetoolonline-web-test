This page renders an Ankylosaurus as a 3D model you can spin in the browser - drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, toggle a 1.8 m person beside it for scale, and click the head, body, a leg, or the clubbed tip of the tail to read a fact about that part.

Ankylosaurus lived in the Late Cretaceous, about 68 to 66 million years ago, in what is now western North America (Canada and the USA). The Natural History Museum cites a length of about 8 m and a mass of about 8 tonnes. A sheath of bony armor covered the body, and the tip of the tail ended in a heavy bony club.

The genus was named by Barnum Brown in 1908 from Montana finds (type species Ankylosaurus magniventris). Adult size estimates vary across incomplete specimens; this page follows the NHM listing and notes that peer-reviewed work often quotes a wider length and mass range.
