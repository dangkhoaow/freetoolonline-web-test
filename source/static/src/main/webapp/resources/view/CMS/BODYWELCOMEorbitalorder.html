Orbital Order

Last reviewed: 2026-09-16

Orbital Order turns the Aufbau principle - the rule that electrons fill the lowest-energy orbitals first - into a reflex game you steer with the mouse. It runs free in the browser with no account or install.

StepAction
LaunchPlay on this page; about ~32 KB loads after you press Play
MoveCursor attracts blue s-electrons and repels orange p-electrons
GoalSeat each electron in an empty orbital without double-hitting a filled shell
ProgressClimb six elements from hydrogen through oxygen
AudioProcedural audio plays after the first click
Saved on-deviceBest time and tutorial progress stay under ftol:orbitalorder keys; nothing is uploaded
