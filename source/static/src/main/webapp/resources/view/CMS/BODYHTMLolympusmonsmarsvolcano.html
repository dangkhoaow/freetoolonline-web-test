About this scene

The facts panel fills when the scene loads. Drag the exaggeration slider to see the real figures update.



The gray column is Mount Everest (8.849 km above sea level); the blue column is Mauna Loa (about 10.211 km, ocean floor to summit) - both scaled by the same exaggeration factor as the volcano.

Olympus Mons is the largest known volcano in the solar system: NASA's Mars Atlas cites a base diameter of about 624 km and a summit roughly 25 km above the surrounding plains, with an 80 km wide caldera complex at the top. Mars has no plate tectonics and only about 38% of Earth's surface gravity, which is why the same hot spot could keep building one shield this large instead of drifting into a chain of smaller ones the way Hawaii's volcanoes did on Earth's moving Pacific Plate.
