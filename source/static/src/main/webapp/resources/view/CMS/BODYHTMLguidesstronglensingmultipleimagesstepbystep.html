How to Explore the Strong Gravitational Lensing 3D Explorer

Last reviewed: 2026-07-25

Reviewed by FreetoolOnline editorial team

Open the Strong Gravitational Lensing 3D Explorer, pick a case, and drag the alignment slider to see how many images or how stretched the arcs appear.

  1. Open /space-3d/strong-lensing-multiple-images.html and wait for the lens marker, the faint ring, and the source to render.
  2. Click "Einstein Cross case" to load the 4-point-image illustration, or "Abell 2218 case" to load the multi-arc illustration.
  3. Drag the alignment slider (0-100). Low values show more images or longer arcs; high values fade the extra images toward a single dominant image.
  4. Read the facts panel for the active case's real redshift, image count, and discovery figures, plus a live count of images or arcs currently visible.
  5. Notice the faint ring - it marks the lens's critical curve, the boundary near which the strongest arcs and extra images form.
  6. Drag to orbit and scroll or pinch to zoom to see the image or arc arrangement from a different angle.
