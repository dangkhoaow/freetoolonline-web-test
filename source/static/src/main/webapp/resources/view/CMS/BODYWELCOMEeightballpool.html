Eight Ball Pool

Eight Ball Pool is a free browser billiards game. Press Play to load it, then start a match - no account and no install needed.


Key features

  • Two ways to play: 1P vs AI or 2P local on one screen
  • Lightweight download - about 13 MB loads only after you press Play

Controls

  • Aim the cue with the mouse
  • Set shot power with the W and S keys
  • Left-click to shoot

Privacy

Nothing is uploaded. Eight Ball Pool runs entirely in your browser, so your gameplay stays on your device.
