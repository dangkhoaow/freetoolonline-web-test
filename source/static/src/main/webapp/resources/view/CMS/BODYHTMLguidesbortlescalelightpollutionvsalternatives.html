Bortle Scale Light Pollution 3D Explorer vs alternatives

The Bortle Scale Light Pollution 3D Explorer shows what every one of the 9 published classes actually looks like in one interactive scene - a Sky Quality Meter wins for a real repeatable number at your own site; a light-pollution atlas wins for a real map of your actual location.

OptionStrengthAccessTrade-off
Bortle Scale Light Pollution 3D (this page)All 9 classes shown side by side with a live star count and Milky Way fadeFree, in browserTeaching illustration; not a real reading of any specific location
Sky Quality Meter (SQM)Real, repeatable brightness reading in magnitudes per square arcsecondRequires buying a physical meterOnly measures your one location; no visual teaching scene
Online light-pollution atlases (e.g. World Atlas of Artificial Night Sky Brightness)Real satellite-derived map of your actual locationFree, onlineShows a modeled brightness value, not what a human eye actually sees at each class
