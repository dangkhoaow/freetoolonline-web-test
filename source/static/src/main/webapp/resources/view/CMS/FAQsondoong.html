Frequently Asked Questions

How big is Son Doong Cave?

Son Doong Cave is the world's largest natural cave by volume. Its main passage stretches about 5.5 to 9 kilometers (reports vary depending on survey methods), with chamber widths of 200 to 240 meters and heights reaching 80 to 200 meters. This enormous cavern houses landscapes and ecosystems that rival the natural wonders on Earth's surface.

What makes the jungle inside the cave unique?

The primeval jungle inside Son Doong Cave is a rare ecosystem. Light enters through a massive sinkhole in the ceiling, allowing plants and trees to grow inside the cave - a phenomenon seldom seen in cave systems. This underground jungle creates an otherworldly landscape where ancient forests exist in permanent twilight.

Is Son Doong Cave safe to explore?

Son Doong Cave is open to tourists, but exploration is restricted to guided tours only. The National Park authority carefully manages visitor numbers and routes to preserve this geological wonder. Tours typically require good physical fitness as the terrain is challenging, involving climbing, wading through the underground river, and traversing uneven surfaces. How did this cave form?

Son Doong Cave was formed over millions of years through the dissolution of limestone by acidic groundwater. The limestone bedrock dates back approximately 270 million years to the Permian period. Over roughly the last 2.7 million years, the distinctive cavern and its formations - stalactites, stalagmites, and dripstone features - gradually sculpted the landscape we see today.
