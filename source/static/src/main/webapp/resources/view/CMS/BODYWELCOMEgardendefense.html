Garden Defense

Last reviewed: 2026-07-04

Pick a defender card, then click or tap a cell to plant it; the first placement starts wave 1 of 5.

  • Shooter - 100 energy.
  • Battery - 50 energy, makes more energy.
  • Wall - 50 energy, soaks damage.

Energy, waves, and how you win

Invaders come in two tiers - fast red ones and tough purple ones - and each wave sends more, faster; a short breather between waves is when to spend the energy you saved on new cards.

Lose only if an invader crosses the left edge; clear all 5 waves and the garden is saved. Your best wave is remembered in this browser.

Playing in your browser

The whole game is JavaScript drawing to a canvas on your device - no install, no account, nothing uploaded. One click plays it fullscreen with the cards and buttons still visible, and Escape returns you to the page.

There is no sound or music, so it is fine to play with speakers off.
