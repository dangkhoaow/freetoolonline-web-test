Neon Crime City is a browser-based open-world crime city sandbox: drive, roam on foot, and explore a neon skyline without installing anything.

NEON CRIME CITY

Open-world crime sandbox with driving and on-foot exploration. The game loads only after you press Play, so the page stays light.

Keyboard + mouse recommended; the in-game settings menu lists every key binding.

Press Play to load the game.
If the game does not start, check that WebAssembly is enabled in your browser.
