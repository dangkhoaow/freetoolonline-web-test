How to Explore the 2I/Borisov Interstellar Comet 3D Explorer

Last reviewed: 2026-07-25

Reviewed by FreetoolOnline editorial team

Open the 2I/Borisov Interstellar Comet 3D Explorer and toggle between 2I/Borisov and Halley's Comet to compare each object's real orbital eccentricity and coma composition side by side.

  1. Open /space-3d/borisov-interstellar-comet.html and wait for both comet models and the Sun to render.
  2. Drag the toggle switch (0-1) to swap the highlighted comet between 2I/Borisov (blue-tinted coma, open path) and Halley's Comet (orange-tinted coma, closed loop).
  3. Read the facts panel for the active comet's real orbital eccentricity, perihelion distance, speed, and CO/H2O ratio.
  4. Check the comparison table below the facts panel - the highlighted row is the active comet.
  5. Notice the two path curves: Borisov's open hyperbola never closes, while Halley's ellipse loops back around the Sun.
  6. Drag to orbit and scroll or pinch to zoom to see each comet's coma density up close.
