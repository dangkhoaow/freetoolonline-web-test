Frequently Asked Questions

Is my text really never uploaded?

Yes, on every tier. The check runs either through the browser's own built-in AI or through a model that was downloaded to your device - there is no server on this page for your text to be sent to.

Do I have to download anything just to try it?

No. A sample with deliberate mistakes and its prepared correction are visible the moment the page loads, and on Chrome versions with the built-in Proofreader the check on your own text also needs no download at all.

How big is the model download, and how often do I pay it?

The exact size appears next to each model in the picker before the download starts - the default Llama 3.2 1B is the smallest of the three options. It is a one-time cost per machine: the browser keeps the weights cached, and the same cache serves the Private AI Chat page too.

How good are the corrections compared with Grammarly-class tools?

Lower - and the page says so. Small 1-3B on-device models reliably catch everyday errors like agreement, tense, and its versus it's, but they miss subtleties a professional cloud checker finds. The change list exists precisely so you can review every fix before accepting it.

What happens with a very long text?

On the on-device model path the input is cut at 8,000 characters so it fits the model's context window; the tool states this cap and tells you when a text was truncated. Check longer documents in sections.

Can it also reword or improve my style?

No - it deliberately keeps your wording and only fixes errors. If you want open-ended rewriting or writing suggestions, use the Private AI Chat page, which runs the same on-device models without a fixed task.
