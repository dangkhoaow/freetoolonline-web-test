Percent Error Calculator Online Step By Step

Last reviewed: 2026-08-02

Percent Error Calculator Online runs in three steps: open the tool, provide your input, then copy the result. This page walks through what each step expects.


Step 1 - open the tool

Go to Percent Error Calculator Online. Nothing needs to be installed or signed in before you start.


Step 2 - provide your input

Enter the accepted (theoretical) value first, then the measured (experimental) value. Use it for a lab measurement against a known reference value, a manufacturing tolerance check, or comparing an estimate against the actual result.


Step 3 - copy the result

The calculation runs locally in the browser - no upload and no account. The percent error, the absolute error, and whether the measurement ran high or low update as you type.

For a plain X% of Y, what-percent-of, or percent-change question, the percentage calculator is the better alternative - this tool is specifically for comparing a measured value against an accepted value.
