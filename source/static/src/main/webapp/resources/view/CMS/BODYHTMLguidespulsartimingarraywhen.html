When to Use Pulsar Timing Array 3D Explorer - Best Sessions and Limits

Last reviewed: 2026-08-04

Open the Pulsar Timing Array 3D Explorer when you need to see how the angular separation between two pulsars predicts the correlation their timing wobbles should share if a gravitational-wave background is passing through.


A gravitational-wave-background lesson

A lesson starts when you sweep the separation slider and watch the live-computed correlation rise from 0.5 near zero separation, dip negative for pairs roughly 50 to 100 degrees apart, and climb back to 0.25 at 180 degrees - the exact shape NANOGrav and its partner arrays compared their real 2023 data against.


Sessions this explorer does not fit

Sessions this page does not fit are lessons about a single pulsar's own spin and beam mechanism - use the Neutron Star Pulsar Explorer for that - or LIGO's kilohertz gravitational-wave chirp from a merging black-hole binary - use the Gravitational Waves Explorer.
