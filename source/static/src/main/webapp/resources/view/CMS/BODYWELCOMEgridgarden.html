Welcome to Grid Garden - a free browser CSS grid teaching game with no account or install.


Key features

  • Write grid properties so water lands on carrot patches across 28 levels.
  • About ~1.0 MB loads after you press Play.

Your progress and privacy

  • Progress stays in this browser under namespaced ftol:gridgarden keys; use Reset in the level menu to clear saved answers.
  • Nothing is uploaded; play stays on-device.
