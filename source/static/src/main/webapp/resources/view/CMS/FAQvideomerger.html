Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account, and does the download carry a watermark?

No to both. There is no account, sign-up or login, and the merged file - named like merged-3-clips.mp4, or .webm - downloads without a watermark. There is no cloud save or project file either; "Merge again" keeps your clips and settings for another run, so you can try a second version with a different frame shape or soundtrack.

Are my videos uploaded to a server?

No. The browser reads your clips and music straight from your device and builds the merged file in the tab's memory, so no clip, music track or finished video is sent anywhere. Pressing "Start over" or closing the tab releases the clips and the result, and nothing is stored once the tab is closed.

Can I merge clips with different formats, sizes or orientations?

Yes. "Choose videos" accepts MP4, MOV, M4V, WebM, MKV, AVI and other video types your browser lists, and one merge can mix them. Portrait phone clips are turned upright, "Frame shape" and "Fit" set how each clip sits in the single output frame, and the output takes the highest clip frame rate, rounded to a standard rate within the engine's cap.

Will merging reduce the video quality?

Only if the clips are re-encoded. A trim, a mute, a transition, music, a "Frame shape" other than Original or a "Quality" other than Auto forces a re-encode, even for clips that would otherwise get the "Lossless join - no re-encode" badge. Re-encoding uses a high quality setting but is not bit-identical, and HDR or 10-bit clips become standard 8-bit video, so their colors can look different.

Is there a file size limit?

Only your device's memory sets one; there is no server-side cap. Before merging, the tool warns when the estimated output passes about 1.5 GB with browser encoding, or when the clips add up to more than about 500 MB in compatibility mode on a desktop. Phones get that warning at lower sizes.

Can I merge videos on my phone?

Yes. It runs in Safari 16.4 or newer and in current Chrome, Edge and Firefox, though Firefox on Android has no WebCodecs and uses the slower compatibility mode. On a phone, keep the tab in the foreground until the merge finishes: some browsers pause background tabs, and closing the tab stops the merge. The tool asks the browser to keep the screen awake where supported.

Can I add background music and still hear people talking?

Yes. After you click "Add music", leave "Clip audio while music plays" on Lower or switch it to Keep, and pull the "Music volume" slider down if voices still get buried under the track. To silence one noisy clip without touching the others, press "Mute" on its card; there is no separate volume level for each clip.

Can I use a different transition between each pair of clips?

Yes - each gap between two cards has its own selector, so one cut can stay plain on "No transition" while the next uses "Crossfade" or "Fade through black". Only the timing is shared: a single "Transition length" of 0.5, 1 or 1.5 seconds covers every gap. Wipes, slides and other styles are not offered.

Why is my merge slow, or why did it stop with an error?

Speed depends on the device and browser. If the badge reads "Compatibility mode (slower)", single-thread FFmpeg.wasm is doing the work. A failed merge shows a red panel naming the clip and the reason - an unsupported codec, an unreadable file or not enough memory. Remove or replace that clip and try again. DRM-protected video cannot be merged.
