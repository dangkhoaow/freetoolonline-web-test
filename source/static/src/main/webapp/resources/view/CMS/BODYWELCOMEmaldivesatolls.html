Maldives Atolls 3D Explorer

Last reviewed: 2026-08-28

Reviewed by FreetoolOnline editorial team

This browser explorer shows the Maldives as a double chain of ring-shaped coral atolls in the Indian Ocean: each ring a raised reef rim enclosing a shallow turquoise lagoon, with low sand-and-coral islets sitting on the rim itself.

The Maldives is made up of about 1,192 individual coral islands, grouped into 26 natural atolls that form a double chain running roughly north-south across the Indian Ocean, south-southwest of India and Sri Lanka. Each atoll is a ring of coral reef built up around the rim of a submerged volcanic seamount, enclosing a shallow lagoon inside. The country's average ground elevation is only about 1.5 m (4.9 ft) above sea level, making it the lowest-lying country on Earth - nowhere in the Maldives rises very far above the waterline. That thin margin is why the country is so widely cited in discussions of sea-level rise: a modest increase in ocean level removes a disproportionate share of the country's usable land. The capital, Male, is itself a densely built island packed onto one of the larger atoll rims.

The whole Maldives scene - the double chain of atoll rings, the shallow lagoons, the reef-rim islets, and the sea-level and lagoon-band toggles - is built and drawn on your own device with WebGL through the site-vendored three.js engine. Orbit the scene from above to see how the two rows of rings line up along the chain, and toggle the sea-level plane on and off to compare it against the reef rims. Nothing about your visit is uploaded to a server, and the engine loads once (about 0.7 MB) and is then cached.

This is an educational approximation - the ring count, spacing, lagoon depth, and reef-rim freeboard are procedural teaching cues, not survey terrain or bathymetry.
