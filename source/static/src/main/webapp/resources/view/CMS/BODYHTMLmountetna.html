Mount Etna rises on the east coast of Sicily, Italy, about 29 km north-northwest of Catania - Europe's tallest and most active volcano. Unlike a single steep cone, Etna is a broad shield-to-strato profile with four separate summit craters clustered near its top. This 3D explorer shows that wide-based silhouette and the flanks scarred by centuries of lava flows.
