Frequently Asked Questions

What does the fuel cost calculator work out?

From trip distance, fuel efficiency and fuel price it computes the fuel needed (gallons or liters), the total trip fuel cost, the cost per mile or km, and - when you enter more than one traveler - the per-person share. Results update live as you type.

Can I use kilometers and L/100km instead of miles and MPG?

Yes. The units dropdown switches between US (miles, MPG, price per gallon) and metric (km, L/100km, price per liter), and it converts the values already in the form so the result stays equivalent rather than resetting.

Does the tool fetch live fuel prices?

No. You enter the price you actually pay, in your own currency - that is why the outputs are plain numbers without a currency symbol. Real-world consumption also varies with traffic, terrain and load, so treat the result as a planning estimate.

Is anything I type uploaded?

No. The arithmetic runs in the page itself - distance, efficiency, price and traveler count never leave your device, and no signup is needed.
