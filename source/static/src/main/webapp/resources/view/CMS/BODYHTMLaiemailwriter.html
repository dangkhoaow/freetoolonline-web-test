A reply mode drafts an answer to an email you paste in; the pasted email never leaves your device.

How to draft an email here

  1. Say who the email is for and what it should achieve - the subject line is built from that goal.
  2. Type your key points, one per line. Each line becomes a paragraph in the draft.
  3. Pick a tone: professional, friendly, short-and-direct, or apologetic. The tone changes the greeting, the opening line, and the sign-off.
  4. Press Draft email. With nothing downloaded you get a template draft immediately; the status line under the output names which mode wrote it.
  5. Edit the draft, press Copy, and paste it into your own mail app - this page never sends anything.

To answer a message instead, switch to reply mode and paste the email you received before adding your points.
