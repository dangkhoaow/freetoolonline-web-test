Where the Meteor Shower Radiant Explorer wins

The Meteor Shower Radiant Explorer wins when the question is why a shower's meteors look like they all radiate from one point in the sky - it is the only page on the site with that sky-geometry view.


Where the Annual Meteor Shower Calendar Explorer wins

The Annual Meteor Shower Calendar Explorer wins when the question is which shower is active tonight, which one peaks next, or how the year's 12 major showers compare in strength - it is the only page on the site with a day-of-year slider and all 12 showers on one dial.
