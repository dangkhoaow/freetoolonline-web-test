Tip Calculator

Last reviewed: 2026-09-03

Split a restaurant or service bill and work out the tip amount per person.


Key features

  • Shows the tip amount, the bill-plus-tip total, and the equal per-person share together, so you do not have to work out the split separately.
  • Updates live as you change the bill, tip percent, or number of people - no submit button to click.
  • Accepts a fractional number of people (for example 2.5) and divides the total without rounding to a whole person.

Does not provide financial advice - results are general estimates and do not account for tax being calculated separately from the tip, or for splitting the tip unevenly across people.


When to use it

Use it to settle a group bill quickly without doing the math by hand.


Compared with a desktop app or an upload-based service

Three common ways to work out a tip are a browser-based calculator like this one, a desktop application, or an upload-based online service. They differ in where your numbers go and what you have to install first.

Your numbers stay private

Your bill total and party size never leave this tab - the per-person math runs locally, skipping any server call or account.


Splitting a bill three or more ways

Tip Calculator rounds the per-person figure to the nearest cent for display, so multiplying that displayed amount back by the number of people can land a cent or two off the actual total once three or more people are splitting an unevenly divisible bill. For example, an 18% tip on a $50 bill brings the total to $59; split three ways, Tip Calculator shows $19.67 per person, and $19.67 times three is $59.01, one cent more than the actual total. A more evenly divisible total, such as $60 split three ways, lands exactly on $20.00 per person with no rounding gap at all. This cent-level difference is a property of dividing money into whole cents, not a bug in the calculator - the tip amount and total shown above the per-person line are always exact, only the per-person split can be off by a cent or two when the group size does not divide the total evenly.
