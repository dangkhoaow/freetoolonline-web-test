Frequently Asked Questions

What does Tip Calculator do?

Split a restaurant or service bill and work out the tip amount per person.

When should I reach for tip calculator?

Use it to settle a group bill quickly without doing the math by hand.

What complementary tools work well alongside tip calculator?

Change any field at any time and every total recalculates immediately.

Does the tip calculator require a whole number of people?

No. The people field only floors at one, so entering a fractional number of people, such as 2.5, is accepted and the total is divided by that exact number rather than rounded to a whole person.

What currency symbol does the calculator show?

The tip amount, total, and per-person share always render with a US dollar sign because the calculator formats every amount as US dollars. Only the thousands and decimal separators follow your browser's regional settings, not the currency itself. If the browser cannot run that formatting for any reason, the amount falls back to a plain dollar sign followed by two decimal places instead - a fallback that is rarely needed, since virtually every current browser supports the formatting it prefers.

Are the bill, tip percent, and people fields pre-filled?

Yes. The tool loads with a working example already entered - a $50 bill, an 18% tip, and one person - so the tip, total, and per-person amounts are visible and correct before you type anything.

Is there a maximum tip percent?

No. The tip percent field has no upper limit, so entering a value over 100, such as 200, is computed literally - a 200% tip on a $50 bill produces a $100.00 tip and a $150.00 total.

Can I enter a decimal tip percent, like 18.5%?

Yes. The field's spinner arrows step by whole percentages, but typing a decimal value directly, such as 18.5, is read and computed exactly - the result is not rounded to the nearest whole percent.
