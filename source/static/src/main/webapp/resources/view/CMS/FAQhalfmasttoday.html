Frequently Asked Questions

What does Are Flags at Half-Mast Today? (US Federal Status) do?

Check whether today is one of the small set of RECURRING US federal half-staff flag observances.

When should I use Are Flags at Half-Mast Today? (US Federal Status)?

It covers the fixed, statute/tradition-based observances that fall on the same calendar rule every year - Peace Officers Memorial Day, Memorial Day, Patriot Day, POW/MIA Recognition Day, and Pearl Harbor Remembrance Day.

Does this track flag orders issued after a death or tragedy?

No. This does NOT track proclamation-driven half-staff orders following a death or tragedy - those are announced case-by-case and cannot be predicted from any fixed rule; the page links the official GSA half-staff notice page for that.
