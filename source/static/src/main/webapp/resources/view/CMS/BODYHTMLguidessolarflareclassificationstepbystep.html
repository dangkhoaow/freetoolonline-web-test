How to Read the Solar Flare Classification 3D Explorer

Last reviewed: 2026-07-25

Reviewed by FreetoolOnline editorial team

Open the Solar Flare Classification 3D Explorer, drag the flare-class slider from A up to X20, and watch the flare loop, particle burst, and Earth reach arc scale with the real GOES and NOAA figures in the facts panel.

  1. Open /space-3d/solar-flare-classification.html and wait for the flare scene and facts panel to render.
  2. Drag the flare-class slider from A toward X20.
  3. Watch the flare loop brighten and the particle burst intensify as the class rises.
  4. Watch the red reach arc appear on the Earth companion once the class reaches M1/R1, and grow wider toward X20/R5.
  5. Read the facts panel for the real GOES peak X-ray flux and the matching NOAA Radio Blackout rating.
  6. Drag to orbit and scroll or pinch to zoom for a closer look.
