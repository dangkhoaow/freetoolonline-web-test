Unlucky Crossing

Last reviewed: 2026-09-20

Unlucky Crossing is a free browser Halloween arcade game you can play without an account or install. Press Play to load the roughly 72 KB canvas engine in a same-origin iframe, then click Start and guide a black cat across an endless run of streets. Nothing leaves this device except the initial page and game files from this site. For more free browser games, open the games hub.


Controls

  • Arrow Up or W advances one row when the lane ahead is clear.
  • Arrow Left or Right (or A and D) strafe along the current row; the cat faces the way it moves.
  • On a phone, tap the on-screen L and R buttons to strafe, or tap the road to step forward.

Scoring and lives

  • Crossing close to the humans earns bad-luck points; passing two at once pays about a 1.25x bonus and three or more about 2x.
  • Each run starts with nine happiness lives. Bumping a human costs one life and gives a brief blink of invincibility.
  • A strong cross can drop a fish cracker on the next row that restores one life when you walk over it.
  • The rows keep generating forever, so the goal is a high score before your lives reach zero.
