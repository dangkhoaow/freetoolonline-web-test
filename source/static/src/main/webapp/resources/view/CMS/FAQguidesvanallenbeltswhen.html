Why are there two separate belts instead of one?

The inner belt is dominated by protons trapped from cosmic-ray collisions with the upper atmosphere; the outer belt is dominated by electrons from the solar wind. Their different charge-to-mass ratios result in different drift directions and altitude ranges - inner at roughly 1,000 - 6,000 km, outer at 13,000 - 60,000 km.
