Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Huacachina?

In the Ica Region of Peru, about 300 km south of Lima.

Is Huacachina's lagoon natural?

Yes, it is a natural desert lagoon, though its water level has fluctuated over the decades due to regional groundwater use and is partially maintained today by pumping.

How tall are the dunes around the oasis?

Commonly cited at up to about 100 m above the oasis floor.

How many people live in Huacachina?

The village population is commonly cited around 200 residents.

What is Huacachina known for?

Sandboarding and dune-buggy tours across the surrounding dunes, plus the local legend that ties the lagoon to a mermaid or water-spirit story.

Is this a scientific terrain model?

No. It is an educational explorer using published figures - the dune ring, ripple pattern, and vertical scale are schematic teaching cues, not survey terrain.
