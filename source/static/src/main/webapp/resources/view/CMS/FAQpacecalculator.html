Frequently Asked Questions

What does the pace calculator do?

It solves the runner's triangle: pick which of distance, time or pace to compute, enter the other two, and the result updates as you type - with the pace shown in both min/km and min/mile, the speed in km/h and mph, and an even-pace split table at the standard race checkpoints.

How do I convert a min/km pace to min/mile?

You do not have to - every result shows the pace both ways. A mile is 1.609344 km, so a 5:00 min/km pace reads 8:03 min/mile, and switching the unit select converts the numbers you already entered in place instead of clearing them.

What pace do I need to finish a marathon in 4 hours?

Pick the marathon preset (42.195 km), enter 4:00:00 and solve for pace: 5:41 min/km, which is 9:09 min/mile. The split table below the result then shows the even-pace passing times at 5K, 10K, half and 30K for that plan.

Are the split times exact race predictions?

No. The table projects a perfectly even pace, and real race splits vary with terrain, weather and fatigue. Use it as a planning estimate - a pacing target per checkpoint - not as a coaching plan or a guarantee.

Does anything I enter leave my browser?

No. The math runs locally in the page - nothing is uploaded, stored or sent, no account is needed, and the tool does not read a GPS watch or training app; every number is one you type in.
