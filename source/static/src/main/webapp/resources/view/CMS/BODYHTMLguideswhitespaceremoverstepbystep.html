Whitespace Remover - Step by Step

Last reviewed: 2026-07-26

Whitespace Remover runs in three steps: open the tool, paste your text, then copy the cleaned result. This page walks through what each step expects.


Step 1 - open the tool

Go to Whitespace Remover. Nothing needs to be installed or signed in before you start.


Step 2 - paste your text

Paste text with extra spaces or tabs into the input. Pick Collapse spaces (default), Trim each line, Drop blank lines, or Remove all spaces. The cleaned output updates live as you type or change the option.


Step 3 - copy the result

Click Copy to put the cleaned text on the clipboard. Cleanup runs locally - no upload and no account. Nothing you paste is saved between visits.

Does not edit files on disk - paste only. Does not remove image whitespace or run AI models.
