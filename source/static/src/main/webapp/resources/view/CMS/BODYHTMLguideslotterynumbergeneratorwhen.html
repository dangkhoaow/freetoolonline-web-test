When to Use a Lottery Number Generator

Last reviewed: 2026-07-30

Use the Lottery Number Generator when you need a quick, fair set of numbers without writing them yourself or relying on a lottery terminal.

Playing a major lottery

If you play Powerball, Mega Millions, EuroMillions, UK 49s, Lotto 6/49, or Oz Lotto, the generator knows the correct number ranges and ball counts for each game. Select the game from the dropdown and your tickets are ready in one click.

Running a raffle or classroom draw

When you need random numbers for a non-standard game - a workplace raffle, a classroom activity, or a custom prize draw - switch to Custom range and enter your own minimum, maximum, and ball count.

Generating several entries at once

If you want to enter the same draw multiple times, select up to 10 tickets per click. Each ticket is drawn independently so you get a varied set of combinations without repeating yourself.

When privacy matters

Every draw runs inside your browser using crypto.getRandomValues. No numbers are sent to any server and nothing is logged. This suits anyone who prefers to keep their lottery entries private.
