Crystal Cave Flyer

Welcome - press Play to load Crystal Cave Flyer. About 610 KB downloads once (mostly the shared 3D engine), then it is cached; best totals stay on this device under ftol:crystalcaveflyer:best. Nothing is uploaded and no account is needed.

The whole game is one input: hold Space, the mouse button, or a touch to climb, release to sink. Every rock wall ahead carries a single circular gap, and the gap shrinks slightly as your distance grows while the forward speed ramps toward double the starting pace - so early runs teach the rhythm and later runs test it. Crystals float in most gaps and add 25 points each on top of the meters flown.

Because a run needs only one finger, it works as well on a phone held in one hand as on a desktop. A wall clip ends the run instantly, and a tap starts the next one.
