Comet Orbit 3D Explorer Step by Step

Last reviewed: 2026-07-14

The Comet Orbit 3D Explorer starts as soon as the page loads: drag to orbit the Sun and path, scroll to zoom, and press Play orbit. Everything renders on your own device - no install, no account.


Step 1 - find the ellipse

Step 1 - find the ellipse na cena Comet Orbit 3D: procure a trajetoria excentrica com o Sol perto de um foco; o painel lista P cerca de 76 yr, q 0.586 AU, Q 35.1 AU e e 0.967 para o preset tipo Halley.


Step 2 - scrub perihelion

Drag Orbit progress toward perihelion so tails lengthen; both cones stay roughly anti-sunward.


Step 3 - toggle tails

Step 3 - toggle tails no visualizador Comet Orbit 3D: clique Ion tail e Dust tail para ocultar ou mostrar cada cauda, depois tela cheia para manter os controles visiveis enquanto voce orbita a vista.
