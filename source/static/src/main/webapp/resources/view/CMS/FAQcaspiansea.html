Frequently Asked Questions

Why is the Caspian Sea called a sea if it has no ocean outlet?

It is the largest inland body of water on Earth (about 371,000 sq km) and behaves like an ocean in scale and salinity, but it is technically an endorheic lake - it has no outlet, only inflow (mainly the Volga River) and evaporation.

How deep is the Caspian Sea?

Depth varies enormously by basin: the north basin averages only about 5-6 m, the middle basin about 190 m, and the south basin reaches about 1,025 m at its deepest point.

Why is the surface below world ocean level?

With no outlet, the surface settles wherever inflow and evaporation balance - about 28 m below world ocean level, similar in concept to the Dead Sea's much larger drop.

Which countries border the Caspian Sea?

Russia and Kazakhstan to the north, Turkmenistan and Iran to the south and southeast, and Azerbaijan to the west.

Is the Caspian Sea salty?

Yes, but unevenly - the north is much less salty (diluted by the Volga's freshwater inflow) while the south is saltier, though still far below ocean salinity.

Is this a scientific terrain model?

No. It is an educational explorer using published figures - the basin profile and vertical scale are schematic teaching cues, not survey data.
