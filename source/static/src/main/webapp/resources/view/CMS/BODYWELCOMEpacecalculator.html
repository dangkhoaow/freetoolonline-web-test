Pace Calculator

Last reviewed: 2026-10-03

The pace calculator solves the runner's triangle: enter any two of distance, time and pace and it gives you the third, entirely in your browser. Ask it what pace finishes a 10K in 50 minutes, what a marathon takes at 6:00 min/km, or how far you covered in 40 minutes at your usual pace - the answer updates as you type.

Pace always shows both ways - min/km and min/mile - together with the speed in km/h and mph, so a treadmill setting, a race plan and a GPS watch reading can be compared without converting anything by hand. A race-preset list fills in the exact 5K, 10K, half-marathon (21.0975 km) and marathon (42.195 km) distances, and switching between kilometers and miles converts the numbers you already entered in place instead of resetting the form.

Below the result an even-pace split table projects cumulative times at the standard checkpoints - 1 km, 1 mile, 5K, 10K, 15K, half marathon, 30K and the full marathon - at the computed pace. The projections assume a perfectly even pace: real race splits vary with terrain, weather and fatigue, so treat the table as a planning estimate, not a coaching plan. Nothing you type is stored or sent anywhere, and no account is needed.

For other everyday math on this site, the percentage calculator handles generic percent questions rather than pacing, the fuel cost calculator does trip math in money rather than minutes, and the TDEE calorie calculator estimates the day's energy burn that training sits inside.
