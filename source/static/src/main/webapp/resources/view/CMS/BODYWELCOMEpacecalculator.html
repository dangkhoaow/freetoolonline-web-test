Pace Calculator

Last reviewed: 2026-10-03

The pace calculator solves the runner's triangle: enter any two of distance, time and pace and it gives you the third, entirely in your browser.

Ask it what pace finishes a 10K in 50 minutes, what a marathon takes at 6:00 min/km, or how far you covered in 40 minutes at your usual pace - the answer updates as you type.


Key features

  • Pace always shows both ways - min/km and min/mile - together with the speed in km/h and mph.
  • A race-preset list fills in the exact 5K, 10K, half-marathon (21.0975 km) and marathon (42.195 km) distances.
  • Switching between kilometers and miles converts the distance and pace you already entered in place instead of resetting the form.
  • An even-pace split table projects cumulative times at 1 km, 1 mile, 5K, 10K, 15K, half marathon, 30K and the full marathon, assuming a perfectly even pace - treat it as a planning estimate, not a coaching plan, since real splits vary with terrain, weather and fatigue.

When to use it

Use it to line up a treadmill setting, a race plan and a GPS watch reading side by side - the pace shown both ways means nothing needs converting by hand first.


Privacy

Nothing you type is stored or sent anywhere, and no account is needed.
