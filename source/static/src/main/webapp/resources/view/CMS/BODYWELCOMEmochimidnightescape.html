Mochi and the Midnight Escape

Mochi and the Midnight Escape is a free browser pixel platformer you can play with no account or install. About ~52 KB loads after you press Play.


Key features

  • Guide Mochi through nine pixel platformer levels.
  • Collect stars scattered across each level.
  • Bounce off springs and ride ziplines to reach the exit.

Saved progress

Your level progress stays in this browser, saved under the key ftol:mochimidnightescape.


Privacy

Nothing is uploaded, and play stays on your device.
