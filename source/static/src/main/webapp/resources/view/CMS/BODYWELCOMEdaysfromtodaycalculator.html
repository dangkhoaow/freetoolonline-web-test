Days From Today Calculator Online

Last reviewed: 2026-07-30

Find out how many days separate a reference date from a target date - leave one field on today to track the countdown to a deadline, or to see how many days have gone by since something happened.

What you can use it for

  • Count the days until a deadline or an upcoming event.
  • Measure the duration between any two dates.
  • Check how long ago something happened.

The day count and a week-plus-day breakdown update as you change either date, and it works in either direction - if the end date is before the start, the result says so.

Every subtraction happens client-side in this tab - no file leaves your device and no sign-up is needed.

To measure the span between two arbitrary dates without a from-today framing, the date difference calculator does the same day-count math.
