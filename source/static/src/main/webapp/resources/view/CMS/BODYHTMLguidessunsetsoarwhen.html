When to Play Sunset Soar

Sunset Soar fits short one-run sessions: hold space / click / touch to dive, release to soar. r restarts after a run ends. Download is ~16 KB once, and bests stay on this device.

Rhythm over reflexes

Rhythm play fits Sunset Soar: the skill is timing dives to the hill shape, not fast reactions. Runs reward smooth, repeated timing more than frantic input.
