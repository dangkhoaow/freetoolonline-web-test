Shilin Stone Forest is a UNESCO karst pinnacle field in Yunnan, China - hundreds of limestone towers rising from a plateau grassland. Toggle the pinnacle height highlight to see the tallest towers pick out against the shorter formations, and the Ashima marker to see the tallest pinnacle in the field, named for the Yi ethnic folk legend.

About this scene

Published figures: core scenic area about 350 sq km; limestone deposited about 270 million years ago; pinnacle landform sculpted over roughly the last 2.7 million years; many towers stand 20-30 m tall, with the tallest approaching about 40 m.
