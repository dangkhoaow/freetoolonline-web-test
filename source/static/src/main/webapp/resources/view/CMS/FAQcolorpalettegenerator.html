Frequently Asked Questions

What does Color Palette Generator Online do?

It pulls the main colors out of an image you choose. Load a local picture, set how many colors to read - five, eight, twelve, or sixteen - and the tool samples the image in your browser and lays out that many swatches, each with a HEX code you can copy in one click. Nothing is uploaded.

When should I reach for color palette generator online?

Reach for it when you want the colors inside an image rather than a single picked shade: load a photo, screenshot, or piece of artwork, choose the swatch count, and copy each dominant color's HEX with one click. The image is sampled in your browser, so nothing is uploaded.

What complementary tools work well alongside color palette generator online?

Once you have the HEX swatches, drop them straight into your CSS or design file. For converting a single color between HEX, RGB, and HSL, or for checking whether two colors meet WCAG AA and AAA contrast, reach for the related developer color tools. Each runs in your browser, so no color value or image is uploaded.
