Frequently Asked Questions

How big is the Sahara Desert?

About 9.2 million sq km (3.6 million sq mi) - roughly the size of the United States - making it the largest hot desert on Earth.

What is the highest point in the Sahara?

Emi Koussi, a shield volcano in Chad, at about 3,415 m (11,204 ft).

How hot does the Sahara get?

Daytime highs can exceed 50C (122F) in summer; because sand and rock lose heat fast after dark, winter nights can drop below freezing.

Is the whole Sahara covered in sand dunes?

No. Dune seas (ergs) cover only about a quarter of the Sahara; most of the rest is rocky hamada plateau and gravel reg plains.

How much rain does the Sahara get?

Under 25 mm (1 inch) a year across most of the interior, among the lowest rainfall totals on Earth.

Is this a geological simulation?

No. It is an educational explorer using published figures - the dune ridges and plateau relief are schematic teaching cues.
