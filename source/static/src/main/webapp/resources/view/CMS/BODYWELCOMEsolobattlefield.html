Solo Battlefield is a free browser last-alive combat game you can play without an account or install. Clear eight escalating levels of melee foes and archers as the last fighter standing.

Controls

  • Move with WASD or the arrow keys.
  • Click or tap to strike a foe next to you.
  • Strike an incoming arrow to knock it back toward the archer who fired it.
  • Press Space on the wait screen after you clear a wave to start the next level.

What to expect

The eight levels raise the enemy count, tighten spawn timing, and add more archers as you advance. Press Play to load the roughly 12 KB game; play is session-only, so nothing is saved between visits.

Privacy

The game runs entirely in your browser: no account, no server calls once it loads, and no saved progress on this device. Solo Battlefield is adapted from Only One by Cemal Gonultas (MIT, js13kGames 2023).
