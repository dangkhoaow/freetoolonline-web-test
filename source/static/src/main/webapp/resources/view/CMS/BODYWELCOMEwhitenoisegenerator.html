White Noise & Ambient Sound Generator

Last reviewed: 2026-07-25

Play white noise, pink noise, brown noise, or a synthesized ocean-waves sound right in this tab. Pick a sound, set the volume, and click Play - the audio needs one click to start because browsers block sound before you interact with the page.

An optional sleep timer (15, 30, 60, or 120 minutes) stops playback automatically and shows a live countdown, so this can run while you fall asleep, focus, or block out background noise without babysitting the tab. Switching sounds while one is already playing rebuilds it instantly - no reload needed.

Every sound here is generated on-device by the browser's Web Audio API at the moment you press Play. None of it is a recording - the "Ocean waves" mode is brown noise with a slow amplitude swell layered on top, not a sampled beach or rain track. No audio is uploaded, downloaded, or sent to a server, and the chosen sound, volume, and timer reset to their defaults on your next visit.
