Frequently Asked Questions

Is the email I write or paste here uploaded anywhere?

No. The brief, the draft, and any email you paste in reply mode are processed entirely in your browser. There is no server behind this page - the template engine is plain JavaScript, and the optional AI model runs on your own device after a one-time download.

Do I have to download an AI model to use it?

No. With nothing downloaded, the tool builds a template draft from your brief: greeting, one paragraph per key point, and a closing matched to the tone. Loading the on-device model is an optional upgrade for more natural wording, and its download size is shown before it starts.

How good are the AI drafts?

The on-device models are small - 1 to 3 billion parameters - so expect a solid first draft, not a finished message. Read it before sending, and double-check names, dates, and numbers, which small models sometimes get wrong.

Can it reply to an email I received?

Yes. Choose "Reply to an email", paste the message, and add your key points. The draft acknowledges their email and answers with your points. The pasted message stays on your device, so this is safe for threads you would not put into a cloud service.

Does it send the email for me?

No. This tool only drafts text. Use the copy button and paste the draft into Gmail, Outlook, or any other mail app, then send it from your own account.
