Feast Night is a free browser medieval first-person game you can play with no account and no install. Press Play to load the ~17 KB WebGL build in a same-origin iframe, then click the canvas to step into the village feast as Brother Doen.

Key features

  • A first-person WebGL feast built with billboard NPCs and simple room meshes.
  • WASD or arrow-key walking plus mouse look.
  • Answer yes-or-no dialogue with a nod (pitch) or head-shake (yaw) gesture.
  • Click-to-fire combat starts when demons appear, and your soul count drives which ending you reach.
  • Loads in about ~17 KB after you press Play and stays session-only in this competition build.
  • Adapted from FEAST NIGHT by Almar, released under the MIT license for js13kGames 2023.

How to play

  1. Press Play, then click the canvas to lock the pointer and begin.
  2. Walk with WASD or the arrow keys and look around with the mouse.
  3. Nod or shake your head to answer villagers during dialogue.
  4. Fire at guests that turn demonic so you can save souls and reach the gate.

Privacy

Everything runs on this device. Nothing leaves your browser except the initial page and the game files served from this site, and the competition build keeps no save data between sessions.
