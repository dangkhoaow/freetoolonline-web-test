Cach Su Dung Cong Cu So Sanh JSON Truc Tuyen - Tung Buoc

Last reviewed: 2026-07-30

Cong Cu So Sanh JSON Truc Tuyen so sanh hai doan van ban JSON va lam noi bat cac muc duoc them, xoa va gia tri da thay doi - tat ca trong trinh duyet, khong can cai dat.

Buoc 1 - Mo cong cu

Truy cap developer-tools/json-diff-checker.html. Cong cu tai ngay trong trinh duyet - khong can cai dat chuong trinh nao.

Buoc 2 - Dan hai doan JSON

Dan JSON goc vao o ben trai (Original JSON) va JSON da sua doi vao o ben phai (Modified JSON). Cac doi tuong long nhau va mang duoc so sanh de qui.

Buoc 3 - Nhan Compare va kiem tra ket qua

Nhan nut Compare. Moi hang ket qua hien thi duong dan day du cua khoa (vi du user.address.city), gia tri cu va gia tri moi. Khoa duoc them co mau xanh la, khoa bi xoa co mau do, gia tri thay doi co mau vang.

Buoc 4 - Sao chep ket qua

Nhan Copy Diff de sao chep tom tat so sanh vao clipboard. Khong co du lieu nao roi khoi trinh duyet cua ban.
