Frequently Asked Questions

Why is the Dead Sea the lowest place on Earth?

Its shoreline sits about 430 m below sea level in the Jordan Rift Valley - the lowest land elevation on the planet, and it keeps dropping about a metre per year as inflow shrinks.

Why do people float in the Dead Sea?

Salinity is about 34% - roughly 9-10 times ocean water - which makes the brine about 1.24 kg/L, denser than the human body, so you float without swimming.

Is the Dead Sea actually a sea?

No - it is a terminal lake. The Jordan River flows in and there is no outlet, so water leaves only by evaporation, concentrating the salt.

How deep is the Dead Sea?

About 300 m at its deepest point, which makes it the deepest hypersaline lake on Earth.

Which countries border the Dead Sea?

Jordan on the east shore, and Israel and the West Bank on the west shore.

Is this a scientific terrain model?

No. It is an educational explorer using published figures - the basin profile, salt terraces, and vertical scale are schematic teaching cues, not survey data.
