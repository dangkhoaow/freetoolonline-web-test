Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Milford Sound?

In Fiordland National Park on the South Island of New Zealand.

Is Milford Sound a fjord or a sound?

It is a true fjord - a glacier-carved valley later flooded by the sea - despite being named a "sound".

How tall is Mitre Peak?

About 1,692 m, rising almost directly from the water at the fjord's edge.

Why is the water dark near the surface?

Heavy rainfall creates a tannin-stained freshwater layer that floats on top of the denser seawater, cutting light below.

How much does it rain at Milford Sound?

About 6,400 mm a year on average, among the wettest inhabited places on Earth.

What are the named waterfalls?

Stirling Falls (about 155 m) and Bowen Falls (about 162 m) are the two permanent cascades down the cliff walls.

Is Milford Sound a UNESCO site?

Yes. It is part of Te Wahipounamu - South West New Zealand, a UNESCO World Heritage Area since 1990.

Is this a scientific terrain model?

No. It is an educational explorer using published figures - the wall shape, waterfall placement, and vertical scale are schematic teaching cues, not survey terrain.
