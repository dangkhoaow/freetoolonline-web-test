Holiday Countdown & Date Lookup Step By Step

Last reviewed: 2026-08-05

Holiday Countdown & Date Lookup runs in three steps: open the tool, provide your input, then copy the result. This page walks through what each step expects.


Step 1 - open the tool

Go to Holiday Countdown & Date Lookup. Nothing needs to be installed or signed in before you start.


Step 2 - provide your input

Holiday dates are computed fresh for any year from durable calendar rules (fixed dates, nth-weekday-of-month, and the Easter algorithm) - never a hard-coded list that goes stale.


Step 3 - copy the result

Everything computes locally in the browser - no upload, no account, and no network request after the page loads.

Share a specific holiday's countdown via the Copy Link button, which encodes the country and holiday in the URL.
