Where the Mars Dust Storm Seasons Explorer wins

The Mars Dust Storm Seasons Explorer wins when the question is about WEATHER and MISSION RISK - it is the only page on the site that connects an orbit's shape to a real dust-storm season and a real rover mission ending.


Where the Earth Perihelion and Aphelion Explorer wins

The Earth Perihelion and Aphelion Explorer wins when the question is about a NEAR-CIRCULAR orbit - it shows why "closer to the Sun" does not mean "hotter" for Earth, a distinction Mars's far more eccentric orbit does not illustrate the same way.


A reasonable rule

As a reasonable rule, use the Mars Dust Storm Seasons Explorer first to see how an eccentric orbit drives a real weather season, then use the Earth Perihelion and Aphelion Explorer to see the same orbit-ellipse scene applied to a much rounder orbit with no dust-storm season at all.
