Frequently Asked Questions

Does this use my microphone?

No. The tone is generated with the Web Audio API. Nothing is recorded and no microphone permission is requested.

Why do I need to click Play?

Browsers block autoplay of audio until you interact with the page. A click on Play Left, Play Right, or Play Both starts the AudioContext.

How do I tell left from right?

Use headphones. Play Left should be louder in the left ear; Play Right in the right ear. Play Both sends the same tone to both channels.

What frequency range can I test?

The Hz slider runs from 220 to 2000, adjustable in steps of 10. The label next to the slider updates live as you drag it, and whatever value you land on carries over to whichever Play button you click next.

Can I make the tone louder or quieter?

No - there is no volume control on the page. Every tone plays at the same fixed level; use your headphone or speaker volume instead if you need it louder or quieter.

Does the meter bar measure how loud the tone actually is?

No. The bar is a visual cue that pulses on a fixed pattern while a tone is playing, so you can see something is happening even without headphones nearby. It does not measure sound pressure or the loudness actually reaching your speakers.

Will this work on a mono speaker or a single Bluetooth speaker?

It will play, but you likely will not hear a difference between Play Left and Play Right. Stereo separation only shows up when the two channels reach two distinct physical outputs - two speakers or two headphone ear cups. On a mono speaker or a single-driver Bluetooth speaker, Play Left, Play Right, and Play Both can sound the same, so use headphones or two separate speakers to actually hear the channel difference.
