Check the next holiday in five short steps.

  1. Pick your country (United States, Canada, United Kingdom, or Australia) from the first selector.
  2. Read today's status and the next public holiday with its live day/hour/minute countdown at the top.
  3. Choose any holiday from this year or next year in the second selector to re-target the countdown.
  4. Click Copy link to share that exact country and holiday - the link restores the same view for whoever opens it.
  5. Scroll down for the full-year holiday table for the selected country.
