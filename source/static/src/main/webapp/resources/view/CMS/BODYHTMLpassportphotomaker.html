Choose a photo, pick the country/document size preset (or enter a custom size in millimeters), drag to position the face between the dashed head guides, and use the zoom slider to fill the frame - then click Generate photo. If the photo was not taken against a plain wall, pick an AI backdrop (white, light blue, or light grey) and the background is replaced on your device.

Cropping a photo to a passport or visa size takes four steps:

  1. Choose a photo file - it opens in the browser and is never uploaded to a server.
  2. Pick a country/document size preset (US, India, Schengen/EU, UK, Australia, Canada, China visa) or switch to "Custom size (mm)" and enter your own width and height in millimeters. Optionally pick a Background: keep the photo's own background (default, no download), or have on-device AI replace it with plain white, light blue, or light grey (~26 MB one-time model download, stated before it starts).
  3. Drag the photo to position the face inside the frame - dashed "top of head" and "chin" guide lines show the band most photo specs want the head to fill - and use the Zoom slider; check "Also generate a 4x6in print sheet" if you want multiple copies tiled onto one printable page.
  4. Click Generate photo, then use the Download links to save the cropped photo (and the print sheet, if you checked that box) - the output is rendered at the preset's exact pixel size for 300 DPI printing.
