Machine Guard Corps is a free browser sci-fi lane-defense auto-battler you can play with no account and no install. Press Play to load the game in a same-origin iframe, then press Start Defense on its title screen and hold your command base against an adaptive alien hive.


Key features

  • 5 machine unit lines - Robot Infantry, Mech Dogs, Attack Drones, Big Fat Bots, and Railgun Walkers - summoned with number keys 1-5 or the on-screen buttons, each with 2 permanent Scrap-bought evolution paths (10 evolutions in all).
  • 4 upgrade trees - Command Tower, Base Turret, Coolant Banks, and Combat Firmware - each with 5 levels, bought with Scrap using keys Z, X, C, and V or the on-screen buttons.
  • 3 active skills on independent cooldowns - Missile Strike, EMP Pulse, and Repair Swarm - to swing tough fights, each improved by the Command Tower and Coolant Banks trees.
  • A heat system - units that attack build heat and shut down for 2 seconds at 100 heat, which Coolant Banks upgrades help mitigate.
  • Both fortresses, your base and the alien hive, auto-repair after 8 seconds without taking damage, at a speed tied to the Command Tower upgrade.

The alien hive adapts on its own

The alien hive is not a fixed wave script. It manages its own Alien Energy, upgrades itself over time, and triggers emergency invulnerability shields at 50% and 15% of its core HP, which also refill its energy.


Boss roster and win condition

The hive eventually sends named bosses. Its 6-boss roster runs Hive Siege Core, Broodmaw Crusher, Void Prism Tyrant, Carapace Sovereign, Dreadspire Artillery, and Star-Eater Monarch, and once that roster is exhausted, rounds escalate to double-boss fights. You win by reducing the hive's core HP to zero before your base falls.


Privacy and saved data

Everything runs on your device. Nothing leaves it except the initial page and the roughly 130 KB of game files loaded from this site - there is no server backend, and the game keeps no scores, progress, or unlocks between sessions.
