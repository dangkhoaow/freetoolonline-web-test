Black Forest 3D Explorer

Last reviewed: 2026-07-28

Reviewed by FreetoolOnline editorial team

This browser explorer shows the Black Forest (Schwarzwald) as a rolling terrain relief: valley farmland and river-cut lowlands giving way to dense dark-conifer canopy on the ridges, climbing toward Feldberg, the range's highest point. The dark, near-continuous fir and spruce canopy that shades the valleys below is the origin of the range's name in both German and English.

The Black Forest sits in the state of Baden-Wurttemberg in southwestern Germany, running roughly 160 km from north to south and 20-60 km wide, for a total area of about 6,009 sq km. Its highest point, Feldberg, reaches about 1,493 m. Two of Europe's notable rivers begin here: the Danube (Donau), Europe's second-longest river, has its conventional source at Donaueschingen on the forest's eastern edge, and the Neckar rises near Schwenningen on the western flank before flowing north to join the Rhine. The Schwarzwaldhochstrasse ("Black Forest High Road"), a scenic ridge route of about 60 km, links Baden-Baden to Freudenstadt along the forest's high crest. The region is also the traditional home of the cuckoo clock, a craft dating to the 18th century.

For other terrain-relief explorers, open the Grand Canyon 3D Explorer (rock layers cut by a river) or the Amazon Rainforest 3D Explorer (a different kind of canopy relief, for comparison against these conifer ridges).

The Black Forest relief you orbit here is drawn entirely in your browser through WebGL, so the conifer-ridge terrain and its river-source markers are rendered on your own device. The 3D engine downloads a single time (about 0.7 MB) and then stays cached, so returning to the explorer starts without another download.

This is an educational approximation - the terrain relief, river-source markers, and scenic route line are procedural teaching cues, not a survey map.
