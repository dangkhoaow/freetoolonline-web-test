Tibetan Plateau 3D Explorer - The Roof of the World

Explore Earth's highest and largest plateau at an average elevation of 4,500+ meters. The Tibetan Plateau, known as the "Roof of the World," covers approximately 2.5 million square kilometers across China, Tibet, Bhutan, Nepal, India, and Central Asia. This vast highland region is surrounded by the world's major mountain ranges: the Himalayas to the south, the Karakoram to the west, the Hindu Kush to the northwest, and the Tian Shan to the north. Everest (8,848m) sits on its southern edge, rising 4,300+ meters above the already-elevated plateau floor.

The Tibetan Plateau is not a barren wasteland but a complex ecosystem supporting unique flora, fauna, and human cultures. Four major rivers that shape civilization across Asia - the Yangtze, Mekong, Brahmaputra, and Indus - all originate in this region. The plateau's elevation dramatically influences Asian monsoons and global weather patterns, making it a crucial climate regulator for the entire continent.
