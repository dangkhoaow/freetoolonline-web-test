Video Merger turns several clips into one video, even a mix of portrait and landscape footage, MP4 and MOV files, and clips with or without sound. All of the work happens inside your browser tab: your footage is never uploaded, and the download carries no watermark.

How to merge videos

  1. Click "Choose videos" or drop files on "Drop video clips here"; later batches join the end of the strip.
  2. Drag a card by its thumbnail into play order, or use "Move left" and "Move right", then use "Trim", "Mute" or "Remove" on any card.
  3. Pick "No transition", "Crossfade" or "Fade through black" in each gap between cards, or choose one under "Every gap" and press "Apply to all".
  4. Set "Frame shape", "Fit", "Quality" and "Transition length", and click "Add music" if you want a soundtrack under the merged video.
  5. Press "Merge videos", keep the tab open until the progress bar finishes, then click "Download merged video".
