Welcome to Flexbox Froggy - a free browser CSS flexbox teaching game with no account or install.


Key features

  • Write flex properties so frogs hop onto matching lilypads across 24 levels.
  • About ~1.0 MB loads after you press Play.

Your progress and privacy

  • Progress stays in this browser under namespaced ftol:flexboxfroggy keys; use Reset in the level menu to clear saved answers.
  • Nothing is uploaded; play stays on-device.
