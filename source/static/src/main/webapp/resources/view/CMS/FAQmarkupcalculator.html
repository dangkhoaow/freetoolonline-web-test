Frequently Asked Questions

What does the markup calculator do?

It turns a cost and a markup percent into a selling price and profit - or solves the reverse from a cost and a margin percent, or from a cost and a price you already know. Every result shows the markup percent and the margin percent together.

What is the difference between markup and margin?

Markup is profit divided by cost; margin is profit divided by price. They share the same profit amount but a different base, so a 100% markup is only a 50% margin, never a 100% margin, except at 0%.

Is my cost or price data sent anywhere?

No. The calculation runs entirely in your browser - nothing you type is stored or sent to this site's servers or anywhere else, and no account is needed.

Does this calculator include tax, shipping or fees?

No. It is plain cost-plus arithmetic on the numbers you enter - taxes, shipping, payment-processing fees and volume discounts are not part of the calculation. Not pricing, accounting or financial advice.
