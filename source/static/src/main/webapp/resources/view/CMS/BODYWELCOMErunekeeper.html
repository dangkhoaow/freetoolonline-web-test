Rune Keeper is a free browser draw-to-move arena you can play without an account or install. Press Play to start, then draw rune symbols to move the runestone, survive enemy waves, and keep casting.

How to play

  • Draw a rune symbol - a wave, triangle, circle, or related shape - with a mouse or a finger; the shape you draw sends the runestone across the arena.
  • Survive the enemy waves as they spawn and place your effects around the stone to keep the pressure off.
  • Keep casting to hold the arena. There is no WASD movement and no keyboard is needed - drawing is the only input.

What loads and privacy

Press Play and the game (about 28 KB) loads in a same-origin iframe from this site. Nothing leaves this device except the initial page and those game files, and the session is not saved between visits.
