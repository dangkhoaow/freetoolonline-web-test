When to Play Potion Brew Shop

Potion Brew Shop fits short breaks where you want a cozy click puzzle with timed orders - about ~32 KB after Play, no install.

Last reviewed: 2026-07-18

Order-matching coffee breaks

Order-matching coffee breaks fit Potion Brew Shop: each customer is one quick recipe decision. Read the card, click two or three bottles, serve before the timer expires.

When you like unlock progression

When you like unlock progression, Potion Brew Shop fits: three shop stages add Life herbs and Emotion crystals with longer ingredient lists. The Done bar shows progress toward 30 correct brews.

When to pick another game

When to pick another game instead of Potion Brew Shop: want pipe valves instead of potions? Use Wash The Cat. Want rotate-to-connect tiles? Use Pipe Rotate Puzzle.
