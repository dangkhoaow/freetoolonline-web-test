Milford Sound (Piopiotahi) is a glacier-carved fjord in Fiordland National Park, New Zealand, where Mitre Peak rises steeply straight out of the water. Toggle the freshwater lens to see the tannin-stained rainwater layer that floats on the seawater, and the waterfall highlight to see the two permanent cascades down the cliff walls.
