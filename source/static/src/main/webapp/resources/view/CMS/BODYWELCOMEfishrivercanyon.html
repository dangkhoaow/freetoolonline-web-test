Fish River Canyon 3D Explorer

Last reviewed: 2026-08-28

Reviewed by FreetoolOnline editorial team

This browser explorer shows Fish River Canyon as a relief of its most famous shape: a single sinuous meander cutting a signature "big bend" loop through rust-red, terraced walls on a flat semi-arid plateau in southern Namibia, with the Fish River still tracing the gorge floor and the rim-hugging hiking trail marked in beacons.

The canyon runs about 160 km, spreads up to 27 km wide, and drops up to 550 m - making it Africa's largest canyon and one of the largest in the world. Its shape traces back to ancient rifting that fractured the plateau, followed by hundreds of millions of years of erosion carved mainly by the Fish River. Ai-Ais/Richtersveld Transfrontier Park protects the gorge today, and the 85 km Fish River Canyon Trail along its rim and floor is one of Africa's best-known multi-day hikes - open only from May to September, when the river's flow is low enough to cross safely.

The terraced walls, the river ribbon, and the trail beacons are all drawn right in your browser with WebGL, so panning across the 160 km gorge stays smooth without a server round-trip. The vendored three.js engine behind the scene is a one-time download of roughly 0.7 MB, and your browser keeps it cached, so returning to explore the big-bend meander again loads instantly.
