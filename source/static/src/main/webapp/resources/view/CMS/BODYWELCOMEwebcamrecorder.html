Webcam Recorder Online

Last reviewed: 2026-07-27

Record your screen, camera, or microphone and download the clip - all in the browser.


Key features

  • Choose your capture source from the Source picker before you start.
  • Pause and Resume let you split a take without stopping the clock.
  • A live timer next to the buttons shows how long the current recording has run.
  • The finished clip downloads straight to you, with no upload step.

When to use it

Use it for a quick screen capture, webcam clip, or voice note without installing an app. One tool covers a short webcam clip and a longer screen recording.


Source modes

Source modeWhat it records
Camera onlyVideo from your webcam
Camera with microphoneWebcam video together with sound
Microphone onlyAudio, for a voice note
Full screen captureEverything shown on your screen

Privacy

The recording stays on your device and is never uploaded. Nothing is stored beyond this browser session, no account or signup is required, and no analytics or tracking service ever sees the recording.
