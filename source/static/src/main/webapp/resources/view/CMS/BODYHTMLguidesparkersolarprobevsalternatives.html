Where the Cassini Grand Finale Explorer wins

The Cassini Grand Finale Explorer wins when the question is why a mission ends in a deliberate destruction rather than being left adrift - it is the only page on the site with a planetary-protection ending and a stylized one-way-light-time countdown.


Where the Parker Solar Probe Explorer wins

The Parker Solar Probe Explorer wins when the question is how a spacecraft is built to survive repeated close approaches rather than end with one - it is the only page on the site where the approached body is the Sun itself, and the only one with a heat-shield survival story.


A reasonable rule

As a reasonable rule, use the Parker Solar Probe Explorer to see a spacecraft designed to survive extreme heat and speed near a star, then use the Cassini Grand Finale Explorer to see a different, deliberately final ending at a planet.
