Kilauea rises on the southeast flank of Mauna Loa on Hawaii Island, inside Hawaii Volcanoes National Park - one of the world's most active volcanoes. Unlike a steep cone such as Vesuvius, Kilauea is a low, very broad shield with a large collapsed caldera at its summit. This 3D explorer shows that gentle shield profile, the caldera, and the Halema'uma'u crater's lava lake near its center.
