Frequently Asked Questions

Why is the Congo River the deepest river on Earth?

Soundings near its lower course have measured over 220 m deep - deeper than any other river channel measured worldwide, likely carved by the strong, steady flow squeezed through a narrow bedrock gorge.

Why does the Congo River flow so steadily year-round?

It is the only major river that crosses the equator twice, so its northern and southern basin halves have offsetting rainy seasons - when one side is dry, the other is wet, which smooths out the combined flow.

Why can't ships sail from the Atlantic to the interior?

The Livingstone Falls, about 350 km of rapids and cataracts near the mouth, drop roughly 270 m in elevation. They are unnavigable, so a rail line bypasses them instead of a shipping channel.

How does the Congo River compare to the Amazon?

The Amazon carries more total water and is longer, but the Congo has the deeper channel and is the world's second largest river by both discharge and basin area.

Which countries does the Congo River run through?

It flows through the Democratic Republic of the Congo and the Republic of the Congo, forming much of their shared border, and passes between the capitals Kinshasa and Brazzaville.

Is this a scientific terrain model?

No. It is an educational explorer using published figures - the channel path, bend geometry, depth banding, and rapids placement are schematic teaching cues, not survey data.
