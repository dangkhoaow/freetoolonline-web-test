Text Similarity Checker - Compare Texts by Meaning (No Upload)

Last reviewed: 2026-08-28

Compare two texts and get a meaning-based similarity score - entirely in the browser, nothing is uploaded. Paste a draft and its rewrite, two product descriptions, or an original and a summary - the score tells you how close they are in meaning.

A per-sentence breakdown shows which sentences of one text match which sentences of the other, so you can see WHERE the overlap in meaning sits. The comparison model (about 24 MB) downloads once on your explicit click, with the size stated first, and is cached by the browser for next time; a basic word-overlap mode works with no download at all.

This page scores MEANING, which makes it different from the text diff tool on this site: the diff shows exact character-level changes between two versions, while this checker can score two passages as similar even when almost no wording is shared. Use the diff when you need to see every edit; use this checker when you need to know whether two texts say the same thing.

It is not a plagiarism detector - there is no source corpus and no originality verdict; the score compares only the two texts you paste. English works best; other languages and very short texts score less reliably.
