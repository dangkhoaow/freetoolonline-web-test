Text Similarity Checker - Compare Texts by Meaning (No Upload)

Last reviewed: 2026-08-28

Compare two texts and get a meaning-based similarity score - entirely in the browser, nothing is uploaded.

A per-sentence breakdown shows which sentences of one text match which sentences of the other, so you can see WHERE the overlap in meaning sits.


Key features

  • Meaning-based similarity score for two pasted texts, so two passages can score as similar even when almost no wording is shared.
  • Per-sentence best-match breakdown showing which sentences of one text match which sentences of the other.
  • Basic word-overlap mode that works with no download at all.
  • AI meaning mode: the comparison model (about 24 MB) downloads once on your explicit click, with the size stated first, and is cached by the browser for next time.
  • Runs entirely in the browser - the text you paste is never uploaded.

When to use it

Paste a draft and its rewrite, two product descriptions, or an original and a summary - the score tells you how close they are in meaning.


Similarity by meaning vs the text diff tool

This page scores MEANING, which makes it different from the text diff tool on this site: the diff shows exact character-level changes between two versions, while this checker can score two passages as similar even when almost no wording is shared.

Privacy and limits

Everything runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded; the text you paste never leaves your device. It is not a plagiarism detector - there is no source corpus and no originality verdict, so the score compares only the two texts you paste. English works best; other languages and very short texts score less reliably.
