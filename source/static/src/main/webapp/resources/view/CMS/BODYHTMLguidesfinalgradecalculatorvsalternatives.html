The Final Grade Calculator is one of three common ways to answer "what score do I need on the final?": a browser tool like this one, a spreadsheet formula, or working the weighted-average algebra by hand. Each trades off differently on setup time, where your numbers go, and how much the result explains.

The bottom line

Use this tool for a quick answer with no setup and a built-in table across seven final scores. Use a spreadsheet when you want to save the model or tweak the formula. Do it by hand when you just need a one-off check and trust the algebra. All three compute the same thing - needed = (target - current x (1 - weight)) / weight - but only the tool flags the locked-in and out-of-reach cases and shows the points already banked.

The math here is plain weighted-average algebra on the numbers you enter, not your course's official grading policy, and the calculation runs in your browser - no upload, no account.
