Frequently Asked Questions

Is my text uploaded anywhere when I translate?

No. All three translation paths run inside your browser: Chrome's built-in engine, the downloadable model, and the phrasebook. This site has no translation server, so there is nowhere for your text to be sent.

How big is the download, and do I pay it on every visit?

A downloadable language pair is about 115 MB, fetched once after you click the load button and then stored by your browser - later visits reuse it without downloading again. On Chrome 138+ the built-in engine needs no download from this site at all; when Chrome fetches its own language pack, the status line says so.

Which languages does it support?

The downloadable models pair English with Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Indonesian, Vietnamese, Hindi, Chinese, Russian and Arabic, so English must be one side of the pair. Chrome's built-in engine may handle additional pairs - the page checks the pair you pick and tells you. The no-download phrasebook covers everyday phrases in seven of those languages.

How accurate are the translations?

The on-device model is small - about 78 million parameters. Everyday sentences translate well, but idioms, slang and rare pairs come out below what large cloud services produce. The output panel names which engine produced the text, and anything important deserves a human read before you send it.

Does it work offline?

Yes, once an engine is on your machine: a downloaded language pair keeps translating with the connection off, and Chrome's built-in packs also run locally. The phrasebook always works offline. Only the first fetch of a model needs a connection.

Can it translate PDF, Word or other document files?

No - this page translates typed or pasted text only, up to 4,000 characters per run. For a document, copy the text out and translate it in parts.
