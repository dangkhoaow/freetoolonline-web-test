Ritual Catacombs is a free browser WebGL horror game - explore tile-based catacombs with WASD and mouse aim, collect shards to open doors, dodge turret idols, and defeat the nine-lived boss; ~94 KB, no install, no account.

Last reviewed: 2026-08-02

Reviewed by FreetoolOnline editorial team

Press Play to load the roughly 94 KB single-file engine in a same-origin iframe, then click inside the frame to lock the pointer. Nothing leaves your device beyond that initial page and the game files served from this site.

How to play

  • Move with WASD and aim with the mouse once the pointer is locked; press Esc to release the cursor.
  • Collect green shards to open runic doors, and grab cyan hearts to restore health.
  • Stone turret idols fire magenta orbs - dodge them or shoot them down.
  • Black cats charge when their eyes flare red; white wraiths punish you for staring too long.
  • The boss avatar has nine lives - shoot its glowing eyes to banish it and win.

Sound and privacy

Ambience and gunfire are generated procedurally through Web Audio, so no external audio files are fetched. Progress is session-only with checkpoint respawn - there is no account, no install, and nothing is saved off your device.
