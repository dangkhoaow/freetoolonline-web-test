Welcome - press Play to load Neon Energy Arena in your browser. Progress saves locally as Energy and upgrades.

Weapons and combat

  • Four weapons ship in the engine - Pulse Blaster, Blast Shotgun, Photon Sniper, and Plasma Rail - each with level-based stats for shot count, fire rate, spread, damage, pierce, and velocity.
  • Your current weapon level and speed level show on the HUD during a run, next to a dash indicator that reads Ready or shows a cooldown bar.
  • A mute button toggles the procedural sound effects and background music without leaving the arena.

Spending Energy in the shop

Energy earned during a run is banked for permanent upgrades that carry across future runs. The shop spends it on Tactical Bomb, Orbiter Escort, Weapon Boost, Quantum Drive, Nano Armor, Time Dilate, Orbiter Expand, Cryo Core, and Recoil Damper - each with a maximum level and a cost that rises as you invest.
