How to Convert AV1 to WebM Online - Step by Step

Last reviewed: 2026-07-31

AV1 to WebM Converter Online remuxes your AV1 video into a WebM container in three steps. No install required; the conversion runs in your browser via WebAssembly.


Step 1 - open the converter

Go to AV1 to WebM Converter Online. The page loads without any sign-in or plugin.


Step 2 - upload your file

Click the upload area or drag your .av1 (or .mkv/.mp4 with an AV1 track) onto the page. On the first conversion the tool downloads the FFmpeg.wasm engine - a one-time 31 MB download that is then cached by your browser. Progress messages appear while FFmpeg initialises and processes your file.


Step 3 - download the result

When the conversion finishes, a "Download" link appears. The file is saved as output.webm. The video quality is identical to the input - the tool uses stream copy (-c copy), so no re-encoding takes place.
