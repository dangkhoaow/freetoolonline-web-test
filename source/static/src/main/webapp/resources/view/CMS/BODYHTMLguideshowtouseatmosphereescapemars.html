How to Use Atmosphere Escape: Mars 3D Explorer - Step by Step

Last reviewed: 2026-08-10

Open the Atmosphere Escape: Mars 3D Explorer, drag the time slider from 4.5 billion years ago toward today, then trigger a solar storm to see the real MAVEN escape-rate spike.


Drag the time slider

Drag the time slider from the far left (about 4.5 billion years ago) toward the right (today) and watch the shield arc fade once it crosses the point where Mars's global magnetic field shut off, roughly 3.7 to 4.2 billion years ago.


Watch the atmosphere thin out

Watch the translucent atmosphere shell thin out and the cyan and violet solar-wind streams intensify as the slider nears today, once the magnetic shield is gone.


Trigger a solar storm

Near "today," click "Trigger a solar-storm (CME) event" to boost the streams and see the facts panel switch to the real MAVEN-measured 10-to-20-times escape-rate figure.


Read the facts panel

Read the facts panel for the era label, field status, escape rate, and - at today's setting - the real 75%/25% tail/polar ion split MAVEN measured.


Orbit and zoom

Drag on the scene to orbit the camera, and scroll or pinch to zoom in on the shield arc or the solar-wind streams.
