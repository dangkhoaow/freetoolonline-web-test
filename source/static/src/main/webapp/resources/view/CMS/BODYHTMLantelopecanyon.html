Antelope Canyon is a narrow sandstone slot canyon on Navajo Nation land near Page, Arizona, carved into flowing wave-like walls by flash-flood erosion. Toggle the midday light beam to see the effect that makes the canyon famous, or toggle the sandstone cross-bedding bands to see the wind-blown dune layering preserved in the rock.
