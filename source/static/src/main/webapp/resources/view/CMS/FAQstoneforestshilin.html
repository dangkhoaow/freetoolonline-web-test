Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the Shilin Stone Forest?

In Shilin Yi Autonomous County, Yunnan province, China, about 120 km southeast of Kunming.

How old are the limestone pinnacles?

The limestone itself was deposited about 270 million years ago during the Permian period. The pinnacle shapes seen today were then carved by water dissolving the rock over roughly the last 2.7 million years.

How tall are the stone pillars?

Many towers stand 20-30 m tall, with the tallest approaching about 40 m.

Who is Ashima?

Ashima is the heroine of a well-known Yi ethnic folk legend. A tall, distinctively shaped pinnacle in the park is named Ashima Rock after her.

Is Shilin a UNESCO World Heritage Site?

Yes. It is part of the South China Karst World Heritage Site, inscribed by UNESCO in 2007.

Is this a scientific terrain model?

No. It is an educational explorer using published figures - the pinnacle field, spacing, and vertical scale are schematic teaching cues, not survey terrain.
