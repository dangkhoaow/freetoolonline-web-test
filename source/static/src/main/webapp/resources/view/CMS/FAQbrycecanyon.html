Frequently Asked Questions

Is Bryce Canyon a real canyon?

Not in the river-carved sense. It is a series of horseshoe-shaped amphitheaters cut into the plateau rim by frost-wedging and rain, not by a river.

How tall are the hoodoos?

Typically 1.5-45 m tall - irregular spires left standing as softer rock erodes away around harder caps.

What is Thor's Hammer?

The park's most famous single hoodoo - a boulder-shaped capstone balanced on a narrower spire, marked in this explorer at the tallest hoodoo near the bowl center.

What rock forms the hoodoos?

The Claron Formation - pink, orange, and white limestone and siltstone deposited about 30-40 million years ago.

How high is the rim?

About 2,400-2,778 m (7,900-9,115 ft) above sea level.

Where is Bryce Canyon?

Southwestern Utah, USA - protected as Bryce Canyon National Park, established in 1928.

Is this a geological simulation?

No. It is an educational explorer using published NPS figures - hoodoo placement and color bands are procedural teaching cues.
