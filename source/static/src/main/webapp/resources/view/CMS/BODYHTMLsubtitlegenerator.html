Turn a video into a subtitle file you can load in a player or hand to an editor. Speech recognition runs on your own device, and you can also time a script you wrote yourself without downloading anything.

How to generate a subtitle file

  1. Click Load caption model once. It fetches about 40 MB and your browser keeps it, so later visits start instantly.
  2. Choose an MP4, M4V, WEBM, MOV, MP3, WAV, M4A or OGG file up to about 15 minutes.
  3. Pick the spoken language, or leave it on auto-detect.
  4. Click Generate captions. Cues appear in the table as each 30-second window finishes.
  5. Correct any wording in the cue table, then click Download .srt or Download .vtt.

No file, no model, or an unsupported container? Paste your script into the second panel and the cue timer spaces it across the media length instead.
