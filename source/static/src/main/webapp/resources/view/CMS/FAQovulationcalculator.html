Frequently Asked Questions

What does the ovulation calculator do?

It estimates your ovulation date, the 6-day fertile window and your next expected period from the first day of your last period and your average cycle length, with a look-ahead table for the next three cycles.

How is the estimate calculated?

With the calendar method: ovulation is assumed to fall a luteal-phase length (14 days by default, adjustable 10 to 16) before the next period starts, so the tool computes it as your last period start plus cycle length minus luteal length. The fertile window is the 5 days before estimated ovulation through ovulation day.

How accurate is it?

It is a planning estimate, not a measurement - the calendar method assumes consistent cycle lengths, and actual ovulation varies from cycle to cycle. It does not measure hormones or temperature, and it must not be used as medical advice or as a contraception method.

Does anything I enter leave my browser?

No. The dates are computed locally in the page - nothing is uploaded, stored or sent, and no account is needed.

Can it give me a due date?

No - this page answers the before question (fertile window and ovulation). For a due date after a positive test, use the pregnancy due date calculator on this site.
