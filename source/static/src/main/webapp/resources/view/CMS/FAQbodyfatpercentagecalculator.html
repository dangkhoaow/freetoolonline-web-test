Frequently Asked Questions

What does Body Fat Percentage Calculator Online do?

It estimates your body fat percentage from height, neck, and waist measurements (plus hip for women) using the U.S. Navy circumference method, and shows a fitness category alongside the number.

How accurate is the U.S. Navy circumference method?

It is a widely used estimate, typically within a few percentage points of more precise methods like DEXA scans or skinfold calipers - close enough to track trends over time, but not a medical measurement.

Do I need to sign up or upload anything?

No. Every calculation runs locally in your browser; no account, no upload, and your measurements never leave the page.

My waist is bigger than my neck - why does it still say to enter measurements greater than zero?

The formula needs a real gap between the two, not just any positive difference, and how large that gap needs to be depends on your height. At the calculator's own defaults (height 175cm, neck 38cm), a 20cm gap still returns the error; the gap needs to reach roughly 30cm before the estimate turns positive. Re-measure both figures, or try a slightly larger waist value, if you see this message.

What are the exact percentage ranges behind each category?

Men: Essential fat below 6%, Athletic 6% to 13.9%, Fitness 14% to 17.9%, Average 18% to 24.9%, Above average from 25%. Women: Essential fat below 14%, Athletic 14% to 20.9%, Fitness 21% to 24.9%, Average 25% to 31.9%, Above average from 32%.

Can the result show 0% or a number over 60%?

No. Once the formula clears zero, the displayed estimate is held between 2% and 60% regardless of how extreme the measurements are - an unusually wide waist-to-neck gap still shows 60% at most, and a gap that only just clears zero shows 2% rather than a smaller fraction.
