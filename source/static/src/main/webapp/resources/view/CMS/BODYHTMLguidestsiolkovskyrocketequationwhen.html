When to Use the Tsiolkovsky Rocket Equation 3D Explorer

Last reviewed: 2026-08-01

Open the Tsiolkovsky Rocket Equation 3D Explorer when you want to see, not just compute, why a rocket's exhaust velocity and propellant fraction set a hard limit on delta-v, and why staging is the standard way around that limit.


Sessions this explorer fits

Students checking why a "just push the propellant fraction higher" argument runs out of room fast, teachers showing the staging trick with one button press, and curious readers who just want to watch a number cross 9.4 km/s all get a direct answer from the sliders and the Ignite animation - no textbook required first.


Sessions this explorer does not fit

Sessions this page does not fit are computing a real mission's exact delta-v budget (that needs the mission's actual per-stage masses, not the shared exhaust-velocity-and-fraction model this scene uses) or comparing energy thresholds like orbital and escape speed, which is on the Escape Velocity 3D Explorer, or planning an already-in-orbit transfer, which is on the Hohmann Transfer 3D Explorer.
