Bangbang Artillery

Bangbang Artillery is a free browser cannon duel against a learning neural-net opponent, with no account or install. Adjust angle and power, fire, and try to hit the AI before it hits you.


Key features

  • Classic angle-and-power artillery you play in the browser, without accounts or installs.
  • The opponent is a small neural net that improves after each miss within the round.
  • Desktop arrows plus Space, or phone drag-to-aim, drag-for-power, and tap-to-fire.
  • A tiny download of about 35 KB fits a one-minute duel break.
  • No save file - just aim, fire, and reset.

When to use it

Reach for Bangbang Artillery when you want a fast head-to-head break that loads in seconds and needs no signup. A single match is over quickly, and a reset starts a new one right away.


Privacy and data

Everything runs in your browser. The match is session-only, so a refresh clears the neural net and nothing is written to a save file or local storage. Bangbang Artillery is adapted from Victor Ribeiro's MIT-licensed bangBang.
