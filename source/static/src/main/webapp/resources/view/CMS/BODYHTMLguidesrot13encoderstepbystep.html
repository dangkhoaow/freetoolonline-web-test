ROT13 Encoder - Step by Step

Last reviewed: 2026-07-26

ROT13 Encoder works in three steps: open the tool, paste text, then copy the result. This page walks each step.


Step 1 - open the tool

Go to ROT13 Encoder. You do not need to install anything or sign in.


Step 2 - paste and shift

Paste text into the box. Output updates as you type - A-Z and a-z shift by 13; digits and punctuation stay as-is. Run the same shift again on the output to restore the original Latin letters.


Step 3 - copy the result

Click Copy to put the current output on the clipboard. The shift runs locally - no upload and no account.

Not encryption for secrecy. Does not apply Caesar shifts other than 13, and does not transliterate non-Latin scripts.
