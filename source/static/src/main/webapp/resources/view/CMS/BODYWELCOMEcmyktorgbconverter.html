CMYK to RGB Converter Online

Last reviewed: 2026-09-27

Pick a colour and this page reads back its RGB values - alongside the matching HEX and HSL - so you can drop an exact rgb(...) string into a platform that wants an on-screen colour rather than a print CMYK one. The colour you choose is read in the browser; there is nothing to upload and no file to download.


How it works

  • Open the colour picker and choose a colour.
  • Read the HEX, RGB, and HSL values shown beneath it.
  • Click Copy next to the value you need and it goes straight to your clipboard.

When to use it

Reach for this when a downstream platform accepts an on-screen RGB or HEX colour but not a print CMYK one, and you need the RGB string for the colour you have in mind without installing a design app.


Everything stays in your browser

The picker reads your chosen colour and works out its HEX, RGB, and HSL values entirely on your device. No colour value is sent to a server, there is no account or signup, and the copy buttons write the value straight to your clipboard.
