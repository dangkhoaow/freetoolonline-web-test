Frequently Asked Questions

Is my story idea really private?

Yes. The page has no server for story text: the premise, the character, the setting and every generated draft stay in your browser on all three engine tiers. Nothing you type is uploaded or logged anywhere.

Do I need an account or a sign-up?

No. Open the page, type a premise and press Generate story. There is no account, no email and no quota.

Why did I get a story without downloading any AI model?

Two reasons are possible, and the status line under the button names which one applied. Recent Chrome browsers ship a built-in on-device AI that needs no download. Every other browser gets the deterministic template tier: a genre-flavored story start assembled from your inputs, clearly labeled as a scaffold to rewrite rather than AI prose.

How big is the model download, and where does it go?

The optional on-device models range from hundreds of MB (Llama 3.2 1B) to a few GB (Llama 3.2 3B). The exact size is read from the model list and shown before the download starts. Files are cached by your browser, so later visits and the private AI chat page reuse the same download.

Can it write a whole novel or a book chapter?

No. The models this page can run on a device are small (1 to 3 billion parameters). They handle flash fiction of about 150 words, short stories of about 300 words and opening scenes well; they cannot keep a plot consistent across chapters. Use the drafts as raw material for your own writing.

Can it generate character names or a short poem?

Yes, on the AI tiers: ask for them directly in the premise box, for example "give me five fantasy character names for a desert kingdom". The no-download template tier writes story starts only. There is deliberately no separate page for name or poem generation.

Who owns the stories this tool writes?

You do, as far as this site is concerned - the text is produced on your device from your inputs and this site never sees, stores or claims it. As with any AI-assisted draft, review it before publishing and rewrite it in your own voice.
