Megalosaurus 3D: The Apex Predator of the Jurassic

Last reviewed: 2026-07-22 | By the FreeToolOnline paleontology team

Megalosaurus was one of the first dinosaurs to be scientifically named (1824). This Middle Jurassic apex predator hunted in Europe and Australia with powerful hind legs, a long counterbalancing tail, and short arms equipped with large claws. At 8 meters long and nearly a tonne in weight, it was smaller than T-rex but ruled its world with the same deadly efficiency.

Key facts: Megalosaurus lived about 165 million years ago during the Middle Jurassic. It was a bipedal theropod with serrated teeth for tearing flesh and a body held roughly horizontal - the posture of a modern predator, not an upright kangaroo. The skeleton is well-known from multiple fossils found across the world.
