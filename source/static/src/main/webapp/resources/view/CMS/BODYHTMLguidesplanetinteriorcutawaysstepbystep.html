Planet Interior Cutaways 3D Explorer Step by Step

Last reviewed: 2026-08-04

The Planet Interior Cutaways 3D Explorer starts as soon as the page loads: drag to orbit Earth's default cutaway, scroll or pinch to zoom, and click a planet button to switch worlds. Everything renders on your own device - no install, no account.


Step 1 - wait for the scene

Wait until the status line under the canvas invites you to click a planet button or a layer. Earth's cutaway loads by default with its inner core (the deepest layer) already selected in the facts panel.


Step 2 - switch planets

Click Mercury, Jupiter, or any other planet button to rebuild the cutaway for that world, drawn on the same shared size scale so you can compare a rocky world's small iron core against a gas giant's huge, diffuse one.


Step 3 - click a layer

Click any band in the cutaway (or read the table in the panel) to open that layer's composition note and its MEASURED or MODELED ESTIMATE evidence badge. Drag to orbit and scroll to zoom while you inspect.
