Grand Canyon 3D Explorer

Last reviewed: 2026-07-27

Reviewed by FreetoolOnline editorial team

This browser explorer shows the Grand Canyon as a stepped cross-section: seven major rock formations stacked from the pale Kaibab Limestone at the rim down to the dark Vishnu Basement at the river, with the Colorado River ribbon still cutting the floor.

The canyon runs 446 km across northern Arizona, spreads up to 29 km wide, and drops about 1.86 km - over a mile. The Colorado River did the carving, with the main incision happening over roughly the last five to six million years, while the rocks it exposes span more than 1.7 billion years of Earth history. Grand Canyon National Park, established in 1919, protects nearly all of it.

  • Toggle layer accents to make each formation stand out
  • Toggle the Colorado River ribbon at the canyon floor
  • Read length, width, depth, and rock-age figures in the panel
  • See the layer stack from Kaibab Limestone to Vishnu Basement
  • Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom
  • Runs fully in the browser with the vendored three.js engine - no account, no upload
