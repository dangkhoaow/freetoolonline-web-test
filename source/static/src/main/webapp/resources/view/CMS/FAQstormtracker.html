Frequently Asked Questions

What does Live Tornado & Storm Tracker do?

It checks the National Weather Service's live feed for active tornado, hurricane, flood, and other severe weather alerts in any US state, and lists them most severe first with the official instructions.

How do I check alerts for my own area?

Choose your state from the dropdown and press Check alerts, or press "Use my location" to let the tool look up alerts near you (this asks for browser location permission).

What happens if there are no active alerts?

The tool shows a clear "No active NWS alerts" message with a last-checked timestamp rather than leaving the page blank.

What is the difference between a warning, a watch, and an advisory?

A warning means the hazard is occurring, imminent, or highly likely - take protective action now. A watch means conditions favor the hazard developing - stay alert. An advisory is less serious but could still cause significant inconvenience or, without caution, threaten life or property. The full definitions table is always shown on the page.
