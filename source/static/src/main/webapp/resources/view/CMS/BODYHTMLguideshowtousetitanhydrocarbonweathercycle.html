How to Use Titan's Methane Weather Cycle 3D Explorer - Step by Step

Last reviewed: 2026-08-09

Open the Titan's Methane Weather Cycle 3D Explorer, drag the season slider or press Auto-play, then watch the cloud band migrate and rain fall as Titan moves through a compressed year.


Drag the season slider

Drag the season slider from 0 to 100 to move through a compressed Titan year. Dragging pauses Auto-play so you control the pace directly.


Press Auto-play

Press Auto-play to let the season advance on its own and loop continuously, so you can watch a full cycle without touching the slider.


Watch the cloud band migrate

Watch the ring of cloud puffs migrate in latitude as the season changes, echoing how Cassini observed Titan's cloud activity shift between hemispheres across the moon's seasons.


Watch rain fall near storm peaks

Watch for two mild storm peaks per compressed year, when rain particles spawn near the cloud band and fall toward the surface before fading - standing in for evaporation restarting the cycle.


Read the facts panel

Read the facts panel for the current cycle state (dry, light showers, or active rain) plus published atmosphere, pressure, temperature, Titan-year-length, and Kraken Mare figures.


Orbit and zoom

Drag on the scene to orbit the camera, and scroll or pinch to zoom in on the cloud band or the polar sea patches.
