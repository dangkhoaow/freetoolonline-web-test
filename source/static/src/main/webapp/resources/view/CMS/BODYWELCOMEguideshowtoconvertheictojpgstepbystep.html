Depth: which output format to pick - JPG, WebP, or PNG

The settings panel in Step 2 offers three output formats - JPG, WebP, and PNG - and the right pick depends on where the file is going. Choose JPG for anything that has to open everywhere: email attachments, government or banking portals, older Windows machines, and social uploads all read it without a plugin, which is why it is the default. Choose WebP when the destination is a modern website or app and size matters - at a matched quality it typically lands 25 to 30 percent smaller than the same photo as JPG, trimming page weight. Reach for PNG only when the picture is a screenshot, a receipt, or flat-color artwork where you need every pixel kept lossless; a photographic HEIC saved as PNG is usually three to five times larger than the JPG with no visible gain. When you are unsure, leave it on JPG and move on - the compatibility is worth more than the few percent WebP would save.
