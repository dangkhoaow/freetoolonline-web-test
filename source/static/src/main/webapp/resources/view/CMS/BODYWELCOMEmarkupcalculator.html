Markup Calculator

Last reviewed: 2026-10-04

The markup calculator answers the pricing question directly: enter your cost and a markup percent, and the selling price plus profit are on screen as you type - alongside the margin percent, so you can see both numbers at once. Everything runs in your browser - nothing you enter is stored or sent anywhere, and no account is needed.


Key features

  • Three solve modes: selling price from a markup %, selling price from a margin %, or the markup % and margin % from a cost and a price you already know.
  • The result always states the markup % and the margin % together, with a one-line reminder that the two differ.
  • A reference table below the result maps common markup percentages (10 to 100%) to the margin % they produce, at any cost.
  • Out-of-range entries are corrected, not errored - a margin at or above 100% is capped just under 100% so the price never divides by zero.

How markup and margin differ

Markup is profit divided by cost; margin is profit divided by price. They use the same profit amount but a different base, so they are never equal except at 0%. A 100% markup - doubling your cost to set the price - is only a 50% margin, not a 100% margin.

Reading the totals

The math is plain cost-plus arithmetic on the numbers you type - it is not pricing, accounting or financial advice. Competitor prices, supplier costs, taxes and fees are not part of the calculation; every number comes from you.
