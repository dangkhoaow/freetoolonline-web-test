Frequently Asked Questions

How tall does Old Faithful's eruption get?

Typically about 106 to 185 ft (32 to 56 m), averaging around 130 ft (40 m), lasting 1.5 to 5 minutes.

How often does Old Faithful erupt?

The interval between eruptions ranges from about 44 to 125 minutes, averaging around 90 minutes - predictable enough that park rangers post forecasts, though the average interval has lengthened somewhat in recent decades.

Is Old Faithful the biggest geyser in Yellowstone?

No. It is not the tallest or the most regular geyser in the park - it is famous for how reliably predictable its eruptions are, in a basin with over 150 other geysers.

How hot is the water?

About 204 F (95.5 C) at the vent, ejecting roughly 3,700 to 8,400 US gallons (about 14,000-32,000 L) per eruption.

What is the Upper Geyser Basin?

The section of Yellowstone National Park holding Old Faithful and the highest concentration of geysers anywhere on Earth - over 150 geysers within about one square mile.

Is this a real-time eruption forecast?

No. It is an educational explorer using published figures, sped up for demonstration - the basin relief, sinter cone, and eruption timing are schematic teaching cues, not survey data or a live prediction feed.
