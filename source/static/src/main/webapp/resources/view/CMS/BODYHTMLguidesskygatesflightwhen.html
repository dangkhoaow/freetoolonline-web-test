Sky Gates Flight When To Use

Last reviewed: 2026-07-05

Sky Gates Flight is worth opening when you need a quick, one-off run without installing anything. This page covers when that fits and when it does not.


When it fits

M3: The world ramps up as you fly - speed climbs every ten seconds, red gates get harsher with time, and an occasional gold x2 gate doubles your power.


What to expect

M4: Your best distance and peak power are saved in this browser's local storage and shown before you even start - no accounts, uploads, or leaderboards.

M5: Gate chimes and buzzes are synthesized on your device and stay muted until you turn the sound on.
