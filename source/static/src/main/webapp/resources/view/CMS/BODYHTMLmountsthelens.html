Mount St. Helens lost its symmetrical summit on May 18, 1980: the north flank collapsed and the eruption blasted out a horseshoe-shaped crater open toward the north. Toggle the ghost outline of the pre-1980 summit and the lava dome that has been rebuilding inside the crater ever since.

About this scene

Published figures: current elevation about 2,549 m; pre-1980 summit about 2,950 m - roughly 400 m of the peak was lost; the crater is horseshoe-shaped, about 2 km wide and 3.5 km long, open to the north; a lava dome has been rebuilding inside the crater since 1980.
