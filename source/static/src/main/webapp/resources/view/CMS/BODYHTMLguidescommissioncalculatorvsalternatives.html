Against mental math

A flat rate on a round number is manageable in your head; a tiered plan is not, because the base-rate portion has to stop being rated once the sale crosses quota, and that split is exactly what mental math tends to get wrong - it is easy to accidentally apply the higher accelerator rate to the whole amount. Here the two portions are always separated and labeled.


Against a phone calculator app

A plain calculator app gets you the flat-rate commission in one multiplication, but a tiered plan needs two separate calculations - one at the base rate on the amount up to quota, one at the accelerator rate on the amount above it - that most people either skip or combine incorrectly. This tool runs both at once and labels which is which.


Against a spreadsheet

A spreadsheet reaches the same arithmetic once the tiered formula is written, and it wins when you are calculating commission for a whole team or a full pay period at once and want the rows saved. For a single quote tonight, the calculator is faster, needs nothing installed, and cannot slip a cell reference - at the cost that nothing here is saved, so copy the number out before closing the tab.


Against a CRM or payroll commission module

A CRM or payroll system with commission plan rules built in wins once a business is already running on one - no second tool needed for a plan that is already configured. This calculator fits the gap before that: a quick quote on a new deal, a sanity check against a quoted rate, or working out what a plan would pay before it goes anywhere near the payroll system, with no account or setup. It never fetches sales data, quotas or plan rules from any CRM or payroll system - every number here is typed in by hand.
