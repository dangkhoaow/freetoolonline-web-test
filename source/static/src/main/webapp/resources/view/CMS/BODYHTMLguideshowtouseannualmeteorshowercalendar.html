How to Use the Annual Meteor Shower Calendar 3D Explorer - Step by Step

Last reviewed: 2026-08-10

Open the Annual Meteor Shower Calendar 3D Explorer, drag the day slider or pick a shower from the list, then read the facts panel to see its real peak date, strength, and whether it is active right now.


Pick a shower from the list

Open #t3dAmcShowerSelect and choose any of the 12 major annual showers. The dial jumps straight to that shower's real published peak day, and the facts panel fills with its peak date, zenith hourly rate (ZHR), radiant, and parent body.


Or drag the day slider yourself

Drag #t3dAmcDaySlider (0 to 365, one value per day of the year) to any day. The Earth marker moves around the calendar-dial ring to match, and any shower marker whose real activity window covers that day turns bright yellow and grows.


Jump to today

Press #t3dAmcTodayBtn to snap the dial to your device's real current date and see which showers, if any, are active today.


Play through the year

Press #t3dAmcPlayBtn to let the dial advance on its own through the whole year, or press it again to pause and scrub manually.


Read the marker colors

Blue markers trace back to a comet; orange markers trace back to an asteroid - only the Quadrantids and the Geminids fall in that second group. Any marker turns bright yellow when the scrubbed day falls inside its published activity window.


Drag to orbit and use fullscreen

Drag to orbit the camera, scroll or pinch to zoom, and use the Fullscreen button to expand the whole wrapper for a clearer view of all 12 markers around the dial.
