This browser explorer shows Huacachina Oasis in southern Peru - a freshwater lagoon surrounded by towering sand dunes. The scene presents the lagoon at the center with surrounding dunes rising high above the desert floor.

Huacachina is the only natural oasis in South America and one of the few remaining in the world. The lagoon sits about 384 metres above sea level in the Ica Region of Peru. The water is tinted green from algae growth, and the surrounding dunes reach heights of over 100 metres, making it a striking contrast of blue-green water against golden sand. The oasis has been a major attraction for adventure tourism, particularly sandboarding and dune buggy tours.
