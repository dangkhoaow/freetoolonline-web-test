Olympus Mons 3D Explorer vs alternatives

Olympus Mons 3D Explorer lets a reader rotate the shield, drag a vertical-exaggeration slider, and see its scale directly against Mount Everest and Mauna Loa in one 3D scene - a NASA Mars Atlas fact sheet wins as the fixed, citable source for the exact published numbers; a static infographic wins for a quick print-ready comparison without needing WebGL.
