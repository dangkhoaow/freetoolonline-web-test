About this scene

The facts panel fills when the scene loads. Use the toggles below the scene.

Published figures: maximum depth about 594 m (1,949 ft) - the deepest lake in the USA; average depth about 350 m (1,148 ft); water clarity readings around 44 m (143 ft), among the clearest lake water on Earth; no rivers flow in or out.

Drag to orbit and scroll or pinch to zoom. Everything renders on your device with WebGL.
