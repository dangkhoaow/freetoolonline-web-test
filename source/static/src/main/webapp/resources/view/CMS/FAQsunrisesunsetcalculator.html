Frequently Asked Questions

What does the sunrise sunset calculator show?

Sunrise, solar noon, sunset, total day length, golden hour (morning and evening), and civil dawn/dusk for any city, device location, or manually entered latitude/longitude, on any date you choose.

Does it work for any location, not just the preset cities?

Yes. The city dropdown is a shortcut for 16 major cities; you can also tap Use my location, or type any latitude and longitude directly, to get times for that exact spot.

What time zone are the results shown in?

Your device's own time zone, since every calculation runs locally in your browser. If you calculate for a location far from your own time zone, remember the displayed time follows your device, not the location.

What is golden hour and why does the calculator show it?

Golden hour is the period of low, warm sunlight shortly after sunrise and shortly before sunset that photographers often prefer. The calculator shows when the morning golden hour ends and when the evening golden hour starts.

Does this tool need an internet connection or send my location anywhere?

No. Once the page loads, every calculation, including the optional "Use my location" position, runs on your device - nothing is uploaded or sent to a server.
