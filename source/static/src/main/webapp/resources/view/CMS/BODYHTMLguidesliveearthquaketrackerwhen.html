Live Earthquake Tracker When To Use

Last reviewed: 2026-08-04

Live Earthquake Tracker is worth opening any time you want a quick check of recent seismic activity. This page covers when it fits and what it does not replace.


When it fits

Reach for it right after you feel a quake and want the official magnitude and location, before checking a week's worth of activity in a region you are visiting, or out of general curiosity about what the USGS is currently reporting worldwide.


What to expect

You get the same live USGS feed the tool reads directly in your browser: magnitude, place, time, and depth, plus an always-visible magnitude-scale reference table. Data can lag the live event by a few minutes and coverage is strongest for US/networked regions, so this is a quick-reference check, not an early-warning system - always follow local official emergency guidance during an active event.
