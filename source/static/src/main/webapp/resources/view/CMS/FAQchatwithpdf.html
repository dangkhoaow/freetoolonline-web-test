Frequently Asked Questions

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. The PDF is opened and read entirely in your browser, the passage search runs on your device, and the answers are generated on your device too. There is no server behind this page, so there is nothing for the document to be uploaded to.

What do I get if I never load an AI model?

The tool still finds and shows the most relevant passages for your question, each labeled with its page number. That works instantly with no download, and for locating where a long document covers something it is often all you need. Loading a model adds a written answer on top of those passages.

Why does my scanned PDF return nothing?

Scanned or photographed documents have no text layer, and this tool reads the text layer. The page warns you when a file looks scanned instead of failing silently. Run it through the image-to-text OCR tool first, then ask your questions against the recognized text.

How reliable are the written answers?

They are drafted by a small on-device model working over the matching passages - a solid first read, not a professional analyst. Every answer prints the passages and page numbers it used, so check the cited pages before relying on the answer.

How large are the downloads, and do they repeat?

The optional matching model is about 25 MB. The language models are larger, and each one's size is read from the model list and shown before the download starts. Both are one-time downloads cached by the browser, and the language-model cache is shared with the private AI chat page.
