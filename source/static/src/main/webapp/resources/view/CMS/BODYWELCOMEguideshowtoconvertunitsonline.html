Length and weight stay simple because they share a zero point - multiply by a fixed factor and the conversion is done, which is why a meter is always exactly 3.28084 feet no matter the value typed. Temperature cannot use that shortcut: Celsius, Fahrenheit, and Kelvin each start their scale at a different point, so the calculator runs an offset formula instead of a plain multiply - the reason 0 degrees Celsius becomes 32, not 0, in Fahrenheit.
