Meme Maker

Last reviewed: 2026-07-25

Upload a photo and add a top and bottom caption to turn it into a meme - no upload, no account, and the photo never leaves your browser.


Key features

  • Type a top caption, a bottom caption, or both.
  • The text auto-shrinks and wraps onto extra lines so a long caption never spills past the edges of the photo.
  • Toggle ALL CAPS for the classic meme look, or turn it off to keep your caption's original casing.
  • Download the finished meme as a PNG or JPEG matching the source image.

How it works

The photo is drawn onto an HTML5 canvas in the browser and the finished meme is exported directly from that canvas as a downloadable file - everything runs client-side, so the photo is never uploaded anywhere.

Privacy and limits

There is no built-in template or stock-photo library, so you supply your own photo. Refreshing or leaving the page clears the canvas, so nothing is saved between visits.
