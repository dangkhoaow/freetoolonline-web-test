Age Calculator Step By Step

Last reviewed: 2026-08-05
freetoolonline.com Editorial Team

Age Calculator runs in three steps: open the tool, provide your input, then download the result. This page walks through what each step expects.


Step 1 - open the tool

Open the tool by going to Age Calculator. Nothing needs to be installed or signed in before you start.


Step 2 - provide your input

Provide your input, then use it to check an exact age for a form, an eligibility cutoff, or just out of curiosity.


Step 3 - get the result

You get the result on the same page. The calculation runs locally in the browser - no upload and no account.

Defaults to today's date for the "as of" field, but you can pick any date to check age as of that day.

Years lived, in total days

The tool's "total days lived" figure is the same age, expressed as one number instead of years/months/days. Here is roughly what common milestones look like in total days (approximate; leap years shift the exact count by a few days).

Frequently asked questions

What happens if I enter a birth date after the "as of" date?

The tool shows a prompt to fix the date instead of returning a wrong or negative age.

Does the tool store or send my birth date anywhere?

No. The date you enter is not stored beyond the page session and is never sent to an analytics or tracking service; no account is required.
