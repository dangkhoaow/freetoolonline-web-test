Frequently Asked Questions

Why is Yosemite Valley shaped like a U instead of a V?

Repeated Ice Age glaciers ground through an older, narrower V-shaped river canyon, widening and deepening it into today's broad, flat-floored U-shaped trough.

How tall is El Capitan?

Its summit sits about 2,307 m (7,569 ft) above sea level, rising about 1,100 m (3,600 ft) above the valley floor near the west entrance - one of the largest single granite monoliths on Earth.

How much higher is Half Dome than the valley floor?

About 1,444 m (4,737 ft) at Tenaya Canyon, with a summit elevation of about 2,695 m (8,846 ft). Its northwest face is famously sheer and near-vertical.

How tall is Yosemite Falls?

Its total drop is about 739 m (2,425 ft) across three tiers, making it the tallest waterfall in Yosemite National Park.

When was Yosemite Valley first protected?

Abraham Lincoln signed the Yosemite Grant on June 30, 1864, protecting the valley and the Mariposa Grove under California state trust - an early precedent for the national park idea. Yosemite National Park itself was established in 1890, and the valley became a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1984.

Is this a scientific terrain model?

No. It is an educational explorer using published figures - the valley profile, cliff placement, and river path are schematic teaching cues, not survey terrain.
