Moropus, a clawed chalicothere mammal rather than a dinosaur, roamed North America during the Miocene, about 20.4-13.6 million years ago. Marsh named the genus in 1877; the larger species M. elatus stood roughly 2.4 m at the shoulder, measured about 3-3.5 m long, with one specimen estimated near 1179 kg.
