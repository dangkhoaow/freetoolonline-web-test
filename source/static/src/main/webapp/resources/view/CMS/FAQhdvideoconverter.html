Frequently Asked Questions

What does the HD Video Converter do?

Convert HD video files between common formats in your browser.

When should I use the HD Video Converter?

When you would rather not upload a clip to a server - the conversion runs locally on your device, so it suits private footage, a slow network, or working offline.

What complementary tool works alongside it?

See the original video converter for the server-based version on this site.

Where do my files end up?

Inputs are processed locally and discarded when the page closes.

How do I pick the output format for my HD video?

The target format comes from the page you open or the dropdown above the file picker, and every conversion runs in your browser with no upload. Choose an audio format (MP3, AAC, OGG, FLAC, M4A, or WAV) to pull the audio track out of a clip, and set the MP3 bitrate first if you want it - 128 kbps keeps the file small, 192 kbps is the standard default, 256 kbps is high, and 320 kbps is the maximum. Choose a video container (MP4, WebM, MOV, AVI, or MKV) to repackage the footage without re-encoding, so the original bitrate, frame rate, and resolution stay exactly as they were. Choose GIF to turn a clip into a short 10 fps, 480 px wide preview that is small enough to share.
