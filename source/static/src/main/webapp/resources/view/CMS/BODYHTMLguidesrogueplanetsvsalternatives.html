Where the Microlensing Exoplanet Explorer wins

The Microlensing Exoplanet Explorer wins when the question is a planet that still orbits a star - it shows the visible lens star, the ring, and the short secondary spike a real orbiting planet adds to that star's own multi-week brightening curve.


Where the Rogue Planets Explorer wins

The Rogue Planets Explorer wins when the question is a planet with no host star at all - why the entire event is short rather than just a spike, why the lens itself is never directly visible, and how the 2011 population estimate compares to the 2017 revision that lowered it.


A reasonable rule

As a reasonable rule, use the Microlensing Exoplanet Explorer first to see the general single-lens brightening shape and how an orbiting planet adds to it, then use the Rogue Planets Explorer to see what changes when there is no host star to add anything to.
