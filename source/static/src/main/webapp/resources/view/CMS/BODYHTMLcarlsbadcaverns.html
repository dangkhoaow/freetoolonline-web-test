About this scene

Big Room measurements appear here once the limestone chamber finishes loading. Below the scene, switch the cave lighting between warm amber show lights and a cooler simulated headlamp, and turn the Giant Dome marker on or off to pick out that formation.

Published figures: Big Room floor area about 8.2 acres (roughly 33,000 sq m); about 230 m (roughly 750 ft) below the surface at its deepest point; carved by sulfuric-acid dissolution in Permian-age limestone; UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1995.
