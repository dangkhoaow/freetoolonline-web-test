How to Use the Stellar Spectral Classification 3D Explorer - Step by Step

Last reviewed: 2026-08-12

Open the Stellar Spectral Classification 3D Explorer, pick a letter class or drag the temperature slider, then read the facts panel to see the real temperature range, dominant line family, and luminosity example for that star.


Pick a letter class button

Press any of the seven O, B, A, F, G, K, M buttons to jump #t3dSscTempSlider straight to that class's representative temperature. The star's color and the three line-strength bars update to match.


Or scrub the temperature slider yourself

Drag #t3dSscTempSlider to move continuously across the full OBAFGKM range. The slider is scaled so both the hottest O stars and the coolest M stars stay reachable in one drag.


Toggle the luminosity class

Press #t3dSscLumBtn-V, #t3dSscLumBtn-III, or #t3dSscLumBtn-I to compare a main-sequence dwarf, a giant, or a supergiant at the same temperature - the star's on-screen size changes using the real Aldebaran and Betelgeuse examples as a teaching scale.


Pin the Sun

Press #t3dSscSunBtn to jump straight to the Sun's real effective temperature, 5,772 K, at main-sequence luminosity - the Sun's true G2V classification.


Play through the sequence hands-free

Press #t3dSscPlayBtn to auto-scrub back and forth across the whole OBAFGKM range, or press it again to pause and take manual control.


Drag to orbit and use fullscreen

Drag to orbit the camera, scroll or pinch to zoom, and use the Fullscreen button to expand the whole wrapper for a clearer view of the star and the three line-strength bars.
