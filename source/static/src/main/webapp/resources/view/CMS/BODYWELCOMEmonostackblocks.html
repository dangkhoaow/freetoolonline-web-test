Welcome - press Play to load Mono Stack Blocks. High scores stay under ftol:monostackblocks:storage.

Mono Stack Blocks rewards planning ahead: line clears, drop bonuses, and rising speed all feed one score you can beat across sessions.

Scoring and levels

  • Line clears score 100, 300, 500, or 800 points for one, two, three, or four rows at once, multiplied by your current level.
  • A soft drop adds 1 point per cell and a hard drop adds 2 points per cell, so committing early pays off.
  • The top bar tracks score, lines, and level; the level rises every 10 lines cleared and gravity speeds up with it.

Pieces and assists

  • Tetrominoes are dealt from a 7-bag randomizer on a 10 by 20 grid, so each of the seven shapes appears once per bag.
  • SRS wall kicks let a rotating piece shift out of a wall or the floor, and a ghost outline previews where the piece will land.
  • Hold sets one piece aside per turn; the settings menu switches to a dark theme or resets your saved high scores.
