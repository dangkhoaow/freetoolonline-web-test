Choose a photo, pick a mode - Quick upscale 2x (about 8 MB model) or Restore & upscale 4x (about 55 MB model, also removes JPEG artifacts) - and click the button; the long edge is resized to 512px before processing so the AI model finishes in a reasonable time even on a large photo.

Restoring or upscaling a photo takes three steps:

  1. Choose a photo - the original previews immediately, before any processing starts.
  2. Pick 2x or 4x and click the button - the first run downloads that mode's model once (about 8 MB for 2x, about 55 MB for 4x; cached offline after that). The 4x mode sharpens, denoises, and upscales; the 2x mode is a quicker plain upscale.
  3. Download the result - the photo is processed entirely in this browser tab and is never uploaded to a server.
