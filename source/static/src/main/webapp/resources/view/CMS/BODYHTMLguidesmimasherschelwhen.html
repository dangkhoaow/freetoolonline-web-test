When to use Mimas Herschel Crater 3D Explorer

Use this explorer when you want to see how large Herschel is relative to tiny Mimas, and to show the central peak marker from published basin descriptions.

  • Comparing a ~130-140 km crater on a ~396 km moon
  • Introducing Voyager 1980 and Cassini 2010 imaging
  • Discussing Death Star coincidence without implying design

This is an educational visualization, not an impact model.
