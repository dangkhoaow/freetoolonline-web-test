Potion Brew Shop is a free browser potion puzzle - click shelf bottles to brew orders correctly before the timer runs out across three shop stages; ~32 KB, no install, no account.

Last reviewed: 2026-08-02

Reviewed by FreetoolOnline editorial team

Each customer shows an order card, and you brew it by clicking warm, cold, life, and emotion ingredients from the shelves into the cauldron, then serving the finished bottle before the timer expires.

Stages and recipes

The shop runs across three stages, and each one unlocks new ingredient types and harder multi-item recipes - a brew that took a single bottle early on can need several later. A few orders also accept an alternative: Water, for instance, can stand in for a cooling potion, so when the exact ingredient is not on the shelf it is worth trying a sensible substitute instead of letting the order time out.

Your data stays on your device

Press Play once and the ~32 KB engine loads into a same-origin iframe on this page. Nothing leaves your device except that initial page and the game files served from this site - there is no account, no upload, and no save data carried between runs.
