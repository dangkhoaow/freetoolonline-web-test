Elm Street Delivery is a free browser courier route game that runs entirely in your browser with no account or install. The skill is route order and bike capacity, not twitch aiming.


Key features

  • Plan courier pickups and deliveries on one WebGL map.
  • Limited bike capacity forces route order choices.
  • About 3 MB loads after Play and then stays in the browser cache.

How to play

Press Play, then click to start. Click houses and the warehouse to pick up fashion boxes and deliver them before customers turn unhappy.


Your data

Progress is session-only on this page, so no save file is stored after you close the tab.
