Frequently Asked Questions

How tall is Mount Fuji?

3,776.24 m above sea level, measured at Kengamine on the crater rim - the highest peak in Japan.

Is Mount Fuji still an active volcano?

Yes. It is classed as active; its last eruption was the Hoei eruption of 1707-1708.

What are the Fuji Five Lakes?

Kawaguchiko, Yamanakako, Saiko, Shojiko, and Motosuko - five lakes at the mountain's northern base, a classic viewpoint for photographing the cone.

Where is Mount Fuji located?

On Honshu island, Japan, straddling the Shizuoka and Yamanashi prefecture border, about 100 km southwest of Tokyo.

Why is Mount Fuji so symmetric?

Repeated eruptions built up even layers of lava and ash over thousands of years, producing the near-perfect conical shape seen today.

Is this a terrain simulation?

No. It is an educational explorer using published figures - the cone shape, snowline, and lake positions are schematic teaching cues, not survey terrain.
