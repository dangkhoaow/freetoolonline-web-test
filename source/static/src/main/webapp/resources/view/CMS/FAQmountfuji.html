Frequently Asked Questions

How tall is Mount Fuji?

Mount Fuji is 3,776 meters (12,388 feet) above sea level, making it Japan's highest mountain. The exact elevation measured in 2014 was 3,775.63 m.

When was Mount Fuji's last eruption?

Mount Fuji last erupted in 1707-1708 during the Hoei era, an eruption that lasted 49 days. It has been dormant for over 300 years since then.

Is Mount Fuji visible from Tokyo?

Yes, on clear days Mount Fuji is visible from Tokyo, which is located about 100-130 kilometers away. The mountain's distinctive symmetric cone shape makes it recognizable across this distance.

When is Mount Fuji snow-capped?

Mount Fuji typically has snow cover for about 6 months each year, roughly from November through May in the Northern Hemisphere, though this varies by season and weather patterns.

Why is Mount Fuji culturally important?

Mount Fuji holds profound spiritual and cultural significance in Japanese tradition. It has been depicted in countless artworks, including Hokusai's famous "The Great Wave off Kanagawa" and other ukiyo-e prints. The mountain represents both natural beauty and sacred tradition.
