How to use New Horizons Pluto Flyby 3D Explorer

  1. Open the New Horizons Pluto Flyby 3D Explorer and wait for the canvas to paint.
  2. Drag to orbit the view and scroll to zoom in on the launch-to-Pluto path.
  3. Choose Pluto flyby for the primary-mission path or Plus Arrokoth for the extended path.
  4. Scrub the phase slider from 0 to 99 to move the spacecraft to any point on the path.
  5. Press Play to animate the full trajectory continuously, or Pause to hold a position.
