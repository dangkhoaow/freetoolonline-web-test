Frequently Asked Questions

How long is the Great Rift Valley?

About 6,000 km (3,700 mi), running from the Jordan Rift in Lebanon south through East Africa to Mozambique.

How was the Great Rift Valley formed?

It formed through tectonic rifting that began about 25-30 million years ago, as the African and Somali plates slowly pull apart.

How wide and deep is the valley?

The East African segment is typically about 30-100 km wide, with escarpment walls rising roughly 600-2,000 m above the flat valley floor.

Which mountains and lakes are along the rift?

Mount Kilimanjaro and Mount Kenya, East Africa's two tallest peaks, rise near it, and a chain of rift lakes - including Lake Malawi, Lake Tanganyika, and Lake Turkana - lies along its floor.

Why is it called the "Cradle of Humankind"?

Major hominid fossil sites, including Olduvai Gorge, lie within the valley, making it a key region for studying early human evolution.

Is this a scientific terrain model?

No. It is an educational explorer using published figures - the valley segment, escarpment profile, and peak/lake positions are schematic teaching cues, not survey data.
