Bounce Back is a free browser boomerang roguelite you can play with no account and no install. Press Play to load the roughly 120 KB game in a same-origin iframe, then throw your returning boomerang, dash through hits, and clear ten procedural dungeon levels.


Key features

  • Boomerang throw and return combat aimed with the mouse.
  • WASD movement plus a Space dash that lets you avoid damage.
  • Ten procedural dungeon levels, a final boss, and a shop you visit between deaths.
  • Persistent coins, warp level, best time, and win flag saved under ftol:bounceback:*.
  • Adapted from Bounce Back by Frank Force under the GNU GPL version 2 (or later).

When to use it

Bounce Back plays best on a desktop or laptop with a mouse and keyboard, since aiming and throwing use the mouse while movement uses WASD. Everything runs in your browser after the initial load, so a run keeps going even without a connection once the game files are cached on this device.


Privacy

Save keys stay on this device under ftol:bounceback:*; nothing else leaves this device except the initial page and game files loaded from this site.
