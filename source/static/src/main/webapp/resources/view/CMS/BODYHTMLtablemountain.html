Table Mountain rises as a flat sandstone plateau, about 1,085 m at its highest point, cut off sharply by near-vertical cliffs on every side. Toggle the "tablecloth" cloud to see the orographic cloud that spills over the flat top, and the Maclear's Beacon & cableway marker for the highest point and the aerial cableway route.

About this scene

The facts panel fills when the scene loads. Use the toggles below the scene.

Published figures: highest point 1,085 m at Maclear's Beacon; flat plateau about 3 km long; sandstone cliff faces on every side; Table Mountain Aerial Cableway opened in 1929.
