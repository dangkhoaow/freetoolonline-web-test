Published figures: NANOGrav's 15-year data set timed 68 millisecond pulsars for gravitational-wave-induced deviations of tens to a few hundred nanoseconds, reporting a stochastic background amplitude of about 2.4e-15 at a one-year reference frequency (2023, with EPTA, PPTA, and CPTA). The correlation curve shown here is the real Hellings-Downs formula (Hellings and Downs, 1983), computed exactly for the angle on the slider.
