Sunrise & Sunset Calculator

Last reviewed: 2026-08-04

Pick a city from the preset list, tap Use my location, or type a latitude and longitude directly, then choose a date - the sunrise sunset calculator instantly shows that day's sunrise, solar noon, sunset, and total day length for the spot you picked, updated live as you change any field.


Key features

  • Pick a city from the preset list, tap Use my location, or type a latitude and longitude directly - the calculator works for any point on Earth.
  • Choose any date, past, present, or future, with the date picker - the calculator is not limited to today.
  • The result table shows sunrise, solar noon, sunset, and the total day length for the chosen date and location.
  • Golden hour, the low-angle-sun window many photographers favor for warm, soft light, is shown as both a morning end-time and an evening start-time.
  • Civil dawn and civil dusk, when the sky is light enough to see outdoors without artificial light just before sunrise and just after sunset, are shown alongside the main sun times.
  • Times are shown in your device's local time zone.

How day length changes

Day length changes with both latitude and the time of year because the sun's angle above the horizon depends on how far north or south a location sits and how far the year has moved past the equinox. Locations near the equator see roughly 12-hour days year-round, while higher latitudes swing between long summer days and short winter days. Set the date to any day, past or future, to see how the times shift.


Privacy and time zone

Times are shown in your device's own time zone, because the coordinates and date stay in the browser and the sun times are computed on this device, with no upload and no account.


What this tool does not do

The calculator does not include a moon-phase mode or a multi-day forecast table, and city input is a curated preset list rather than free-text search - use your device location or manual coordinates for a spot that is not on the list.
