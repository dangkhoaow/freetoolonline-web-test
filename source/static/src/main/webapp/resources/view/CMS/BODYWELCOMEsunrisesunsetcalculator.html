Sunrise & Sunset Calculator

Last reviewed: 2026-08-04

Pick a city from the preset list, tap Use my location, or type a latitude and longitude directly, then choose a date - the sunrise sunset calculator instantly shows that day's sunrise, solar noon, sunset, and total day length for the spot you picked, updated live as you change any field.

Golden hour and civil dawn/dusk are shown alongside the main sun times. Golden hour is the low-angle-sun window many photographers favor for warm, soft light; the calculator gives both the morning end-time and the evening start-time. Civil dawn and civil dusk mark when the sky is light enough to see outdoors without artificial light, just before sunrise and just after sunset.

Day length changes with both latitude and the time of year because the sun's angle above the horizon depends on how far north or south a location sits and how far the year has moved past the equinox. Locations near the equator see roughly 12-hour days year-round, while higher latitudes swing between long summer days and short winter days. Set the date to any day, past or future, to see how the times shift.

Times are shown in your device's own time zone, because the coordinates and date stay in the browser and the sun times are computed on this device, with no upload and no account. The calculator does not include a moon-phase mode or a multi-day forecast table, and city input is a curated preset list rather than free-text search - use your device location or manual coordinates for a spot that is not on the list.
