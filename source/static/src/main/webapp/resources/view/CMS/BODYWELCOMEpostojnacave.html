Postojna Cave 3D Explorer

Last reviewed: 2026-08-27

Reviewed by FreetoolOnline editorial team

This browser explorer shows Postojna Cave as a chain of limestone halls cut through the Slovenian karst by the Pivka River, widening at its center into the cave's largest chamber, the Concert Hall, and studded throughout with dripstone columns built up drip by drip over thousands of years.

Postojna is one of Europe's oldest tourist caves, opened for regular guided tours in 1818 and lit by electricity from 1884 - among the first public spaces in Europe to have it. A small underground railway, first laid in 1872 and later electrified, still carries visitors along part of the roughly 5 km round-trip route. The cave is also home to Proteus anguinus, the blind, pale cave olm salamander found only in the Dinaric karst, kept on view in an underground vivarium along the route.
