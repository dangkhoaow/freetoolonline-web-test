This page renders a Maiasaura as a 3D model you can spin in the browser - drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, toggle a 1.8 m person beside it for scale, and click the head, neck, or a leg to read a fact about that part.

Maiasaura lived in the Late Cretaceous, about 80 to 75 million years ago, in the USA. The Natural History Museum lists length about 9.0 m and mass about 2500 kg. It was a herbivorous hadrosaurine duckbill. Named by Horner and Makela in 1979 (type M. peeblesorum) for nest colonies and evidence of nestling care - the name means "good mother lizard."
