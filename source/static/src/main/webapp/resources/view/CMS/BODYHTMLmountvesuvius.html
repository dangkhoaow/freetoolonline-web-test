Mount Vesuvius rises about 9 km east of Naples, Italy - a modern volcanic cone growing inside the horseshoe-shaped remnant of an older, larger volcano called Monte Somma. This 3D explorer shows both the modern cone and the ancient caldera rim that survives only as a partial ridge open toward the south.

About this scene

Published figures: modern cone about 1,281 m; Monte Somma ridge up to about 1,132 m at Punta Nasone; famous for the 79 AD eruption that buried Pompeii and Herculaneum; last erupted in March 1944; about 600,000 people live in today's Red Zone evacuation area around its base.
