Frequently Asked Questions

How tall are the dunes at Sossusvlei?

Big Daddy, the tallest, rises about 325 m above Deadvlei - one of the tallest dunes on Earth. Dune 45, the most-visited, is about 170 m tall.

What is Deadvlei?

A white clay pan at the foot of the dunes, dried out when shifting sand cut it off from the Tsauchab River. Its dead camel thorn trees are estimated at 600-900 years old but have not decomposed because the climate is too dry.

Why are the dunes red?

Iron oxide coats the sand grains. Older dunes have had more time to oxidize, so they are deeper red - younger sand nearer the washes stays paler.

What is a star dune?

A dune with several ridges radiating from a central peak, built where wind blows from more than one direction over time - unlike a simple crescent dune shaped by wind from a single direction.

Where is Sossusvlei?

Inside Namib-Naukluft National Park in Namibia, part of the Namib Sand Sea UNESCO World Heritage Site and the Namib Desert, widely cited as the world's oldest desert.

Is this a geological simulation?

No. It is an educational explorer using published park and geographic figures - the dune field, tree placement, and color bands are procedural teaching cues.
