How to Use the ROI Calculator Online - Step by Step

Last reviewed: 2026-07-30

The ROI Calculator computes your return on investment in three steps. Enter the cost, enter the return, and read the result - nothing to install and no account needed.

Step 1 - Enter the initial investment

Type the total amount you spent in the "Initial investment" field. This is the capital you put in - the purchase price of shares, the budget for a campaign, or the property price you paid.

Step 2 - Enter the final value

Type the total return you received in the "Final value" field. For a stock this is the sale price. For a campaign, it is the revenue you attribute to that spend. The tool computes net profit and ROI percentage as you type.

Step 3 - Add a holding period (optional)

If you held the investment for more than one year, enter the number of years in the "Holding period" field. The tool then shows the annualized ROI alongside the total ROI, letting you compare investments of different lengths on a like-for-like basis.

Reading the result

The tool shows net profit (final value minus initial investment), ROI as a percentage, and - when you entered a holding period - the annualized rate. The formula used is displayed below the result: (Final value - Initial investment) / Initial investment x 100.
