Frequently Asked Questions

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. The text is read out of the photo on your device by an engine served from this site, and the translation also runs on your device - with Chrome's built-in engine or with a model your browser downloads once and keeps. There is no server that ever sees the image or the text.

What does it download, and when?

The reader fetches about 5 MB from this site the first time you click "Read text from photo". Translation needs either nothing (when Chrome's built-in translation covers the pair) or a one-time model of about 115 MB per language pair, and the size is shown on the button before you start - nothing downloads without your click.

Which languages does it translate to?

The text in the photo is read as English, and you can translate it into Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Indonesian, Vietnamese, Hindi, Chinese, Russian or Arabic. Pairs that the on-device model cannot serve are not listed, so every option in the menu actually works.

Why did it misread part of my photo?

The reader works best on sharp, well-lit, straight-on printed text; blur, glare, tiny fonts and steep angles cause slips. The recognized text sits in an editable box exactly for this - fix the slip there, then translate. Handwriting is a different problem and has its own page with a model trained for it.

Does it replace the text inside the image?

No. The result is text - the recognized original and its translation, both copyable. The photo itself is not re-rendered with translated text drawn over it.
