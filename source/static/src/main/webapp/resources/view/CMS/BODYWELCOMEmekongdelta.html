Mekong Delta 3D Explorer

Last reviewed: 2026-08-14

Reviewed by FreetoolOnline editorial team

This browser explorer shows the Mekong Delta as a braided river fan: a single trunk enters from inland Cambodia, converges into one channel, then splits into a network of muddy distributary channels crossing a flat, low-lying plain, reaching the South China Sea through several mouths.

The Mekong splits into two main branches on entering Vietnam - Tien Giang (the Mekong proper) and Hau Giang, also called the Bassac - and each branch divides further as it nears the coast. The delta was historically described as having nine mouths reaching the sea, giving it the Vietnamese name Cuu Long, "Nine Dragons"; two of those mouths have since silted shut, so present-day maps commonly show seven to eight still open.

The Mekong Delta scene assembles entirely inside your browser using the site-vendored three.js WebGL build, so the braided distributary network, the flood-season recolor, and the Cai Rang floating-market marker all redraw on your own device. The engine downloads once (about 0.7 MB), is cached for return visits, and nothing about your session - no account, no upload - is ever sent to a server.

This is an educational approximation - the channel network, plain profile, and vertical scale are schematic teaching cues, not survey hydrology.
