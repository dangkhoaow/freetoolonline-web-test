Paraphrase Text On Your Device - Step by Step

Last reviewed: 2026-08-12
freetoolonline.com Editorial Team

A full run on the AI Paraphrasing Tool (On-Device) is five controls long: read the sample pair, paste your text, pick a tone, get an engine ready, and copy the rewrite you like. Nothing typed into the page is uploaded at any step - the new wording is produced on your own device.


Step 1 - judge the output shape before pasting anything

The page loads with a deliberately wordy sample of about 100 words already sitting in the input box, and its prepared plainer rewrite already rendered below, labeled as prepared ahead of time. No download has happened and no engine has run - the pair exists so you can judge the output shape at zero cost.

That canned pair is guarded. It appears only while the input is byte-identical to the built-in sample; change a single character and your text is treated as your text. It never receives a prepared answer.


Step 2 - paste your text

Replace the sample with your own passage. A live word and character count runs under the box as you type. On the on-device model path a single run accepts up to 8,000 characters; anything longer is cut to fit, and the output label reports the truncation when it happens.


Step 3 - pick a tone

The tone selector offers Neutral, Formal, Casual, and Simpler wording. The meaning of your text is preserved on every setting - the tone only steers how the wording comes out. Friendlier-or-stiffer requests are this dial too: pick Casual to loosen a formal paragraph, or Formal to do the reverse.


Step 4 - get an engine ready

On Chrome versions that ship the built-in Rewriter AI there is nothing to prepare - the page detects it and the rewrite runs with zero download. On every other setup, pick a model and press Load model: Llama 3.2 1B is the default, with Llama 3.2 3B and Phi 3.5 mini as upgrades, and each option shows its download size next to its name before anything starts. A progress bar tracks the one-time download from start to finish.


Step 5 - run it, compare, copy

Press the run button and the result renders below the input, so the original and the rewrite sit one above the other for a direct read-through. The status line under the output names exactly which engine produced it - built-in browser AI, the named on-device model, or the prepared sample demo - so you always know what you are reading. When the rewrite holds up, Copy result puts it on the clipboard. Give it a final read before sending or publishing: a small model can occasionally drop a nuance or flatten a detail.


If the page answers "needs an AI engine"

With no model loaded and no built-in AI available, running your own text produces an honest notice - paraphrasing needs an AI engine - plus the invitation to load the on-device model or use a browser with built-in AI. The page never fakes a rewrite for user text; only the built-in sample has a prepared answer.

Clean up when you are done

The downloaded weights stay cached in the browser, so the next rewrite on this machine skips the download - and the cache plus the last-model choice are shared with the Private AI Chat page. When you want the space back, press "Clear cached models" and the weights are gone.
