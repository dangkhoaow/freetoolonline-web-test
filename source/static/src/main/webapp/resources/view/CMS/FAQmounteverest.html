Frequently Asked Questions

How tall is Mount Everest?

8,848.86 m above sea level, per the 2020 China-Nepal joint survey - the highest point on Earth.

What is the death zone?

The altitude band above about 8,000 m where air pressure gives only about a third of sea-level oxygen - the human body cannot acclimatize there and most climbers use bottled oxygen.

Who climbed Everest first?

Edmund Hillary of New Zealand and Tenzing Norgay, a Sherpa of Nepal, reached the summit on 1953-05-29 via the southeast ridge.

Where is Everest exactly?

On the border of Nepal and Tibet (China) in the Mahalangur Himal section of the Himalaya - Sagarmatha in Nepali, Chomolungma in Tibetan.

Is Everest still growing?

Yes - the collision of the Indian and Eurasian plates lifts the Himalaya a few millimetres per year, though earthquakes can also change its height slightly.

Is this a terrain simulation?

No. It is an educational explorer using published figures - the ridge shapes and snowline are schematic teaching cues, not survey terrain.
