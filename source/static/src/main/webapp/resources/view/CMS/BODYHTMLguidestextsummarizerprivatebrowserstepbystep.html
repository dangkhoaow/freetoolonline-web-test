Text Summarizer - Private, In Your Browser Step By Step

Last reviewed: 2026-08-09

Text Summarizer - Private, In Your Browser runs in three steps: open the tool, provide your input, then copy the result. This page walks through what each step expects.


Step 1 - open the tool

Go to Text Summarizer - Private, In Your Browser. Nothing needs to be installed or signed in before you start.


Step 2 - provide your input

A sample text and its basic summary render as a demo the moment the page loads, so you can see the output shape before pasting anything.


Step 3 - copy the result

When your browser ships built-in AI the page uses it with zero download; otherwise load a small on-device model (size shown before download, cached for next time) or stay on the instant no-model mode.

The engine-status line always names which mode produced the output - built-in browser AI, an on-device model, or the basic no-model pass.
