Frequently Asked Questions

Can I add video clips, or only images?

Video Maker builds the slideshow from a sequence of still images plus one optional audio track - it does not accept existing video clips as input. Upload the photos in the order you want them to appear, and the tool renders each one for the duration you set. If you already have a video file to work with, use one of the converter tools linked in the last question below instead.

Is there a built-in music library, or do I upload my own track?

You upload your own audio track - Video Maker has no built-in music library. Toggle audio on, add one file, and the tool mixes it in at export time, trimmed to the slideshow's total length (frame count times per-image duration). Pick a track whose length is close to that total so the audio does not cut off mid-note or leave a silent tail.

Can I add text captions or effects to the slideshow?

Video Maker does not overlay text or apply arbitrary visual effects. Each image is shown as-is, and the only motion between frames is the transition you choose - Fade or Zoom. If you want a caption on a slide, add the text to the image itself before uploading (any image editor works), then include that finished image in the sequence.

What settings control how the video looks?

Three settings: per-image duration (1 to 5 seconds each), the transition between frames (Fade or Zoom), and the output resolution (320×240 up to 1680×1050). Higher resolutions look sharper but take longer to encode. Keep your images at a consistent aspect ratio - mixed portrait and widescreen images get letterboxed or cropped to fit the resolution you pick.

What format is the output, and can I post it to social media?

The output is a single MP4 file encoded with H.264, so it plays in every modern browser, phone, and video player without conversion. Video Maker does not post to social platforms directly - you download the MP4, then upload it yourself to Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn, or wherever you need it. Keep the total length under 60 seconds if you are targeting autoplay feeds.

Have an existing video to convert?

Video Maker builds an MP4 from a sequence of still images and optional audio - it is the build path, not the format-switch path. To convert a video file you already have (MP4, WebM, MOV, AVI, MKV) to a different container or extract the audio track to MP3, AAC, OGG, FLAC, M4A, or WAV, use All Video Converter (server-side, supports large clips) or HD Video Converter (runs locally in your browser via FFmpeg.wasm, no upload). For precise filter chains and custom encoder flags, FFmpeg Online accepts the same input set.
