Annuity Calculator Online

Last reviewed: 2026-07-30

Project how a series of regular contributions - an annuity-style payment stream - grows alongside a starting balance over the years you keep paying in.

Enter the starting principal, monthly contribution, annual rate, term, and compounding frequency; the final value, total contributed, and interest earned update as you type.

Use the monthly-contribution field to model a regular annuity payment on top of an initial lump sum, which suits retirement-savings and annuity-planning estimates.

Compare monthly, annual, and daily compounding to see how frequency changes the result over the same term.

All math runs locally in the browser - no upload, no account, and your numbers never leave your device. Results are illustrative estimates based on a constant rate and are not financial advice.

To treat the same projection as plain compound interest, the compound interest calculator runs the same growth formula.
