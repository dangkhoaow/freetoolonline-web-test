Commission Calculator

Last reviewed: 2026-10-04

The commission calculator answers the pay question directly: enter a sales amount and a flat commission rate, and the commission earned is on screen as you type. Switch to the tiered structure to model a quota - everything up to the quota pays a base rate, and only the amount above the quota pays a separate accelerator rate, so the base-rate portion never gets re-rated at the higher percentage. Everything runs in your browser - nothing you enter is stored or sent anywhere, and no account is needed.


Key features

  • Two structures: a flat rate on the whole sale, or a tiered quota plus accelerator rate.
  • The tiered breakdown shows the quota-rate portion and the accelerator-rate portion as separate line items, so the arithmetic is visible, not just a final number.
  • A reference table below the result shows what a flat 3% to 25% rate would pay on the sales amount entered - useful for comparing plans.
  • Gross commission only, before any draw, clawback, cap or tax a real plan might apply.

Flat vs tiered commission

A flat-rate plan pays the same percentage on every dollar sold. A tiered plan pays a base rate up to a quota and a higher accelerator rate only on the amount above it - so hitting quota does not retroactively raise the rate on the dollars already sold below it, it only raises the rate on the dollars above it.

Reading the totals

The math is plain arithmetic on the numbers you type - it is not payroll, tax or financial advice. Draws against future commission, clawbacks on returned sales, plan caps and withholding are not part of the calculation; every number comes from you.
