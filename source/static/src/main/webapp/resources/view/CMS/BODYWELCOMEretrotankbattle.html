Retro Tank Battle

Last reviewed: 2026-07-04

Drive your tank with the arrows or WASD, fire with Space, and clear the wave of 6 enemy tanks before they destroy your base or your 3 lives run out.

Enemy tanks steer themselves toward your base and shoot when they line up with you, so standing still is a losing strategy.

Your best destroyed-tanks count is saved in this browser and shown in the score line - no account, no leaderboard.

Play it fullscreen with one click - the lives and score line stay visible, and Escape returns to the page.

The arena is a 13x13 tile map: brick walls break apart under your shells so you can carve your own firing lanes, while steel blocks stop everything and force you to go around. Touch works too - swipe to steer and tap to fire, or use the on-screen pad that appears automatically on phones and tablets. There is one arena and one wave design with no level select, no sound effects or music, and the enemy tanks run simple random-walk-and-align logic rather than pathfinding - so a repeat run stays fast to load, not more complex.
