When to Use the 2I/Borisov Interstellar Comet 3D Explorer - Best Sessions and Limits

Last reviewed: 2026-07-25

Open the 2I/Borisov Interstellar Comet 3D Explorer when you want to compare a confirmed interstellar visitor's unbound orbit and unusual coma chemistry against a typical bound solar-system comet - not to track 2I/Borisov's current position (it left the solar system years ago).


A composition-and-orbit contrast, not a live tracker

A session starts by toggling to 2I/Borisov to see its hyperbolic eccentricity of about 3.36 and its CO-rich coma (about 35-155% CO/H2O), then toggling to Halley's Comet to see a bound eccentricity of about 0.967 and a typical 4% CO/H2O ratio - while the comparison table stays fixed, ranking both objects side by side.


Sessions this explorer does not fit

Sessions this page does not fit are tracking 2I/Borisov's current position (it passed perihelion in December 2019 and is now leaving the solar system forever) or 1I/'Oumuamua's own shape and tumbling story, which is on the 'Oumuamua 3D Explorer. Walk through the controls in the step-by-step guide.
