Frequently Asked Questions

What does the hourly to salary calculator do?

It converts an hourly rate into weekly, biweekly, monthly and annual gross pay from your hours per week and paid weeks per year - and in the other direction it turns an annual salary into its equivalent hourly rate.

How is the annual salary calculated?

Annual gross pay equals the hourly rate times hours per week times paid weeks per year. The monthly figure is the annual divided by 12, and biweekly is two weekly paychecks. The defaults are 40 hours and 52 weeks; both are editable.

Does it show take-home pay after taxes?

No. Every figure is gross pay before taxes and deductions - the tool makes no tax, deduction or take-home-pay claims, and the result says so directly. It is not tax or financial advice.

Can I use it part-time or with unpaid weeks?

Yes. Hours per week accepts half-hour steps from 1 to 100, and paid weeks per year accepts 1 to 53 - set 20 hours or 48 paid weeks and every line in the breakdown follows.

Does anything I enter leave my browser?

No. The conversion is computed locally in the page - nothing is uploaded, stored or sent, and no account is needed.
