How to use Galactic Year: Sun's Orbit 3D Explorer

  1. Open the Galactic Year: Sun's Orbit 3D Explorer and wait for the canvas to paint.
  2. Drag to orbit the view and scroll to zoom in on the spiral disk and the orbit ring.
  3. Choose Top-down view to see the orbit as a circle, or Edge-on view to see the Sun bob through the disk plane.
  4. Scrub the elapsed-time slider from 0 to 4,600 million years to move the Sun to any point since it formed.
  5. Press Play to animate elapsed time continuously, or Pause to hold a position and read the facts panel.
