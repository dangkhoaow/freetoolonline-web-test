How the neural bots evolve

The opponents here are not scripted. Each bot is driven by a small neural network, and after every wipe the survivors breed the next generation, so the wave you clear teaches the wave that follows. Play a few rounds and the bots begin to dodge, chase, and lead their shots noticeably better than the first generation you met. Because the run is session-only, refreshing the page throws that learning away and drops you back to generation 1 with a fresh, easy field - handy when a lineage has grown too sharp and you want to start the arms race over.

Tips for a longer run

  • Use the borders as a weapon: the edges damage you, but they damage the bots too, so bait a chaser toward a wall and let the arena finish it.
  • Expect each generation to be tougher: open cautiously and keep moving early, because the evolved waves punish standing still far harder than the first one does.
  • Go fullscreen for the aim: once the match is running the Fullscreen button enlarges the arena, which gives the mouse more room to track a fast target.
  • Prefer a desktop mouse: touch play falls back to on-screen control sticks, so a real mouse aims and fires with more precision.
