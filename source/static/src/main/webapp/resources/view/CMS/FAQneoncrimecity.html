Frequently Asked Questions

What is Neon Crime City?

It is a browser-based open-world crime city sandbox where you can drive, roam on foot, and explore a neon downtown without installs or accounts.

Do I need to install anything or sign in?

No. Open the page, press Play, and the game loads in the same browser tab.

Can I play offline after the first load?

Offline play depends on the game host configuration. If the host caches assets locally (packed or cache mode), later sessions can launch with little or no network; otherwise you will need a connection to start.

Is this affiliated with any commercial franchise?

No. This is a community-built browser port and is not affiliated with any commercial franchise or publisher.
