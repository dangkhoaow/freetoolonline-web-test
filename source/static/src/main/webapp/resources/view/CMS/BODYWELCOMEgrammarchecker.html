AI Grammar Checker - Private, In Your Browser

Last reviewed: 2026-08-11

Fix grammar, spelling, and punctuation without sending your text to anyone. The check runs on your own device: Chrome versions with built-in AI correct with zero download, and every other modern browser can load a small open model with one click. Nothing you paste is uploaded anywhere, on any tier.


What you get back

The result is the corrected text plus a change list: every fix is shown as the original wording, the replacement, and a short reason, so you can accept each correction knowing exactly what moved. Your wording is preserved - this page fixes errors, it does not rewrite your tone or style. One click on Copy result puts the corrected version on the clipboard.

Honest limits before you rely on it

The on-device models are small (1-3 billion parameters). They catch common errors - agreement, tense, its versus it's, capitalization, everyday punctuation - but they stay below professional cloud checkers, so treat the output as a strong draft pass, not a final edit. On the model path, input is capped at 8,000 characters and the cap is stated right in the tool. Corrections are validated for English text, and there is no document upload - paste the text itself.


Where your data and the downloads live

Text stays in the page while you work and is not stored beyond the session; there is no account and no signup. The model cache and your last model choice are shared with the Private AI Chat page, so loading a model once serves both pages, and a Clear cached models button removes the downloaded weights whenever you want the disk space back.
