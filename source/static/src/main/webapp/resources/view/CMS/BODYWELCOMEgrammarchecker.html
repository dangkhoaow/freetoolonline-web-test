AI Grammar Checker - Private, In Your Browser

Last reviewed: 2026-08-11

Fix grammar, spelling, and punctuation without sending your text to anyone. The check runs on your own device: Chrome versions with built-in AI correct with zero download, and every other modern browser can load a small open model with one click. Nothing you paste is uploaded anywhere, on any tier.


What you get back

The result is the corrected text plus a change list: every fix is shown as the original wording, the replacement, and a short reason, so you can accept each correction knowing exactly what moved. Your wording is preserved - this page fixes errors, it does not rewrite your tone or style. One click on Copy result puts the corrected version on the clipboard.


Three ways the check can run

For each grammar pass, the tool takes the least costly engine your browser can honestly run and names which one produced the corrected text, so you always know what did the proofreading before you accept a fix.

How far to trust each proofreading pass

Because the proofreading runs on a compact 1 to 3 billion parameter model, it reliably flags the mistakes that trip up most writing - subject-verb agreement, tense slips, its versus it's, stray capitals, and everyday punctuation - yet it does not match a professional cloud editor, so read the change list as a confident draft pass rather than the final word on your text. Each fix keeps your own wording and touches only the error, never your tone or style. The on-device path accepts up to 8,000 characters per check, a limit spelled out right beside the input box; it proofreads English sentences only, and because there is no document import you paste the paragraph straight in rather than uploading a file.


Where your sentences and the proofreading models live

The sentences you are proofreading and the change list of every correction stay in the page for the working session only - nothing you paste is kept once you close the tab, and there is no account or signup to check a paragraph. Because the on-device model weights are cached after the first one-time download, later grammar passes on the same machine reuse those weights and skip the download completely; that cache and your last model choice are also shared with the Private AI Chat page, so loading a proofreading model once readies both pages at no extra cost. When you have finished correcting, a Clear cached models button hands the disk space straight back.
