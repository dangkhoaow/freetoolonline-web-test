Quetzalcoatlus was a giant azhdarchid pterosaur from the Late Cretaceous of North America, about 72-66 million years ago - a flying reptile, not a dinosaur. Lawson named the genus in 1975.

Wingspan and mass figures

Wingspan estimates for Quetzalcoatlus commonly fall near 10-11 m, and the facts panel uses 10.5 m as its wingspan baseline. Mass estimates vary widely, often about 70-250 kg; this page shows 200 kg as a mid-upper published figure.
