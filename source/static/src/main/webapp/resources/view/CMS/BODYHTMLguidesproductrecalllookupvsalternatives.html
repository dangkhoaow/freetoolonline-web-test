CPSC Product Recall Lookup vs News and Store Notices - Compared

Last reviewed: 2026-09-13

The product recall lookup, CPSC's own announcement feed, and news or retailer notices answer three different questions. The lookup searches the official saferproducts.gov recall database by product, brand, or manufacturer; announcements break the news; a store or manufacturer notice reaches you only when the seller knows you bought the item. Here is an honest comparison so you reach for the right one.

When the lookup wins

When you need the details in one place: the hazard, the remedy badges, the affected models and unit counts, reported incidents, where it was sold, and the link to the official cpsc.gov notice - searchable by product type, brand, or manufacturer and sorted newest first. The recalls from the last 60 days render on load, and your query goes only to the official US-government CPSC API - never to this site's servers.


When the alternatives win

For a recall announced this morning, cpsc.gov/Recalls has it first - database records can arrive hours to days later. For an item you bought with a card or an account, the retailer can tell you the one thing no public database can: that your specific purchase is affected - and many large retailers contact registered buyers directly. For vehicles, food, or medicines, use this site's vehicle, food, or drug recall lookups - different agencies, different databases.
