Frequently Asked Questions

How do I draw and download a signature?

Sign inside the box with your mouse, finger, or stylus, then click Download PNG. Use Undo to remove the last stroke or Clear to start over.

Can I get a signature with a transparent background?

Yes - turn on the transparent-background option before downloading. The PNG exports with no white fill behind your strokes, so it can be pasted over a document, letterhead, or slide.

Is my signature uploaded or saved anywhere?

No. Drawing and downloading both happen in your browser - nothing is uploaded, and refreshing or leaving the page clears the canvas.

Does this tool verify whose signature it is?

No - it only draws and exports whatever you sign. It does not check authenticity or match a signature to a real person's handwriting.
