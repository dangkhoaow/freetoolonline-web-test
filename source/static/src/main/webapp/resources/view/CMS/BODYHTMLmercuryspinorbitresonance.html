The two orange dots on Mercury are its "hot pole" markers, 180 degrees apart on the equator. The red dot on the orbit line is perihelion (closest to the Sun); the blue dot is aphelion (farthest).

Mercury rotates once every 58.646 Earth days and orbits the Sun once every 87.969 Earth days - a 3:2 ratio, not the 1:1 tidal lock once assumed before 1965 radar observations proved otherwise.
