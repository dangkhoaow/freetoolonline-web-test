About this scene

The facts panel fills when the scene loads. Scrub the elapsed-time slider or switch view modes.

The Sun carries the Solar System around the Milky Way's center at roughly 220-230 km/s, about 26,000-28,000 light-years out - one full lap, a "galactic year," takes roughly 225-250 million years.

Drag to orbit and scroll or pinch to zoom. Switch between the top-down and edge-on views, press Play or Pause, or drag the elapsed-time slider to jump anywhere across the last 4.6 billion years.
