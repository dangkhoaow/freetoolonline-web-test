Remove Objects from Photos

Last reviewed: 2026-08-13

Brush over the person, sign, watermark, or other distraction you want gone, and the tool fills the spot back in with matching background - a photo eraser that runs entirely on your own device. Your picture is never uploaded to a server; every step happens inside your browser.

The first time you click Remove object (AI), a one-time model download of about 27 MB starts, and your browser caches it afterwards so later edits need no download. If you would rather not download anything, the Quick patch button fills the painted area instantly from the surrounding pixels - lower quality than the AI model, but immediate and still fully local.

Results save as a standard PNG. The AI is strongest on clutter set against sky, walls, ground, water, or other repeating texture; a large subject in front of complex detail is harder, so paint tightly around the object and review the fill before you keep it.

Erasing is not the only way to deal with a person in a photo: when you only need to hide who someone is rather than remove them from the scene, the face blur tool detects faces on-device (70 percent confidence bar; a manual brush covers any it misses) and pixelates them, no upload there either.
