About this scene

The facts panel fills when the scene loads. Use the toggles below the scene.

Published figures: length about 6,400 km; discharge about 209,000 m3/s average (about 1/5 of world river discharge); basin area about 7,050,000 km2; seasonal flood inundation up to 40 m above baseline; discharge plume extends 100+ km into the Atlantic; tropical basin supports about 1 in 10 of all known species.

Drag to orbit the meandering channels from the headwaters down toward the Atlantic mouth, and scroll or pinch to zoom in on the silt plume that pushes past 100 km offshore. This Amazon River scene renders entirely on your device with WebGL - the seasonal flood level, the channel meanders, and the discharge plume are drawn locally, and nothing about your visit is sent to a server.
