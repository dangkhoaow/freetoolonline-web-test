How to Explore the Io Plasma Torus 3D Explorer

Last reviewed: 2026-07-24

Reviewed by FreetoolOnline editorial team

Open the Io Plasma Torus 3D Explorer, cycle through its three real radial layers, and toggle the co-rotating bulge to watch a documented bright sector lap Io's much slower orbit.

  1. Open /space-3d/io-plasma-torus.html and wait for Jupiter, Io, and the torus layers to render.
  2. Click the layer button to cycle through all three layers together, the cold disk alone, the warm ribbon alone, and the extended hot torus alone.
  3. Read the facts panel after each click for that layer's published electron temperature, and, for the warm ribbon, Juno's peak density figure.
  4. Click the bulge button to show the co-rotating bright sector, then watch it sweep past Io's own orbit over a few seconds.
  5. Drag to orbit and scroll or pinch to zoom on the scene for a closer look at the layers and the bulge.
  6. Toggle the bulge off and on to compare its fast 10.07-hour rotation against Io's slow 42.5-hour orbit side by side.
