Frequently Asked Questions

What does Percentage Calculator do?

Work out a percentage of a number, the reverse (what percent one number is of another), or a percent change.

When should I reach for percentage calculator?

Use it for a discount, a tax add-on, a grade, or comparing two values as a percent change.

What complementary tools work well alongside percentage calculator?

Switch calculations at any time; the two number fields carry over so you can compare results.

Do the field labels change depending on which calculation I pick?

Yes. The two input labels switch to match the selected mode: "Percent (%)" and "Of value" for what is X% of Y, "Value" and "Out of (total)" for X is what percent of Y, and "From value" and "To value" for percent change from X to Y.

What happens if I leave a field blank or type something that is not a number?

It is read as 0, not rejected. That is safe for the first mode (what is X% of Y), but the second and third modes catch a zero denominator first and show a message asking for a non-zero value instead of calculating.

How many decimal places does the result show?

Up to 4, formatted with your browser's own thousands separator - fewer digits when the result is a whole number, so the display never shows more precision than the calculation actually has. For example, 1 is what percent of 3 shows 33.3333%, not the full repeating decimal.
