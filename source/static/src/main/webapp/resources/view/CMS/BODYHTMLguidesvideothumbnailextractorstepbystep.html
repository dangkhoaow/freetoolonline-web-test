Extract a Thumbnail from a Video - Step by Step

Last reviewed: 2026-09-17

The video thumbnail extractor grabs a still frame from a video at any timestamp, entirely in your browser - pick the file, scrub to the moment, and download the frame as a PNG or JPEG. The video is never uploaded. Here is each step with what to expect.


Step 1 - open the tool and check the demo frame

Open the extractor page. A sample frame from a small built-in demo clip appears as soon as the page loads, so you can confirm the extractor is working in your browser before touching your own file. Nothing needs to be installed and there is no signup.


Step 2 - pick your video

Use the file picker at the top. MP4 (H.264) and WebM work in mainstream browsers; MOV, M4V and OGV depend on the browser. The file opens directly in the page's own player - it never leaves your device. If the browser cannot decode the codec, the page shows a clear message instead of a silent black image.


Step 3 - scrub to the exact moment

Drag the timestamp slider under the preview. The preview shows the frame you are on, and the frame you see is the frame you save - there is no re-encoding between the preview and the capture.


Step 4 - choose the image format

PNG is the default and is lossless. Switch to JPEG when you want a smaller file, and pick 95, 85 or 70 percent quality. Either way the frame is captured at the video's native resolution.


Step 5 - capture and download

Press "Capture this frame". A thumbnail card appears with the timestamp and pixel size, plus its own download link. Filenames carry the source name and timestamp, e.g. myclip-t2.5s.png, so a folder of grabs stays sortable.


Optional - grab a 6-frame contact sheet

"Grab 6 evenly spaced frames" captures one frame from each sixth of the clip in a single click - a quick overview of the whole video without stepping through it manually. You can take up to 24 thumbnails per session.


What this tool does not do

It only captures still frames: it does not convert, trim or edit the video, and it cannot open DRM-protected streams. For GIF files use the GIF frame extractor.
