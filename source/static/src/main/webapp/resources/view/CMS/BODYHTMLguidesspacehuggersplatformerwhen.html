When to Play Space Huggers

Space Huggers fits when you want a fast, procedurally generated run-and-gun session with no accounts and no saved progress to manage.

Last reviewed: 2026-08-01

When you want a quick arcade run

Space Huggers starts you with 10 lives and adds 3 more per level cleared - a session ends when your lives run out, so it fits a short break as easily as a longer one.

When you want replay value

Levels are procedurally generated and blocks/cover are destructible with spreading fire, so no two runs play out the same way. Pick it when you want a roguelike with no fixed ending.

When you have gamepads for local co-op

Up to 4 players can join with connected gamepads for local co-op. Skip it if you only have a mouse - Space Huggers has no mouse control and no touch controls.

When to pick another game instead

Want a best score or progress saved in this browser? Space Huggers keeps nothing between sessions - try Retro Tank Battle (best destroyed-tanks count saved) or Level 13 Incremental (autosaved progress) instead. Want a short fixed campaign instead of a roguelike? Try Retro Arcade Shooter's 3 levels.
