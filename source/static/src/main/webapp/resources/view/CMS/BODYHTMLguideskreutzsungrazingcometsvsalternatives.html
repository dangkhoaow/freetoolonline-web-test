Where the Comet Orbit Explorer wins

The Comet Orbit Explorer wins when the question is how a normal, periodic comet's orbit and tails work at a moderate, survivable distance from the Sun - it is the reference case every extreme comet is compared against.


Where the Kreutz Explorer wins

The Kreutz Explorer wins when the question is why some comets pass almost at the Sun's surface and mostly do not survive it - it is the only page on the site visualizing the extreme sungrazer population and three real historical comets, including two that briefly outshone the full Moon in daylight.


A reasonable rule

As a reasonable rule, use the Comet Orbit Explorer first to see how a normal comet's orbit and tail work, then use the Kreutz Explorer to see the extreme case where a family of fragments nearly touches the Sun and mostly does not survive.
