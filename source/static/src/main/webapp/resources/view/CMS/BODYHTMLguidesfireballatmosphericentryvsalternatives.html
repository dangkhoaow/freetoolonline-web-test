Where the Fireball / Atmospheric Entry Explorer wins

The Fireball / Atmospheric Entry Explorer wins when the question is "what if" - it is the only page on the site where you choose the diameter, speed, and angle yourself and see the model's outcome for that exact combination, including bodies much smaller or much larger than the Chelyabinsk case.


Where the Chelyabinsk Meteor Explorer wins

The Chelyabinsk Meteor Explorer wins when the question is "what actually happened" - it replays one real, well-documented event on its own timeline with a brightness curve and airburst, which a parametric sandbox does not attempt to do for any single case.


A reasonable rule

As a reasonable rule, use the Fireball / Atmospheric Entry Explorer first to explore how diameter, speed, and angle change the outcome in general, then open the Chelyabinsk Meteor Explorer to see the specific real event that the "Chelyabinsk-class airburst" outcome band is named after.
