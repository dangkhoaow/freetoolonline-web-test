Quantum Optics Puzzle is a free browser photon puzzle with no account or install. Press Play, pick a level, and place optics tiles so photons hit the detectors.

How it works

  • About 1.2 MB loads after you press Play.
  • Level progress stays on your device under ftol:quantumoptics keys, so you return to where you left off.
  • Nothing is uploaded; the puzzle runs entirely in your browser.
