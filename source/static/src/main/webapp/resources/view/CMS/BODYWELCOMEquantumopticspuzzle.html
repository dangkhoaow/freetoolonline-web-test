Quantum Optics Puzzle is a free browser photon puzzle. Press Play, pick a level, and place optics tiles so photons hit the detectors.


Key features

  • No account and no install - the game loads after you press Play.
  • About ~1.2 MB downloads on the first Play.
  • Level progress is saved on this device under ftol:quantumoptics keys.

Privacy

Nothing is uploaded. The game runs entirely in your browser.
