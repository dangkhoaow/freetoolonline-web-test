Frequently Asked Questions

Is Tower Building Game free to play?

Yes, Tower Building Game is completely free to play in your browser. No download, no account, no hidden fees. Just open, play, and try to beat your score.

What devices can I play on?

Tower Building Game works on desktop browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge) and mobile browsers (iOS Safari, Chrome Mobile, Samsung Internet). Click with your mouse on desktop or tap on mobile to drop the swinging block.

How do I control the blocks?

You do not rotate or steer them. A block swings from a hook, and one mouse click on desktop or one tap on mobile releases it. Time the click so the block lands squarely on the top of the tower.

What happens if my tower falls?

The game ends after three missed blocks, and you can start a new round afterwards. There's no penalty - each game is independent.

Can I play offline?

The game loads from the web and requires an internet connection. However, once it's fully loaded, short internet hiccups won't interrupt gameplay. For a fully offline experience, you'd need to save the game to your device, which isn't currently supported.

Is there a time limit?

The game ends when you miss three blocks. Wait for the swing you want before you click, and chain perfect landings to build a bigger bonus.
