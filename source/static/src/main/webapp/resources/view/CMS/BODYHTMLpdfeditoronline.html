To drop a caption or label onto a PDF page, load the file and enter the words you want on it. Set which page those words land on by its number, then give the spot as an X%/Y% pair measured from the top-left corner of that page. Pick a font size and a color, press Add text, and the file you download carries your text at exactly that position.

No account or sign-up is required, and everything runs on your device - run it again as many times as you like to fine-tune the page, position, size, or color.
