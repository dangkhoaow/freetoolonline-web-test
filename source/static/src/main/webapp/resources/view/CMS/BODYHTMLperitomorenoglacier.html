The Perito Moreno Glacier ends in a wall of ice about 60-70 m tall and about 5 km wide, calving straight into Lago Argentino. Toggle the crevasse bands to see the fractured ice near the front, and the ice-dam marker to see where the glacier's snout can touch the far shore.
