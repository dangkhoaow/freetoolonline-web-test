Why the Moon Recedes

Tidal friction arises because Earth's rotation is faster than the Moon's orbit. The tidal bulge on Earth (raised by the Moon's gravity) leads the Moon's position, pulling it forward gravitationally. This accelerates the Moon and transfers energy from Earth's rotation to the Moon's orbit. Over deep time, the Moon spirals outward while Earth's day grows longer.
