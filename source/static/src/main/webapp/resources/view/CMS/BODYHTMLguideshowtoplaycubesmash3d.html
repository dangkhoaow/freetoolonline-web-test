How to Play Cube Smash 3D - Step by Step

The Cube Smash 3D page loads the game in an iframe (about 610 KB once, then cached). Move around the meadow, let the auto-swinging hammer smash the blobs, and pick upgrades as you level. Press Play to start.

Controls: WASD / arrows or drag to move; SHIFT sprint; SPACE or tap jump; hammer swings automatically; P pause, M mute, R restart. Best scores: ftol:cubesmash3d:best.

Step 1 - press Play

Press Play to inject the game iframe (about 610 KB once, mostly the shared 3D engine, then cached). Your character appears on a sunny meadow with the Smash Hammer equipped and three of its eight upgrade pips visible in the top-left weapon badge.

Step 2 - move, sprint, jump

Move with WASD or the arrow keys, hold SHIFT to sprint, and press SPACE to jump. On touch, drag to move and tap to jump. Drag the mouse to orbit the camera and scroll to zoom.

Step 3 - let the hammer work

The Smash Hammer swings automatically at the nearest blob in range - your job is positioning. Walk toward small groups, let the swing land, and back away before more blobs pile on.

Step 4 - gems, coins, and levels

Smashed blobs drop gems that fill the yellow level bar and occasional coins. Each level up pauses the run and offers three of six upgrades: heavier hammer, swift boots, hearty meal, gem magnet, thick skin, or quick swings.

Step 5 - survive the escalation

Bigger dark-green blobs arrive after about the first minute and hit harder, and the spawn pace climbs steadily. When the health bar empties, the run ends; your best score stays on this device under ftol:cubesmash3d:best.
