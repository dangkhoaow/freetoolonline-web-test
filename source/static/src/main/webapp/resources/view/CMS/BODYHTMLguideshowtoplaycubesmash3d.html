Controls: WASD / arrows or drag to move; Space or tap to dash-smash; R restarts. Best scores: ftol:cubesmash3d:best.

Step 1 - press Play

Press Play to inject the game iframe (about 610 KB once, mostly the shared 3D engine, then cached). The arena opens with your glowing orb at the center and three hearts in the corner.

Step 2 - move

Move with WASD or the arrow keys; on touch, drag toward where you want the orb to go. Stay off the rim - cubes spawn there and walk straight at you.

Step 3 - dash to smash

Press Space or tap to dash. Every cube inside the yellow ring is smashed for points. The dash has a roughly one-second cooldown, so time it for clusters instead of single cubes.

Step 4 - chain combos

Smashes within about two seconds of each other build a combo; every five combo hits raises the score multiplier. A cube reaching your orb costs one heart and resets the combo.

Step 5 - waves and bests

Clearing score thresholds advances the wave: cubes spawn faster, your move speed rises, and the dash ring grows a little. Your best score stays on this device under ftol:cubesmash3d:best.
