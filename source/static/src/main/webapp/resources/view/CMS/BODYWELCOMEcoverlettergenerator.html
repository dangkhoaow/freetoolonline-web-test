Cover Letter Generator - Private, CV Never Uploaded

Last reviewed: 2026-08-30

Paste the job description, add a few highlights from your CV, and press Draft cover letter - the tool writes a complete letter naming the role and the company, right in your browser. Unlike cloud cover-letter sites, neither the posting nor your CV material is uploaded: everything stays on your device from paste to copy.

A letter that answers the ad you pasted

A generic cover letter reads like one, and recruiters skim past it. This generator starts from the actual posting: the opening names the role and the company, the body paragraph is built from the highlights you typed, and a keyword pass over the pasted description adds a line naming what the posting emphasizes - so the letter visibly responds to the ad rather than to jobs in general. Three tones change how it reads: professional keeps it formal, enthusiastic warms up the opening and closing, and short-and-direct trims the letter down for roles where brevity wins.

Sample inputs and their finished letter render the moment the page loads, so you can see the output shape before typing anything. Add a hiring contact's name and the salutation switches from "Dear Hiring Manager" to addressing them directly; add your own name and it replaces the sign-off placeholder.

Why private matters for a job application

A cover letter brief is sensitive by nature: it pairs your work history with the fact that you are applying somewhere - sometimes while you still hold another job. Cloud letter builders receive both halves the moment you paste them. Here there is no server involved at all: the job description and your highlights are processed on your device on every engine tier, nothing is stored beyond the page session, and no account or signup is asked for. The tool also does not apply for the job or send anything anywhere - it drafts text that you copy into your own application.

Three ways the letter gets written

With no download at all, a template engine assembles the letter deterministically: salutation, an opening naming the role, one paragraph from your highlights, the posting-emphasis line, and a closing matched to the tone. If your browser ships built-in writing AI, the page uses it with nothing to download. And for more natural wording, you can load a small open model (Llama 3.2 class) into the browser with one click - the download size is shown before it starts, and the files are cached so the next visit loads from disk. The same model cache is shared with the private AI chat and AI email writer pages, so a model downloaded on any of them works on all. The status line under the button always names which mode produced the letter on screen.

Honest expectation: the on-device models are small, 1-3 billion parameters. They produce a solid first draft that uses only the facts you typed in - but read every line before you send it, because small models can still word things loosely. The tool cannot read your CV file or verify your claims; what you type is what it works from. Writing a work email rather than a job application? The AI email writer drafts emails with a subject line from a short brief, with the same privacy. And if you want a final polish pass, the grammar checker reviews the finished text in the same in-browser way.
