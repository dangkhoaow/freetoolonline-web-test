City Time Machine 3d Step By Step

Last reviewed: 2026-07-05

City Time Machine 3D runs in three steps: open the tool, provide your input, then download the result. This page walks through what each step expects.


Step 1 - open the tool

Go to City Time Machine 3D. Nothing needs to be installed or signed in before you start.


Step 2 - provide your input

M3: Each lot keeps its own footprint through time, so the small 1945 corner shop grows into the same corner's 1985 office and 2055 tower.


Step 3 - get the result

M4: The change is animated, not a cut - buildings shrink and regrow one lot after another over about two seconds while the sky, light, and fog crossfade to the era.

M5: It is a stylized, artistic take on generic architectural eras with invented shop names - not a historical record of any real city, and 2055 is openly speculative fiction.
