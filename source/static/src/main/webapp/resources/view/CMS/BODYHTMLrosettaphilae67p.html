About this scene

The facts panel fills when the scene loads. Scrub the progress slider to move through the mission's real timeline.

Published figures (ESA Rosetta mission pages, cross-checked 2026-08-11): Rosetta reached comet 67P/Churyumov-Gerasimenko on 6 August 2014, and Philae made the first-ever landing on a comet nucleus on 12 November 2014 - though the anchoring harpoons failed to fire and the lander bounced before settling at a site later named Abydos. The mission ended on 30 September 2016 with Rosetta's own controlled descent onto the comet's small lobe.

Drag the progress slider (or press Play) to watch orbit insertion, Philae's separation and bounce-and-recover landing, the comet's most active period at perihelion, and Rosetta's final descent, in the real order they happened.
