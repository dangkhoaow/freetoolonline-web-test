GPA Calculator

Last reviewed: 2026-07-26

Work out a weighted grade point average from your course grades and credit hours. Use this when you need a GPA from a list of courses - for straightforward percentage math instead, use the Percentage Calculator.

Add a row for each course, pick a letter grade, and enter the credit hours; the weighted GPA updates as you type. Remove a row with the × button if you added one by mistake.

The grade scale is the standard unweighted 4.0 scale (A = 4.0 down to F = 0.0, with the usual +/- steps) - the same scale most US colleges use for a general GPA estimate.

The calculation runs locally in the browser - no upload and no account. Nothing you type is stored beyond the page session or sent to an analytics or tracking service.

This is a general estimate, not an official transcript calculation - your school's registrar may use a different weighted or honors scale.
