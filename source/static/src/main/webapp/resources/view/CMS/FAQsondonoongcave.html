Frequently Asked Questions

How big is Son Doong Cave?

Son Doong is the world's largest cave by volume, containing about 14.375 million cubic meters of space. The main chamber alone (Doline Hall) reaches 200 meters high, 150 meters wide, and stretches for about 240 meters. It is large enough to fit a 40-story building inside.

Is there water in the cave?

Yes, Rao Thuong River flows through the cave floor year-round. The river is fed by underground springs and by water seeping down through fractures in the limestone from the surface above. The cave maintains constant temperature (about 20-25°C) due to the water's underground origin.

Can you see sunlight inside the cave?

Light enters the cave through several roof collapse holes where the cave ceiling gave way during its formation. These openings allow sunlight to reach parts of the cave floor and walls, creating the conditions for a unique hanging jungle to thrive - an entire ecosystem sustained inside the cave by the sunlight penetrating through the roof.

When was Son Doong discovered?

Local hunters discovered the cave in 1991, but it remained unknown to the wider world until 2009, when an international team of cave explorers mounted the first scientific expedition. Son Doong is so large and remote that its existence was not formally documented in the geological literature until decades after locals first found it.

Where is Son Doong located?

Son Doong Cave is located in Phong Nha-Ke Bang National Park, in Quang Binh Province in central Vietnam. The national park is a UNESCO World Heritage Site known for its spectacular limestone karst landscape and complex cave systems. Son Doong is part of this larger karst region formed over millions of years.
