When to Use Bortle Scale Light Pollution 3D Explorer

Last reviewed: 2026-07-25

Reviewed by FreetoolOnline editorial team

Open the Bortle Scale Light Pollution 3D Explorer when you need to see what a light-pollution class actually hides, or decide whether a planned stargazing site is dark enough for what you want to observe.


A light-pollution lesson

A lesson starts when you drag the class slider from 1 to 9 and watch the star count and Milky Way visibility change, then jump between the excellent-dark-site and city-sky presets to see the two extremes side by side.


Sessions this explorer does not fit

Sessions this page does not fit are learning the magnitude NUMBER SYSTEM itself - use the Stellar Magnitude Explorer for that - or getting a real, repeatable brightness reading for your own backyard - use an actual Sky Quality Meter or a real-time light-pollution map for that.
