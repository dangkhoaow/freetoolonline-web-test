Product Recall Lookup

Last reviewed: 2026-09-13

Wondering whether a stroller, space heater, or toy in your home was recalled? Enter the product type, the brand, or the company that makes it and this page pulls the matching US Consumer Product Safety Commission recall records, newest first, from the official saferproducts.gov database. Each result links to the cpsc.gov notice and names the hazard, the remedy, the affected models and unit counts, and where the product was sold.

Before you type anything, the page already lists the recalls announced in the last 60 days, so you can see the shape of a real result and the fields each card carries.


Key features

  • One keyword search covers recall titles first and automatically retries by manufacturer name when the title search comes up empty - so a product type like "stroller", a brand, and a firm name all find their reports.
  • An optional hazard keyword (like "choking" or "fire") narrows results to recalls whose hazard field matches.
  • Each recall card shows the official title linked to the cpsc.gov notice, the announcement date and recall number, the manufacturer and country of manufacture, remedy badges (refund, repair, or replace), the product models and unit counts, the hazard, reported incidents, what to do, and where it was sold.
  • A no-match search states plainly that no recall matches the query, echoes the query back, suggests a shorter or more generic keyword, and links to cpsc.gov/Recalls to confirm - never a blank panel.
  • Long result sets stay readable: the page shows the newest 25 recalls with a count line naming how many matched in total.
  • Results come back newest first from the CPSC database.

Privacy

The product you look up never touches this site's servers: the query goes straight to the US-government CPSC endpoint at saferproducts.gov and is not logged here. Nothing is uploaded and no processing runs on this site, and because the CPSC API is keyless and free, neither this page nor the API asks you to sign in.

Remedies and coverage

The built-in remedy table explains what a refund, repair, or replacement means for you, plus a numbered what-to-do list for a recalled product at home - starting with stop using it immediately, especially anything for babies or children, and noting that the remedy is provided free of charge by the firm. This page covers US consumer products only - toys, nursery gear, appliances, furniture, electronics, and similar goods. Vehicles are NHTSA territory (use the vehicle recall lookup), food and medicines are FDA systems (use the food or drug recall lookup), and recalls in other countries are not queried. New recalls can take hours to days to appear in the database, so this is not a live-alert feed - always follow the remedy instructions on the official CPSC notice.
