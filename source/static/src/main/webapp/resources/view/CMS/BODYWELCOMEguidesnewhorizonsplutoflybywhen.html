Companion guide for the New Horizons Pluto Flyby 3D Explorer. This page sits alongside the step-by-step walkthrough and the comparison against alternatives, so you can move between timing guidance, control instructions, and the numbers table without leaving the guide set.

The explorer itself runs entirely client-side in the browser with the vendored three.js engine - no account, no install, and no scene data leaves your device. For the wider picture, the Space 3D collection also has a Pluto-Charon Double Planet explorer and a Kuiper Belt and Oort Cloud explorer.
