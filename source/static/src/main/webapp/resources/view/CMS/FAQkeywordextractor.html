Frequently Asked Questions

Does my text get uploaded anywhere?

No. Both modes run entirely in your browser. The only network download is the optional AI model itself (about 24 MB, fetched once from a public model host and cached); your text is never sent with it.

What is the difference between the instant mode and the AI mode?

The instant mode counts phrase frequency - fast, deterministic, and download-free. The AI mode embeds your text and every candidate phrase with a small on-device language model and ranks phrases by meaning, so an important phrase that appears only once can still rank near the top.

Can it tell me search volume or how hard a keyword is to rank for?

No. The relevance score measures how close a phrase sits to the meaning of the text you pasted. The tool has no web or search data, so it cannot report search volume, competition, or ranking difficulty.

Does it work in languages other than English?

English gives the highest-quality AI ranking. Other languages usually produce usable lists but with lower quality. The instant frequency mode is language-neutral apart from its English stopword list.

How long can the text be?

Long documents are processed in chunks on your device, so a full article is fine - it just takes longer in AI mode. Very short snippets under about 30 words rank poorly because the model has too little context.

Does the AI mode need a specific browser or graphics card?

No specific hardware is required. The AI mode uses your device's graphics chip through the browser when that path is available, which runs faster; when it is not available, or if that path fails to start, the tool automatically switches to a slower browser-only method instead. Either way it keeps working without any setting for you to change.

What happens if the AI model fails to download or load?

The tool automatically falls back to the instant frequency mode and shows a short message explaining why, so a blocked download or an unsupported browser never leaves you with no results at all.
