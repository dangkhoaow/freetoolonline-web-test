Frequently Asked Questions

How many lakes are in Plitvice Lakes National Park?

Sixteen named lakes, split into twelve larger Upper Lakes and four smaller Lower Lakes.

How much does the water drop from the highest lake to the lowest?

About 133 m, from Prosacansko Lake at the top down to Novakovica Brod at the bottom.

What makes the stepped terraces?

Travertine (tufa) dams - calcium carbonate that precipitates onto moss, algae, and fallen wood, building barriers at roughly 1 to 3 cm a year. Old barriers can collapse and reroute the water, so the terraces keep changing.

Which is the tallest waterfall at Plitvice?

Veliki Slap (Great Falls), at 78 m - the tallest waterfall in the park and in Croatia, near the Lower Lakes. Galovacki buk, at 25 m, is the second-tallest, in the Upper Lakes.

Which lake is the largest?

Kozjak Lake - the largest and deepest lake in the park, and the boundary between the Upper and Lower groups. It is wide enough that visitors cross it by the park's own ferry.

Is this a hydrological simulation?

No. It is an educational explorer using published park and UNESCO figures - the terrace count, widths, and drop sizes are schematic teaching cues.
