Sahara Desert 3D Explorer

The Sahara Desert 3D Explorer renders Earth's largest hot desert in your browser. The Sahara spans approximately 9 million square kilometers across 11 countries in North Africa - from Morocco and Western Sahara in the west to Egypt and Sudan in the east.

Landscape and climate

  • Sand dunes, called ergs, cover about 25-30% of the Sahara; the rest consists of rocky plateaus, salt flats, and gravel plains.
  • The vast dune seas are characterized by towering sand formations that commonly reach 100-200 meters in height, with the tallest recorded dunes reaching approximately 600 meters.
  • Daytime temperatures typically range from 40-50°C during summer months, but plummet to near freezing at night during winter - the extreme daily temperature swing is one of the Sahara's defining environmental features.

A crossroads of civilization

The Sahara has been a crossroads of human civilization for thousands of years. Historic trans-Saharan caravan routes connected sub-Saharan Africa with the Mediterranean basin, carrying trade in salt, gold, and other goods. Today, sparse nomadic and settled populations inhabit oases and margin regions, adapted to one of Earth's most extreme environments.
