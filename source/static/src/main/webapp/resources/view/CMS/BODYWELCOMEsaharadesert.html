Sahara Desert 3D Explorer

Last reviewed: 2026-07-28

Reviewed by FreetoolOnline editorial team

This browser explorer shows the Sahara as a rolling relief: wind-formed dune ridges rippling in long bands over a rocky hamada plain, with a low sun that can swing from a scorching day to a near-freezing desert night.

The Sahara spans about 9.2 million square kilometers of North Africa - roughly the size of the United States - stretching about 4,800 km east to west and 1,800 km north to south across 11 countries. Dune seas called ergs cover only about a quarter of it; the rest is rocky hamada plateau and gravel reg plains. Its highest point, Emi Koussi in Chad, is a shield volcano rising to about 3,415 m. Most of the interior gets less than 25 mm of rain a year, yet daytime highs can pass 50C while winter nights can fall below freezing.

  • Toggle Day/Night to see the extreme desert temperature swing
  • Toggle dune ridge accents to see the wind-formed erg bands
  • Read area, length, elevation, and temperature figures in the panel
  • See the dune sea (erg) rolling toward the rocky hamada plain
  • Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom
