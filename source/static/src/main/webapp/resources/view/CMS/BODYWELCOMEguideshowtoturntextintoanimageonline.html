Fine-tuning the shadow, padding, and export

When a drop shadow looks muddy, the fix is usually the blur and offset values rather than the color: a small offset of 2 to 4 pixels with a blur near 4 keeps the edge crisp, while pushing the offset past 8 pixels or the blur into the double digits softens the text into a halo. Give the shadow somewhere to land by raising the padding a few pixels, since a tight padding value clips both the shadow and the descenders on letters like g, y, and p at the bottom edge of the image. If you plan to drop the result onto a colored banner or another photo, leave the background color empty so the PNG exports with transparency instead of a solid rectangle behind the text. Remember that the download is a flat raster PNG, so once it is on your disk you cannot click back into a word to retype it - to change the wording, reopen the text box, edit the line, and use Download again to render a fresh image. For a quick paste into a chat or a document, the Copy button places the same image on your clipboard so you skip the saved file entirely.
