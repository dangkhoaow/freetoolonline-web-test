Passport Photo Maker: When To Use It, Steps, Alternatives

Last reviewed: 2026-08-10

Open Passport Photo Maker when you need one passport or visa photo quickly, with nothing to install and no account. It composites your portrait onto a plain white background at the preset's exact pixel size, computed at 300 DPI for printing.


When it fits

It fits a quick, one-off run: one portrait, one size, one download. Presets cover US, India, Schengen/EU, UK, Australia, Canada and China visa sizes, and "Custom size (mm)" takes any width and height in millimeters when your destination is not on that list. Check "Also generate a 4x6in print sheet" to also download a tiled sheet with as many copies as fit on one page.


The three steps

  1. Open the tool. Nothing needs to be installed or signed in before you start.
  2. Choose a portrait photo and pick your size preset, then drag the photo to position the face inside the frame and use the Zoom slider to fill it.
  3. Click Generate photo and save the result from the Download links. Everything runs locally via the Canvas API, so the photo is never uploaded to a server.

How it compares with a desktop app or an upload-based service

This is one of three common ways to do the job: a browser-based tool like this one, a desktop application, or an upload-based online service. Each trades off differently on where your data goes and what you need installed.

What it does not do

This crops and resizes to the right dimensions on a white background - it does not remove or replace an existing background, auto-detect or center the face, or verify compliance with a specific passport office's full requirements (lighting, expression, head coverings); check your destination's official rules before submitting.
