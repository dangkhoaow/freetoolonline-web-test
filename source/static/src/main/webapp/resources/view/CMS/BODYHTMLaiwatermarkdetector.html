What a clean result does and does not mean. Screenshots, re-saves, and most messaging apps strip C2PA and metadata marks, and Google's SynthID watermark lives invisibly in the pixels where only Google's own checker can read it. So "no marks found" never proves an image is human-made - it only means none of the removable, readable marks survived. A found mark, on the other hand, is strong evidence: this page shows you the exact bytes-level label the generator wrote. For full cryptographic validation of Content Credentials, use the official Content Credentials Verify site.
