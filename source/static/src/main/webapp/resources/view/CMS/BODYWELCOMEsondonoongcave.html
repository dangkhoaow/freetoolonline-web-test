Son Doong Cave 3D Explorer

Son Doong is the world's largest cave by volume, stretching about 5.5 kilometers through the limestone mountains of Quang Binh Province, Vietnam. The main chamber, Doline Hall, soars 200 meters high and spans 150 meters wide - large enough to fit a 40-story building inside. Rao Thuong River flows through the cave floor year-round, fed by underground springs and surface water seeping through the rock.

Unlike most caves, Son Doong harbors a complete ecosystem suspended inside - hanging jungle vines and trees thrive where sunlight enters through roof collapse holes. The cave maintains a constant temperature of about 20-25 degrees Celsius regardless of the season outside, making it a climate-stable refuge for its unique flora and fauna.
