Tennis Grand Slam Prize Money Lookup When To Use

Last reviewed: 2026-08-07

Tennis Grand Slam Prize Money Lookup is worth opening when you want the official singles prize money of a Grand Slam by round, without digging through press releases. This page covers when that fits and where the dataset stops.


When it fits

Use it to answer questions like how much a first-round loser at Wimbledon takes home, how the US Open champion payout compares with the Australian Open, or what a tournament's total pool is. The cross-majors comparison table lines up the champion payout, first-round payout, and total pool of all four majors side by side.


What to expect

Each tournament's figures are shown in its own official payout currency (AUD, EUR, GBP, USD) exactly as published - the page never converts between currencies. The dataset covers the singles main draws of the 2025 editions; doubles, qualifying, and wheelchair draws are not included, and there is no live feed - figures refresh with an annual dataset update.

Everything renders locally in the browser from the embedded dataset - no upload, no account, and no network request; the Copy Link button shares a specific tournament's table via the URL.
