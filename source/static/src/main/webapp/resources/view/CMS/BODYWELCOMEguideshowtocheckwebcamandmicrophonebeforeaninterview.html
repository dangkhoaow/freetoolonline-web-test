A closer look: the 5-minute pre-interview check, step by step

Work through the four checks in order, because each one rules out a different failure before the call connects. Open the camera test first and use the front/back switch to confirm the lens you plan to use is not hidden behind a privacy shutter, then read the resolution readout - anything below 720p tends to look soft once the meeting app crops the frame to 16:9. Next open the microphone test and watch the live waveform while you speak at a normal interview distance of about 40 to 60 cm; if the trace barely moves your input level is too low, and if it clips flat across the top you are sitting too close. Turn on speaker loopback on that same page to catch the echo that headset-less setups create, since the interviewer hears it long before you do. For lighting, face a window rather than sitting with it behind you, and save the network-lag check for last - a quick speed test or a trial call to a friend on the exact platform you will use surfaces the stutter that a bandwidth number alone hides. None of these steps records or uploads anything; the audio and video stay inside the browser tab.
