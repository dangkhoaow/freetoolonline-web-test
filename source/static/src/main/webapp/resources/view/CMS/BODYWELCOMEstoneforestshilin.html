Stone Forest (Shilin) 3D Explorer

Last reviewed: 2026-07-28

Reviewed by FreetoolOnline editorial team

This browser explorer shows the Shilin Stone Forest in cross-section: a dense field of gray-tan limestone pinnacles rising from a plateau grassland, with a marker at the tallest pinnacle in the scene, named after the Yi ethnic folk heroine Ashima.

Shilin Stone Forest sits in Shilin Yi Autonomous County, Yunnan province, China, about 120 km southeast of Kunming. The limestone was laid down as marine sediment roughly 270 million years ago during the Permian period, when the region lay under a shallow sea; the pinnacle landform seen today was then carved by water infiltrating and dissolving the rock along cracks over roughly the last 2.7 million years, leaving hundreds of narrow towers standing apart on the plateau. The site is part of the South China Karst UNESCO World Heritage Site, inscribed in 2007. A famous named formation, Ashima Rock, is tied to a well-known Yi folk legend about a young woman turned to stone.

  • Toggle the pinnacle height highlight to see the tallest towers pick out from the shorter field
  • Toggle the Ashima marker to see the tallest pinnacle in the scene
  • Switch the View button to the real karst plateau around the field, built from measured elevation data and satellite imagery, once it finishes loading
  • Read the scenic area, rock age, and pinnacle height figures in the panel
  • Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom
  • Runs fully in the browser with the vendored three.js engine - no account, no upload

The Surface view streams in real measured elevation (Mapzen/AWS Open Data terrain tiles, derived from NASA/USGS DEMs) and satellite imagery (contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data) of the plateau surrounding the scenic area. The individual limestone pinnacles are far smaller than that data can resolve, so the close-up pinnacle field with the Ashima marker stays the schematic teaching view.
