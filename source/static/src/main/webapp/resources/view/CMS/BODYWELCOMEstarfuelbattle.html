Welcome to Star Fuel Battle - a free browser spaceship dogfight with no account or install. Press Play to load about 0.67 MB of canvas art and audio, then click Start game inside the frame.

How to play

  • Fly with WASD and fire with Space.
  • Keep fuel above zero by grabbing falling bottles - you start at 15, each bottle adds 15, and the tank caps at 30.
  • Hit enemies for five points and planets for ten after two hits; avoid friends, because each one costs ten score.
  • Press P to pause and M to mute when you need a break.

After a run you can enter a name for on-device ranking that stays in this browser only - nothing is uploaded. For more free browser games, open the games hub. Related picks: Andromeda Star Shooter and Nova Star Barrage.
