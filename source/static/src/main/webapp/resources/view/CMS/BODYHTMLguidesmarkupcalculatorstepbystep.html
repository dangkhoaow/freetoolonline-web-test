How to Use the Markup Calculator - Step by Step

Last reviewed: 2026-10-04

The markup calculator turns a cost and a markup percent into a selling price and profit, live as you type - or solves the reverse from a margin percent or a price you already know. These are the exact inputs and how to read the result.


Step 1 - open the tool

Open the calculator and a full result is already on screen: the form loads pre-filled with a cost of 50 and a markup of 40%, so the selling price (70), the profit (20), and both percentages - markup 40.00%, margin 28.57% - are visible before you type anything.


Step 2 - pick what to solve for

The first select offers three questions: selling price from cost and markup % (the default), selling price from cost and margin %, or the markup % and margin % from a cost and a price you already know. Switching modes relabels the percent field and shows or hides the price field, so the form only ever asks for the two numbers it needs.


Step 3 - enter your cost

The cost field stays visible in every mode - it is what you paid, or what the item costs you to make or stock.


Step 4 - enter the percent, or the price

In the two price-solving modes, type the markup % or margin % you want - whichever the label currently reads. In the third mode, type the price you already charge instead; the percent fields hide and a price field appears. Every change recalculates immediately, and a missing or zero cost shows a plain prompt instead of a broken result.


Step 5 - read the result

The answer line states the selling price and the profit amount, followed by the markup % and the margin % together with a one-line reminder that the two differ - a 100% markup is only a 50% margin, never a 100% margin, except at 0%.


Step 6 - check the reference table

Below the result, a table lists what margin % each of 10/20/25/30/40/50/75/100% markup produces, independent of the cost entered above - useful for sanity-checking a number without retyping your own figures. Nothing you type is stored or sent anywhere, so note the result before closing the tab.
