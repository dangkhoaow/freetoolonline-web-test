Pamukkale's calcium-rich hot springs have built white travertine terraces down a Turkish hillside for thousands of years. Toggle the pool water to see the shallow basins that pool at the front of each terrace, and the Hierapolis ruins marker to see where the ancient Greco-Roman city stood on the plateau above.
