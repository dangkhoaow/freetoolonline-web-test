Body Fat Percentage Calculator Online Vs Alternatives

Last reviewed: 2026-08-01

Body Fat Percentage Calculator Online is one of four common ways to estimate body fat: a browser-based tape-measure calculator like this one, bioelectrical-impedance (BIA) scales, skinfold calipers, or a clinical DEXA scan. Each trades off differently on accuracy, cost, and what you need on hand.
