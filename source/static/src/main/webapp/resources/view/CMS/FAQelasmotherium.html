Frequently Asked Questions

Was Elasmotherium a dinosaur?

No. Elasmotherium was a giant rhinoceros, a mammal, that lived tens of millions of years after the dinosaurs went extinct. This viewer includes it because it is one of the most-searched prehistoric animals.

Why is it called the "Siberian unicorn"?

Its skull had a single very large boss on the nasal bones, larger than on any other known rhinoceros. Many reconstructions show it topped with one big horn, but no horn material has ever been found fossilized, so the exact size and shape of any horn is inferred, not directly observed.

How big was Elasmotherium, and when did it live?

Published length estimates put it at about 4.5 m with a shoulder height over 2 m, and mass estimates range from about 3.5 to 5 metric tons depending on the study. It lived from roughly 2.6 million years ago until about 39,000 years ago, based on direct radiocarbon dating published in 2018 - far more recent than once thought.

Is the model scientifically accurate?

The body proportions follow the fossil skeleton, but the skin color and texture are an artistic reconstruction. Fossils preserve bone, not color or soft tissue. The figures shown in the panel are real published values.
