Tham khao nhanh cho doc gia toi day tu mot tim kiem ve "dinh dang JSON truc tuyen vs cac lua chon khac". Huong dan nay di kem voi cong cu tai Parser va Dinh Dang JSON.


Tai sao dieu nay quan trong

Tai sao dieu nay quan trong voi JSON Parser and Formatter: nhieu duong dan den JSON da dinh dang - editor desktop nhu VS Code, jq tren command line, JSON.parse trong console trinh duyet, hoac cong cu in-browser. Huong dan nay can nhac moi duong theo kich thuoc paste, nhu cau rieng tu, va tan suat ban format.


Khi nao jq phu hop voi pipeline shell

Dong lenh jq la tuyen duong dung khi buoc dinh dang la mot giai doan trong mot pipeline shell dai hon. Ban doc mot tep JSON tu dia, dua no cho jq de thut le hoac mot bieu thuc loc, va dan dau ra sach den lenh tiep theo.


Cach cong cu phu hop

Cach cong cu phu hop: voi cac danh doi o tren, mo JSON Parser and Formatter va thu duong in-browser. Giu payload khoi server, render cay mo rong, va van muot voi kich thuoc paste ma editor desktop hay jq CLI cung xu ly tot.

De quyet dinh tinh huong nao cu the can su dung cong cu dinh dang thay vi cac phuong an khac, JSON formatter - khi nao dung huong dan ban qua quyet dinh do.
