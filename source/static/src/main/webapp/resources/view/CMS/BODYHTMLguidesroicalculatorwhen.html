When to Use the ROI Calculator Online

Last reviewed: 2026-07-30

The ROI Calculator is a quick, in-browser tool for any situation where you know the cost and the return. Below are the main scenarios where it saves time.

Evaluating an investment after the fact

You bought shares at $3,000 and sold at $4,500. Enter $3,000 as the initial investment and $4,500 as the final value. The tool shows a 50% ROI and $1,500 net profit in under a second - no spreadsheet needed.

Comparing investments held over different periods

A two-year investment returning 60% sounds better than a one-year investment returning 40% - but the annualized rates are 26.5% and 40% respectively. Enter the holding period and the tool shows the annualized ROI so you compare on equal terms.

Measuring marketing or campaign spend

Enter your campaign budget as the initial investment and the attributed revenue as the final value. You get the ROI percentage instantly, which is the standard way performance marketing reports justify spend to stakeholders.

When it does not fit

If your investment has multiple cash flows over time (regular contributions, dividends, or partial withdrawals), you need an IRR or NPV calculation rather than a single-entry ROI. This tool handles one initial cost and one final value only.
