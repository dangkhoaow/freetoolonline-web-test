Wadi Rum 3D Explorer

Last reviewed: 2026-08-27

Reviewed by FreetoolOnline editorial team

This browser explorer shows Wadi Rum as a field of rugged, flat-topped sandstone jebels rising sheer from the red desert floor of southern Jordan - the landscape known locally as the Valley of the Moon. A slim natural rock bridge stands in the corridor between the two largest massifs, one of several such arches scattered through the valley.

Wadi Rum sits inside the Wadi Rum Protected Area, a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2011 recognized for both its desert scenery and thousands of years of rock art and inscriptions left by passing cultures. The mesas are built from Rum sandstone laid down more than 500 million years ago over an older granite basement, and centuries of wind, heat, and rare flash floods have carved their sheer cliff faces. Exposed rock surfaces slowly darken with desert varnish - a thin coating of manganese and iron oxides that builds up where the same face has faced the sun undisturbed for a very long time.

  • Toggle desert-varnish color bands to see the dark streaking that collects near exposed cliff tops and rims
  • Toggle landmark markers at Jebel Rum's summit and the rock bridge
  • Read peak elevation, cliff height, and rock-bridge figures in the panel
  • See the flat-topped mesa shape carved by wind and rare flash floods
  • Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom
