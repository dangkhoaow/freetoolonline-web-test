Video Thumbnail Extractor - Grab Video Frames in Your Browser

Last reviewed: 2026-09-23
freetoolonline.com editorial team

This tool grabs still frames from a video file at any timestamp, entirely in your browser. Pick a video, scrub to the exact moment, and save that frame as a lossless PNG or a JPEG at 95, 85 or 70 percent quality - captured at the video's native resolution.

What you can do

  • Scrub to any moment and save the exact frame you see in the preview.
  • Save a single frame as a lossless PNG, or as JPEG at 95, 85 or 70 percent quality, at the video's native resolution.
  • Grab 6 evenly spaced frames in one click for a quick contact sheet of the whole clip, without stepping through it manually.
  • Every thumbnail card shows its timestamp and pixel size and has its own download link; filenames carry the source name and timestamp, e.g. myclip-t2.5s.png.
  • A sample frame from a built-in demo clip appears as soon as the page loads, so you can see the extractor working before you pick a file.

When to use it

Reach for it when you need a poster frame, a sharp still for an article or a slide, or a reference shot pulled from a screen recording.

Your video stays on your device

The file opens directly in your browser's own player and each frame is drawn to a canvas on the page, so nothing is uploaded and there is no server. Everything you capture stays with you for the length of the session.

Format support follows your browser

When a codec cannot be decoded the page shows a clear message instead of a silent black image, so you always know whether the file played.
