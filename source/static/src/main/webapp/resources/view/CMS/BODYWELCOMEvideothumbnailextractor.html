Video Thumbnail Extractor - Grab Video Frames in Your Browser

Last reviewed: 2026-09-17

This tool grabs still frames from a video file at any timestamp, entirely in your browser. Pick a video, scrub to the exact moment, and save that frame as a lossless PNG or a JPEG at 95, 85 or 70 percent quality - captured at the video's native resolution.

Use it for a poster frame, a sharp still for an article or slide, or a reference shot from a screen recording. The one-click option grabs 6 evenly spaced frames - a compact contact sheet of the whole clip without stepping through it manually.

The video never leaves your device: the file opens directly in your browser's own player and each frame is drawn to a canvas on the page. Every thumbnail card shows its timestamp and pixel size with its own download link; filenames carry the source name and timestamp, e.g. myclip-t2.5s.png. A sample frame from a built-in demo clip appears on load, proving the extractor works before you pick a file.

Format support follows your browser: MP4 (H.264) and WebM work in mainstream browsers, while MOV, M4V and OGV depend on the browser. When a codec cannot be decoded, the page shows a clear message instead of a silent black image.
