Frequently Asked Questions

How wide and how tall is Victoria Falls?

About 1,708 m wide and about 108 m tall - one of the widest continuous curtains of falling water in the world, and roughly twice the height of Niagara Falls.

Why is it called Mosi-oa-Tunya, "The Smoke That Thunders"?

The mist plume thrown up by the falls can be seen rising like smoke from up to about 30 km away at peak flood, usually around April, and the roar of the water carries a similar distance.

Which countries border Victoria Falls?

Zambia on the north/east side and Zimbabwe on the south/west side; the Zambezi River forms the border at this point.

What is the Batoka Gorge?

The narrow, zigzagging basalt canyon the Zambezi River flows into just downstream of the falls, formed as the river cut backward through cracks in the basalt over geologic time.

Does the flow change with the season?

Yes, dramatically. Peak flood flow (around April) can exceed about 500 million litres per minute, while in the dry season (roughly September to December) some channels on the basalt lip nearly stop flowing.

Is this a scientific terrain model?

No. It is an educational explorer using published figures - the plateau relief, gorge zigzag, and curtain sections are schematic teaching cues, not survey data.
