About this scene

Published figures: Serengeti National Park covers about 14,750 sq km; the greater Serengeti ecosystem spans roughly 30,000 sq km across Tanzania and Kenya; the Great Migration moves about 1.5 million wildebeest plus hundreds of thousands of zebra and gazelle each year.
