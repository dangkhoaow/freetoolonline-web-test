Iso City Sandbox

Last reviewed: 2026-09-13
freetoolonline.com Editorial Team

Iso City Sandbox is a free browser isometric tile sandbox with no account or install. Pick landscape tiles from the Kenney palette and paint a 7x7 diamond city of roads, water, and buildings.


Key features

  • There is no budget meter and no simulation - the map itself is the point, so you build for the look rather than to hit a goal.
  • About 480 KB the first time you press Play, then it loads from your browser cache.

Save and share your city

The iframe URL hash encodes your layout, so you can bookmark or share a build. Nothing is written to localStorage; clearing the hash or refreshing resets to grass.
