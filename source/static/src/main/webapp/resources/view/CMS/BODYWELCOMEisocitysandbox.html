Iso City Sandbox

Last reviewed: 2026-09-13
freetoolonline.com Editorial Team

Iso City Sandbox is a free browser isometric tile sandbox with no account and no install. Pick a landscape tile from the side palette, then click or drag on the 7x7 diamond grid to place roads, water, and buildings at your own pace.

Good to know:

  • There is no budget meter and no simulation running underneath - the map itself is the point, so you build for the look rather than to hit a goal.
  • Right-click clears a cell, and the game plays with a mouse or touch; it reads best on a wide screen so the tile palette stays visible.
  • Your layout is encoded in the iframe URL hash, so you can bookmark a build or paste the link to share the exact city you made.
  • Nothing is written to localStorage - clearing the hash or refreshing the page resets the board to plain grass.
  • The tiles come from Kenney's CC0 art set, and the whole toy is about 480 KB the first time you press Play, then it loads from your browser cache.
