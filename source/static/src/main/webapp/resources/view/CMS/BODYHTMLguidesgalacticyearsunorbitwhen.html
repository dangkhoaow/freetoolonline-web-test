When to use Galactic Year: Sun's Orbit 3D Explorer

Use this explorer when you want to connect a historical or deep-time date to how far around the galaxy the Solar System has traveled since then.

  • Teaching how orbital speed, radius, and period relate to each other using the Sun's own galactic orbit
  • Explaining why "the Sun has completed about 20 laps of the galaxy" is a real, citable way to talk about deep time
  • Showing why the Sun's path is not a flat circle - published studies find it also bobs up and down through the galactic disk plane

This is an educational approximation, not a dynamical simulation.
