About this scene

The facts panel fills in when the scene loads. Pick a planet, then click a layer in its cutaway to read what it is made of and whether the boundary is a direct measurement or a modeled estimate.

Every planet is drawn on one shared size scale (Jupiter as the largest reference), so the true size difference between a rocky world's iron core and a gas giant's fuzzy core is visible at a glance. Layers marked MEASURED come from an instrument that directly probed the interior - InSight's seismometer on Mars, decades of seismographs on Earth. Layers marked MODELED ESTIMATE come from gravity-field data (Juno at Jupiter, Cassini's Grand Finale at Saturn) or mass/moment-of-inertia calculations, not a direct probe.
