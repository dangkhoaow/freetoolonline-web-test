Namib Desert 3D Explorer

Last reviewed: 2026-08-28

Reviewed by FreetoolOnline editorial team

This browser explorer shows the Namib Desert as a coastal dune field along Namibia's Atlantic edge: a flat, fog-fed gravel plain near the coast rising into linear dune ridges and two taller star-dune peaks further inland. Toggle the Atlantic fog bank to see the marine fog that rolls in most mornings, and the welwitschia plant markers scattered where that fog moisture reaches the desert floor.

The Namib is widely described as the oldest desert in the world, with arid to semi-arid conditions persisting for at least 55 to 80 million years. It stretches roughly 2,000 km along Namibia's Atlantic coast (reaching into southern Angola and northwestern South Africa) and is 50 to 160 km wide. Dune sand is coated in iron oxide, so younger dunes near the coast read pale apricot while older dunes further inland read deep burnt orange to red as the coating oxidizes further over time. The cold offshore Benguela current drives a persistent Atlantic fog belt that rolls inland most mornings - the desert's main source of moisture, and the reason the fog-fed northern strip is known as the Skeleton Coast, named for the shipwrecks and whale and seal bones exposed on its beaches. That same fog sustains welwitschia mirabilis, a slow-growing gravel-plain plant found nowhere else, with some individuals estimated to be over a thousand years old.

The whole scene - the coastal dune ridges, the sand-age color gradient, the fog-bank toggle, and the welwitschia markers - is built and drawn on your own device with WebGL through the site-vendored three.js engine. Orbit the scene to trace how the dunes grow taller and redder away from the coast, and toggle the fog bank to see how it cools the coastal band. Nothing about your visit is uploaded to a server, and the engine loads once (about 0.7 MB) and is then cached.

This is an educational approximation - the dune shapes, color gradient, fog bank, and plant markers are schematic teaching cues, not survey data.
