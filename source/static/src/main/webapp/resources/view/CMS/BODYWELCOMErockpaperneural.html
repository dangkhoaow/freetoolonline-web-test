Rock Paper Neural pits you against a learning opponent right in your browser - free, with no account and no install. The longer you play, the more the net learns your habits and starts countering them.


Key features

  • A quiet break against a learning opponent - nothing to sign up for, and only about 20 KB loads after you press Play.
  • The net predicts your next pick from your recent history, so you notice your own move habits the moment it starts countering you.
  • Session-only by design: scores and training stay in this tab and clear on refresh, with nothing written to localStorage.
  • Adapted from Victor Ribeiro's jokenpo under the MIT license.

When to play

Reach for it as a quick mental break, to spot the patterns in how you throw Rock, Paper, or Scissors, or to compare tiny client-side AI toys on this site.


Privacy

Everything runs in your browser tab. Your scores and the model's training stay on this page only until you refresh, and nothing leaves your device.
