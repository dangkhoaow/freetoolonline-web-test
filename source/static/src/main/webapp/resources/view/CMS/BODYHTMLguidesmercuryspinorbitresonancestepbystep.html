How to Explore the Mercury Spin-Orbit Resonance 3D Explorer

Last reviewed: 2026-07-25

Reviewed by FreetoolOnline editorial team

Open the Mercury Spin-Orbit Resonance 3D Explorer, scrub through a full 176-day solar day, and watch Mercury complete exactly 3 rotations for every 2 orbits while its two hot-pole markers swap which one faces the Sun at successive perihelion passes.

  1. Open /space-3d/mercury-spin-orbit-resonance.html and wait for the Sun, the eccentric orbit ellipse, and the Mercury sphere with its two hot-pole markers to render.
  2. Use the Object selector to pick the 3:2 resonance itself, Mercury's rotation, Mercury's orbit, or the Sun.
  3. Drag the Day-of-solar-day scrub slider (0-176) or click "Play Solar Day" to advance Mercury around its real-eccentricity orbit while it spins at its real rotation rate.
  4. Watch the two orange hot-pole markers swap which one leads toward the Sun between one perihelion pass (the red dot) and the next.
  5. Look at the facts panel for the selected view's real rotation period, orbital period, ratio, solar-day length, and eccentricity, plus a live count of rotations and orbits completed at the current day.
  6. Switch views and scrub again to compare how the numbers and the marker swap line up.
