AI Story Generator - Offline, Private, No Sign-Up

Last reviewed: 2026-09-05

This story generator turns a one-line premise into flash fiction or the opening scene of a longer story, and it does it in your browser: no account, no upload, and the premise you type never leaves your device. Cloud story sites keep your ideas on their servers; this page has no server to send them to.


What it writes

Fill in a premise, then optionally a main character and a setting, and choose one of six genres - adventure, fantasy, science fiction, mystery, romance or comedy. Three lengths are available: flash fiction of about 150 words, a short story of about 300 words, or an opening scene of about 200 words that ends on a hook you can continue yourself. The output appears below the form with a copy button, ready to paste into your own document.


Three ways the story gets written

The page always produces something, and it always tells you which engine produced it. If your browser ships built-in on-device AI (recent Chrome), the story is written by that with nothing to download. Otherwise you can load a small open language model (Llama 3.2 or Phi 3.5) into the browser with one click - the download size is shown before it starts, the files are cached so later visits reuse them, and the writing itself happens on your device. Without either, a deterministic template assembles a genre-flavored story start from your inputs: a title, an opening line that places your character in your setting, your premise as the inciting incident, and a closing hook. The template output is labeled as a scaffold to rewrite - it is honest structure, not AI prose.


What a small model can and cannot do

The downloadable models are small (1 to 3 billion parameters). They write serviceable flash fiction and scene openings; they do not write novels, keep a plot consistent across chapters, or match a professional author. Treat every draft as raw material: keep the ideas that work, cut the rest, and rewrite the sentences in your own voice. Need character-name ideas or a short poem instead of a story? Ask for those in the premise box on the AI tiers - there is no separate page for them, on purpose.
