Emoji Slot Machine

Emoji Slot Machine is a free browser emoji slot machine with no account or install. Nothing is uploaded, and about 0.3 MB loads after you press Play.

How to play

  1. Press Play, then dismiss the instructions overlay.
  2. Tap to spin the reels.
  3. Tap again to stop each reel.

Your coins and preferences stay on the device under ftol:emojislot keys - nothing is sent to a server. For more free browser games, open the game hub. Related picks: Eight Ball Pool and Classic Pong.
