White Noise & Ambient Sound Generator is one of three common ways to get background sound: a browser-based tool like this one, a phone or desktop white noise app, or a long looping video on a streaming site. Each trades off differently on setup and what you need installed.

The main advantage of a browser-based generator over an app is zero setup - it works the moment the page loads, on any device with a browser, and every sound is synthesized on the spot rather than looping a fixed recording. The main advantage over a streamed video is that nothing buffers or needs a network connection once the page has loaded.
