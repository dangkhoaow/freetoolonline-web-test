Space Huggers - js13k Platformer

Keyboard Controls: Arrow Keys to move and jump, Z to shoot, X to dodge, C to throw a grenade

How to Play Space Huggers

Space Huggers is a roguelike run-and-gun platformer where you clear out enemy outposts on procedurally generated planets. Shoot your way through enemies, dodge to put out fire or dash through danger, and save your grenades for tough spots. Kill every enemy on a level to move on; run out of lives and it's game over.

Game Controls

  • Arrow Keys - Move left/right, up to jump
  • Z (or left mouse click) - Shoot
  • X (or middle mouse click) - Dodge - brief invulnerability, extinguishes fire, damages nearby enemies
  • C (or right mouse click) - Throw a grenade (3 per life)
  • R - Restart

Game Features

  • Destructible Environments - Weapons fire and grenades tear apart the level's blocks and cover
  • Local co-op - Up to 4 players can drop in with connected gamepads
  • Roguelike Gameplay - Every level is procedurally generated; you start with 10 lives and earn 3 more per completed level
  • No Installation - Play directly in your browser, no download required
  • Free and Open Source - GPL-3.0 licensed game by Frank Force
