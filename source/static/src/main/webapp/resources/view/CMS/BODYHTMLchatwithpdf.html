Ask your PDF a question in three browser-only steps:

  1. Open your PDF with the file picker - it is read locally in your browser and never uploaded anywhere.
  2. Type your question in plain language and press Ask the document - no query syntax to learn.
  3. Read the result: the most relevant passages are listed with their page numbers so you can check the source, and a written answer is drafted when your browser ships built-in AI or an on-device model is loaded.
