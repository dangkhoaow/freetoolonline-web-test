Frequently Asked Questions

Why is the Perito Moreno Glacier unusual?

Most glaciers worldwide are retreating, but Perito Moreno is commonly cited as relatively stable, still advancing far enough to reach the far shore and periodically dam part of the lake.

How tall is the ice wall?

About 60-70 m above the water, across a terminus about 5 km wide where the glacier meets Lago Argentino.

What is the ice-dam "rupture"?

When the advancing glacier touches the Peninsula de Magallanes, it can block the Brazo Rico arm of the lake. Water backs up until it eventually breaches through or under the ice in a dramatic collapse - documented repeatedly, though the timing is irregular.

Where is the Perito Moreno Glacier?

In Los Glaciares National Park, Santa Cruz Province, Argentina, part of the Southern Patagonian Ice Field - a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1981.

How long and how big is it?

About 30 km long with an area of about 250 sq km.

Is this a scientific ice model?

No. It is an educational explorer using published figures - the ice surface, crevasse pattern, and vertical scale are schematic teaching cues, not survey glaciology.
