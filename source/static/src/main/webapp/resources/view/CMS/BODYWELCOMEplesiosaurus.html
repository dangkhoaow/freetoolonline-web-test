Plesiosaurus 3D Viewer

Last reviewed: July 22, 2026
By Claude Code, with paleontological research from the Natural History Museum, London and Smithsonian NMNH

Explore a 3D model of Plesiosaurus, one of the most iconic marine reptiles from the Jurassic seas. This interactive viewer lets you rotate the creature, zoom in on its distinctive features - the long neck, four flippers, and compact body - and read real published facts about how it lived, where it was first discovered, and what made it such a successful hunter 180 million years ago.

An Early Jurassic marine reptile, not a dinosaur. Plesiosaurus lived roughly 183 to 176 million years ago. At 4 meters long it was mid-sized for its group, built for speed and agility in ancient seas.

Fact Value
Length 3.5 - 4.5 meters (commonly cited: 4 m)
Height ~0.8 meters (flipper-to-flipper span)
Weight ~200-300 kg (estimated from fossil material)
Era Early Jurassic (Toarcian), ~183-176 million years ago
Period Toarcian Stage (Early Jurassic)
Diet Carnivore - fish, squid, small marine reptiles
First Described 1824 by Conybeare & De la Beche
Discovery Site Lyme Regis, Dorset, England (Lower Jurassic)
Classification Sauropterygia, family Plesiosauridae (NOT Dinosauria)

How the viewer runs. The 3D model runs entirely in your browser using WebGL - no server or download is needed. The procedural geometry renders instantly, and then a licensed scan-based model (if available) loads smoothly after first paint. The long neck is the first distinctive feature you'll notice - different from the relatively short-necked mosasaurs that came later in the Cretaceous.

Important note on the model: The colors and skin shown here are artistic reconstructions. Fossils preserve bone but rarely preserve soft tissue or color. The actual skin, belly pattern, and eye color of Plesiosaurus are unknown. All measurements and facts you see (length, weight, era, discovery date) are real published figures from museum collections and peer-reviewed research.
