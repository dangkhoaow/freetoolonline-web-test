Head Soccer Arena is a keyboard head-soccer duel that runs in your browser - jump and kick to score, play 1P against a reactive CPU or hotseat 2P on one keyboard, and race to the goals target or the match clock. Press Play to load it.


Key features

  • Monochrome arena - 1P vs CPU or hotseat 2P, win by goals-to-win (default 5) or on time.
  • Physics heads knock the ball into side goals; the crossbar bounces and a power bar fills for rocket shots.
  • Four power-ups - fire for a stronger kick, ice to freeze the opponent, a giant head, and multi-ball.
  • Adjustable settings - mode, match length (60/90/120/180 s), goals to win (3/5/7/10), and theme.

Keyboard controls

ActionPlayer 1Player 2
MoveA / DLeft / Right arrows
JumpWUp arrow
KickSDown arrow
Power shotQ/

Space pauses, R restarts, and Esc returns to the one-game menu. A keyboard is required and there is no dedicated touch layout.


Runs in your browser

The pitch duel is about 88 KB and downloads once from this site when you press Play, with no install and no account. High scores and settings stay on your device under ftol:headsoccerarena:storage. It is adapted from the Cursor Arcade soccer module by flancast90 under the MIT license, and this build stubs the VS Code webview API and ships LICENSE and CREDITS.
