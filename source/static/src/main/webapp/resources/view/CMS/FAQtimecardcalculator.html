Frequently Asked Questions

What does the time card calculator do?

It adds up a week of work hours from the clock-in time, clock-out time and break minutes you enter for each day, and shows daily and weekly totals in both h:mm and decimal hours. An optional hourly rate adds a gross-pay line.

Why does payroll want decimal hours instead of hours and minutes?

Payroll systems multiply hours by a rate, so 7 hours 30 minutes has to become 7.50 before the math works. The calculator shows both forms side by side, and the reference table below the result lists the standard conversions - 15 minutes is 0.25, 30 is 0.50, 45 is 0.75.

Can it handle an overnight shift?

Yes. When a day's clock-out time is at or before its clock-in time, the calculator rolls the shift past midnight - a 22:00 to 06:00 night shift counts as 8 hours before the break, not a negative number.

How does the overtime split work?

You set the weekly threshold yourself (it is preset to 40). When the week's total exceeds it, the result splits the hours into regular and over-threshold portions. Overtime rules differ by country, state and contract, so the calculator applies your number - it does not look up or apply any jurisdiction's rule, and it is not legal, tax or payroll advice.

Does anything I enter leave my browser?

No. The math runs locally in the page - nothing is uploaded, stored or sent, and no account is needed. It also means the time sheet is not saved between visits, so copy the totals into your own records before closing the tab.
