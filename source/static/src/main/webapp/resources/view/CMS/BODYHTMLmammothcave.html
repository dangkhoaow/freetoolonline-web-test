About this scene

The facts panel fills when the scene loads. Use the toggles below the scene.

Published figures: more than 400 miles (about 650 km) of surveyed passages across at least 5 mapped levels; a sandstone caprock over limestone has kept passages from collapsing for millions of years; the Echo River runs through the lower levels.

Drag to orbit and scroll or pinch to zoom. Everything renders on your device with WebGL.
