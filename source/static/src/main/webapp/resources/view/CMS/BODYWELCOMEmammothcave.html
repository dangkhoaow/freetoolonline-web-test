Mammoth Cave 3D Explorer

Last reviewed: 2026-07-28

Reviewed by FreetoolOnline editorial team

This browser explorer shows Mammoth Cave as a stack of three rock levels, each carved with its own winding passage and linked by vertical shafts - a schematic view of the real cave's multi-level maze, which is why it holds the record for the longest known cave system on Earth rather than the deepest or widest single chamber.

Mammoth Cave sits under south-central Kentucky, USA, inside Mammoth Cave National Park. Surveyors have mapped more than 400 miles (about 650 km) of connected passages - more than twice the length of the next-longest known cave system - and the total keeps growing most years as new passages are explored and linked to the known map. The system has at least 5 distinct levels stacked vertically, connected by shafts and pits, which is what makes it a true three-dimensional maze rather than one long corridor. A sandstone caprock sits over the limestone bedrock and has protected the passages from collapsing while they slowly formed over roughly the last 10 million years. The Echo River and Roaring River flow through the lowest levels; the Echo River was historically used for underground boat tours.
