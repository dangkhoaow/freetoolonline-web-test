Explore Earth's Longest Cave System

Mammoth Cave is the world's longest known cave system, with over 680 kilometers of surveyed passages winding through Kentucky limestone. For centuries, explorers have discovered layer upon layer of geological history in this massive underground maze. The cave features stalactites hanging from cathedral ceilings and stalagmites rising from cavern floors—delicate mineral formations sculpted over millions of years. The Echo River flows through the lower passages in absolute darkness, supporting a unique ecosystem of blind cave fish and other adapted species.
