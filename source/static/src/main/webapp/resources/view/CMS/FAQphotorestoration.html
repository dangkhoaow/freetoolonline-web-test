Frequently Asked Questions

What does AI Photo Restoration do?

Sharpen, denoise, and upscale a blurry or compressed photo - 2x or 4x - right in this browser - no upload, no app to install.

Can I use this as an AI image upscaler?

Yes. The Quick upscale 2x mode is a plain AI image upscaler for reasonably clean photos (about 8 MB model, several times faster), and the Restore & upscale 4x mode enlarges while also removing blur and JPEG blocking artifacts (about 55 MB model, slower). Both run entirely in your browser - the photo never leaves your device.

When should I reach for ai photo restoration?

When a photo is too small, too blurry, or shows JPEG blocking - before printing it, zooming in on it, or reusing it at a larger size. The original previews immediately; the first run downloads the model for the mode you picked once (cached offline after that), and on the WASM fallback (no WebGPU) the 4x mode can take up to a minute.

Does it repair scratches or colorize old photos?

No. These are fixed 2x/4x super-resolution models - they sharpen, denoise, and enlarge, but they do not remove scratches, tears, or stains, and they do not colorize a black-and-white photo.

What complementary tools work well alongside ai photo restoration?

The object remover erases a distraction from the frame before you upscale, and the face blur tool hides who someone is - both run on-device with no upload, like this page.

Which mode: quick 2x upscale or 4x restore?

Pick Quick upscale 2x when the photo is reasonably clean and you just need more pixels - a small avatar, an old web image, a picture you want to print larger. Its model is about 8 MB and runs several times faster than the 4x one. Pick Restore & upscale 4x when the photo is blurry or was saved with heavy JPEG compression: that model also removes blocking artifacts and sharpens edges while quadrupling the resolution. Neither mode repairs physical damage - scratches, tears, and stains stay - and a black-and-white photo stays black-and-white.
