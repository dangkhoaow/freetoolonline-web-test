Welcome to Connect Four AI - a free browser Connect Four game with no account or install. Press Play, pick offline AI, local human, or AI vs AI, then click a column to drop a disc. About 40 KB loads after Play. Online multiplayer is not available here. For more free browser games, open the game hub. Related picks: Mor Chess 2 and Rock Paper Neural.

Game details

  • Board size: 7 columns by 6 rows.
  • Win condition: line up four of your discs in a row - horizontal, vertical, or diagonal.
  • Source: adapted from the open-source kenrick95/c4 project (MIT license).
