Sales Tax Calculator

Last reviewed: 2026-07-30

It shows the tax amount on its own line and the final total beside it, so you can see exactly how much the tax adds to the price.

Works for any currency and any rate. Useful for checking a receipt at the store, estimating the real cost of an online purchase, or verifying a quoted total before checkout.

The calculation runs locally in the browser - no account, no install, no server round-trip.
