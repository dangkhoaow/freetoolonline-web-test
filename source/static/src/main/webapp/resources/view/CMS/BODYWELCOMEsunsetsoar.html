Welcome - press Play to load Sunset Soar. At about 16 KB of plain Canvas 2D with zero dependencies it is the lightest game bundle on this site, so it starts near-instantly even on a slow connection. Best distances stay on this device under ftol:sunsetsoar:best; nothing is uploaded.

The rhythm is the game: hold to dive while the hill falls away, release right at the crest, and your speed converts into a launch across the next valley. Mistimed dives scrub speed, and a grounded stall of about a second and a half ends the run - though the first one and a half kilometers carry a warm-up grace so a first run never dies immediately. The sun sinks and the sky fades toward dusk purple as your distance grows, which makes your farthest runs visibly different from your first ones.

One input covers everything - Space, mouse, or touch - so it plays equally well on desktop and phone.
