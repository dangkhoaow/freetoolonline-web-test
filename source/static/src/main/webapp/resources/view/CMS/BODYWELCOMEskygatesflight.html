Sky Gates Flight

Last reviewed: 2026-07-26

Fly a little plane over the ocean and steer through the gates - green ones add their number to your power, red ones subtract it, and the run ends at zero.


How the run builds

  • Speed climbs every ten seconds, so later gates rush at you faster.
  • Red gates get harsher the longer you last.
  • An occasional gold x2 gate doubles your power.

Gate chimes and buzzes are synthesized on your device and stay muted until you turn the sound on.


Scores stay on your device

Your best distance and peak power are saved in this browser's local storage and shown before you even start - no accounts, uploads, or leaderboards. Sky Gates Flight is an arcade game with simplified physics, not a flight simulator - everything on screen is procedural geometry drawn live in your browser.
