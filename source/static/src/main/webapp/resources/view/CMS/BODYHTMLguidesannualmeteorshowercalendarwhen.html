When to Use the Annual Meteor Shower Calendar 3D Explorer

Last reviewed: 2026-08-10

Open the Annual Meteor Shower Calendar 3D Explorer when you want a fast, real-dates answer to which of the 12 major annual meteor showers is active right now, which one peaks next, and how strong each one is.


Sessions this explorer fits

Skywatchers deciding which night to stay up late for, students or teachers comparing all 12 major annual showers on one dial instead of 12 separate articles, and anyone who wants to know whether tonight falls inside a real published shower window all get a direct answer here.


Sessions this explorer does not fit

This calendar answers WHICH shower and WHEN, not WHY the meteors seem to fan out from a single spot overhead - that geometry of the radiant point lives on the Meteor Shower Radiant 3D Explorer. It also does not cover a single named fall or airburst from history, such as the 2013 event mapped on the Chelyabinsk Meteor 3D Explorer. If you have the calendar open but are unsure how to read the peak dates and moon-phase dial, walk through every control in the step-by-step guide.
