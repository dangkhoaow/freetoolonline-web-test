Frequently Asked Questions

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. The image is opened and edited entirely inside your browser. Nothing is sent to a server - the object removal runs on your own device.

How do I remove an object?

Load a photo, brush over the object you want gone (adjust the brush size as needed), then click Remove object (AI). The painted area is filled in with matching background and the result is ready to download as a PNG.

Do I have to download anything?

The AI eraser downloads a model of about 27 MB the first time you use it, then caches it for later. If you prefer no download, the Quick patch button fills the painted area instantly from nearby pixels - faster but rougher than the AI.

What does it work best on?

It is strongest on distractions set against sky, walls, ground, water, or other repeating texture. Very large subjects in front of complex detail are harder, so paint closely around the object and check the fill before saving.

What image formats can I use?

JPG, PNG, and WebP images work. The download is always a PNG.
