Welcome to Particle Clicker - a free browser particle-physics incremental from CERN Webfest 2014. Press Play, click the detector for data, research discoveries along a historic timeline, hire lab workers for automatic data, and buy upgrades with funding. About ~2 MB loads after you press Play. Progress stays under ftol:particleclicker keys on this device. Nothing is uploaded and no account is required. The workers you hire keep collecting data while you plan your next move, and the funding upgrades you buy speed up how fast the detector and your team gather it, so the lab keeps growing between clicks. A fullscreen button hands the detector the whole screen when you want to focus, and the game works with a mouse or touch. Landscape mode works best on phones. For more free browser games, open the game hub. Related picks: Idle Capitalist Loop for a business idle, and Emoji Slot Machine for emoji reels. The lab itself traces back to CERN Webfest 2014, where a small team built the original incremental as an open source project and released it under the MIT license, and the About panel inside the game still credits those original authors by name. Because the license is permissive, this build keeps the same detector-and-research loop while running the whole thing locally in your browser, with no server, no runtime analytics, and no third party script pulled in after the one-time load.
