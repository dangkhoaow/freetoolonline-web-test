Cube Smash 3D is a sunny-meadow survival game in the browser: grumpy cube blobs hop toward you from the treeline, and your Smash Hammer swings automatically whenever one is in range. Level-up picks add two more auto-weapons - orbiting Spin Blades and lobbed Boom Stones - plus boots, heals, magnets, and regeneration. A crowned Boss Blob with its own health bar charges in every couple of minutes, and the meadow cycles from bright day to firefly-lit night as you survive.

More one-run games from the same shelf: Night Swarm Survivor and One Tap Platformer.

CUBE SMASH 3D

Original hammer-survival game built for this site (MIT). About 610 KB downloads once when you press Play - mostly the shared 3D engine - then it is cached. Best scores stay in your browser.

Move: WASD / arrows or drag. SHIFT sprint, SPACE jump. Hammer swings automatically. P pause, M mute, R restart.
