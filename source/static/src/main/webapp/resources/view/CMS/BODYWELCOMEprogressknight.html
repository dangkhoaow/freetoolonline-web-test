Welcome to Progress Knight - a free medieval life-sim incremental with no account or install. About 0.12 MB loads after you press Play, and everything runs in your browser.

How it plays

  • Climb jobs from beggar upward.
  • Learn skills as you go.
  • Buy gear in the shop.
  • Watch your age advance against your lifespan.
  • Prestige on the Amulet tab for a stronger next life.

Progress stays on-device under ftol:progressknight:gameDataSave and is never uploaded. Use Pause in the sidebar when you step away. For more free browser games, open the games hub. Related picks: Idle Capitalist Loop for a business idle loop, and Void Trader for voyage trading.
