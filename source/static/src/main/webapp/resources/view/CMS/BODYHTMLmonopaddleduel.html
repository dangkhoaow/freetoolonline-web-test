Grab a keyboard and face off in Mono Paddle Duel, a monochrome paddle game right in the browser tab. Play 1P vs CPU or share one keyboard for hotseat 2P, pick Easy / Normal / Hard / Insane, and race to the target score (default 11). About ~54 KB after Play - no install, no account.

This paddle duel is part of a small monochrome arcade pack - Mono Minefield Grid is the logic-puzzle entry in the same set.

MONO PADDLE DUEL

The underlying code traces back to the Cursor Arcade pong project (MIT-licensed); everything loads in one ~54 KB fetch the first time you click Play, and your best runs stay saved locally.

1P: W/S or arrows move your paddle. 2P: W/S left, arrows right. Space pause, R restart, Esc menu.

Press Play to load the game.
Scores use ftol:monopaddleduel:storage.
