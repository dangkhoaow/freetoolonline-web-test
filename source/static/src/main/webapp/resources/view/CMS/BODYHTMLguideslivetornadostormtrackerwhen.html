Live Tornado & Storm Tracker When To Use

Last reviewed: 2026-08-04

Live Tornado & Storm Tracker is worth opening any time severe weather is in the forecast for your area. This page covers when it fits and what it does not replace.


When it fits

Reach for it during tornado, hurricane, flood, or other severe weather season when you want a quick, no-install check of what the National Weather Service currently has active for a state - useful before a trip, during a storm watch, or when a news alert makes you want the official detail.


What to expect

You get the same live NWS alert feed the tool reads directly in your browser: event, area, severity, expiry countdown, and the official instruction text. Coverage is US-only, and alerts can lag the live event by a few minutes, so this is a quick-reference check, not a substitute for official emergency notifications during an active event.
