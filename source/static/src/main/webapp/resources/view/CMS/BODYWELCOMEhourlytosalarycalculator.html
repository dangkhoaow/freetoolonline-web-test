Hourly to Salary Calculator

Last reviewed: 2026-10-01

The hourly to salary calculator turns a wage into the full gross-pay picture: enter an hourly rate, your hours per week and your paid weeks per year, and the weekly, biweekly, monthly and annual amounts are on screen as you type. Flip the direction and it works backwards - an annual salary becomes its hourly equivalent. Everything runs in your browser; nothing you enter is stored or sent anywhere.

The math is plain gross-pay arithmetic: annual pay equals the hourly rate times hours per week times paid weeks per year, the monthly figure is the annual divided by 12, and biweekly is two weekly paychecks. Hours per week accepts 0.5-step values up to 100 and defaults to 40; paid weeks defaults to 52 and accepts 1 to 53, so part-time schedules and unpaid-break years are one edit away. Switching direction converts the amount you already typed in place, so the result stays equivalent instead of resetting.

Below the result, a reference table lists common hourly rates from 15 to 50 with their weekly and annual gross pay at a standard 40-hour, 52-week schedule - a quick anchor when comparing a job offer against the number you are used to hearing.

Every figure is gross pay before taxes and deductions - the result says so itself, and the page makes no take-home or tax claims. For other money math on this site, the percentage calculator handles raises and generic percent questions, the sales tax calculator prices a purchase, and the loan calculator covers repayment schedules - this page only converts between wage and salary.
