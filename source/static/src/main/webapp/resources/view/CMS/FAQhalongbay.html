Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Ha Long Bay?

In the Gulf of Tonkin off Quang Ninh province in northeastern Vietnam.

How many islands does Ha Long Bay have?

An estimated 1,600-2,000 limestone islands and islets, about 775 of which are named.

How old are the limestone islands?

The islands were shaped over roughly the last 20 million years as rain and sea water dissolved the limestone along cracks and fault lines.

What does "Ha Long" mean?

It translates to "descending dragon", tied to a folk legend of dragons sent to defend the coast.

What is Sung Sot Cave?

Sung Sot, or Surprise Cave, is one of the largest caves in Ha Long Bay, found inside one of its limestone islands.

Is Ha Long Bay a UNESCO World Heritage Site?

Yes. It has been a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1994, with a core protected zone covering about 1,553 sq km.

Is this a scientific terrain model?

No. It is an educational explorer using published figures - the island field, spacing, and vertical scale are schematic teaching cues, not survey terrain.
