About this scene

The facts panel fills when the scene loads. Scrub the timeline or click a marker to read the real published figures.

Published figures (Zucker et al. 2022, Nature 601, 334-337; CfA/Harvard-Smithsonian press release; cross-checked 2026-08-13): the Local Bubble is about 1,000 light-years across, formed by about 15 supernovae (published range 8-26) beginning about 14 million years ago, and is still expanding today at about 4 miles (about 6 km) per second. The Sun was over 1,000 light-years from the bubble's birthplace when it began forming and drifted into it only about 5 million years ago.

Drag the timeline slider (or press Play) to watch the bubble grow and the Sun drift inward, click the seven blue markers for the real star-forming regions on the bubble's surface, and press "Show Perseus & Orion" to see two well-known regions that are NOT on this bubble despite common assumption.
