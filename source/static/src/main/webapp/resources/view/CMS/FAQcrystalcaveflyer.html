Frequently Asked Questions

What does Crystal Cave Flyer do?

It flies you forward through an endless procedural cavern. One input controls height: hold to climb, release to sink. Passing through each rock wall's gap continues the run; clipping a wall or the cave edge ends it.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes - the whole game is one touch. Press and hold anywhere to climb, release to sink. Desktop can use Space or the mouse button instead.

Is anything saved in my browser?

Yes. Your best distance-plus-crystal score uses localStorage key ftol:crystalcaveflyer:best. Nothing is uploaded.

What are the crystals worth?

Each collected crystal adds 25 points to the final score on top of the meters flown. Crystals float inside roughly six of every ten wall gaps, so lining up with a gap usually lines you up with its crystal too.
