Nile River 3D Explorer

The Nile River is the lifeblood of North Africa, flowing 6,650 kilometers from Lake Victoria across the East African highlands through Sudan and Egypt to the Nile Delta and Mediterranean Sea. This 3D explorer lets you visualize the world's longest river, its meanders through the Sahara, and the ancient Egyptian cities that thrived along its banks.

Use the controls to toggle the ancient Egypt city markers (red cones for Khartoum, Cairo, and Alexandria) and the seasonal flood zones showing the historical inundation extent. Drag to rotate the view, scroll to zoom.
