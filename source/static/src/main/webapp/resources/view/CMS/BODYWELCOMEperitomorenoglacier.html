Perito Moreno Glacier 3D Explorer

Last reviewed: 2026-08-01

Reviewed by FreetoolOnline editorial team

This browser explorer shows the Perito Moreno Glacier as a cross-section: the ice flowing down a mountain valley, a band of crevasses that thickens near the front, and the calving wall where the glacier meets Lago Argentino in Los Glaciares National Park, Argentine Patagonia.

Perito Moreno is unusual among the world's glaciers: while most glaciers are retreating, this one is commonly cited as relatively stable, still advancing far enough to reach the far shore (the Peninsula de Magallanes) and block the Brazo Rico arm of the lake. When that happens, water backs up behind the ice dam until it eventually breaches under or through the ice in a dramatic collapse - an event locally called a "rupture", documented repeatedly over the decades though its timing is irregular.

  • Toggle crevasse bands to see the fractured ice near the calving front
  • Toggle the ice-dam marker to see where the glacier's snout can touch the far shore
  • Read length, area, ice-face height, and terminus width in the panel
  • Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom
  • Runs fully in the browser with the vendored three.js engine - no account, no upload

For another slow-moving giant shaped by gravity, see the Amazon River 3D Explorer, or for the opposite ice extreme at altitude, the snow line on the Mount Everest 3D Explorer.

The whole Perito Moreno scene - the valley ice, the crevasse bands, and the calving wall on Lago Argentino - is drawn on your device with WebGL, so nothing about your session leaves the browser. The three.js engine downloads once (about 0.7 MB) and is cached for later visits, and your crevasse and ice-dam toggle choices are saved in browser storage so the model reopens the way you left it.

This is an educational approximation - the ice surface, crevasse pattern, and vertical scale are schematic teaching cues, not survey glaciology.
