Frequently Asked Questions

What does Monitor Refresh Rate Test (Hz) measure?

It samples requestAnimationFrame timestamps and estimates Hz from the median frame delta. Results stay in the tab.

Do I need camera or microphone access?

No. This tool uses animation-frame timing only - no getUserMedia.

Why does the reading drop when I switch tabs?

Background tabs throttle requestAnimationFrame. Keep this tab in the foreground while measuring.

Is timing data uploaded?

No. Samples stay in the page session. Reset clears them; closing the tab drops them.

Can it detect an adaptive refresh display like ProMotion or a VRR gaming monitor?

Yes, by behavior. The Adaptive refresh tracking panel records the session minimum and maximum rate, lists each stable rate observed, and reports rate switching when two clearly different stable rates appear - the signature of ProMotion, LTPO, or variable-refresh panels. It observes what the display does in this browser; it cannot read the panel's full supported range from hardware.

Why does my 120 Hz iPhone or MacBook show only ~60 Hz?

Some devices cap web animation below the panel maximum. Common causes: Low Power Mode on iPhone, Safari without its full frame-rate setting enabled, battery-saver profiles on laptops, or an external monitor cable or OS setting fixed at 60 Hz. Remove the cap and measure again.

What do the stable-rate list and the chart mean?

A stable rate is a stretch of frames the browser held near one speed, such as ~60 Hz or ~120 Hz. The chart shows how much of the session was spent near each common rate, so an adaptive panel typically shows two or more bars while a fixed panel shows one.
