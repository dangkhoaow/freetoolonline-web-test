Seasonal Witchcat

Last reviewed: 2026-09-17

Seasonal Witchcat is a free browser adventure you can play with no account and no install. Press Play to load the ~34 KB canvas engine in a same-origin iframe, then explore a large pixel map, switch seasons to reshape the world, and rescue every hidden cat.


Controls

  • WASD or arrow keys move the witch across a large tile map.
  • Space or Enter shoots a fireball in the facing direction and reads up-facing signs.
  • Stand on a stone trigger and press action to cycle between opened seasons.
  • A standard gamepad (D-pad plus a face button) also works; the game is keyboard and gamepad first.

Seasons and the world

Four season orbs open winter, summer, fall, and spring and raise your maximum lives. Stand on a trigger to switch the active season, and the map rewrites itself. Fireballs clear bushes and foes such as skulls and moles as you travel between temples.

SeasonWhat changes in the world
WinterFreezes water into ice.
SummerGrows climbable roots.
AutumnFills holes with leaves and grows mushrooms.
SpringBlooms stone flowers.

Goal and saving

Twelve cats are hidden across the temples. Walk into a cat to send it back to its hub marker, and rescue all of them to finish. Checkpoints and key events save your position, active season, lives, opened seasons, and rescued cats locally under ftol:seasonalwitchcat:save.


Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing leaves this device except the initial page and the game files served from this site. There is no external asset CDN, and the music and sound effects are generated procedurally in the browser.
