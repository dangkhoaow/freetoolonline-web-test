About this scene

The facts panel fills when the scene loads. Use the toggles below the scene.

Published figures: valley floor about 1,200 m elevation, about 12 km long and 1.6 km wide; El Capitan rises about 1,100 m above the floor; Half Dome rises about 1,444 m above the floor at Tenaya Canyon; Yosemite Falls drops about 739 m in three tiers.
