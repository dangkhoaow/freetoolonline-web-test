Add Page Numbers to PDF

Last reviewed: 2026-07-22

Add page numbers to every page of a PDF, right in the browser.

Key features

  • Upload the PDF, pick the starting number, and click Add page numbers.
  • The download prints "Page N" centered at the bottom of every page, counting up from the number you chose.
  • Gives a multi-page report or contract a consistent running count without opening a desktop editor.

What it does not do

  • Applies one fixed bottom-center style and numbering sequence.
  • Does not support custom position, custom text or format, skipping a numbered cover page, or restarting the count partway through the document.

Your files stay private

The PDF you number stays inside this browser tab from upload to download - the numbering runs on your own device, so the document is never sent to or stored on a server.

To split, merge, rotate, delete pages from, or watermark the PDF instead, see the related-tools section below.
