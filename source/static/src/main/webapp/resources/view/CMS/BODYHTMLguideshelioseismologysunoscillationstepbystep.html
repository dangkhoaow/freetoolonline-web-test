How to Explore the Helioseismology Sun Oscillation 3D Explorer

Last reviewed: 2026-07-25

Reviewed by FreetoolOnline editorial team

Open the Helioseismology Sun Oscillation 3D Explorer, watch the rippling surface, then toggle the interior cutaway to see the real measured boundary the ripple pattern helps astronomers find.

  1. Open /space-3d/helioseismology-sun-oscillation.html and wait for the rippling sphere and the facts panel to render.
  2. Drag the wave amplitude slider to make the ripple pattern easier or harder to see.
  3. Click "Next wave pattern" to cycle through three simplified example oscillation patterns.
  4. Click "Show interior cutaway" to switch to a cross-section showing the measured 0.713-solar-radii convection-zone boundary and the tachocline.
  5. Drag to orbit and scroll or pinch to zoom for a closer look.
  6. Read the facts panel for the oscillation period, mode count, and boundary figures.
