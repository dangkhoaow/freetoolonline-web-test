How to Explore Bortle Scale Light Pollution 3D Explorer

Last reviewed: 2026-07-25

Reviewed by FreetoolOnline editorial team

Open the Bortle Scale Light Pollution 3D Explorer, drag the class slider from 1 to 9, and watch a 260-star sky field thin out exactly as the published scale describes.

  1. Open /space-3d/bortle-scale-light-pollution.html and wait for the star field and ground to render.
  2. Drag the Bortle class slider from 1 upward and watch faint stars vanish one by one as the limiting magnitude drops.
  3. Click "Excellent dark site (1)" then "City sky (8)" to see the full range in two clicks.
  4. Watch the Milky Way band fade and the horizon light dome rise as the class number climbs.
  5. Toggle "Milky Way band" or "Horizon light dome" off to see the star field alone.
  6. Read the facts panel for the current class's limiting magnitude, visible-star count, and Milky Way description.
