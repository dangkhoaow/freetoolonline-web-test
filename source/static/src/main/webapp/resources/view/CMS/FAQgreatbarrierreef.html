Frequently Asked Questions

How big is the Great Barrier Reef?

It runs about 2,300 km along the Queensland coast and covers roughly 344,400 sq km, made up of more than 2,900 individual reefs and over 900 islands and cays rather than one continuous structure.

Is the Great Barrier Reef one continuous wall of coral?

No - it is a dense field of separate reef patches with open channels between them, which is why the scene shows scattered reefs rather than a solid ridge.

Can you really see the Great Barrier Reef from space?

Its scale is often cited as making it the only living structure visible from space, given its size and the color contrast of shallow reef water against the surrounding ocean.

What is coral bleaching?

Coral bleaching happens when ocean heat stress causes coral to expel the algae that give it color and much of its food, turning the coral pale or white. The reef has had several documented mass bleaching events, including 2016, 2017, 2020, and 2022.

What is Heart Reef?

Heart Reef is a small, naturally heart-shaped individual reef near the Whitsunday Islands, well known as an aerial photography landmark within the wider reef system.

Is this a scientific terrain model?

No. It is an educational explorer using published figures - the reef pattern, lagoon, and vertical scale are schematic teaching cues, not survey data.
