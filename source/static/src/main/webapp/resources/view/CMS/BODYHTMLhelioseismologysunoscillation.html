Pattern shown: a simplified standing wave - the real Sun combines millions of these at once.

The Sun's whole visible surface rises and falls in a five-minute rhythm, produced by sound waves (called p-modes) trapped inside the Sun. Instruments on SOHO, GONG, and SDO/HMI track millions of these modes at once; inverting that data tells astronomers where the Sun's outer convecting layer meets its calmer interior - a boundary pinned down at 0.713 solar radii - without ever sending a probe inside.
