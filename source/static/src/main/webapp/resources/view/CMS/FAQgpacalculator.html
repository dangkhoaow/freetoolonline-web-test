Frequently Asked Questions

What does GPA Calculator do?

Work out a weighted grade point average from a list of courses, each with a letter grade and credit hours.

When should I reach for gpa calculator?

Use it when you have several courses graded on the standard 4.0 letter scale and want the combined average - for a single straightforward percentage instead, use the Percentage Calculator.

What complementary tools work well alongside gpa calculator?

Add or remove courses freely - there is no limit on how many rows you can list before the average updates.

What will my semester GPA be after finals, and how much does one class move it?

List each course as a row, pick its letter grade on the 4.0 scale, and enter its credit hours; the weighted GPA is the sum of each course's grade points times its credit hours, divided by the total credit hours. One course moves the average by its own grade points weighted against the whole load. Worked example you can reproduce here: four 3-credit courses all at B (3.0) give a 3.000 GPA; change just one of those B grades to an A (4.0) and the GPA becomes 3.250. To work backwards from a course grade you still need - the score required on the final to reach a target - use the Final Grade Calculator.
