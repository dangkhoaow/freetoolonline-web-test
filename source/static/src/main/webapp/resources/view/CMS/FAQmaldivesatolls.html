Frequently Asked Questions

Why is the Maldives called the world's lowest country?

Its average ground elevation is only about 1.5 m (4.9 ft) above sea level - lower than any other country - which is why it appears so often in discussions of sea-level rise.

How many islands and atolls make up the Maldives?

About 1,192 individual coral islands, grouped into 26 natural atolls that form a double chain running roughly north-south across the Indian Ocean.

What is an atoll, and how does it form?

An atoll is a ring of coral reef built up around the rim of a submerged volcanic seamount, enclosing a shallow lagoon inside the ring.

Where is the Maldives located?

In the Indian Ocean, south-southwest of India and Sri Lanka. The capital and largest city is Male.

What do the two toggles in this scene show?

The sea-level plane shows today's mean sea level against the reef rims, and the lagoon depth bands compare the shallow rim water against the deeper lagoon floor.

Is this a scientific bathymetric model?

No. It is an educational explorer using published figures - the ring count, spacing, lagoon depth, and reef-rim freeboard are procedural teaching cues, not survey terrain.
