Frequently Asked Questions

Can I delete every page from the PDF?

No - at least one page must remain in the output.

Does deleting pages change the order of the ones that remain?

No - the output keeps the original page order, minus the pages you removed.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No - the file is processed in this browser tab and is never uploaded to a server.
