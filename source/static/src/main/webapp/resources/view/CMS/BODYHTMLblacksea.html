The Black Sea holds the largest anoxic (oxygen-free) water body on Earth - about 90 percent of its volume sits below a thin oxygen-rich surface layer, with no life beyond microbes down to a floor 2,212 m deep. Toggle the oxic/anoxic bands to see where the oxygen runs out.

About this scene

Published figures: surface area about 436,400 sq km; maximum depth about 2,212 m; the oxygen-rich surface layer is only about 150-200 m thick, so roughly 90 percent of the sea's volume is anoxic below that boundary (the chemocline).
