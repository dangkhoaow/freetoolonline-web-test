Paste text and convert it to UTF-8 binary (8-bit groups) in your browser - no upload and no account. Prefer this for class demos, protocol notes, or quick checks of how a short string looks as bytes.

Distinct from Number Base Converter (integer radix notation). Does not support arbitrary bit-widths other than 8-bit UTF-8 bytes.
