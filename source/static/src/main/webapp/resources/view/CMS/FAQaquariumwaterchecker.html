Frequently Asked Questions

Are my aquarium photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Both photos are read on an invisible canvas inside this browser tab, the metadata is parsed on your device, and the optional AI passes also run locally. Nothing is sent to a server.

Can this tool measure ammonia, nitrite or pH?

No, and no photo-based tool can. The checker measures optical properties only: sharpness, haze, particle texture and color tint. Use a liquid test kit for water chemistry; use this page to track whether the visible situation is improving between tests.

How does it tell a bacterial bloom from gravel dust?

By texture. A bacterial bloom scatters light evenly, so the frame shows a smooth milky haze with little fine grain. Suspended dust and silt produce high-frequency speckle in the midtones. The report names which pattern the current photo matches and adjusts its suggested steps accordingly.

Why does it say my photos are not comparable?

The two shots differ too much in overall brightness, which usually means different lighting rather than different water. Retake one photo from the same spot at the same time of day with the same lights, and run the comparison again.

What exactly does the optional 147 MB AI download do?

It fetches an on-device vision model (CLIP) once, after you click and see the size. The model cross-checks the cloudiness verdict against what it sees and suggests which fish may be in the photo; those suggestions only prefill the stocking form, and you confirm or correct the counts yourself. The browser caches the model, so the next visit does not download it again.

Does the clarity score work for saltwater and planted tanks?

Yes. The measurements are about how light passes through the water, not about what lives in it. Heavily tinted blackwater setups will read as amber by design, and dense plant walls give the sharpness metric less background to lock onto, so keep some hardscape or equipment visible in the frame.

Where does the capture time under each photo come from?

From the photo's own metadata, read on your device. Screenshots and images passed through chat apps often have that field stripped; the tool then falls back to the file date and says so instead of guessing.

How accurate is the stocking-pressure estimate?

It is a rule-of-thumb waste-load calculation from the species and counts you enter against the tank volume, in the spirit of classic stocking guidelines. It flags obvious overstocking, such as goldfish in a nano tank, but it is an estimate for planning, not a biological measurement.
