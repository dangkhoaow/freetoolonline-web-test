Space Pi Defense

Space Pi Defense is a mouse-drawn line-defense game across 13 levels: draw lines to stop incoming enemy rays, grow your base out to its target radius, and spend coins on upgrades while chasing a lower score. Press Play to load it; progress saves in this browser under ftol:spacepidefense:user, so you can close the tab and resume later.

Key features

  • 13 escalating levels - enemy speed and damage rise stage by stage, played with the mouse or trackpad.
  • Coins and four powerups - earn coins and use four powerup types, then upgrade power, base, magnet, coins, and powerups between levels in the shop.
  • Score to beat - lower scores are better, and a perfect clear marks any level finished with zero base hits.
  • Overall stats and reset - the game tracks overall stats and can reset all game data for a clean start.
  • Runs in the browser - about 60 KB loads after Play on a fixed 960 by 600 canvas, with no server, no account, and nothing fetched from a CDN, fonts, or analytics after the first same-origin load.

How to play

  • Click and drag to draw defensive lines; their length is limited by a power meter that regenerates over time.
  • Press Space to pause.
  • Keep the base growing toward its target radius - hits shrink it, and you fail if it drops near radius 2.
  • Clear each level to advance through all 13 stages.

When to jump in

The line-drawing loop suits a quick mouse-accuracy break or a longer push for perfect clears. It is adapted from Jack Rugile's SpacePi js13k entry under the MIT license; this build renames the save key and ships its LICENSE and CREDITS, runs on no server, and needs no account.
