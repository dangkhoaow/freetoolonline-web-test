Discount Calculator

Last reviewed: 2026-10-02

The discount calculator answers the checkout question directly: enter the sticker price and the percent off, and the sale price plus the amount you save are on screen as you type. Everything runs in your browser - nothing you enter is stored or sent anywhere, and no account is needed.

Stacked deals are where mental math usually goes wrong, so the calculator handles them explicitly: an extra coupon applies to the already-reduced price, not the original. 25% off followed by an extra 10% coupon is 32.5% off in total - not 35% - and the result spells that line out whenever a coupon is entered. An optional sales-tax percent adds a flat tax after the discounts and shows the final register price, with a breakdown table covering every step from original price to final amount.

Below the result, a reference table prices the common discounts - 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 40, 50 and 70 percent off - against the amount you entered, each with its sale price and savings. The math is plain arithmetic on the numbers you type; stores sometimes round line items differently, so the register total can differ by a cent, and the result says so itself.

For other money math on this site, the percentage calculator answers generic percent questions (X% of Y, percent change), the sales tax calculator starts from the tax side of a purchase, and the tip calculator splits a bill - this page is for percent-off deals only: price in, sale price and savings out.
