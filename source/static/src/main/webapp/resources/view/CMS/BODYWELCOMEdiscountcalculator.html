Discount Calculator

Last reviewed: 2026-10-02

The discount calculator answers the checkout question directly: enter the sticker price and the percent off, and the sale price plus the amount you save are on screen as you type. Everything runs in your browser - nothing you enter is stored or sent anywhere, and no account is needed.


Key features

  • An optional sales-tax percent is applied after the discounts, adding the tax amount and the final register price to the breakdown.
  • A breakdown table walks every step - original price, after the first discount, after the coupon, tax, and final price.
  • A reference table below the result prices the common discounts - 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 40, 50 and 70 percent off - against your amount, each with its sale price and savings.
  • Out-of-range entries are corrected, not errored - the percents clamp to 0-100 and the price floors at 0.

How stacked discounts add up

An extra coupon applies to the already-reduced price, not the original, so two deals multiply rather than add. The result states the true combined percentage whenever a coupon is entered.

Reading the totals

The math is plain arithmetic on the numbers you type. Stores sometimes round line items differently, so the register total can differ by a cent - the result says so itself.

For other money math on this site, the percentage calculator answers generic percent questions (X% of Y, percent change), the sales tax calculator starts from the tax side of a purchase, and the tip calculator splits a bill - this page is for percent-off deals only: price in, sale price and savings out.
