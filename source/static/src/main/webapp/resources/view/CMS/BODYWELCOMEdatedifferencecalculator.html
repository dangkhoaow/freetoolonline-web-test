Date Difference Calculator

Last reviewed: 2026-08-25

Date Difference Calculator counts the days between two dates in your browser. Pick a start date and an end date - both fields open on today - and the answer appears straight away as a day count plus a weeks-and-days breakdown. Nothing is installed, nothing is uploaded, and there is no calculate button to press.


What you get

  • Two fields, Start date and End date, both pre-filled with today, so you only change what you need.
  • A live result: the day count and the weeks-plus-days breakdown update the moment either field changes.
  • Reversed dates still work - set the end date earlier than the start date and the tool returns the same day count and notes that the end date is before the start date, instead of flashing a negative number.
  • A blank or unreadable date holds the result back and asks for two valid dates rather than printing a broken figure.

When to use it

  • Count the days until a deadline, an event, or a due date.
  • Measure how long a trip or a project lasted between two known dates.
  • Check how long ago something happened, from an old date up to today.
  • Read a long gap in weeks instead of raw days - 100 days shows as 14 weeks and 2 days, with no arithmetic on your part.

How it counts

Both dates are read as midnight local time, so the answer is a calendar-date difference rather than a precise elapsed-time count across time zones. A trip from the 1st to the 8th therefore counts as 7 days whatever hour you check it, and partial days never enter the total. Figures are general day counts and are not medical or financial advice.

This tool is the pick when you want the number now, correctly ordered, with the week breakdown included - and for whole calendar days it returns the same total a DATEDIF formula would.


Privacy

The two dates you pick stay in the browser - the day count is worked out on this device, with no upload, no account, and nothing sent to a server or to an analytics service.
