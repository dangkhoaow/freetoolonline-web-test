Savings Goal Calculator Online

Last reviewed: 2026-07-30

Find out whether a savings target is within reach: enter your starting balance, a monthly contribution, and the number of years, and watch compound interest carry the balance toward the goal.


Key features

  • Starting balance, monthly contribution, annual interest rate, and term in years - each a numeric field with sensible defaults ($5,000 balance, $100 a month, 7% rate, 10 years).
  • Compounding frequency selector: monthly, annually, or daily.
  • Final value, total contributed, and interest earned all update on every keystroke, formatted as USD currency using your reader locale for separators.
  • A standard compound-interest-with-contributions formula runs the projection - no external library.

When to use it

Use it to check whether a target - a house deposit, a car, an emergency fund - is reachable on what you can put aside each month. Adjusting the monthly contribution or the term lets you see the fastest path to your target balance.


Your numbers stay on this device

The target, starting balance, and monthly contribution stay in the browser - the savings projection runs entirely on this device, with no upload and no account. Results are an estimate based on a constant rate; actual returns vary, and this is not financial advice.
