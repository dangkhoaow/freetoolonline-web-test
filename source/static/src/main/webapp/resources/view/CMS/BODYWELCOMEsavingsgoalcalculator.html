Savings Goal Calculator Online

Last reviewed: 2026-07-30

Project how a starting balance and regular contributions grow with compound interest over time.

Use it to estimate a savings or retirement balance, or compare how compounding frequency changes the result. Adjusting the monthly contribution or the term lets you see the fastest path to your target balance.

The math runs locally in the browser - no upload and no account. Results are an estimate based on a constant rate; actual returns vary and this is not financial advice.
