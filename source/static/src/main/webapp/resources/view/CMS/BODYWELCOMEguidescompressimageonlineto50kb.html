This guide is the decision layer; the actual compression runs on compress-image.html, where an AI auto-pick or a manual quality slider from 1 to 100 handles the JPG, PNG, or WebP file once format and dimensions are already sorted. Working through the three steps here first - convert to JPG, resize the pixel dimensions, then adjust quality - means that tool only has to do the last, smallest job: dialing a quality number down to a target you can already see in its side-by-side live preview before you download the result.
