Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Amazon the longest river on Earth?

The Nile is about 6,650 km. The Amazon is about 6,400 km. However, the Amazon discharges far more water - about one-fifth of all river discharge on Earth.

Why is the discharge plume visible from space?

The Amazon discharges 209,000 m3/s carrying tons of silt. The freshwater plume extends 100+ km into the Atlantic, visible via satellite as lighter water from suspended sediment.

What is the várzea?

Várzea is the Amazon's flooded forest. During wet season (May-June), the river rises and inundates by up to 40 m above dry-season baseline - a dramatic seasonal cycle defining the floodplain ecosystem.

How many species live in the Amazon?

The basin supports about 1 in 10 of all known species on Earth - roughly 10% of global biodiversity - across its 7,050,000 km2 area.
