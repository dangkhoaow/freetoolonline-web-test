Colca Canyon 3D Explorer

Last reviewed: 2026-08-28

Reviewed by FreetoolOnline editorial team

This browser explorer shows Colca Canyon as a single deep gorge with stepped agricultural terraces on the upper slopes and bare rock walls below them down to the Colca River. Toggle the terrace accents to make each farmed shelf stand out, or reveal condor markers soaring above the gorge near the Cruz del Condor viewpoint.

The canyon reaches about 3,270 m deep along roughly 100 km in the Arequipa region of southern Peru, making it one of the deepest canyons on Earth. The Colca River carved it, and the upper slopes still carry pre-Inca agricultural terraces built by the Collagua and Cabana peoples - some have been farmed continuously for more than 1,000 years. At the Cruz del Condor viewpoint, Andean condors ride the rising air over the gorge; their wingspan of up to about 3.2 m is the widest of any living flying bird.

For other deep-gorge extremes, open the Grand Canyon 3D Explorer (rock-layer stack) or the Fish River Canyon 3D Explorer (rift-style gorge).

The terraced walls, gorge profile, and condor markers are drawn right in your browser with WebGL, so orbiting the 100 km gorge stays smooth without a server round-trip. The vendored three.js engine behind the scene is a one-time download of roughly 0.7 MB, and your browser keeps it cached, so returning to explore the terraces again loads instantly.

This is an educational approximation - the terrace steps, gorge profile, and condor markers are procedural teaching cues, not survey geology.
