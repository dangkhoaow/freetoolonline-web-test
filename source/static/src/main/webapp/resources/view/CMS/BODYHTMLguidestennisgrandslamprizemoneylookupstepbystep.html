Tennis Grand Slam Prize Money Lookup Step By Step

Last reviewed: 2026-08-07

Tennis Grand Slam Prize Money Lookup runs in three steps: open the page, pick a tournament, then read the tables or copy a share link. Nothing is uploaded and nothing is installed - the figures render locally from an embedded dataset of the 2025 editions.


Step 1 - open the tool

Go to Tennis Grand Slam Prize Money Lookup. The page loads with a tournament already selected and its table rendered - no install, no account, no network request after the page loads.


Step 2 - pick a tournament

Use the dropdown to switch between the Australian Open, French Open (Roland-Garros), Wimbledon, and the US Open. The table below it lists the published singles prize money per player for every round reached, from a first-round exit up to the champion, plus the total tournament pool.


Step 3 - read the comparison or share a link

Below the per-round table, a cross-majors comparison lines up the champion payout, first-round payout, and total pool of all four majors side by side. Each tournament's figures stay in its own official payout currency (AUD, EUR, GBP, USD) - the page never converts between currencies.

To share a specific tournament's table, use the Copy Link button - it copies a URL that reopens the page on that tournament.
