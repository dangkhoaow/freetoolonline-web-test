Sign PDF (Add Signature) - Vs Alternatives

Last reviewed: 2026-07-26

Sign PDF (Add Signature) is one of three common ways to put a signature on a PDF: a browser tool like this, a desktop app, or an upload-based e-sign service. Each trades off where your files go and what you must install.


How Sign PDF (Add Signature) compares

OptionWhere your data goes
Sign PDF (Add Signature) (this tool)Stays on this device - runs in the browser
Desktop PDF appStays on this device
Upload-based e-sign serviceSent to a server

This tool places one uploaded signature image on one page at a fixed corner. A desktop app or e-sign service may offer freehand drawing, multi-party workflows, or cryptographic certification - at the cost of installing software or uploading the PDF.

The original files are not kept beyond the page session, and neither the PDF nor the signature image is uploaded for this tool.
