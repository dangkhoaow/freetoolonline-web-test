Frequently Asked Questions

What does AI Background Remover do?

Remove the background from an image in your browser - your image never leaves your device.

When should I reach for ai background remover?

Reach for it when you need a subject cut cleanly out of a photo as a transparent PNG - product shots, profile pictures, or logos - and you would rather the picture stay on your device instead of being uploaded anywhere. Everything runs in your browser on a canvas; the one-time model download (roughly 40 MB) is fetched on your first cut-out and reused after that.

What can I do with the cut-out after removing the background?

The transparent PNG this page produces is your starting point for whatever comes next. Because the cut-out is exported as a downloadable blob with no server round-trip and keeps its alpha channel, you can drop it straight onto a solid color, a fresh photo, or a slide in whatever image editor or deck you open afterward. That compositing step happens outside this page, which focuses only on producing the clean, ready-to-place cut-out.

Can I swap in a new background color or photo on this page?

Not here - the output is a transparent PNG only. Placing the cut-out on a solid color or a new photo is a separate step you do afterward in whatever image editor or slide deck you drop the PNG into; this page focuses on producing a clean, uploadable-free cut-out, not compositing a new scene behind it.

Does this tool need an account or send my photo anywhere for tracking?

No. There is no signup and no analytics tied to your image. The only network request the page makes is the one-time download of the AI model's weights from the Hugging Face model hub the first time you use the tool in a given browser - after that, the model is cached offline and no further requests go out.
