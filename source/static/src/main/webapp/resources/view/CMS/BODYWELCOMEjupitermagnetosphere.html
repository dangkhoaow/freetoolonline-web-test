Which Space 3D explorer covers which scale

This explorer owns the giant-planet case: about 420 uT, roughly 20,000x Earth's volume, and a magnetotail reaching past Saturn. Two siblings cover the scales it leaves out - Earth Magnetosphere 3D for the near-Earth boundary in Earth radii, and Solar Wind Heliosphere 3D for the whole heliosphere in AU.

Rotating Jupiter's dipole field-line cage, magnetopause shell, and night-side magnetotail happens entirely on your device: the vendored three.js engine downloads once (about 0.7 MB), then stays cached for return visits. The banded planet, Saturn distance marker, and optional solar-wind stream are all drawn locally with WebGL, so nothing about the view you build is uploaded or sent to a server.

This is an educational dipole + magnetopause + magnetotail schematic - not an MHD model. Distances are compressed for one-screen teaching.
