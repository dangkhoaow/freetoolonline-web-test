Online Metronome

Last reviewed: 2026-09-04

Set a BPM and hear a steady practice click in the browser.

This page does not keep time after you close the tab and does not offer swing patterns.

What Start, Stop, and the tempo slider actually change

Metronome runs two independent things at once: a visual beat lamp that pulses from the moment the page loads, and an audible click that only plays after you press Start click. Pressing Stop turns off just the audible click - the visual beat lamp and the row of meter dots underneath it keep pulsing at the same tempo, so you can keep watching the beat with the sound off. Dragging the tempo slider while the click is already playing updates the tempo right away; Metronome does not need a Start/Stop restart to pick up a new speed. Switching the Beats per bar selector while the click is stopped rebuilds the row of meter dots to match and resets the count back to the first beat of the bar, so the accent lines up with beat one again. The tempo slider itself is limited to 40-240 BPM, and Tap tempo clamps a tapped speed into that same range if your taps land faster or slower than the slider allows.

How Metronome's click sounds and looks

Metronome accents the first beat of every bar: it plays at a higher pitch and slightly louder than the other beats, and it lights the beat lamp and the matching meter dot in a different color (the accent flashes blue, the other beats flash red) so you can see the downbeat as well as hear it. Every click, accented or not, is a short blip that fades out in about 55 milliseconds rather than a sustained tone, which keeps a fast tempo from sounding like one continuous buzz. Tap tempo only looks at your most recent 6 taps when averaging a speed, so an old, unrelated tap does not keep skewing the result. If your browser does not support the Web Audio API that the audible click depends on, pressing Start click shows an inline error message instead of doing nothing - the visual beat lamp keeps working either way since it does not need Web Audio.
