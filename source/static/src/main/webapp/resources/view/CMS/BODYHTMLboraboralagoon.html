Bora Bora is the eroded core of an extinct shield volcano: twin peaks, Mount Otemanu and Mount Pahia, rise from a shallow turquoise lagoon that is ringed by a barrier reef and scattered motu. Toggle the lagoon depth bands to see the color-coded shift from shallow to deeper water, and the motu markers to see the small sand-and-coral islets strung along the reef.
