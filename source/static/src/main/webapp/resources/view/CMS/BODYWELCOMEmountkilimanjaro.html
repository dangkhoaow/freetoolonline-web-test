Mount Kilimanjaro 3D Explorer

Last reviewed: 2026-07-29

Reviewed by FreetoolOnline editorial team

This browser explorer shows Mount Kilimanjaro's distinctive cone rising from the Tanzanian scrubland to 5,895 metres - the highest mountain in Africa, standing completely independent with no surrounding range. The dormant volcano retains a permanent snow cap despite its equatorial location, though the ice has retreated dramatically since 1912.

Kilimanjaro's height makes it Africa's highest peak and the world's highest free-standing mountain - it rises entirely above the surrounding plateau with no connection to a mountain range. The summit, Uhuru Peak, sits at 5,895 m and carries permanent ice and snow. The asymmetrical cone, steeper on the south flank and gentler on the north, rises from arid scrubland at roughly sea-level equivalent elevation.

  • Toggle the snow cap to see the permanent ice coverage of the summit region above about 4,000 m
  • Toggle altitude bands to see colour-coded elevation zones: brown scrubland base, green mid-slopes, grey high-altitude, white perpetual snow
  • Read height, eruption history, ice-loss figures, and climbing statistics in the info panel
  • Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom
  • Runs fully in the browser with the vendored three.js engine - no account, no upload
