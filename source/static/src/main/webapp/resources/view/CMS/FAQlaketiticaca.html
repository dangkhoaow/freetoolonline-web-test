Frequently Asked Questions

How high above sea level is Lake Titicaca?

About 3,812 m - it is the highest large lake on Earth and the highest lake in the world that supports commercial navigation.

How deep is Lake Titicaca?

The main Lago Grande basin reaches about 284 m, but the smaller Lago Pequeno (Winaymarka) basin rarely passes about 40 m - the two are joined by the narrow Strait of Tiquina.

Which countries share Lake Titicaca?

Peru borders the lake to the north and west, and Bolivia borders it to the south and east.

What are the Uros floating islands?

They are man-made islands woven from totora reeds near Puno, Peru, where the Uros people have lived for generations - marked in this scene by the small cluster near the west shore.

Why is Isla del Sol significant?

It is an island within Lago Grande on the Bolivian side, held sacred in Inca mythology as the legendary birthplace of the sun.

Is this a scientific terrain model?

No. It is an educational explorer using published figures - the basin profile, connecting strait, and vertical scale are schematic teaching cues, not survey data.
