Frequently Asked Questions

Why is Huacachina water green?

The water is tinted green from algae growth and mineral content. The high salinity and mineral concentration supports algae that gives the lagoon its distinctive greenish-blue color. This is why the explorer allows you to toggle between fresh and algae-tinted views.

Is Huacachina the only oasis in South America?

Huacachina is the only natural oasis in South America, making it extremely unique. The Nazca Desert region where it sits is one of the most arid places on Earth, so the presence of this freshwater lagoon is remarkable and has been valued by locals and travelers for centuries.

How tall are the dunes around Huacachina?

The sand dunes surrounding Huacachina can reach heights of over 100 metres tall. These towering dunes create the dramatic landscape that makes the oasis such a popular tourist destination for adventure activities like sandboarding and dune buggy tours.

What activities are possible at Huacachina?

Huacachina is famous for adventure tourism activities including sandboarding down the dunes, dune buggy tours, quad biking, and desert camping. Visitors come from around the world to experience the extreme desert environment and the unique landscape created by the towering dunes surrounding the oasis.

When should I visit Huacachina?

Optimal conditions occur during May through September when temperatures are cooler (around 15-20 degrees Celsius at night). The dry season runs roughly May to October. During the austral summer (December-February), temperatures can exceed 35 degrees Celsius, making daytime outdoor activities challenging.

How did Huacachina form?

Huacachina is a natural oasis that formed around underground springs feeding the lagoon. The fresh water emerging from beneath the desert creates this life-giving pool surrounded by sand dunes. The geological formation is unique to this region of southern Peru.
