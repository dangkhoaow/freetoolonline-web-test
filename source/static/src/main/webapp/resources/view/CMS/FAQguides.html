Frequently Asked Questions

What are freetoolonline.com guides?

Each guide is a short, hands-on walkthrough that pairs a real task - compress a ZIP, convert HEIC to JPG, test a laptop screen - to the exact tool on this site that does it, then walks the steps and names the trade-offs. Most take a couple of minutes to follow end to end.

Do I need an account or an install to follow a guide?

No. Every guide points to a browser tool that runs on this page, so there is nothing to download and no sign-up. Where a task is handled in your browser, your files stay on your device and are never uploaded.

How do I find the right guide for my task?

Scan the groups on this page - files and archives, images, device tests, developer, and more - and pick the one that matches what you already hold: a file, a folder, or a device to test. You can also type a keyword into the search box on the home page to jump straight to any guide or tool.

Are the guides and the tools they use free?

Yes. Every guide and every tool it links to is free to use with no usage cap and no account. The site is supported by ads, which you can turn off from the header on any page.
