Where the Magnetosphere Explorer wins

The Magnetosphere Explorer wins when the question is what the field looks like right now - its dipole tilt, its sunward magnetopause boundary under quiet versus compressed solar-wind conditions, and the surface field strength - a spatial snapshot rather than a history.


Where the Pole Wander Explorer wins

The Pole Wander Explorer wins when the question is how far the pole has actually moved and how fast - it is the only page on the site visualizing the pole's real recorded and modelled positions from James Clark Ross's 1831 discovery to NOAA's 2025 measurement that it is now drifting about four times faster than the south magnetic pole.


A reasonable rule

As a reasonable rule, use the Magnetosphere Explorer first to see the field's present-day shape and scale, then use the Pole Wander Explorer to see how the single point where that field points straight down has crawled across the Arctic over nearly two centuries.
