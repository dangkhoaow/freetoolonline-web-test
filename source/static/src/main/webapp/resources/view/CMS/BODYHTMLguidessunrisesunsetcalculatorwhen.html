Sunrise & Sunset Calculator When To Use

Last reviewed: 2026-08-04

Sunrise & Sunset Calculator is worth opening whenever you need exact sun times for a plan, not just a rough sense of "it gets dark around evening." This page covers the situations where that precision matters.


When it fits

Planning an outdoor photo shoot around golden hour, timing a sunrise hike or a sunset picnic, checking how much daylight is left before a trip, or comparing day length between two dates or two places. It also fits when you want civil dawn or dusk for an early start or a late finish while there is still enough natural light to see by.


What to expect

Pick the location and date once and the full result table appears immediately: sunrise, solar noon, sunset, day length, golden hour, and civil dawn/dusk together, in your device's own time zone. There is no signup and no waiting on a forecast to load.

The calculation runs locally in the browser - no upload and no account.
