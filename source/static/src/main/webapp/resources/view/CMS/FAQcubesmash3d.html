Frequently Asked Questions

What does Cube Smash 3D do?

It is an arena survival game: cube enemies converge on your glowing orb from the arena rim, and your dash smashes every cube inside its ring. Score rises with each smash and combo; the run ends when all three hearts are gone.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Drag on the arena to move and tap to dash. A keyboard (WASD / arrows plus Space) gives finer control on desktop.

Is anything saved in my browser?

Yes. Your best score uses localStorage key ftol:cubesmash3d:best. Nothing is uploaded - the game runs entirely client-side.

Why does my dash sometimes miss?

The dash only smashes cubes inside the yellow ring around your orb at the moment you press it, and it has a short cooldown of about one second. The ring grows slightly as you clear waves.
