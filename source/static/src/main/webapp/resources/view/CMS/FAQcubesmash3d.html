Frequently Asked Questions

What does Cube Smash 3D do?

It is a hammer-survival game: cube blobs hop toward you on a sunny meadow, your Smash Hammer swings automatically at anything in range, and smashed blobs drop gems and coins. Gems level you up; each level offers a choice of three upgrades. The run ends when your health bar empties.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Drag to move and tap to jump - the hammer already swings on its own. A keyboard (WASD / arrows, SHIFT, SPACE) plus mouse camera control gives finer play on desktop.

Is anything saved in my browser?

Yes. Your best score uses localStorage key ftol:cubesmash3d:best. Nothing is uploaded - the game runs entirely client-side.

What do the upgrades do?

Each level up offers three of six upgrades: Heavier Hammer (+40% damage, wider swing), Swift Boots (+18% speed), Hearty Meal (+30 max HP and full heal), Gem Magnet (longer pull range), Thick Skin (regeneration), and Quick Swings (25% faster hammer). The weapon pips next to Smash Hammer track hammer upgrades.
