Frequently Asked Questions

What does Cube Smash 3D do?

It is a hammer-survival game: cube blobs hop toward you on a sunny meadow, your Smash Hammer swings automatically at anything in range, and smashed blobs drop gems and coins. Gems level you up; each level offers a choice of three upgrades. The run ends when your health bar empties.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Drag to move and tap to jump - the hammer already swings on its own. A keyboard (WASD / arrows, SHIFT, SPACE) plus mouse camera control gives finer play on desktop.

Is anything saved in my browser?

Yes. Your best score uses localStorage key ftol:cubesmash3d:best. Nothing is uploaded - the game runs entirely client-side.

What do the upgrades and weapons do?

Each level up offers three picks from a pool of eight: Heavier Hammer, Quick Swings, Spin Blades (a NEW weapon - blades orbiting your character, growing +1 blade per pick), Boom Stones (a NEW weapon - splash-damage stones lobbed at the nearest blob), Swift Boots, Hearty Meal, Gem Magnet, and Thick Skin. Owned weapons show as badges with up to eight upgrade pips each.

What is the Boss Blob?

Every couple of minutes a crowned, oversized blob storms in with its own health bar under the timer. It periodically charges at high speed, hits harder than normal blobs, and bursts into a shower of gems and five coins when smashed.

Why does it get dark?

The meadow runs a day-night cycle of about two and a half minutes per day. At night the sky darkens, fireflies come out, butterflies rest, and blobs spawn about 20% faster - the clock in the corner shows Day or Night.
