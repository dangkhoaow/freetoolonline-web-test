Companion guide for the Cassini Grand Finale 3D Explorer, covering the phase slider and Play/Pause controls in detail so you can move straight to the mission phase you want to see.

The explorer itself runs entirely client-side in the browser with the vendored three.js engine - no account, no install, and no scene data leaves your device. For the wider picture, the Space 3D collection also has a DART Asteroid Deflection explorer for another real spacecraft-trajectory mission and a Titan Methane Lakes explorer for the moon Cassini studied for 13 years.
