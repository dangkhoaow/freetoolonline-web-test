How to Use the Ovulation Calculator - Step by Step

Last reviewed: 2026-09-30

The ovulation calculator needs two numbers - your last period start date and your average cycle length - and shows your estimated ovulation day, fertile window and next period immediately. Here is each field, in order.


Step 1 - open the tool

Go to the ovulation calculator. Nothing needs to be installed or signed into, and the page opens with the date field pre-set two weeks back, so a full example result is already on screen.


Step 2 - enter your dates and cycle length

Set the first day of your last period in the date field. Then set your average cycle length - the calculator accepts 21 to 45 days and defaults to 28. If you know your luteal-phase length, adjust it (10 to 16 days); if not, keep the default 14. The result recalculates on every change.


Step 3 - read the result

Three lines appear first: the estimated ovulation date, the 6-day fertile window (the 5 days before ovulation through ovulation day), and the date your next period is expected. Below them, a table projects the same three dates for the current and next two cycles. If the date field is cleared, the tool shows a prompt instead of broken dates.


Good to know

Everything runs locally in your browser - nothing you enter is stored or sent. The result is a calendar-method estimate, not medical advice, and the page says so under the result.
