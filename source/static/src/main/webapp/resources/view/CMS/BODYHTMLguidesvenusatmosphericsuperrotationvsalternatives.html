Where the Superrotation Explorer wins

The Superrotation Explorer wins when the question is how fast the atmosphere itself moves - it is the only page on the site with a live readout of cloud-top laps completed against solid-body degrees turned, on one clock tuned to the real ~60x ratio.


Where the Retrograde Rotation Explorer wins

The Retrograde Rotation Explorer wins when the question is Venus's own spin direction and day length - it scrubs Earth days across a full orbit and toggles an Earth-spin comparison, which the superrotation page does not show at all.


A reasonable rule

As a reasonable rule, use the Retrograde Rotation Explorer first to see that Venus's solid body spins backward once every ~243 days, then use the Superrotation Explorer to see that the atmosphere sitting on top of that same slow-spinning body circles the planet in only ~4 days.
