Random Team Generator

Last reviewed: 2026-07-24

The Random Team Generator splits a list of names into evenly sized, randomly assigned teams, right in your browser. Type or paste the names, choose how many teams you need, and get a fresh split in one click.

How it splits your names

  • Paste one name per line into the box above and set how many teams you want.
  • The tool shuffles the list fairly using your browser's crypto random number generator, then splits the shuffled names evenly across that many teams.
  • Each team appears as its own card. Change the names or the team count and click "Generate teams" again for a fresh split.
  • The box opens pre-filled with eight sample names already split into two teams, so a result is on screen before you type anything.

When to use the Random Team Generator

Use the Random Team Generator when you need to divide a group of people into teams or smaller groups - a sports league, a classroom, an office icebreaker, or a game night. It is not for generating a single value: the password generator, QR code generator, and barcode generator on this site each produce ONE result instead of splitting a list of names into groups.

Your names stay on your device

Everything runs in-browser with no upload and no account - the names you type never leave your device, and nothing is saved once you leave the page.
