Random Team Generator

Last reviewed: 2026-07-24

Paste one name per line into the box above, set how many teams you want, and click "Generate teams." The tool shuffles the list fairly using your browser's crypto random number generator, then splits the shuffled names evenly across that many teams and shows each team as its own card. Change the names or the team count and generate again for a fresh split.

Use this when you need to divide a group of people into teams or smaller groups - a sports league, a classroom, an office icebreaker, or a game night. It is not for generating a single value: the password generator, QR code generator, and barcode generator on this site each produce ONE result instead of splitting a list of names into groups.

Everything runs in-browser with no upload and no account - the names you type never leave your device, and nothing is saved once you leave the page.
