Yosemite Falls is North America's tallest waterfall: Yosemite Creek drops off a sheer granite cliff in three tiers - Upper Yosemite Fall, the Middle Cascades, and Lower Yosemite Fall - for a combined total of about 739 m before reaching the valley floor. Toggle the seasonal flow to compare the roaring peak-snowmelt column against the thin late-summer trickle, and toggle the tier markers to see where each of the three drops ends.

About this scene

Published figures: total height about 739 m (2,425 ft) across three tiers; Upper Yosemite Fall about 436 m (1,430 ft) single drop; Middle Cascades about 206 m (675 ft); Lower Yosemite Fall about 98 m (320 ft); fed by Yosemite Creek; located in Yosemite Valley, Yosemite National Park, California, USA.
