Comet Orbit 3D Explorer Step by Step

Last reviewed: 2026-07-14

The Comet Orbit 3D Explorer starts as soon as the page loads: drag to orbit the Sun and path, scroll to zoom, and press Play orbit. Everything renders on your own device - no install, no account.


Step 1 - find the ellipse

Step 1 - find the ellipse di adegan Comet Orbit 3D: cari lintasan eksentrik dengan Matahari dekat satu fokus; panel fakta memuat P sekitar 76 yr, q 0.586 AU, Q 35.1 AU, dan e 0.967 untuk preset mirip Halley.


Step 2 - scrub perihelion

Drag Orbit progress toward perihelion so tails lengthen; both cones stay roughly anti-sunward.


Step 3 - toggle tails

Step 3 - toggle tails di visualizer Comet Orbit 3D: klik Ion tail dan Dust tail untuk sembunyikan atau tampilkan tiap ekor, lalu layar penuh agar kontrol tetap terlihat saat Anda mengorbit tampilan.
