Yutyrannus huali, discovered in 2012, was an early feathered tyrannosauroid - an ancestor of the famous T-rex. At 9 meters long and weighing about 1.4 tonnes, it lived 125 million years ago in what is now northeastern China. This viewer lets you rotate, walk around, and compare the dinosaur with a human-sized figure.

Yutyrannus is famous for having proto-feathers covering its body - real feathering preserved in 125-million-year-old fossils. Its color is unknown (fossils don't preserve color), so the skin tone shown here is an artistic reconstruction.

How it works: The 3D model is rendered entirely in your browser using WebGL. No data is sent to our servers. Click "Walk" to step through the scene, "Rotate" to spin the dinosaur, and "Scale" to compare it with a human figure.

Important: This scene is an artistic reconstruction, not a scientific fossil model. The proportions, posture, and general anatomy are based on published skeletal data. Skin texture, color, and feather detail are interpretive - fossils don't preserve these features.
