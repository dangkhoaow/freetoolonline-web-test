Where the Curiosity Rover Traverse Explorer wins

The Curiosity Rover Traverse Explorer wins when the question is how far a real Mars vehicle has actually driven, or when comparing named ground milestones like Pahrump Hills and Vera Rubin Ridge along one continuous route.


Where the Ingenuity Explorer wins

The Ingenuity Explorer wins when the question is why flight itself is hard on Mars - it is the only page on the site with a side-by-side Earth-vs-Mars rotor-speed comparison, and it covers a mission with a definite beginning and end rather than a still-ongoing one.


A reasonable rule

As a reasonable rule, use the Ingenuity Explorer to see why flying on Mars demands a completely different rotor design than on Earth, then use the Curiosity Rover Traverse Explorer to see how far a wheeled vehicle can get by simply driving, one real milestone at a time.
