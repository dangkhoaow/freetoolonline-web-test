Thirteen Case Files is a free browser detective puzzle - read daily police reports, inspect suspect files, then protect a potential victim or arrest a suspect; one choice per day, ~10 KB, no install, no account.

Last reviewed: 2026-07-29

Reviewed by FreetoolOnline editorial team


The case

You investigate a serial killer whose crimes are tied to the number 13. Each in-game day brings fresh police reports and suspect files to weigh before you commit to a single, irreversible call.


Controls and loading

  • Press Play to load the canvas game in a same-origin iframe; it downloads in seconds and runs entirely in your browser.
  • Read the reports and inspect each suspect's file on a 320x240 canvas.
  • Click or touch to interact - no keyboard needed, so it plays on phones and tablets.
  • The build is session-only, so your progress resets each time you reload.

Privacy

Only the initial page and the game files load from this site, and nothing about your play is sent anywhere.
