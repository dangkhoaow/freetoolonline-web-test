Frequently Asked Questions

Does my recording get uploaded anywhere?

No. The file is decoded and cleaned entirely on your device. The only download is the noise-removal engine itself - about 0.1 MB, fetched once after you click the AI button and cached for next time.

What noise can it remove - and what can it not?

It is built for steady background noise behind one speaking voice: hiss, hum, fan noise, street rumble. It cannot separate loud overlapping speech, undo clipping, or fix sudden bangs, and it is not a music mastering tool - music and vocals lose quality.

What format does the cleaned file come out as?

Always a mono 48 kHz WAV, whatever you loaded (MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG, FLAC and other formats the browser can decode). Re-encode it with the audio converter on this site if you need a different format.

What is the difference between the AI button and Basic cleanup?

The AI button runs a small neural noise-suppression engine on your device and is the real cleanup. Basic cleanup needs no download at all: it applies a rumble filter and quiets the pauses, which helps, but it does much less against hiss and babble.
