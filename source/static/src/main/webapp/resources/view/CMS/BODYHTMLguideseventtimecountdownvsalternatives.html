Honest tradeoffs

A calendar invite is the better tool when you need reminders and attendance tracking: it notifies, syncs across devices, and lands in everyone's agenda. Event Time Countdown deliberately does none of that - it needs no account, stores nothing about you, and works for a one-off audience (stream viewers, forum readers) that would never accept a calendar invite from a stranger.

Hand conversion with a world-clock site works, but it is the only option in the table where daylight saving is your problem: convert "8 PM Eastern" a week before a DST changeover and it is easy to be an hour off. Event Time Countdown resolves the announced wall-clock time into one exact instant through the browser's timezone database, so the countdown and every zone row stay correct across changeovers.

A phone timer ticks reliably in the background and rings - but it counts a duration, not a calendar moment, and cannot be shared. Its in-browser sibling here is the countdown timer; for pure zone-to-zone conversion without a countdown, use the time zone converter.
