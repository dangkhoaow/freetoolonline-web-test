Frequently Asked Questions

Is my text really never uploaded?

Yes, on every tier. The rewrite runs either through the browser's own built-in AI or through a model that was downloaded to your device - there is no server on this page for your text to be sent to.

Do I have to download anything just to try it?

No. A wordy sample and its prepared rewrite are visible the moment the page loads, and on Chrome versions with the built-in Rewriter the paraphrase of your own text also needs no download at all.

What do the tone options actually change?

Neutral rephrases in plain, even wording; Formal tightens the text for a report or application; Casual loosens it for a chat message; Simpler wording swaps hard constructions for easier ones. In every case the meaning stays and only the wording changes.

How big is the model download, and how often do I pay it?

The exact size appears next to each model in the picker before the download starts - the default Llama 3.2 1B is the smallest of the three options. It is a one-time cost per machine: the browser keeps the weights cached, and the same cache serves the Private AI Chat page too.

How good is the rewrite compared with big cloud paraphrasers?

Lower - and the page says so. Small 1-3B on-device models produce a solid first draft, but they can occasionally drop a nuance or flatten a detail, so review the result before sending or publishing it. Keeping your original on screen above the rewrite exists precisely for that check.

What happens with a very long text?

On the on-device model path the input is cut at 8,000 characters so it fits the model's context window; the tool states this cap and tells you when a text was truncated. Rework longer documents in sections.

Can it fix my grammar without changing how I wrote it?

That is deliberately a different page: this tool rewrites your wording, while the AI Grammar Checker keeps your wording and only corrects errors. Both run the same private, in-browser engines.
