Son Doong is the world's largest known cave passage by volume. Toggle the two doline skylights that let jungle and sunlight pour into the passage, and the river highlight that marks the Rao Thuong river channel on the cave floor.

About this scene

Published figures: main passage about 5 km long (about 9 km of connected system); chamber up to about 200 m high and about 150 m wide; two large dolines let sunlight and jungle into the passage; discovered locally in 1990, surveyed in 2009.
