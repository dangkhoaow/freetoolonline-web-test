Choose an image or paste a screenshot, then click Extract text; the recognized text appears below once the scan finishes.

Extracting text from a photo or screenshot takes three steps:

  1. Pick an image file with the file picker, or press Ctrl+V (Cmd+V on a Mac) to paste a screenshot straight from the clipboard - either way it opens in the browser and is never uploaded to a server.
  2. Click Extract text. The first run on a device downloads the OCR engine in the background; later runs on the same device start faster.
  3. Review the recognized text in the box below - copy it or download it as a .txt file. OCR is not perfect, so double-check names, numbers, and punctuation. The Tidy text button joins words the scan split across line breaks; in browsers with a built-in proofreading model, a Fix errors with AI button can also correct likely misreads on your device.

Worked example

Raw OCR output keeps the line breaks of the original image, so words get split by hyphens and sentences get chopped mid-line. This is what the scan of a memo snippet returns:

The quarterly re- port is due Fri- day at 5 pm. Please send it to the finance team before the meeting.

One click of Tidy text joins the split words and unwraps the lines - without touching spelling:

The quarterly report is due Friday at 5 pm. Please send it to the finance team before the meeting.

Lines that end in punctuation or start a new list item keep their line breaks, so numbered lists and headings survive the tidy pass unchanged.
