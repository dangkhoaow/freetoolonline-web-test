Reach for this checklist when you want a fast, throwaway list and do not want to download anything or sign in first. Because everything stays inside one browser, you get the most from it by finishing in a single sitting on the device where you started - it will not follow you to a phone or another laptop on its own. For anything that needs reminders, due dates, or shared access, a fuller planner is the better choice; this one trades those extras for speed.
