Huacachina Oasis 3D Explorer

Last reviewed: 2026-07-28

Reviewed by FreetoolOnline editorial team

This browser explorer shows Huacachina in cross-section: a small teal lagoon at the center, ringed by wind-rippled sand dunes rising in every direction, with a set of palm markers circling the shoreline.

Huacachina sits in the Ica Region of Peru, about 300 km south of Lima. The village grew up around a small natural desert lagoon set among towering sand dunes, earning it the nickname the "Oasis of America." The village population is commonly cited around 200 residents, though the oasis draws far more visitors for sandboarding and dune-buggy tours across the surrounding dunes, which are commonly cited at up to about 100 m above the oasis floor. A local legend ties the lagoon to a mermaid or water-spirit story. The lagoon's water level has fluctuated over the decades due to regional groundwater use, and is partially maintained today by pumping - a documented local concern.

The lagoon, dune ring, and palm markers are all drawn by your own browser using WebGL, so no imagery of Huacachina is fetched from a server as you orbit the oasis. The three.js engine that draws the scene downloads a single time (about 0.7 MB) and is then cached, so returning to this desert view later loads without repeating that fetch.

This is an educational approximation - the dune ring, ripple pattern, and vertical scale are schematic teaching cues, not survey terrain.
