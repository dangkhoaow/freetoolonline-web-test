Lottery Number Generator - Browser vs Alternatives

Last reviewed: 2026-07-30

There are three common ways to pick lottery numbers: a browser tool, a lottery-terminal quick-pick, or a dedicated mobile app. Each option trades off differently on privacy, convenience, and preset formats.

When to choose each option

Use this browser tool when you want to pick numbers quickly before buying a physical ticket, when you prefer not to share your picks with an app, or when you need a custom range for a raffle. Use a terminal quick-pick when you want the official lottery provider's random numbers printed straight onto your ticket. Use a mobile app when you buy tickets digitally and want the app to fill in numbers for you.
