Frequently Asked Questions

How deep is Lake Victoria?

The maximum depth is only about 84 m, and the mean depth across the whole 68,800 sq km lake is about 40 m - shallow for a lake this large.

Why is Lake Victoria so shallow compared to its size?

Unlike rift-valley lakes such as Lake Baikal or Lake Tanganyika, Victoria sits in a broad, shallow depression rather than a deep tectonic trench, so its floor stays close to a nearly uniform depth from shore to shore.

Is Lake Victoria the source of the Nile?

It is the traditionally cited source of the White Nile, which leaves the lake at Jinja, Uganda, near the historic Ripon Falls site (now the Nalubaale Dam).

Which countries border Lake Victoria?

Kenya and Tanzania to the east and south, and Uganda to the north and west.

Has Lake Victoria ever dried up?

Yes. Geological evidence shows the lake dried up completely around 17,300 years ago during a past arid period, then refilled to roughly its present size - it is a geologically young lake, about 400,000 years old.
