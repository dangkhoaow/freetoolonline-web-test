Welcome to The House Point and Click - a free browser house adventure with no account or install. Press Play to start; nothing is uploaded and your progress stays on your device under ftol:thehousepointclick keys.

How to play

  • Click hotspots and room exits to explore each scene.
  • Open the inventory tray to keep the items you collect.
  • Move between the linked HTML scenes and follow the dialogue as the story unfolds.
  • About 18 MB loads after you press Play, then stays cached for your next visit.
