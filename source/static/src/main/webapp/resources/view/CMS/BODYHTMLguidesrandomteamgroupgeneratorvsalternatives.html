Random Team Generator Vs Alternatives

Last reviewed: 2026-07-24

Random Team Generator is one of three common ways to split a group into teams: a browser-based tool like this one, drawing names from a hat by hand, or a spreadsheet formula. Each trades off differently on speed, fairness, and what you need to set up first.


How Random Team Generator compares

Setup time0 minutes - paste names and go0 minutes, but needs paper slips and a container2-5 minutes to write the formula and sort columns
Team count supported2 to 20 teams in one clickUnlimited, but manual sorting into pilesUnlimited, limited by spreadsheet row count
Where your data goesStays on this device - runs in the browser, no uploadStays on paperStays in your spreadsheet file
Install requiredNoNoSpreadsheet software or an account
Re-run with a different splitOne click ("Generate teams" again)Reshuffle and redraw by handRe-apply the formula and re-sort

The hat-and-slips method is fine for a small, one-off group and needs no screen at all. A spreadsheet formula is worth it when the team list already lives in a spreadsheet you are tracking elsewhere. For everything in between - a quick, fair split you can redo instantly - the in-browser generator is the fastest of the three.

Generation runs entirely client-side on this tool - no inputs reach a server, and nothing is saved once you leave the page.
