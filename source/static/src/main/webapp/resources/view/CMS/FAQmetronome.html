Frequently Asked Questions

Why is there no sound until I click Start?

Browsers block audio until a user gesture. The visual beat runs on load; Start click enables Web Audio ticks.

Is anything uploaded or recorded?

No. Timing and audio run in this tab. The microphone is not used.

What BPM range is supported?

40 to 240 BPM, plus Tap tempo to match a pulse you hear.

Does the click keep running in the background?

No. Background tabs may throttle timers, and closing the tab stops the click.

Does Stop also turn off the visual beat lamp?

No. Stop only ends the audible click; the visual beat lamp and meter dots keep pulsing at the same tempo.

Can I change the tempo while the click is already playing?

Yes. Dragging the tempo slider updates the tempo immediately, with no need to press Start or Stop again.

What happens if I change Beats per bar while the click is stopped?

The row of meter dots rebuilds to match the new count, and the beat count resets back to the first beat of the bar.

Can I set the tempo below 40 or above 240 BPM?

No. Both the tempo slider and Tap tempo clamp any value outside 40-240 BPM back into that range.

Why does the first beat sound and look different from the others?

The first beat of every bar is accented: it plays at a higher pitch and louder volume, and lights the beat lamp and meter dot in a different color than the rest.

Is the click a continuous tone?

No. Each click is a short blip that fades out in about 55 milliseconds, not a sustained tone.

How many taps does Tap tempo use to calculate a speed?

It averages the gaps between your most recent 6 taps.

What happens if my browser does not support the Web Audio API?

Pressing Start click shows an inline error message instead of doing nothing. The visual beat lamp still works, since it does not depend on Web Audio.
