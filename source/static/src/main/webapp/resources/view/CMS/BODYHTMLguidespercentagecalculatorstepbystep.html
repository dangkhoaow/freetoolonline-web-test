Percentage Calculator Step By Step

Last reviewed: 2026-07-27

A percentage calculator answers three jobs: what is X% of Y, what percent is X of Y, or how much did a value change from X to Y. The Percentage Calculator runs those three modes in the browser with live updates as you type - no upload and no account.


Step 1 - open the tool

Open the Percentage Calculator tool - nothing needs to be installed or signed in before you start.


Step 2 - pick the mode and enter two numbers

Pick the mode on the Percentage Calculator dropdown (X% of Y, X is what % of Y, or percent change from X to Y) and enter two numbers. Typical uses: a discount, a tax add-on, a grade, or comparing two values as a percent change. The same two fields stay filled when you switch modes so you can compare results without retyping.


Step 3 - read the live result

Read the live Percentage Calculator result as it updates on every keystroke and mode change, computed locally in the tab. Copy the number you need and close the page when finished - nothing is stored beyond the session.


What this will not do

What this will not do is medical or financial advice - the Percentage Calculator only returns general arithmetic estimates, not tax, loan, or clinical guidance.
