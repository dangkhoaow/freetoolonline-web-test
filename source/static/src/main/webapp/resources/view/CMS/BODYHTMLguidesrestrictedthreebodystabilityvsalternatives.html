Where the Lagrange Points Explorer wins

The Lagrange Points Explorer wins when the question is simply where the five Sun-Earth points sit and which real missions - SOHO, JWST - use them, with no slider to change.


Where the Lagrange Point Stability Explorer wins

The Lagrange Point Stability Explorer wins when the question is why L1, L2, and L3 always drift away while L4 and L5 hold only under a real threshold - it is the only page on the site with a live mass-ratio slider, four real-system presets, and a genuine numerical integration deciding the outcome.


A reasonable rule

As a reasonable rule, use the Lagrange Points Explorer first to see where the five Sun-Earth points are and which spacecraft sit near them, then use the Lagrange Point Stability Explorer to see why only two of those five points could ever hold something in place - and exactly when even those two let go.
