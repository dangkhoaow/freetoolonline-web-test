AVIF to JPG Converter

Last reviewed: 2026-09-11

AVIF packs a photo into a small, modern codec that many editors, chat apps, and upload forms still cannot open. This tool re-encodes that AVIF as a widely supported JPG, so the same picture lands in a format almost any destination will accept - the usual reason being a downstream platform that accepts JPG but rejects AVIF.


How the conversion works

The page decodes your AVIF on an HTML5 canvas inside the browser and re-encodes it as a JPG. Your file opens straight from disk and never uploads to a server; the finished JPG comes back as a client-side download. Output sizing and quality are controlled by the option fields above the file picker, with defaults tuned to the most common downstream use.


When to convert AVIF to JPG

Reach for this direction when the picture has to open somewhere older or stricter than a current browser:

  • A desktop photo editor that predates AVIF and will not import the file.
  • An email client that shows JPG inline but treats an AVIF attachment as an unknown file.
  • A print shop or job-application form whose upload field lists only JPG and PNG.

Because JPG re-encodes with lossy compression, treat this as a one-way convenience step rather than an archival move - keep the original AVIF if you may want to re-edit at full quality later, since converting the JPG back will not restore the detail AVIF preserved.
