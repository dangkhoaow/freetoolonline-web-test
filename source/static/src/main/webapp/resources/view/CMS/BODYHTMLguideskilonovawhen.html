When to use Kilonova 3D Explorer

Use this explorer when you want to see the real event that was detected in both gravitational waves and light, and why that combination mattered.

  • Showing the real 1.74-second gamma-ray delay that tightened the gravity-speed-equals-light-speed constraint
  • Explaining where heavy elements like gold and platinum actually come from
  • Contrasting a neutron-star merger's real light show with a black-hole merger's total darkness

This is an educational visualization with a schematic inspiral and an illustrative gamma-ray jet angle - the real jet was not pointed at Earth for this event.
