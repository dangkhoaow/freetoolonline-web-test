Type a premise, pick a genre and a length, and get a story start you can rewrite in your own words - everything runs on this page, in your browser.

How to generate a story here

  1. Type a premise of one or two sentences - the situation your story starts from.
  2. Optionally name a main character and a setting.
  3. Pick a genre (adventure, fantasy, science fiction, mystery, romance or comedy) and a length: flash fiction of about 150 words, a short story of about 300 words, or an opening scene that ends on a hook.
  4. Press Generate story. The status line names which engine wrote the result - built-in browser AI, an on-device model you chose to download, or the labeled template scaffold.
  5. Press Copy story and rewrite the draft in your own words.
