Daylight Saving Time Countdown - Next Clock Change

Last reviewed: 2026-08-24

This page answers one question with no setup: when do the clocks change next where you are? Your timezone is auto-detected, so the result appears the moment the page opens - no date entry required.


Key features

  • States the exact local date and minute of the next change, whether the clocks go forward or back, and the size of the shift (for example 1 hour).
  • A live countdown in days, hours, minutes and seconds ticks toward the change every second, and recomputes the moment it passes.
  • Shows the UTC offset before and after the change, so you can see whether you lose or gain an hour of clock time.
  • Type to search any IANA timezone to check the next change for another place.

How it runs and your privacy

Everything is computed locally in your browser from its built-in timezone database; nothing is uploaded and no account is needed. Note the countdown reflects the timezone rules your browser ships with, so a law change a government has only just announced may not appear until browsers update.
