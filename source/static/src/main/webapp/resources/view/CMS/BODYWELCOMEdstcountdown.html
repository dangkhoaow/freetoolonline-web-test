Daylight Saving Time Countdown - Next Clock Change

Last reviewed: 2026-08-24

This page answers one question with no setup: when do the clocks change next where you are? Your timezone is auto-detected, and the result states the exact local date and minute of the next daylight saving transition, whether clocks go forward or back, and the size of the shift - with a live countdown ticking toward it in days, hours, minutes and seconds.

You can type to search any IANA timezone to check another place, and a comparison table lists the upcoming change in New York, Los Angeles, London, Berlin, Sydney and Auckland alongside your selected zone. The result also shows the UTC offset before and after the change, so you can see whether you lose or gain an hour of clock time.

Timezones that observe no daylight saving - Vietnam, India, Japan and most of Asia, for example - get a clear answer too: no clock changes in the next 13 months, with the year-round UTC offset stated. Everything is computed locally in your browser from its built-in timezone database; nothing is uploaded and no account is needed. Note the countdown reflects the timezone rules your browser ships with, so a law change a government has only just announced may not appear until browsers update.
