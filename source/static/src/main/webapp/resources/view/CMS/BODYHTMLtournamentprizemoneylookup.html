Pick a tournament from the dropdown to see its per-player payout for every round reached, from first-round exit to champion, plus the total tournament pool.

The dataset covers the singles main draws of the 2025 editions of the Australian Open, French Open (Roland-Garros), Wimbledon, and the US Open. Each tournament's figures stay in its own official payout currency - AUD, EUR, GBP, or USD - exactly as published, and a comparison table lines up all four majors side by side. Everything renders locally in the browser from the embedded dataset, and the Copy Link button shares a specific tournament's table via the URL.
