How to Use the Lottery Number Generator - Step by Step

Last reviewed: 2026-07-30

Open Lottery Number Generator, pick your lottery format, set the number of tickets, and click Generate. The full process takes under 10 seconds and requires no account.

Step 1 - Choose your lottery format

Open the tool and select your lottery from the dropdown: Powerball, Mega Millions, EuroMillions, UK 49s, Lotto 6/49, Oz Lotto, or Custom range. Each preset fills in the correct ball count and number range automatically.

Step 2 - Set the number of tickets

Use the "Number of tickets" dropdown to pick 1, 2, 3, 5, or 10 tickets. Each ticket gets its own independent draw - the same number will not appear twice within a single ticket.

Step 3 - Click Generate Lucky Numbers

Press the green Generate Lucky Numbers button. Your tickets appear immediately below the button. Main-draw balls appear in red circles and bonus or Powerball numbers appear in blue circles so you can read each ticket at a glance.

Custom range

If your lottery is not in the list, choose Custom range, then set the minimum number (at least 1), the maximum number (up to 999), and how many balls to pick per ticket. Press Generate to get your numbers.
