Santorini's lagoon is a sea-flooded volcanic caldera - a near-complete ring of cliffs broken by a channel on the southwest, with two dark lava-dome islands rising from the water near its center. About this scene

Published figures: caldera about 12 km by 7 km; maximum water depth inside the lagoon about 400 m (1,300 ft); the tallest clifftop rim (near Imerovigli) rises about 300 m (984 ft) above the sea - a total vertical relief of roughly 700 m (2,300 ft) from clifftop to caldera floor.
