Frequently Asked Questions

What does ROT13 Encoder do?

Paste text and apply ROT13 in your browser - A-Z and a-z shift by 13; digits and punctuation stay as-is. Output updates as you type; Copy puts the current result on the clipboard.

Is ROT13 encryption?

No. Not encryption for secrecy - anyone who knows ROT13 can reverse it instantly. ROT13 is its own inverse - run it again on the result to restore the original Latin letters.

Does my text leave this device?

No. The shift runs locally in your browser. Nothing you paste is saved between visits.
