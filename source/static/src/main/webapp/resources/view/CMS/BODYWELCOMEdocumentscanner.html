Document Scanner (to PDF)

Last reviewed: 2026-08-25

Document Scanner turns one or more photos of a paper document into a single PDF, right in this browser. Choose the photos, optionally tick "Enhance for text", then click Scan to PDF and download the file. Each photo becomes one page, in the order you selected them, so a receipt, a form, or a signed multi-page letter arrives as one file.


What you get

  • Exactly one PDF page per selected photo, in selection order - no page shuffling step.
  • An "Enhance for text" checkbox that applies a grayscale and contrast boost, which helps a photo taken under uneven light.
  • A file picker that also offers your camera on mobile, so you can shoot the page and scan it in one pass.
  • A Download scanned PDF button as soon as the PDF is built - nothing is queued and nothing is emailed to you.

When it fits

Reach for it when you have a few photos of a paper document and want them combined into one PDF to send or file, without installing a scanner app or signing up for a service. Each page is saved as a JPEG image at high quality inside the PDF, not as vector text, so a stack of several full-resolution phone photos can add up to a multi-megabyte file - worth checking the size before you email a long scan.


What it does not do

The tool captures and combines photos; it does not run OCR, so the pages are images rather than searchable text, and it does not auto-detect or crop to the document's edges. Frame the paper before you take the photo, or crop the photo first. When you need editable text instead of a PDF image, use image to text (OCR) instead.


How it compares with a scanner app or an upload service

Three approaches turn paper photos into a PDF: this browser tool, a desktop or phone scanner application, or an upload-based online service. They differ mainly on where your file goes and what you have to set up first.

A dedicated app is worth its install when you scan every day and want edge detection and deskewing. An upload service makes sense only when you are happy for the document to leave your device. For a one-off scan of a page you would rather keep local, this tool asks the least of you.


Privacy

Everything runs locally in this browser tab; photos are never uploaded to a server and no account is involved.
