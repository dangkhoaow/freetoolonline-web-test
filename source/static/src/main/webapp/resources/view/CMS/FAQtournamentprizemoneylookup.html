Frequently Asked Questions

What does Tennis Grand Slam Prize Money Lookup do?

Look up official singles prize money by round for the four tennis Grand Slams - Australian Open, French Open (Roland-Garros), Wimbledon, and the US Open.

When should I reach for tennis grand slam prize money lookup?

A cross-majors comparison table lines up the champion payout, first-round payout, and total pool of all four majors side by side.

What complementary tools work well alongside tennis grand slam prize money lookup?

Everything renders locally in the browser from an embedded dataset of the 2025 editions - no upload, no account, and no network request; the Copy Link button shares a specific tournament's table via the URL.

How much does a first-round loser earn at each Grand Slam?

For the 2025 editions: A$132,000 at the Australian Open, €78,000 at the French Open, £66,000 at Wimbledon, and $110,000 at the US Open - each in that tournament's own official currency.

Is Grand Slam prize money equal for men and women?

Yes. Singles prize money is equal for men and women at all four Grand Slams, at every round from the first round through the champion.

Does the lookup include doubles or qualifying prize money?

No - only singles main-draw prize money for the four Grand Slams is in the embedded dataset. Doubles, mixed doubles, qualifying, and wheelchair draws are not covered.
