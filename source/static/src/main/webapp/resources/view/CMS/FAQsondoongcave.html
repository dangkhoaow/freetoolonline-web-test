Frequently Asked Questions

Why is Son Doong called the world's largest cave?

It is the largest known cave passage by volume - sections of its main chamber run up to about 200 m high and about 150 m wide along a passage about 5 km long.

What are the dolines in Son Doong?

Dolines are places where part of the cave ceiling collapsed long ago, opening a skylight. Son Doong has two large dolines, each with its own patch of jungle growing on the cave floor below the light.

When was Son Doong discovered and explored?

A local farmer found the entrance in 1990. The passage was first surveyed by the British Cave Research Association in 2009, which confirmed its size.

Is there a river inside Son Doong?

Yes - the Rao Thuong river runs through part of the cave, and its flow varies with the season.

Where is Son Doong Cave located?

In Phong Nha-Ke Bang National Park, Quang Binh Province, Vietnam, part of the Phong Nha-Ke Bang UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Can visitors go inside Son Doong?

Only through limited guided multi-day treks with capped permits, offered since 2013 - it is not open for casual walk-in visits.

Is this a scientific cave survey model?

No. It is an educational explorer using published figures - the passage shape, doline openings, and vertical scale are schematic teaching cues, not survey cave geology.
