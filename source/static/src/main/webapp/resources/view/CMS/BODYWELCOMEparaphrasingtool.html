AI Paraphrasing Tool - Private, In Your Browser

Last reviewed: 2026-08-12

Reword a sentence or passage without sending it to anyone. The rewrite runs on your own device: Chrome versions with built-in AI paraphrase with zero download, and every other modern browser can load a small open model with one click. Nothing you paste is uploaded anywhere, on any tier.


What you get back

The result is a rewrite that keeps your meaning while changing the wording. Your original stays in the input box above the result, so you can read the two against each other before deciding, and one click on Copy result puts the rewrite on the clipboard. This page changes how a text sounds - to fix grammar mistakes while keeping your exact wording, use the AI Grammar Checker instead.


Four tones, one job

A tone selector steers the rewrite: Neutral rephrases in plain, even wording; Formal tightens it for a report or an application; Casual loosens it for a chat message; Simpler wording keeps the register but swaps hard constructions for easier ones. Rewording a stiff paragraph into something friendlier - or the reverse - is the same one job with a different tone picked, so there is no separate "tone changer" to hunt for.

Honest limits before you rely on it

The on-device models are small (1-3 billion parameters). They produce a solid first draft - review it before sending or publishing, because a small model can occasionally drop a nuance or flatten a detail a larger cloud rewriter would keep. On the model path, input is capped at 8,000 characters and the cap is stated right in the tool. Rewrites are validated for English text, and there is no document upload - paste the text itself.


Where your data and the downloads live

Text stays in the page while you work and is not stored beyond the session; there is no account and no signup. The model cache and your last model choice are shared with the Private AI Chat page, so loading a model once serves both pages, and a Clear cached models button removes the downloaded weights whenever you want the disk space back.
