Mount Fuji

Last reviewed: 2026-07-29. By the FreeToolOnline team.

Elevation 3,776 m (12,388 ft)
Type Stratovolcano (inactive)
Location Honshu, Japan (Shizuoka / Yamanashi)
First ascent ~7th century; modern trails 1707 (Hoei eruption)
UNESCO status World Heritage Site (2013)

Mount Fuji is Japan's most iconic mountain and a symbol of the nation. Its perfectly symmetrical cone shape, rising 3,776 meters above sea level, is instantly recognizable across art, culture, and geography. The mountain is considered sacred in Shinto tradition and has inspired artists, poets, and pilgrims for centuries.

Educational note: This visualization is a schematic teaching aid combining published survey data and cultural significance. Real Mount Fuji experiences dramatic seasonal changes - the snow cap forms around October and typically melts by mid-July, while the forested zone transitions from larch to dwarf pine at higher elevations.
