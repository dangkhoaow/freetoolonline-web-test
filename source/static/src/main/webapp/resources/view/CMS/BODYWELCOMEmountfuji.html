Mount Fuji 3D Explorer

Mount Fuji rises 3,776 meters above sea level on the Shizuoka-Yamanashi border, Japan's most iconic volcanic cone. Its perfectly symmetrical shape, eternal snow cap, and deep cultural reverence make it a global symbol of Japanese natural beauty. Approximately 300,000 climbers annually attempt the summit via four main routes during the July-September climbing season.

Key Facts

Height3,776 m (12,388 ft)
Base Diameter~50 km
Last Eruption1707-1708 (Hoei)
Age~100,000 years
Climbing SeasonJuly - September

Climbing Routes

Yoshida Trail (northeast): 5-6 hours, most popular. Subashiri Trail (southeast): 6-8 hours. Gotemba Trail (southwest): 8-10 hours, longest. Fujinomiya Trail (south): 4-5 hours, shortest. Use the toggles to visualize elevation bands and climbing paths.
