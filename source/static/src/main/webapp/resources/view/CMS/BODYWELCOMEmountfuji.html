Mount Fuji 3D Explorer

Mount Fuji is Japan's most iconic mountain and tallest peak at 3,776 meters above sea level. The nearly perfect symmetric cone rises majestically from the landscape near Tokyo, visible from about 100-130 kilometers away on clear days. This explorer lets you rotate around the volcano and toggle two key features: the snow-capped summit (visible roughly November through May) and the perpetual cloud bands that often encircle the mountain's lower slopes.

The last eruption occurred in 1707-1708 (the Hoei era eruption, lasting 49 days), and Mount Fuji has been dormant since then. The mountain holds profound cultural significance in Japanese tradition and aesthetics, and it was designated a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2013.
