Mount Fuji 3D Explorer

Last reviewed: 2026-07-28

Reviewed by FreetoolOnline editorial team

This browser explorer shows Mount Fuji as a schematic solitary stratovolcano: a smooth symmetric cone rising to a 3,776 m summit crater, a snow cap above the schematic snowline, and five lake markers ringing the base.

Mount Fuji stands on Honshu island, about 100 km southwest of Tokyo, straddling Shizuoka and Yamanashi prefectures. Its almost-perfect conical symmetry comes from repeated eruptions building the volcano in even layers of lava and ash; the last eruption was the Hoei event of 1707-1708. The Fuji Five Lakes - Kawaguchiko, Yamanakako, Saiko, Shojiko, and Motosuko - sit at its northern base and are a classic viewpoint for the mountain. UNESCO listed Mount Fuji as a cultural World Heritage Site in 2013 for its role as a sacred site and wellspring of Japanese art.

  • Toggle the snow cap above the schematic snowline
  • Toggle the Fuji Five Lakes markers at the base
  • Read summit, eruption, and heritage figures in the panel
  • See the summit crater at the top of the cone
  • Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom
