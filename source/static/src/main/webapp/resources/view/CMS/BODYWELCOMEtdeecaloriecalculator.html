TDEE & Calorie Calculator

Last reviewed: 2026-07-24

Estimate daily calorie needs from sex, age, weight, height, and activity level.


Key features

  • Enter sex, age, weight, and height in metric or imperial, pick an activity level, and BMR plus TDEE update as you type.
  • Set a starting calorie target for maintaining, losing, or gaining weight.
  • No upload and no account - the calculation runs locally in your browser.

How it works

Uses the Mifflin-St Jeor formula to estimate BMR (calories at rest), then applies your activity level to get TDEE (total daily energy expenditure) and a loss/gain target 500 kcal/day either side of maintenance.


When to use it

Use this calculator when you want a calorie target for maintaining, losing, or gaining weight - it is not the same check as the BMI calculator (weight-for-height only, no calorie number). Not sure this is the right fit? See when to use the TDEE & calorie calculator.


Limits

Does not account for medical conditions, medications, or individual metabolic variation - this is a population-average estimate, not medical or nutritional advice.
