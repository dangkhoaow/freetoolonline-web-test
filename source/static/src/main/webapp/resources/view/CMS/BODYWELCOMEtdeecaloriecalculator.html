TDEE & Calorie Calculator

Last reviewed: 2026-09-04

Estimate daily calorie needs from sex, age, weight, height, and activity level.


Key features

  • Enter sex, age, weight, and height in metric or imperial, pick an activity level, and BMR plus TDEE update as you type.
  • Set a starting calorie target for maintaining, losing, or gaining weight.
  • No upload and no account - the calculation runs locally in your browser.

How it works

Uses the Mifflin-St Jeor formula to estimate BMR (calories at rest), then applies your activity level to get TDEE (total daily energy expenditure) and a loss/gain target 500 kcal/day either side of maintenance.


When to use it

Use this calculator when you want a calorie target for maintaining, losing, or gaining weight - it is not the same check as the BMI calculator (weight-for-height only, no calorie number). Not sure this is the right fit? See when to use the TDEE & calorie calculator.


Limits

Does not account for medical conditions, medications, or individual metabolic variation - this is a population-average estimate, not medical or nutritional advice.


What happens when you switch units or enter an out-of-range value

TDEE & Calorie Calculator does not convert the number already sitting in the weight or height box when you switch the metric/imperial toggle - it only relabels the field and reinterprets those same digits under the new unit. If you typed 70 while "Metric" was selected and then switch to "Imperial", the box still reads 70, but the calculation now treats it as 70 lb instead of 70 kg, so re-enter both weight and height after switching units. Age is raised to a floor of 15 if you type anything lower, and falls back to 30 if the field cannot be read as a number at all. The activity-level dropdown defaults to moderate (1.55x) when no level is present and never applies less than the sedentary floor (1.2x). A weight or height of zero or less, checked after the unit conversion above, blocks the estimate with an inline message instead of showing a wrong number.


Why the numbers update without a submit button

TDEE & Calorie Calculator has no calculate button. Every field - the unit toggle, sex, age, weight, height, and activity level - is wired to refresh BMR, TDEE, and both the loss and gain targets the instant you type a digit or pick a new option, so the four numbers stay in sync with whatever is currently in the form. There is no separate "recalculate" step and nothing is sent anywhere to produce the update; the same four numbers you saw a keystroke ago are simply replaced with the new ones in place.
