Crystal Cave Flyer is a one-button 3D flight through an endless glowing cavern: hold Space, the mouse button, or a touch to climb, release to sink, and thread the circular gaps in the rock walls ahead. Crystals floating in the gaps add score, the cave speeds up as you fly deeper, and one wall clip ends the run.
