About this scene

The facts panel fills in when the scene loads. Adjust diameter, speed, and entry angle, then press Send it in.

The model places the ablation-onset altitude inside the real 75-100 km band using entry speed alone, then sorts the outcome into one of three bands: ablates high up with no fireball, a Chelyabinsk-class airburst, or a larger fragmenting descent. This page is a parametric what-if tool for any size, speed, and angle you choose - not a fixed replay of one historical case.
