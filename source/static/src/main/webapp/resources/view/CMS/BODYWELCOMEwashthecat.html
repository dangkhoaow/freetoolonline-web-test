Welcome to Wash The Cat - a free browser pipe puzzle you can play without an account or install. Press Play to load the ~50 KB canvas engine in a same-origin iframe, then route water toward the shower until the cat is clean enough to finish the level.

How to play

  1. Press Play to load the game canvas in a same-origin iframe on this page.
  2. Click valves and taps to open and close them, routing water through the tanks and pipes.
  3. Guide the flow to the shower head until the cat is clean enough to clear the level.
  4. Clear a level to open the next one; the nine levels play in a fixed order.

Levels and progress

Your highest cleared level is stored in localStorage under ftol:washthecat:lj on this device only and is never uploaded, so returning on the same device keeps your progress.

Privacy

Nothing leaves this device except the initial page and game files from this site.
