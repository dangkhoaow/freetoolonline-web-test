WebP to JPG Converter

Last reviewed: 2026-07-09

Convert a WebP photo to a JPG in the browser, in one step.


Key features

  • Output is fixed to JPG; the sizing and quality fields sit above the file picker, with defaults tuned to the most common downstream use.
  • WebP to JPG Converter opens your file straight from disk and paints it onto an HTML5 canvas in this browser tab.
  • The canvas re-encodes the image as a JPG and hands it back as a direct download link built on your device.

Common uses for WebP to JPG Converter

This converter helps when a downstream tool accepts JPG but will not take a WebP:

  • a photo viewer that will not open a WebP
  • a print workflow that expects a JPG
  • an upload form that rejects WebP files

Transparency and quality of the JPG output

DetailWhat to expect
TransparencyA JPG has no alpha channel, so the result comes out fully opaque - any transparency the source WebP carried is not preserved.
Quality and sizeWhere your browser supports it, a quality slider trades file size against visual fidelity before you download.

Privacy and data handling

Because the canvas re-encode and the download are both built on your device, the WebP you pick in this converter is never uploaded to a server. Nothing you convert is kept once the page session ends, and none of it is passed to an analytics or tracking service. Saving the JPG needs no account and no signup.
