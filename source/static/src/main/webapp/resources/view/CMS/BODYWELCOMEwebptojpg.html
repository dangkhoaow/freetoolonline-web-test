WebP to JPG Converter

Last reviewed: 2026-07-09

Convert a WebP photo to a JPG in the browser, in one step.

Typical use: a photo viewer, a print workflow, or an upload form that expects JPG and will not accept a WebP.

Output sizing and quality are set by the option fields above the file picker, with defaults tuned to the most common downstream use. A JPG has no alpha channel, so the result comes out fully opaque - any transparency the source WebP carried is not preserved. Where your browser supports it, a quality slider trades file size against visual fidelity before you download.

Your WebP is opened straight from disk and painted onto an HTML5 canvas in this browser tab, then re-encoded as a JPG that the page hands back as a direct download link. Because the canvas re-encode and the download are both built on your device, the WebP you pick here is never uploaded to a server. Nothing you convert is kept once the page session ends, and none of it is passed to an analytics or tracking service. Saving the JPG needs no account and no signup.

Need the opposite direction? Use the JPG to WebP converter, or browse the image conversion tools hub for other formats.
