Angel Falls is the world's tallest waterfall: the Churun River pours off the sheer edge of Auyan-tepui, a table mountain in Venezuela's Canaima National Park, in one long free-fall of about 807 m before the lower cascades carry it the rest of the way to the valley floor, about 979 m below the rim. About this scene

Published figures: total height about 979 m (3,212 ft); longest single, uninterrupted free-fall drop about 807 m (2,648 ft); fed by the Churun River off the edge of Auyan-tepui, summit elevation about 2,535 m (8,320 ft); located in Canaima National Park, Bolivar State, Venezuela; indigenous Pemon name Kerepakupai Vena.
