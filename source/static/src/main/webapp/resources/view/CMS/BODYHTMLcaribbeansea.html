The Caribbean Sea is not a bowl of even depth - it is a broad basin ringed by the Greater Antilles to the north and the curving Lesser Antilles to the east, with a narrow, far deeper notch cut into the northwest floor: the Cayman Trough. Toggle the Cayman Trough highlight to see how much deeper that one notch runs, or switch on the Antilles island arc to see the ring of islands framing the basin.
