Welcome to Rollermaze - a free browser maze puzzle you can play without an account or install. Roll through tile mazes, trigger floor buttons to open routes, dodge chasing sentinels, and beat the 13-second countdown to the forest exit.


What loads and what is saved

Press Play to load the ~59 KB WebGL game in a same-origin iframe. Nothing is saved on this device; only the initial page and game files load from this site.

For more free browser games, open the games hub.
