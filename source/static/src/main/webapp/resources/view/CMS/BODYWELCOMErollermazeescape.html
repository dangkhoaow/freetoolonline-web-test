Rollermaze is a free browser maze puzzle you can play with no account and no install - guide a rolling marble to the forest exit before the 13-second countdown runs out.


How to play

  • Roll through the tile maze to find a path toward the forest exit.
  • Step on floor buttons to open blocked routes.
  • Dodge the sentinels that chase you across the maze.
  • Beat the 13-second countdown to reach the exit in time.

What loads and what is saved

Press Play to load the ~59 KB WebGL game in a same-origin iframe. Nothing is saved on this device; only the initial page and game files load from this site.
