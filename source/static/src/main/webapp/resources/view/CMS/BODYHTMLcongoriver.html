About this scene

Once the Congo River scene finishes loading, this panel fills with its published figures and a reminder that the model is an educational approximation rather than survey terrain. The controls that appear beneath the scene let you raise or hide the deepest-point rod and the Livingstone Falls rock outcrops.

Published figures: length about 4,700 km; maximum sounded depth over 220 m; average discharge about 41,000 m3/s at the mouth; basin area about 4,000,000 km2; Livingstone Falls drops roughly 270 m over about 350 km before the Atlantic.

Drag to orbit and scroll or pinch to zoom. Everything renders on your device with WebGL.
