Caspian Sea 3D Explorer

Last reviewed: 2026-07-27

Reviewed by FreetoolOnline editorial team

This browser explorer shows the Caspian Sea as three connected basins in cross-section: a shallow northern shelf, a mid-depth middle basin, and a southern basin that plunges past 1,000 m - the depth contrast that makes this landlocked giant behave more like an ocean than a lake.

At about 371,000 sq km the Caspian is the largest inland body of water on Earth, bordered by Russia, Kazakhstan, Turkmenistan, Iran, and Azerbaijan. It has no outlet to any ocean, so its surface sits about 28 m below world ocean level and is fed mainly by the Volga River. Despite the name, low salinity in the north (closer to fresh water) versus the saltier south is one more way the "sea" is really an enormous endorheic lake.

  • Toggle the depth bands to see the shallow north, middle, and deep south basins compared
  • Toggle the sea-level plane to compare the surface with 0 m world ocean level
  • Read area, depth, and basin figures in the panel
  • Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom
  • Runs fully in the browser with the vendored three.js engine - no account, no upload
