Convert HD video to MP4, WebM, MOV, AVI, or MKV - or pull the audio track to MP3, AAC, OGG, FLAC, M4A, or WAV - right in your browser. Files stay on your device: the converter runs locally via FFmpeg.wasm, with no upload, no install, and no account.


Key features

  • Convert in the browser. Pick the output format from the dropdown above the file picker, drop the source, and download the result.
  • Extract just the audio. Pull the track out as MP3, AAC, OGG, FLAC, M4A, or WAV without sending the source to a server.
  • Choose the MP3 bitrate. 128 kbps for a small file, 192 kbps standard (the default), 256 kbps high, or 320 kbps maximum; FLAC and WAV stay lossless and ignore the setting.
  • Make a GIF preview. A clip becomes a GIF downsampled to 10 frames per second and 480 px wide, small enough to share.

When to use it

When to use it: open HD Online Video Converter when you would rather not upload a clip, when the network is slow, or when the source should stay local; pick remux to preserve bitrate or re-encode when the target player needs a different codec. It is the right pick when the source file is already in good shape and the only thing between you and a usable copy is a container or codec mismatch - an MP4 a teammate cannot open, or a format an editor rejects.


Advanced controls (optional re-encode)

The container swap above copies the video stream untouched. When a repackage is not enough, the optional controls re-encode instead: pick a video codec (H.264, H.265, or VP9), set a quality level from 1 (best) to 5 (smallest), choose a 720p, 480p, 360p, or 9:16 vertical preset or a custom width and height, set a frame rate, add a fade in and out, or split the output into fixed-length segments. Each one runs the same way as a plain convert - in your browser through FFmpeg.wasm, with nothing uploaded. Re-encoding rewrites the video, so it takes longer than a container swap; reach for it when you need a smaller file, a specific codec or resolution, a vertical clip for a phone-first feed, or several short segments instead of one long file. Leave those controls untouched and the convert stays a fast stream copy.
