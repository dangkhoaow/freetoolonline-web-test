Signature Maker - Draw & Download a Signature

Last reviewed: 2026-07-25

Draw a signature with your mouse, finger, or stylus, then download it as a PNG - no upload, no account, and nothing you draw leaves your browser.

Key features

  • Pick a pen color and width, then sign inside the box.
  • Use Undo to remove the last stroke, or Clear to start over.
  • Download with a white background for documents, or turn on the transparent-background option to paste the signature over a contract, letterhead, or slide.

Privacy and limits

Refreshing or leaving the page clears the canvas - nothing is saved between visits, and the tool does not verify a signature's authenticity or match it to a real person's handwriting.
