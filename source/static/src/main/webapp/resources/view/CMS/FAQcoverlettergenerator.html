Frequently Asked Questions

Is my CV or the job description uploaded anywhere?

No. Every engine tier - the instant template letter, the built-in browser AI, and the optional on-device model - runs inside your browser. The posting and your highlights are never uploaded, nothing is stored beyond the page session, and no account is asked for.

Does it work without downloading an AI model?

Yes. With zero download the template engine assembles a complete letter - salutation, an opening naming the role, one paragraph from your highlights, a line naming what the posting emphasizes, and a closing matched to your tone. If your browser ships built-in writing AI, the page uses that too, still with nothing to download.

How good are the on-device AI drafts?

The optional models are small, 1-3 billion parameters, so expect a solid first draft rather than a finished letter. The download size is shown before the download starts, the files are cached for later visits, and the status line always names which mode wrote the letter on screen.

Can the letter claim experience I do not have?

The template mode literally cannot - it only rearranges the facts you typed. The on-device model is instructed to use only your pasted posting and highlights, but small models can still word things loosely, so read every line before sending. The tool cannot read your CV file or verify your claims.

How is this different from the AI Email Writer?

That page composes an email with a subject line from a short brief. This one writes a formal letter - salutation, three paragraphs, sign-off, no subject line - grounded in two documents: the job description and your CV highlights. For work correspondence use the AI email writer; for a job application use this page.
