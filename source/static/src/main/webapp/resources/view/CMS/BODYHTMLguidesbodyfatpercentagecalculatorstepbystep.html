Body Fat Percentage Calculator Online Step By Step

Last reviewed: 2026-08-01

Body Fat Percentage Calculator Online runs in three steps: open the tool, provide your input, then copy the result. This page walks through what each step expects.


Step 1 - open the tool

Go to Body Fat Percentage Calculator Online. Nothing needs to be installed or signed in before you start.


Step 2 - provide your input

Pick your sex and preferred unit (metric cm or imperial in), then enter height, neck, and waist - plus hip if you selected female. A soft tape measure at the narrowest point of the neck and waist gives the most consistent result.


Step 3 - copy the result

The estimated body-fat percentage and fitness category appear below the inputs and update as you adjust any number. The calculation runs locally in the browser - no upload and no account.

Switch between metric and imperial units at any time to double-check a measurement.
