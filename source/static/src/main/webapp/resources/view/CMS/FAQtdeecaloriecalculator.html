Frequently Asked Questions

What does TDEE & Calorie Calculator do?

Estimate daily calorie needs from sex, age, weight, height, and activity level.

When should I reach for tdee & calorie calculator?

Use it to set a starting calorie target for maintaining, losing, or gaining weight.

How is the calorie estimate calculated?

Uses the Mifflin-St Jeor formula for BMR, then applies an activity multiplier to estimate total daily energy expenditure (TDEE), with a loss and gain target 500 kcal/day either side of maintenance.

Does switching between metric and imperial convert the numbers I already typed?

No. TDEE & Calorie Calculator reinterprets the same digits under the new unit instead of converting them, so re-enter your weight and height after switching units.

What happens if I type an age younger than 15?

The calculator raises it to 15, the youngest age it will estimate for. An age field that cannot be read as a number falls back to 30 instead.

What activity level does TDEE & Calorie Calculator use if none is selected?

It falls back to moderate (1.55x); the lowest multiplier it will ever apply is sedentary (1.2x).

What happens if weight or height is zero?

TDEE & Calorie Calculator shows "Enter a weight and height greater than zero" instead of a number, checked after converting whichever unit you have selected.

Do I need to click a button to see the estimate update?

No. BMR, TDEE, and the loss/gain targets recompute the instant you type a digit or change any dropdown, including the activity level and the metric/imperial toggle.
