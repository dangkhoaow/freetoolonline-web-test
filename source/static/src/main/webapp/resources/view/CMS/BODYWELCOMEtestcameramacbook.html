MacBook Camera Test

Last reviewed: 2026-10-21

Confirm your MacBook's built-in FaceTime HD camera works right in the browser - click Start, grant permission once, and watch the live preview.

What this test checks

  • Live preview. The video feed appears the moment permission is granted, so you can see exactly what the FaceTime HD camera captures.
  • Resolution readout. The page reports the detected capture resolution (for example 1280x720 or 1920x1080) so you can confirm the camera is delivering the quality you expect.
  • Privacy. Nothing is recorded, captured, or uploaded - the preview stays on your device, and the Stop button releases the camera so its indicator light turns off.
  • Blocked permission. If the browser has blocked camera access, the page names the exact next step to allow it in your site permissions.
  • Multiple cameras. When more than one camera is connected, switch between the built-in and an external camera without reloading the page.
