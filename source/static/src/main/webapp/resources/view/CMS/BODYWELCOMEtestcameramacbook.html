MacBook Camera Test

Last reviewed: 2026-10-21

Confirm your MacBook's built-in FaceTime HD camera works right in the browser - click Start, grant permission once, and watch the live preview.

Nothing is recorded, captured, or uploaded - the preview stays on your device, and the Stop button releases the camera so its indicator light turns off.

If the browser has blocked camera access, the page names the exact next step to allow it in your site permissions.
