Boing Cat Platformer

Boing Cat Platformer is a free browser auto-run platformer you can play without an account or install. Press Play to load the ~130 KB canvas engine in a same-origin iframe, then press Space or a gamepad button on the title screen to start.


Key features

  • Auto-run movement - your cat runs forward on its own, so you control only jump timing.
  • Space on a keyboard or a gamepad bottom face button jumps; no other input exists.
  • Three tile-built levels, each ending at a flag held by a duck friend.
  • Session-only play - no save data in this build.

How it plays

Time each Space press to hop over spikes and gaps while the cat auto-runs across the three levels. Falling into a gap or touching a spike ends the run immediately. Reach the flag a duck friend holds to clear a level, then clear all three for a final score screen.


Privacy

Nothing leaves this device except the initial page and game files from this site.
