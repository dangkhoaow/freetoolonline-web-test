Khi nao choi Layer Flip Platformer

Layer Flip Platformer hop khi ban muon van ngan tren ban phim: lat lop bang Space, qua man lam tay, roi thoat khong can luu. Tai khoang ~44 KB sau Play, khong can tai khoan.

Last reviewed: 2026-07-17

Khi nao hop

Layer Flip Platformer phu hop khi ban co vai phut, ban phim hoac gamepad, va thich timing puzzle-platform hon duoi diem vo tan. Co che dac trung la lat lop dang dung giua nhay de platform va gai doi cho nhau.

Khi nao khong phai lua chon dung

Layer Flip Platformer khong phai lua chon dung neu ban can dieu khien chi cam ung (ban build nay khong co), neu muon endless runner nhu Pixel Spike Run, hoac thich platforming one-tap nhu One Tap Platformer.

Ky vong gi

Ky vong gi la Layer Flip Platformer voi hai muoi lam man thu cong, dem chet va sao, Web Audio thu tuc, va menu tieu de co huong dan dieu khien. Chuyen the tu ONOFF (js13kGames 2018, MIT). Mien phi, khong quang cao trong iframe, khong cai dat.
