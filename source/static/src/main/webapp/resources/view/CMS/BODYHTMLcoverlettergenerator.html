Nothing is uploaded on any engine tier: the job posting and your CV material stay on your device from paste to copy.

Five steps, all in your browser:

  1. Paste the job description into the first box - it stays on your device.
  2. Add a few highlights from your CV, one per line.
  3. Pick a tone: professional, enthusiastic, or short and direct.
  4. Press Draft cover letter - a complete letter appears instantly, no download needed.
  5. Edit anything, then use the copy button to paste the letter into your application.
