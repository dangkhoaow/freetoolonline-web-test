About this scene

The facts panel fills when the scene loads.

The eight E buttons jump through the ellipticity sequence E0 (round) to E7 (the most flattened ellipticals actually observed); S0 is the lenticular transition class; the a/b/c buttons scrub bulge size and arm-winding tightness on whichever branch - normal spiral or barred spiral - is selected. "Real-galaxy challenge" picks a real, named galaxy and gives you a clue; set your own guess with the buttons above, then press Reveal to see its real classification and the sourced fact behind it.
