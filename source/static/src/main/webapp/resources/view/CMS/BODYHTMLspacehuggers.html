Space Huggers - js13k Platformer

Keyboard Controls: Arrow Keys to move, Space to jump, E to throw enemy

How to Play Space Huggers

Space Huggers is a roguelike run-and-gun platformer where you dodge enemies and destroy obstacles. Dodge incoming attacks, throw enemies to clear obstacles, and survive as long as possible. Each run gets progressively harder with new enemies and hazards.

Game Controls

  • Arrow Keys - Move left/right and look up/down
  • Space - Jump
  • E - Grab and throw enemies

Game Features

  • Destructible Environments - Break blocks and obstacles by throwing enemies at them
  • Progressive Difficulty - New enemies and hazards appear as you survive longer
  • Roguelike Gameplay - Each run is unique with randomized enemy placement
  • No Installation - Play directly in your browser, no download required
  • Free and Open Source - GPL-3.0 licensed game by Frank Force
