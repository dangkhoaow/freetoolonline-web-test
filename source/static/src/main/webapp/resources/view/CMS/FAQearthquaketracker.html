Frequently Asked Questions

What does Recent Earthquakes Near Me (USGS Live Feed) do?

It reads the US Geological Survey's live GeoJSON feed for the past hour, day, or week, and lists earthquakes at or above the magnitude you choose, with time, place, and depth.

How do I sort earthquakes near my own location?

Press "Sort by distance from me" to let the tool use your browser location (this asks for location permission); results then sort nearest first with a distance column, instead of most-recent-first.

What happens if nothing matches my filters?

The tool shows a clear "No earthquakes at or above that magnitude" message rather than leaving the page blank.

How current is this data?

The feed comes directly from USGS and typically updates within minutes of an event, but it is not an early-warning system - always follow official emergency guidance during an active event.
