About this scene

The facts panel fills in when the scene loads. Scrub the event timeline, toggle the planet spike, or press Play event to watch a full microlensing brightness curve.

Play event advances one full brightening event in about 14 seconds; pause and scrub 0 to 100 percent to hold the lens star mid-alignment while the curve below traces the exact brightness shape at that moment.

The main curve is a Paczynski-style single-lens brightening; the planet toggle adds the short secondary spike a real orbiting planet contributes to the same light curve. This is an educational approximation of the geometry and the real curve shape, not a ray-traced simulation and not a specific observed event.
