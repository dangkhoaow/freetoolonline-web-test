Frequently Asked Questions

What does the discount calculator do?

It turns an original price and a percent off into the sale price and the amount you save, live as you type. An optional extra coupon and an optional sales-tax percent extend the result to the final price at the register.

How are stacked discounts calculated?

Multiplicatively: the extra coupon applies to the already-reduced price, not the original. 25% off followed by an extra 10% coupon is 32.5% off in total - not 35% - and the result states the true combined percentage whenever a coupon is entered.

Does it include sales tax?

Optionally. Enter a flat tax percent and it is applied after the discounts, with the tax amount and the final register price added to the breakdown table. It is a plain percent you supply - not a lookup of your region's tax rate.

Does anything I enter leave my browser?

No. The math runs locally in the page - nothing is uploaded, stored or sent, and no account is needed.

Why does the store's register show a slightly different total?

Stores may round each line item differently (and some apply tax per item rather than on the subtotal), so the register total can differ from this result by a cent. The calculator is plain arithmetic on the numbers you enter - it is not pricing or tax advice.
