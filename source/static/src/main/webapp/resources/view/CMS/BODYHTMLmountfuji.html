About this scene

About Mount Fuji: Japan's highest mountain at 3,776 m above sea level. The iconic symmetric cone volcano is visible from Tokyo on clear days (about 100-130 km away). Mount Fuji has not erupted since 1707-1708 and is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. The snow-capped peak is visible for about 6 months each year (November through May in the Northern Hemisphere).
