Mount Fuji - Key Facts
Elevation: 3,776 m (highest peak in Japan) | Base: ~50 km diameter | Crater: 500-600 m
Geology: Composite stratovolcano formed ~100,000 years ago. Last eruption: 1707-1708 (Hoei). Currently dormant.
Access: 4 main climbing routes - Yoshida (popular), Subashiri, Gotemba (long), Fujinomiya. Official season: July-September. ~300,000 climbers annually.
