Frequently Asked Questions

How tall is Table Mountain?

Its highest point, Maclear's Beacon, sits at 1,085 m (3,563 ft) above sea level, overlooking Cape Town, South Africa.

Why is the top of Table Mountain flat?

The summit is capped by Table Mountain Sandstone, a hard rock layer deposited roughly 450-500 million years ago. Erosion wore away the softer surrounding rock while the resistant sandstone cap stayed largely intact, leaving a long, level table bounded by steep cliffs.

What is the "tablecloth" cloud?

When the southeaster wind blows moist air up from the Atlantic, it condenses into an orographic cloud on the flat summit. The cloud caps the top and spills down over the northern cliff edge, resembling a tablecloth draped over the mountain - hence the name.

How do you get to the top of Table Mountain?

The Table Mountain Aerial Cableway, which opened October 4, 1929 and introduced a rotating cable car in 1997, carries visitors from Tafelberg Road to the upper station in about 5 minutes. Hiking trails also reach the summit for those who prefer to walk up.

How long is the plateau at the top?

The flat summit runs roughly north-south for about 3 km (1.9 mi), making it more of an elongated table than a single peak.

Why does Table Mountain matter for plant life?

The mountain sits within the Cape Floral Region Protected Areas, a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2004, and hosts more than 2,200 plant species on its own - by widely cited comparison, more species than the entire British Isles.

Is this a scientific terrain model?

No. It is an educational explorer using published figures - the cliff profile, cloud shape, and marker positions are schematic teaching cues, not survey terrain.
