Night Swarm Survivor is a browser horde-survival roguelite: drift through a scrolling field as auto-fire clears slimes and bats, bank XP level-ups, and meet a stage boss once each wave-based stretch ends. No install, no account, nothing uploaded.

NIGHT SWARM SURVIVOR

Steer with WASD or the arrow keys; auto-fire handles the nearest enemy for you. Clear each wave-based stretch and a stage boss spawns. About 28 KB loads when you press Play.

Night Swarm Survivor moves your survivor with WASD or the arrow keys and offers no touch controls. On a phone or tablet the field appears but nothing responds to taps, so open it on a desktop to play.

Press Play to start the game.
WASD or arrows to move; R to restart; M to mute.
