GPU Test So Voi Benchmark Native va Thong Tin GPU

Last reviewed: 2026-07-12

Kiem tra phan cung do hoa cua ban khong phai la viec cua chi mot cong cu. GPU Test la mot cach de lam dieu do - ngay lap tuc, ngay trong trinh duyet, khong can cai dat - nhung khong phai cach duy nhat, va khong phai cach chinh xac nhat. Duoi day la mot cai nhin trung thuc ve nhung gi moi lua chon mang lai.


Cac lua chon canh nhau

Lua chonCai datBan nhan duoc giRoi khoi thiet bi ban
GPU Test (trang nay)Khong can - mo ngay lap tuc, dong co ~0.7 MB duoc cache sau lan tai dauBao cao kha nang ngay lap tuc + diem benchmark throughput trinh chieu cung lich suKhong - chay hoan toan trong trinh duyet nay
Mot bo benchmark nativeMot ung dung tai xuongDiem so cap do phong thi nghiem, cap do phan cung, cho GPU, CPU, VRAM, va nhiet doKhong, nhung can cai dat phan mem truoc
Trang thong tin GPU rieng cua trinh duyetKhong can - co san trong trinh duyetTrang thai driver tho, feature flag, va tang toc phan cung - khong co diem benchmarkKhong

GPU Test nam giua hai lua chon do: khac voi trang thong tin rieng cua trinh duyet, no tao tai cho chong do hoa va cho diem so co the so sanh, va khac mot bo native, khong can gi cai dat hay tai len.


Luc nao GPU Test thang

Day la con duong nhanh nhat den mot cau tra loi ma khong can thiet lap gi - mo trang va bao cao kha nang da co san, va mot lan nhan bat dau mot benchmark trinh chieu thuc su. 20 lan chay gan nhat cua ban o lai trong trinh duyet nay de ban theo doi thiet bi cua chinh minh theo thoi gian, va tat ca chay tren bat ky thiet bi nao co trinh duyet, gom dien thoai va tablet, khong co phan mem nao phai go bo sau do.


Luc nao cac lua chon khac thang

Mot bo benchmark native di sau hon GPU Test bao gio co the: no co the doc cam bien phan cung thuc su cho nhiet do va muc tieu thu dien, do bo nho video truc tiep, va tao ra diem so danh de so sanh voi phan con lai cua internet, khong chi voi cac lan chay truoc do cua chinh ban. Trang thong tin GPU rieng cua trinh duyet la cach nhanh nhat de kiem tra mot su thuc duy nhat, nhu tang toc co dang bat hay khong, ma khong can chay gi ca.


Quy tac thuc te

Dung GPU Test khi ban muon mot cai nhin ngay lap tuc, khong can cai dat, ve chong trinh duyet ban lam duoc gi, va mot diem benchmark ban co the theo doi theo thoi gian. Dung mot bo native khi ban can so lieu cap do phan cung - VRAM, nhiet do, muc tieu thu dien - van dung ngoai trinh duyet nay. Dung trang thong tin GPU rieng cua trinh duyet khi ban chi can xac nhan mot thiet lap, nhu tang toc co dang bat hay khong, ma khong can chay gi ca.
