Image EXIF Metadata Viewer

Last reviewed: 2026-07-21

See what camera, date, and GPS tags your photos still carry before you share them.

Everything runs in the browser - the photo is never uploaded and no account is required.


Tags this viewer shows

  • Camera - make, model, lens, ISO, and aperture.
  • Date and time the photo was taken.
  • GPS location - latitude, longitude, and altitude.
  • Other - software, copyright, and comments.

Supported photo formats

The viewer reads EXIF, IPTC, and XMP tags from HEIC, HEIF, TIF, and TIFF files as well as standard JPEG photos. The file opens locally in the browser without uploading it to a server.


Before you share

If GPS is embedded, a privacy warning and OpenStreetMap link show the exact spot the shot was taken. To erase those tags before you share, use the image metadata remover. For a verdict on AI origin instead of a full tag table - Content Credentials and generator marks - use the AI watermark detector.
