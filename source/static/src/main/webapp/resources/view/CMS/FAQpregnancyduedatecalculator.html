Frequently Asked Questions

What does Pregnancy Due Date Calculator Online do?

It estimates a due date from either the first day of your last period or a known conception date, and reports how far along the pregnancy is today in completed weeks plus days, along with the matching trimester.

Which method should I pick, last period or conception date?

Use the first day of your last period (LMP) when you know that date - it is the usual starting point and lets you set your average cycle length. Pick the conception date method when you know roughly when conception happened, for example after tracking ovulation.

Why does the average cycle length field change the due date?

The last-period method adds 280 days to your date on a 28-day cycle. A longer cycle means ovulation happens later, so the estimate moves later by the same number of days; a shorter cycle moves it earlier. The field accepts 20 to 45 days and defaults to 28.

How accurate is the estimated due date?

It follows the Naegele rule, the same arithmetic as a paper pregnancy wheel, so treat it as a due window rather than a fixed appointment - about 1 in 20 babies arrive on the exact date. An early dating ultrasound is more accurate than any date-based formula. Results are general estimates and are not medical advice.

Are my dates uploaded anywhere?

No. The due date is worked out in your browser on this device, with no upload and no account.
