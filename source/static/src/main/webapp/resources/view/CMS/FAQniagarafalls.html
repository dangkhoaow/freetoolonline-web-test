Frequently Asked Questions

How much water flows over Niagara Falls?

About 2,832 cubic meters per second on average (about 168,000 cubic meters per minute), combined across both falls.

Why does Horseshoe Falls carry more water than American Falls?

Horseshoe Falls has a longer curved crestline, about 670 m (2,200 ft), compared to American Falls' straighter edge. That extra crest length lets it carry about 90% of the total flow, leaving American Falls about 10%.

How tall are the falls?

Horseshoe Falls is about 57 m (188 ft) tall. American Falls is about 21-34 m (70-110 ft), and its height varies because a pile of fallen rock (talus) at its base has built up over time.

What is the Niagara Whirlpool?

About 4.5 km (2.8 mi) downstream of the falls, the Niagara River makes a sharp bend inside the gorge. The river's current doubles back on itself there, forming a churning circular whirlpool.

Where exactly is Niagara Falls?

On the Niagara River, on the border between Ontario, Canada, and New York, USA.

Is this a scientific hydrological model?

No. It is an educational explorer using published figures - the crestline curve, gorge profile, and whirlpool placement are schematic teaching cues, not a survey-grade model.
