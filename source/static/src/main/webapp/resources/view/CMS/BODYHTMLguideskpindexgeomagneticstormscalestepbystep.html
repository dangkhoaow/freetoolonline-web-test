How to Explore the Kp-Index Geomagnetic Storm Scale 3D Explorer

Last reviewed: 2026-07-24

Reviewed by FreetoolOnline editorial team

Open the Kp-Index Geomagnetic Storm Scale 3D Explorer, drag or pick a Kp value on the 0-9 slider, and watch the aurora ring, the outlined 0-9 grid cell, and a synthetic magnetometer-style trace all change together while NOAA's real G-scale label updates.

  1. Open /space-3d/kp-index-geomagnetic-storm-scale.html and wait for Earth, the illustrative field arcs, and the 0-9 grid to render.
  2. Drag the Kp slider (0-9) to pick a storm-severity level.
  3. Watch the aurora ring widen and change color, and the outlined cell move across the 0-9 grid.
  4. Watch the magnetometer-style trace canvas swing wider as Kp rises.
  5. Click "Jump to May 2024 Superstorm (G5)" to load the real G5 case with its Dst and Ap figures.
  6. Compare a quiet Kp of 2-3 against the G5 extreme case side by side.
