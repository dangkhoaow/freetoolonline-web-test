Frequently Asked Questions

What are the Pamukkale terraces made of?

White travertine, a mineral deposited by calcium-carbonate-rich thermal spring water as it flows down the hillside. The buildup has been happening over thousands of years.

What does "Pamukkale" mean?

"Cotton castle" in Turkish, a reference to the white, cotton-like appearance of the travertine terraces.

Can visitors still bathe in the terraces?

Many terraces have been closed to bathing since the 1990s to curb erosion from foot traffic. Boardwalks route visitors between open zones, and bare feet are required wherever the terraces themselves are crossed.

What are the ruins near the terraces?

Hierapolis, an ancient Greco-Roman city, sits on the plateau above the terraces. Together the terraces and ruins form the combined Hierapolis-Pamukkale UNESCO World Heritage Site, inscribed in 1988.

Where is Pamukkale located?

In Denizli Province, southwestern Turkey (Anatolia).

Is this a scientific terrain model?

No. It is an educational explorer using published figures - the stepped terraces, basin pools, and ruin markers are schematic teaching cues, not survey terrain.
