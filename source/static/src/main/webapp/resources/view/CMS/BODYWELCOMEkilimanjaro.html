Mount Kilimanjaro 3D Explorer

Last reviewed: 2026-07-29

Reviewed by FreetoolOnline editorial team

This browser explorer shows Mount Kilimanjaro from a three-quarter view: the distinctive volcanic cone standing 5,895 m tall rising alone from the surrounding African savanna plateaus, with bands of color showing elevation from base to the snow-covered summit.

Mount Kilimanjaro is an inactive volcano located in northeastern Tanzania, about 330 km southeast of Nairobi. Despite being close to the equator, the mountain is high enough to retain glaciers year-round, though the ice cap has shrunk dramatically - from about 12 square km in 1912 to about 1.76 square km in 2026, representing an 85% loss of glacial coverage. The mountain is accessed by about 50,000 climbers each year, mostly via the Marangu (tourist) route, and is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

  • Toggle the elevation bands to see the color-coded altitude zones
  • Toggle the snowcap to compare 1912 (historical) to 2026 (modern coverage)
  • Read elevation, glacier extent, and climbing statistics in the panel
  • Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom
