Frequently Asked Questions

What does the text similarity checker do?

It compares two pasted texts and returns a meaning-based similarity score, plus a per-sentence breakdown showing which sentences of one text match which sentences of the other. Everything runs in your browser - nothing is uploaded.

Is this a plagiarism detector?

No. There is no source corpus and no originality verdict - the score compares only the two texts you paste. Two texts can score high with zero copied wording, and a low score does not prove originality.

How is this different from the text diff tool?

The text diff shows exact character-level changes between two versions of a text. This checker scores MEANING, so two passages can score high even when almost no wording is shared. Use the diff to see every edit; use this checker to judge whether two texts say the same thing.

Do I have to download anything?

The basic word-overlap mode works instantly with no download. The AI meaning mode downloads a language model of about 24 MB once, on your explicit click, and your browser caches it for next time - it is the same model the keyword extractor on this site uses, so if you have used that tool the download is already cached. Your texts never leave the device in either mode.

Does it work in every language?

English works best. Other languages produce scores but less reliably, and very short texts (under about ten words) score unreliably in any language.
