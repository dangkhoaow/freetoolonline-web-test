Retirement Calculator Online

Last reviewed: 2026-07-30

See what a retirement balance could reach by the time you need it. Enter your starting principal, the monthly contribution you plan to add, the expected annual interest rate, and the number of years to project the balance.


Key features

  • Compound-interest growth calculator: final value, total contributed, and interest earned.
  • Live update: results recalculate on every field change, with no button press required.
  • Compounding frequency selector - monthly, annually, or daily - shows how frequency affects the final balance.
  • Currency-formatted output for every figure.

When to use it

Use it to sanity-check whether your current monthly contribution reaches the balance you want by retirement, and to see what a few more years of the same contribution would add. Switching the compounding frequency shows how much the final balance shifts - for example, a $5,000 start with a $200 monthly contribution at 7% over 30 years:

Compounding frequencyFinal value
Monthlyroughly $243,000
Annuallyroughly $234,000

Privacy

The starting balance and monthly contribution stay in the browser - the projection runs on this device, with no upload, no login, and no account, because all math uses a standard closed-form compound-interest formula with no server call. Results are illustrative estimates based on a constant rate; actual investment returns vary and this is not financial advice.
