Retirement Calculator Online

Last reviewed: 2026-07-30

Project how a starting balance and regular contributions grow with compound interest over time. Enter your starting amount, how much you plan to add each month, the expected annual rate, the number of years, and how often interest compounds - the final value, total contributed, and interest earned update instantly as you type.

Use it to estimate a savings or retirement balance, or compare how compounding frequency changes the result. Monthly compounding at 7% over 30 years with a $200 monthly contribution grows a $5,000 start to roughly $243,000 - versus $234,000 with annual compounding.

The math runs locally in the browser - no upload and no account. Results are illustrative estimates based on a constant rate; actual investment returns vary and this is not financial advice.
