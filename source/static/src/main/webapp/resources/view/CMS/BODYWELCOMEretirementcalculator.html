Retirement Calculator Online

Last reviewed: 2026-07-30

See what a retirement balance could reach by the time you need it. Enter your starting amount, how much you plan to add each month, the expected annual rate, the number of years, and how often interest compounds - the final value, total contributed, and interest earned update instantly as you type.

Use it to sanity-check whether your current monthly contribution reaches the balance you want by retirement, and to see what a few more years of the same contribution would add. Monthly compounding at 7% over 30 years with a $200 monthly contribution grows a $5,000 start to roughly $243,000 - versus $234,000 with annual compounding.

The starting balance and monthly contribution stay in the browser - the retirement projection runs on this device, with no upload and no account. Results are illustrative estimates based on a constant rate; actual investment returns vary and this is not financial advice.
