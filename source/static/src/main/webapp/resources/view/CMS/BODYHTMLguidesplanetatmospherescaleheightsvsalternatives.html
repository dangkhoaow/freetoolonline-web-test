Where the Scale Heights Explorer wins

The Scale Heights Explorer wins when the question is a world-to-world comparison - it is the only page on the site that puts Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, and Titan's pressure decay side by side under one shared formula.


Where the Karman Line Explorer wins

The Karman Line Explorer wins when the question is Earth-specific - it names Earth's own four real atmosphere layers and pins the ISS orbit, meteor ablation, and aurora zone to their own real altitudes, none of which the scale-height comparison shows at all.


A reasonable rule

As a reasonable rule, use the Scale Heights Explorer first to see how different worlds compare to each other, then use the Karman Line Explorer to see where Earth's own named layers and real objects sit within Earth's own atmosphere.
