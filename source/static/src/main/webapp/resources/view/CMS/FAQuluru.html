Frequently Asked Questions

How tall is Uluru?

About 348 m (1,142 ft) above the surrounding plain, with its summit about 863 m above sea level.

Why does Uluru change color?

Its surface holds iron oxide, which scatters low-angle sunlight most strongly at sunrise and sunset, producing a deep red-orange glow.

What is Kata Tjuta?

A group of large domed rock formations about 25 km west of Uluru, marked in this explorer as a nearby landmark.

What rock is Uluru made of?

Arkose sandstone, deposited about 550 million years ago and later tilted to near-vertical, which is why its grain runs steeply up the rock face.

How big is Uluru around its base?

About 9.4 km in circumference.

Where is Uluru?

Central Australia, within Uluru-Kata Tjuta National Park, Northern Territory.

Is this a geological simulation?

No. It is an educational explorer using published survey figures - rock texture, weathering, and sunset lighting are procedural teaching cues.
