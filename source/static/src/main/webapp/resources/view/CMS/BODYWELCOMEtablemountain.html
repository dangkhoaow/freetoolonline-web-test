Table Mountain 3D Explorer

Last reviewed: 2026-08-27

Reviewed by FreetoolOnline editorial team

This browser explorer shows Table Mountain as a flat-topped sandstone plateau rising directly above Cape Town, South Africa. Instead of tapering to a point like most mountains, the summit levels off into a long table about 3 km (1.9 mi) end to end, bounded by near-vertical cliffs that drop away on every side. Its highest point, Maclear's Beacon, sits at 1,085 m (3,563 ft).

The flat top and steep collar form because the mountain is built from Table Mountain Sandstone, a hard, resistant rock layer deposited roughly 450-500 million years ago and later uplifted and eroded from the surrounding softer rock, leaving the harder cap standing as a table. When the southeaster wind blows moist air up from the Atlantic, it condenses into an orographic cloud on the flat summit and spills down over the northern cliff edge - locals call this the "tablecloth". Toggle the tablecloth cloud to show or hide that spill, and the beacon/cableway marker to see the highest point plus the route of the Table Mountain Aerial Cableway up the city-facing face.

Your tablecloth-cloud and beacon-marker choices are saved between visits, so the explorer reopens the way you last left it.
