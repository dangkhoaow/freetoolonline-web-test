Signature Maker - Step By Step

Last reviewed: 2026-07-25

Signature Maker runs in three steps: open the tool, draw your signature, then download the file. This page walks through what each step expects.


Step 1 - open the tool

Go to Signature Maker. Nothing needs to be installed or signed in before you start.


Step 2 - draw your signature

Pick a pen color and width, then sign inside the box with your mouse, finger, or stylus. Use Undo to remove the last stroke or Clear to start over if it doesn't look right.


Step 3 - download the result

Click Download PNG. Keep the white background for documents, or switch on the transparent-background option first to paste the signature over other content.

Everything runs in the browser - no upload, no account, and the drawing is never sent anywhere. Refreshing or leaving the page clears the canvas, so download before you navigate away.
