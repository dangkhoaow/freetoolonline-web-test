Online Voice Recorder

Last reviewed: 2026-09-04

Record your screen, camera, or microphone and download the clip - all in the browser.

Use it for a quick screen capture, webcam clip, or voice note without installing an app.

Recording stays on your device and is never uploaded; the clip downloads straight to you.

Not sure which mode fits your task? See when to use Online Voice Recorder for scenarios by recording type.

What each recording mode actually captures

Online Voice Recorder offers four source options: Camera (video only, no microphone audio), Camera + microphone, Microphone only, and Screen. Screen mode asks the browser for both video and audio together, so if the site or app you are sharing plays sound and your browser grants audio capture, that system or tab sound is included in the download alongside the screen video - not just a silent recording.

While a camera or camera + microphone recording is running, a live muted preview shows what the camera currently sees, so you can check your framing without hearing yourself through the speakers. The preview disappears again once you stop. Microphone-only mode skips the preview entirely and shows just the running timer, since there is no video feed to display.

If you end a screen share from the browser's own "Stop sharing" control instead of clicking Stop on the page, Online Voice Recorder notices the share ending and finishes the recording for you, so the clip you started is still saved.

Pause and resume, file formats, and a very short clip

The Pause button freezes the on-screen timer without ending the take; clicking Resume continues the same recording and the timer picks back up excluding the paused time. A clip you paused for five minutes in the middle still reports its true recorded length in the filename and the on-screen counter, not the full wall-clock time you spent on the page.

Behind the scenes, Online Voice Recorder tries several file formats in order and uses whichever one the current browser reports it can actually produce: for video, VP9/Opus first, then VP8/Opus, then plain WebM; for microphone-only audio, Opus/WebM first, then plain WebM, then Ogg/Opus as a last resort. Each downloaded file is named with its own recorded length, for example voice-recorder-01m05s.webm, so a folder of clips sorts by length without opening each one.

If you click Stop a fraction of a second after Start, the browser may not have produced any data yet. Online Voice Recorder detects that empty result and shows "No data was recorded. Try recording for a moment longer." instead of offering a broken or silent download.

What happens when you click Stop, and why permission errors differ

The Source dropdown locks while a recording is running, so you cannot switch from camera to screen mid-take; it only unlocks again once you click Stop. The moment Stop finishes assembling the clip, Online Voice Recorder releases the camera, microphone, or screen-share track it was using, which is why your browser's recording indicator turns off right away - nothing keeps capturing in the background after the download link appears.

Two different messages can appear before a recording ever starts. If the browser itself has no MediaRecorder API at all, the tool shows a support message immediately. If the API exists but you deny the camera, microphone, or screen-share permission prompt - or no matching device is available - a separate message reports that the recording could not start for that reason instead. Recognizing which message you saw tells you whether the fix is a different browser or just re-granting the permission.
