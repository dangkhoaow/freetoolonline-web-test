Welcome - press Play to load Cube Smash 3D. The whole game runs in your browser: about 610 KB downloads once (mostly the shared 3D engine), then it is cached, and best scores stay on this device under ftol:cubesmash3d:best. Nothing is uploaded and no account is needed.

A run is short and readable: cubes spawn at the glowing rim and walk straight at your orb, so the skill is positioning plus dash timing. The dash carries a roughly one-second cooldown - spend it on clusters, not single cubes, and keep smashes inside the two-second combo window so the multiplier holds. Losing a heart resets the combo, which usually hurts more than the heart itself.

It plays the same on desktop and touch: WASD or arrow keys plus Space on a keyboard, drag plus tap on a phone. When the third heart goes, R or a tap restarts instantly, so it suits two-minute breaks as well as long best-score chases.
