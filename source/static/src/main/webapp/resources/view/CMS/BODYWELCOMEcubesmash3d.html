Welcome - press Play to load Cube Smash 3D. The whole game runs in your browser: about 610 KB downloads once (mostly the shared 3D engine), then it is cached, and best scores stay on this device under ftol:cubesmash3d:best. Nothing is uploaded and no account is needed.

The loop is the classic survivor rhythm: cube blobs hop toward you across a sunny low-poly meadow, the Smash Hammer swings automatically whenever one is in range, and the gems they drop fill the level bar. Each level up pauses the action and offers three upgrades - heavier hammer, swift boots, a hearty meal, gem magnet, thick skin, or quicker swings - so every run builds differently. Bigger dark-green blobs arrive after the first minute and hit harder, and the spawn pace keeps climbing, so the meadow always wins eventually; the score is how long and how loudly you resisted.

Desktop plays with WASD / arrows, SHIFT to sprint, SPACE to jump, mouse drag to orbit the camera and scroll to zoom; P pauses and M mutes the small synth sound effects. On touch, drag to move and tap to jump. When the health bar empties, Play or R restarts instantly.
