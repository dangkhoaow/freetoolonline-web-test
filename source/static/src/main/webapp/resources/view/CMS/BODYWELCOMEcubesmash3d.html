Cube Smash 3D runs entirely in your browser - press Play and about 610 KB downloads once (mostly the shared 3D engine), then it is cached. Nothing is uploaded and no account is needed, and your best score stays on this device under ftol:cubesmash3d:best.

How a run unfolds

The loop is the classic survivor rhythm: cube blobs hop toward you across a sunny low-poly meadow, the Smash Hammer swings automatically whenever one is in range, and the gems they drop fill the level bar. Each level up pauses the action and offers three picks from a pool of eight - including two whole extra weapons, orbiting Spin Blades and splash-damage Boom Stones - so every run builds differently.

Bigger dark-green blobs arrive after the first minute, a crowned Boss Blob with its own health bar charges in every couple of minutes, and the day rolls into a firefly-lit night with faster spawns. The meadow always wins eventually; the score is how long and how loudly you resisted.

Controls

  • Move (desktop): WASD / arrows, SHIFT to sprint, SPACE to jump.
  • Camera: mouse drag to orbit, scroll to zoom.
  • Touch: drag to move, tap to jump.
  • Anytime: P pauses, M mutes the small synth sound effects, and Play or R restarts instantly when the health bar empties.
