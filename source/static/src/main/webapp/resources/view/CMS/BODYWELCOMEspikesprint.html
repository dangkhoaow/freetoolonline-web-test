Welcome to Spike Sprint - a free browser pixel runner with no account or install. About ~25 KB loads after you press Play, and the session high score stays in this tab only; nothing is uploaded.

How to play

  • Press Play, then tap or hold Space to clear the pits.
  • A spiked barrel chases from behind and closes in whenever you stall.
