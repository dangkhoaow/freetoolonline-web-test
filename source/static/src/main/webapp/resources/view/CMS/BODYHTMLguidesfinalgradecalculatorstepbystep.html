Final Grade Calculator - Step by Step

Last reviewed: 2026-10-30

The Final Grade Calculator works in three steps: open the tool, enter three numbers, then read the result. It updates live on every keystroke, with nothing to submit or download.


Step 1 - open the tool

Go to the Final Grade Calculator. There is nothing to install and no account to create before you start.


Step 2 - enter your three numbers

Type your current grade so far as a percent, the final exam's weight toward the overall grade as a percent, and the target overall grade you want. Each field updates the result the moment you type or change a value - there is no submit button.


Step 3 - read the score you need

The result shows the exact score needed on the final, worked out with weighted-average algebra. It also tells you plainly when the target is already locked in (even a 0% on the final still meets it), when it is out of reach (you would need over 100% on the final, so it shows your best possible finishing grade instead), and how many of the target's points your current grade has already banked. A reference table lists the resulting overall grade for seven sample final scores (0, 50, 60, 70, 80, 90 and 100%). The math is plain weighted-average algebra on the numbers you enter, not your school's official grading policy, so curves, rounding and dropped-lowest rules can change the real outcome. The whole calculation runs in your browser - no upload, no account.
