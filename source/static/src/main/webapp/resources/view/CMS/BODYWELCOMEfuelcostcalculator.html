Fuel Cost Calculator

Last reviewed: 2026-09-27

The fuel cost calculator turns three numbers - trip distance, your vehicle's fuel efficiency, and the local fuel price - into the fuel a trip needs and what it costs: per trip, per mile or km, and per person. It works in miles + MPG or km + L/100km, entirely in your browser.

Pick US units (miles, MPG, price per gallon) or metric (km, L/100km, price per liter). Switching converts the values you have already typed in place - 300 miles becomes 482.8 km, 30 MPG becomes 7.8 L/100km - so the result stays equivalent instead of resetting.

A round-trip checkbox doubles the distance for the return leg. If several people share the ride, enter the traveler count and the total is split per person - handy for carpools and shared road trips.

The price field takes whatever you pay at the pump, in your own currency; the tool shows plain numbers rather than guessing a currency symbol, and it never fetches live prices. Every calculation runs locally in the page - nothing you enter leaves your device, and no account is needed.
