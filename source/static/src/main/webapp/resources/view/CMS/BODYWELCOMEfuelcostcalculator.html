Fuel Cost Calculator

Last reviewed: 2026-09-27

The fuel cost calculator turns three numbers - trip distance, your vehicle's fuel efficiency, and the local fuel price - into the fuel a trip needs and what it costs: per trip, per mile or km, and per person. It works in miles + MPG or km + L/100km, entirely in your browser.

Key features

  • US or metric units. Pick miles, MPG and price per gallon, or switch to km, L/100km and price per liter.
  • In-place unit conversion. Switching units converts the values you have already typed in place - 300 miles becomes 482.8 km, 30 MPG becomes 7.8 L/100km - so the result stays equivalent instead of resetting.
  • Round trip. A round-trip checkbox doubles the distance for the return leg.
  • Cost per person. If several people share the ride, enter the traveler count and the total is split per person - handy for carpools and shared road trips.
  • Your own currency. The price field takes whatever you pay at the pump; the tool shows plain numbers rather than guessing a currency symbol, and it never fetches live prices.

Private by design

Every calculation runs locally in the page - nothing you enter leaves your device, and no account is needed.
