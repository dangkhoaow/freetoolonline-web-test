Welcome - press Play to load Mono Minefield Grid. Best scores stay in this browser under ftol:monominefieldgrid:storage.

New to a board? Open anywhere you like. The first cell you click is always safe, because the mines are placed only after that first open, so a fresh grid never ends on your opening move - you always get a foothold to start reading from.

It makes a compact logic puzzle for a short break - one focused run at a time, quick to start and quick to leave, so it fits a spare few minutes without turning into a long session.
