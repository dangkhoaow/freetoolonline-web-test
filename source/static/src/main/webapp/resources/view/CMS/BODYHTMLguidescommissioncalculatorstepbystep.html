How to Use the Commission Calculator - Step by Step

Last reviewed: 2026-10-16

The commission calculator turns a sales amount and a commission rate into the commission earned, live as you type - or splits a tiered quota-plus-accelerator plan into its base and bonus portions. These are the exact inputs and how to read the result.


Step 1 - open the tool

Open the calculator and a full result is already on screen: the form loads pre-filled with structure "Flat rate", a sales amount of 10,000 and a commission rate of 10%, so the result - commission earned: 1,000.00 - and the flat-rate reference table are visible before you type anything.


Step 2 - pick a structure

The first select offers two structures: flat rate (the default, one rate on the whole sale) or tiered - quota plus accelerator rate. Switching structure shows or hides the matching input group, so the form only ever asks for the numbers that structure needs.


Step 3 - enter your sales amount

The sales amount field stays visible in both structures - it is the value of the sale the commission is calculated on.


Step 4 - enter the rate, or the quota and two rates

In flat mode, type the single commission rate %. In tiered mode, type the quota threshold the sale needs to cross, the base rate % that applies up to that quota, and the accelerator rate % that applies only to the amount above it. Every change recalculates immediately, and a missing or zero sales amount shows a plain prompt instead of a broken result.


Step 5 - read the result

Flat mode shows a single commission-earned figure. Tiered mode shows a breakdown: the up-to-quota line at the base rate, the above-quota line at the accelerator rate (only when the sale reached quota), and the total - with a sentence stating outright when the sale has not reached quota yet, instead of a silent zero.


Step 6 - check the reference table

Below the result, a table lists what a flat 3/5/7.5/10/12.5/15/20/25% rate would pay on the sales amount you entered, independent of which structure is selected - useful for sanity-checking a quoted rate without retyping anything. Nothing you enter is stored or sent anywhere, so note the result before closing the tab.
