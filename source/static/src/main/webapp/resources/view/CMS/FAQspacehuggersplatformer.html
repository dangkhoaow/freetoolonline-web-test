Frequently Asked Questions

What are the keyboard controls?

Use Arrow Keys to move left/right and jump, Z to shoot, X to dodge, and C to throw a grenade. A connected gamepad works too, and up to 4 gamepads support local co-op.

How do I beat the game?

Space Huggers has no fixed ending - clear the enemies on each level to move on, and try to survive as many procedurally generated levels as you can with your 10 starting lives (plus 3 more per level cleared).

Is Space Huggers free?

Yes, Space Huggers is completely free to play in your browser. No account, no download, no ads. The game is open source under GPL-3.0 license.

Can I play offline?

Once the page loads, the game runs entirely in your browser with no server connection needed. There is no save system - each session starts a fresh run.

What browser do I need?

Space Huggers runs on any modern browser that supports HTML5, JavaScript, and Canvas. It is a desktop game controlled with a keyboard or a connected gamepad; touch controls are not supported.
