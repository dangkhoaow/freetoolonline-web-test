Welcome to Egg Time Rewind - a free Nokia-style arena shooter where dying rewinds time and leaves parallel lives on the field to help you. Break space eggs for big points, chase a high score saved only in this browser, and reset anytime with R. About ~60 KB after Play, adapted from Frank Force's GPL-2.0 EggTimeRewind (Nokia 3310 Jam). Privacy note: the only save is the local high-score key. For more free browser games, open the games hub. Related titles include Bounce Back and Rock Paper Neural. Once the game loads, the Fullscreen button expands the arena to fill your screen and keeps keyboard focus on the game, so you can jump straight back into moving and shooting without clicking into the frame first. The game itself only downloads once per browser - come back later and the cached copy jumps straight to the launch screen instead of fetching anything again.


Controls

  • Move with WASD, the arrow keys, the numpad, the mouse, or a gamepad stick.
  • Shoot with Space, Shift, a mouse click, or a gamepad button.
  • Press R to reset for a fresh procedurally generated arena at any time.
