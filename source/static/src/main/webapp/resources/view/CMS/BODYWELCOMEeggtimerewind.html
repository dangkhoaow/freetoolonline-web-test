Egg Time Rewind is a free Nokia-style arena shooter you play right in the browser - when you die, time rewinds and your earlier runs stay on the field as parallel lives that help you clear the arena. Break space eggs for big points and chase a high score kept only on this device.


Key features

  • Nokia 3310 Jam look and feel, playable instantly with no install or download.
  • Dying rewinds time and leaves your earlier runs behind as parallel helper lives.
  • Break space eggs for big points and chase a high score saved only in this browser.
  • Reset any run at once by pressing R for a fresh start.

How your score is saved

The only thing this game stores is your best score, kept in your browser under a single local high-score key - nothing is uploaded and there is no account or server. The playable build is about 60 KB after you press Play, adapted from Frank Force's GPL-2.0 EggTimeRewind entry for the Nokia 3310 Jam.
