Space Huggers

Space Huggers puts you in a fast-paced run-and-gun platformer where you shoot, dodge, and grenade your way through enemy outposts spread across procedurally generated planets. The roguelike format means no two runs look the same - progressive difficulty, randomized levels, and fully destructible environments reward learning enemy patterns and managing your limited grenades. Drop in solo or with up to 3 friends on gamepads for local co-op, making it a perfect browser-based arcade challenge.
