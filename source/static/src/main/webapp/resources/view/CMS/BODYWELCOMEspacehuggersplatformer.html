Space Huggers

Space Huggers is a fast-paced run-and-gun platformer: shoot, dodge, and grenade your way through enemy outposts scattered across procedurally generated planets. Its roguelike format means no two runs play the same, so every attempt rewards reading enemy patterns and spending your limited grenades wisely.

Key features

  • Roguelike runs - procedurally generated levels with randomized layouts and no fixed ending, so no two runs look alike.
  • Destructible environments - blocks and cover break from weapons fire and grenade blasts, with fire that spreads.
  • Progressive difficulty - you start with 10 lives and earn 3 more for each level cleared; clear every enemy on a level to advance.
  • Local co-op - drop in with up to 3 friends (4 players total) on connected gamepads.
  • Runs in the browser - vanilla JavaScript on an HTML5 Canvas, no install and no account, and playable offline once it has loaded.

How to play

  • Move and jump with the Arrow Keys.
  • Shoot with Z or a left click.
  • Dodge with X or a middle click - it gives brief invulnerability and puts out fire.
  • Throw a grenade with C or a right click, limited to 3 per life.
  • Restart a run with R.

When to play

The short-burst loop suits a quick arcade break as well as a longer roguelike grind. It is fully open-source and free with no ads, tracking, or downloads, and the same loop that plays well solo scales straight to four players on one screen.
