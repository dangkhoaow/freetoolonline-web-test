Cuando usar Carrington Event 3D Explorer

Use this explorer when you want to see the single most extreme real space-weather event on record and why modern grid-risk warnings reference it.

  • Showing the first solar flare ever recorded (1859) and its real aftermath
  • Explaining how far an aurora can reach in an extreme storm
  • Connecting a 19th-century telegraph disruption to modern power-grid risk

This is an educational visualization with a heavily compressed timeline - the real event took about 17.6 hours to reach Earth and the storm lasted more than 6 days.
