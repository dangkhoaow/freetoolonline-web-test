Body Fat Percentage Calculator Online

Last reviewed: 2026-08-01

Estimate body fat percentage from a few tape-measure numbers using the U.S. Navy circumference method: height, neck, and waist for men; height, neck, waist, and hip for women.

Switch between metric (cm) and imperial (in) units at any time; the result and its fitness category (essential fat / athletic / fitness / average / above average) update as you type.

Your tape-measure figures stay in the browser - the estimate is worked out on this device, with no upload and no account. It is an estimate, not a medical measurement - a DEXA scan, bioelectrical-impedance scale, or skinfold calipers will be more precise.
