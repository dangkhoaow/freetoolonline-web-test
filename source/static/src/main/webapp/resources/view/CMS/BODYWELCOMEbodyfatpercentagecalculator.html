Body Fat Percentage Calculator Online

Last reviewed: 2026-09-02

Estimate body fat percentage from a few tape-measure numbers using the U.S. Navy circumference method, right in your browser.


Key features

  • Uses the U.S. Navy circumference method: height, neck, and waist for men; height, neck, waist, and hip for women.
  • Switch between metric (cm) and imperial (in) units at any time; the result and its fitness category update as you type.
  • Shows a fitness category alongside the number: essential fat, athletic, fitness, average, or above average, by sex.

Why a "waist bigger than neck" measurement can still show an error

The error message asks for measurements greater than zero, but the underlying formula needs more than just waist exceeding neck - it needs a real gap between the two, and how big that gap has to be depends on height. At the calculator's own metric defaults (height 175cm, neck 38cm), a waist of 58cm - a 20cm gap - still returns the error, because the formula's result is below zero at that gap. The gap has to widen to roughly 30cm (a waist around 68cm) before the estimate turns positive, and from there it climbs steadily: the same 38cm neck with an 85cm waist (a 47cm gap) works out to about 17%, in the Fitness band. Taller measurements need an even wider gap to clear zero - the same 32cm gap that is already positive at 175cm tall is still slightly negative at 200cm tall. Every result, once it clears zero, is also held between 2% and 60% - an unusually large gap does not push the displayed number past 60%, and a gap that only just clears zero shows as 2% rather than a fraction of a percent.
