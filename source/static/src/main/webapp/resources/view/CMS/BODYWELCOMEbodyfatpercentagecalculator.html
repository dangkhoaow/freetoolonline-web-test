Body Fat Percentage Calculator Online

Last reviewed: 2026-08-01

Estimate body fat percentage from a few tape-measure numbers using the U.S. Navy circumference method, right in your browser.


Key features

  • Uses the U.S. Navy circumference method: height, neck, and waist for men; height, neck, waist, and hip for women.
  • Switch between metric (cm) and imperial (in) units at any time; the result and its fitness category update as you type.
  • Shows a fitness category alongside the number: essential fat, athletic, fitness, average, or above average, by sex.

Estimate vs precise measurement

It is an estimate, not a medical measurement. For a more precise reading, use one of the methods below.

Privacy

Your tape-measure figures stay in the browser - the estimate is worked out on this device, with no upload and no account.
