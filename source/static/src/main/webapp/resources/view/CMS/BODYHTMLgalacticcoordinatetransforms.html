Galactic coordinates measure star positions relative to our Milky Way, tilted about 62° from the celestial equator used in Earth-based astronomy. This interactive tool displays three coordinate systems together: galactic (cyan), celestial (yellow), and ecliptic (magenta), each rotating with its own frame. Watch how they transform as you change view angles to understand the fundamental tilt between them.

Drag to rotate. Scroll to zoom. The three colored spheres represent different coordinate frames, shown simultaneously to reveal their relative tilts and orientations.
