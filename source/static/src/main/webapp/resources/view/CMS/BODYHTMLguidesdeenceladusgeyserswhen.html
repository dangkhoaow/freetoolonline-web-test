When to use Enceladus Geysers 3D Explorer

Use this explorer when you want to see how Enceladus vents its subsurface ocean through the tiger-stripe fractures.

  • Showing the four ~135 km south-polar tiger stripes
  • Connecting the plumes to the ~30-40 km ice shell and ~10 km ocean
  • Explaining how plume ice feeds Saturn's E ring

This is an educational visualization, not a full hydrodynamic plume model.
