Walking the controls, preset by preset

Five moves cover the scene. Run them in order and the grid depth, the geodesics, and the r_s ring each land on their own.

  1. Open the explorer and wait for the curved grid and the teal geodesics to paint.
  2. Read the facts panel: r_s = 2GM/c^2, Sun ~2.95 km, Earth ~8.87 mm.
  3. Press Earth, then Sun, then 10 Msun BH to compare how far the grid deepens.
  4. Press Hide geodesics and Hide r_s ring to strip the scene back to the grid alone.
  5. Press Fullscreen for a projector, then compare with Gravity Well 3D for escape velocities or Light Cone 3D for flat causal regions.

Where the schematic stops

Everything renders on your device with WebGL. The 3D engine loads once (about 0.7 MB) and is cached; no scene data is sent to a server.

This is an educational curved-grid schematic - not a Schwarzschild metric solver. The r_s ring is exaggerated for one-screen teaching.
