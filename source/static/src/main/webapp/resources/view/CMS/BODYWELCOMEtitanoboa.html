Titanoboa spins in 3D below - drag, zoom, and click a body part for facts (full controls on the Dinosaurs 3D hub).

Not a dinosaur but a boid snake of enormous size, Titanoboa inhabited the Cerrejon Formation of Colombia roughly 60-58 million years ago. Head and colleagues named T. cerrejonensis in 2009; mean adult length runs about 12.8 m (up to roughly 14.3 m), with an estimated mass near 1135 kg (range about 730-1819 kg).
