Frequently Asked Questions

How big is the Black Forest?

It covers about 6,009 sq km in Baden-Wurttemberg, southwest Germany, running roughly 160 km north-south and 20-60 km wide.

What is the highest point in the Black Forest?

Feldberg, at about 1,493 m, is the highest peak in the range.

Why is it called the Black Forest?

The name comes from the dense, near-continuous canopy of fir and spruce trees that shades the ground below, making the forest interior look dark from a distance.

Which rivers begin in the Black Forest?

The Danube (Donau) has its conventional source at Donaueschingen on the forest's eastern edge, and the Neckar rises near Schwenningen on the western flank.

What is the Schwarzwaldhochstrasse?

The "Black Forest High Road" is a scenic ridge route of about 60 km connecting Baden-Baden to Freudenstadt along the forest's high crest.

Is this a survey map?

No. It is an educational explorer using published Black Forest figures - the terrain relief, river-source markers, and scenic route line are schematic teaching cues, not a survey map.
