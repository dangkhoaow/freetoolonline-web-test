Plitvice Lakes step down through Croatia's oldest national park in a staircase of sixteen turquoise pools, each one held back by a living travertine dam. Orbit the terraces, tell the Upper Lakes apart from the Lower Lakes, and find Veliki Slap, the tallest waterfall in the park.

About this scene

Published figures: sixteen named lakes (twelve Upper Lakes plus four Lower Lakes) drop about 133 m from Prosacansko Lake to Novakovica Brod; Kozjak Lake, the largest and deepest, separates the two groups; Veliki Slap plunges 78 m, the tallest waterfall in Croatia.
