Amazon River 3D Explorer

Last reviewed: 2026-07-31

Reviewed by FreetoolOnline editorial team

This browser explorer shows the Amazon River basin in top-down view: a meandering river channel cutting through a lowland tropical basin, the seasonal floodplain extent, and the silt discharge plume fanning 100+ km into the Atlantic Ocean.

The Amazon drains about 7,050,000 km2 across nine countries (about 60% Brazil) and discharges roughly 209,000 m3/s of water - about one-fifth of all river discharge on Earth. The river meanders extensively through the basin, and during flood season (May-June) the várzea flooded forest inundates by up to 40 m above baseline. The estuary releases a freshwater plume into the Atlantic visible from satellite imagery.

  • Toggle between dry season (Sep-Oct) and wet season (May-Jun) to see the floodplain extent
  • Toggle the discharge plume on and off to see the silt plume reaching into the Atlantic
  • Read length, discharge volume, basin area, and flood inundation figures in the panel
  • Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom
  • Runs fully in the browser with the vendored three.js engine - no account, no upload
