Amazon River 3D Explorer

Last reviewed: 2026-07-31

Reviewed by FreetoolOnline editorial team

This browser explorer shows the Amazon River basin in top-down view: a meandering river channel, seasonal floodplain extent, and the silt discharge plume fanning 100+ km into the Atlantic.

The Amazon drains about 7,050,000 km2 across nine countries and discharges about 209,000 m3/s - roughly one-fifth of all river discharge on Earth. During flood season (May-June) the várzea flooded forest inundates by up to 40 m above baseline.

  • Toggle between dry and wet seasons to see floodplain extent
  • Toggle the discharge plume on/off
  • Read published figures in the panel
  • Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom
  • Runs fully in the browser - no account needed
