Amazon River 3D Explorer

Last reviewed: July 31, 2026 | by: FreeToolOnline Editorial Team

The Amazon River is the largest river on Earth by water discharge volume, carrying about one-fifth of the world's river water into the Atlantic Ocean. Flowing through South America for approximately 6,400 kilometers, the Amazon cuts through the world's largest rainforest, shaping the landscape and supporting an estimated 10% of all known species. Its massive network of tributaries - including the Rio Negro, Rio Madeira, Rio Xingu, and Solimões - collects water from a drainage basin spanning about 5.5 million square kilometers across nine countries, with about 60% in Brazil.

The Amazon's sediment plume is visible from space, extending hundreds of kilometers into the Atlantic and creating a distinct brownish coloration where the river's fresh water meets the ocean. This massive discharge, averaging about 209,000 cubic meters per second, makes the Amazon a unique geographical and ecological wonder that sustains local communities, indigenous cultures, and a vast biodiversity that has not been fully catalogued by science.
