Helioseismology Sun Oscillation 3D Explorer vs alternatives

Helioseismology Sun Oscillation 3D Explorer lets a reader see a simplified 3D model of the Sun's five-minute surface oscillation and toggle straight to the interior boundary it reveals - a SOHO/GONG/SDO-HMI data archive wins as the fixed, citable source for actual observational data; a textbook diagram wins for a quick static summary without needing WebGL.
