How to Play Level 13 - Step by Step

The Level 13 page loads a ~8 MB text-based survival incremental in an iframe. Explore a ruined underground City sector by sector, scavenge resources, build a camp, and unlock a tech tree - everything is click-driven. Press Play to start.

Last reviewed: 2026-08-05

Controls: every action (move, scavenge, build, craft, fight) is a button click - no keyboard shortcuts, no gamepad, no key-remap menu to learn.

Step 1 - press Play on this page

Press Play to inject the iframe (~8 MB: engine, audio, and images, downloaded once and cached). Level 13 is an open-source Apache-2.0 game by nroutasuo; its own in-app credits and changelog ship unchanged.

Step 2 - explore sector by sector

Each new game generates a unique underground map from a random seed. Click your way from sector to sector, scavenging resources while radiation, hostile scouts, hunger, and cold work against you.

Step 3 - build up your camp

Spend scavenged resources on camp buildings - workshops, greenhouses, hospitals - as you find the blueprints that unlock them. The tech tree compounds the longer a single run goes.

Step 4 - let it autosave

Progress autosaves in this browser roughly every 2 minutes, and a manual save option is also available. Multiple save slots let you keep more than one run going side by side.

Step 5 - come back later

There is no account and no server - the save lives only in this browser's site data, so clearing it erases the run. Close the tab any time; your last autosave picks up where you left off.
