URL Slug Generator - Step By Step

Last reviewed: 2026-07-26

URL Slug Generator works in three steps: open the tool, paste a title, then copy the slug. This page details what each step expects.


Step 1 - open the tool

Go to URL Slug Generator. You do not need to install anything or sign in before you start.


Step 2 - paste a title

Type or paste a title - or one phrase per line for bulk. The slug updates live. Pick "-" or "_" as the separator and optionally cap the length.


Step 3 - copy the slug

Click Copy to put the current slug on the clipboard. Slugify strips Latin and Vietnamese diacritics via Unicode normalize (NFD) and runs locally - no upload and no account.

Nothing you type is saved between visits. Chinese, Arabic, and Cyrillic scripts are not transliterated.
