Frequently Asked Questions

Is Lake Superior really the largest freshwater lake?

Yes, by surface area - about 82,100 sq km, the largest freshwater lake on Earth. Lake Baikal holds more water by volume, but Superior covers more surface.

How deep is Lake Superior?

About 406 m at its deepest point, with a mean depth of about 147 m across the whole basin.

What happened to the Edmund Fitzgerald?

The SS Edmund Fitzgerald, a 729-foot ore freighter, sank in a severe Lake Superior storm on November 10, 1975 near Whitefish Bay, with the loss of all 29 crew.

Which countries and states border Lake Superior?

The United States (Minnesota, Wisconsin, Michigan) and Canada (Ontario).

Why does the water stay so cold?

Its large volume, depth, and northern location mean the lake absorbs and releases heat very slowly, so deep water stays cold year-round even in summer.
