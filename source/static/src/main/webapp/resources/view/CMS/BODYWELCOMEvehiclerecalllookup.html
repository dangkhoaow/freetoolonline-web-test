Vehicle Recall Lookup

Last reviewed: 2026-08-10

Check whether a vehicle has open US safety recalls - by 17-character VIN or by year, make, and model. An example 2020 Toyota Camry lookup renders on load, so you see results before typing.


Key features

  • Each campaign card shows the campaign number, report date, affected component, the problem, the safety risk, and the fix, newest first.
  • High-severity flags render as badges straight from the NHTSA data: do-not-drive (park it), park-outside (fire risk), and over-the-air update.
  • A no-recalls result states plainly that NHTSA lists no active campaign for the query, linking to nhtsa.gov/recalls.

The two lookup modes

ModeWhat you enterHow it finds recalls
By VINThe 17-character VINDecodes the year, make, and model with the official NHTSA vPIC service, then lists that model's campaigns.
By year, make, and modelA model-year dropdown plus make and model suggestion listsFetches matching recall campaigns from NHTSA.

After a match

Recall repairs are free of charge at the manufacturer's authorized dealers, shown next to the results, and a what-to-do list explains the steps after a match.


Privacy

Your VIN or vehicle query is sent only to the official US-government NHTSA API - never to this site's servers - and this page does not store it.
