Vehicle Recall Lookup

Last reviewed: 2026-09-12

Check whether a vehicle has open US safety recalls - by 17-character VIN or by year, make, and model. An example 2020 Toyota Camry lookup renders on load, so you see results before typing.


Key features

  • Each campaign card shows the campaign number, report date, affected component, the problem, the safety risk, and the fix, newest first.
  • High-severity flags render as badges straight from the NHTSA data: do-not-drive (park it), park-outside (fire risk), and over-the-air update.
  • A no-recalls result states plainly that NHTSA lists no active campaign for the query, linking to nhtsa.gov/recalls.

After a match

Recall repairs are free of charge at the manufacturer's authorized dealers, shown next to the results, and a what-to-do list explains the steps after a match.


Privacy

Your VIN or vehicle query is sent only to the official US-government NHTSA API - never to this site's servers - and this page does not store it.


Checking a recall outside the United States

Vehicle Recall Lookup queries only the US NHTSA database, so a VIN or year, make, and model search here will not surface an Australian, Canadian, or UK safety campaign. Each of those markets keeps its own recall register under its own regulator, and a defect reported in one country is not automatically listed in another - even for the same model.

CountryRecall registerWhat it covers
Australiarecalls.gov.auConsumer product and vehicle safety recalls reported by distributors selling in Australia
CanadaTransport Canada Vehicle Recalls DatabaseRecall campaigns issued for vehicles sold new in Canada
United KingdomDVSA vehicle recalls checkerSafety recalls for vehicles first registered in the UK

Search the relevant register directly by the vehicle's local make, model, and year - a campaign such as a software or head-up-display update on a specific model can exist in one market's list months before or after it appears in another, since each regulator only tracks the vehicles its own market imported or sold. If the local register shows nothing, that also does not mean the US result above applies - confirm with the manufacturer's local dealer network instead.
