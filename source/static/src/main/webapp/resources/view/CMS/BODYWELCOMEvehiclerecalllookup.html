Vehicle Recall Lookup

Last reviewed: 2026-08-10

Check whether a vehicle has open US safety recalls - by 17-character VIN or by year, make, and model. An example lookup renders the moment the page loads, so you can see what results look like before typing anything.

The VIN mode decodes the year, make, and model with the official NHTSA vPIC service first, then pulls the matching recall campaigns. The vehicle mode offers a model-year dropdown plus make and model suggestion lists. Each campaign card shows the campaign number, report date, affected component, the problem, the safety risk, and the fix, newest first.

High-severity flags render as badges straight from the NHTSA data: do-not-drive (park it), park-outside (fire risk), and over-the-air update. A no-recalls result states plainly that NHTSA lists no active campaign for the query, with a link to confirm at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Recall repairs are performed free of charge by the manufacturer's authorized dealers - the page says so next to the results, and a what-to-do list explains the steps after a match. Your VIN or vehicle query is sent only to the official US-government NHTSA API - never to this site's servers - and this page does not store it.
