Online Stopwatch

Last reviewed: 2026-07-02
freetoolonline.com Editorial Team

This stopwatch runs entirely in your browser and times an activity to the hundredth of a second, with a lap list for comparing splits. There is nothing to download, install, or sign up for - click Start and the display begins counting up immediately.


Controls

  • Start begins timing, and the display counts up in MM:SS.hh format.
  • Lap records the current elapsed time into the list below without stopping the clock, so you can capture as many splits as you need while the stopwatch keeps running.
  • Stop pauses the count; click Start again to resume from where you left off.
  • Reset clears the display and the lap list back to 00:00.00 for a fresh run.

Common uses

Reach for it whenever you need a simple, no-fuss stopwatch without installing an app, for tasks like:

  • Timing a workout set.
  • Timing a cooking or study session.
  • Rehearsing a presentation against the clock.
  • Comparing lap splits during a run or a race.

Accuracy and background behavior

The display updates continuously in MM:SS.hh format while the stopwatch is running, driven by your browser's own clock rather than a server. The tab has to stay open while you time - closing it or navigating away stops the count, because there is no background process keeping it running.


Privacy

All timing runs locally on your browser's own clock, so there is no upload and no account. Nothing you time or record here is sent anywhere or stored, and it clears the moment you leave the page.
