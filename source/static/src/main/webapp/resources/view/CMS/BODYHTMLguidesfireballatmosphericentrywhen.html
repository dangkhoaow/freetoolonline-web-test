When to Use the Fireball / Atmospheric Entry 3D Explorer

Last reviewed: 2026-08-02

Open the Fireball / Atmospheric Entry 3D Explorer when you want to test a diameter, speed, or angle that is NOT one of the site's fixed historical cases, and see where the model places the ablation onset for that specific combination.


Sessions this explorer fits

Students asking "what if the body were twice as big" or "what if it came in steeper", teachers demonstrating that outcome depends on diameter more than on angle, and curious readers who want to drag the diameter slider up and watch the outcome band flip from harmless to Chelyabinsk-class all get a direct answer from the sliders - no fixed case required.


Sessions this explorer does not fit

Sessions this page does not fit are watching the real 2013 Chelyabinsk event unfold on its own actual timeline, which is on the Chelyabinsk Meteor 3D Explorer, or comparing three specific real historical cases side by side on one shared altitude scale, which is on the Meteor Airburst Size Comparison 3D Explorer.
