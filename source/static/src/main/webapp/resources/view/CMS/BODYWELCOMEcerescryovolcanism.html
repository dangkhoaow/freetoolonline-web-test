Ahuna Mons is a 4-5 km tall cryovolcanic dome on the dwarf planet Ceres - the closest cryovolcano to the Sun and one of the most geologically young features in the solar system. Explore it in 3D with real NASA measurements and scale comparisons against familiar mountains and volcanoes.

One of Many - But the Loneliest

Ceres' surface hosts roughly two dozen candidate cryovolcanic domes, but Ahuna Mons is by far the most prominent and isolated. Others are either smaller, degraded by impacts, or clustered in multi-feature complexes. Ahuna stands alone - hence the nickname "the lonely mountain of Ceres."
