Strong Gravitational Lensing 3D Explorer vs alternatives

The Strong Gravitational Lensing 3D Explorer illustrates how alignment turns a background source into multiple images or arcs, switching between the real Einstein Cross and Abell 2218 cases in one 3D scene - NASA/ESA Hubble's own imagery wins as the source for the real photographs of both objects; the Einstein Ring 3D Explorer wins for the special symmetric case where the images merge into one full ring.
